# n1-fortune-reading

- **Scene ID:** n1-fortune-reading
- **Night:** 1, at grey dawn after the procession (close of chapter 1), set piece 3
- **Kingdom slot / building:** K1 / Bazaar: Old Vadoma's wagon at the edge of the market
- **Setting:** A painted wagon that has not moved in forty years, wheels sunk in the mud. Candles, an accordion on a hook, a deck of cards worn soft as cloth.
- **Mood:** Hushed, uncanny, the first real hope.
- **Cast:** Narrator, Old Vadoma, Sister Agnes Hallow, Emeric Lutz, Tomas Varga (variant)
- **Targets:** untagged moments, lines and cues play on `dashboard` (Mac screen and speakers; tower cues fire from the Mac). Moments tagged `ipad:<hunter>` play only on that hunter's iPad, never on the dashboard. Variant moments (`scene.moment~variant`) replace the base moment of the same number when their condition holds.
- **Kingdoms are never spoken.** The drawn kingdom is shown by the drum glyph and the board image; Vadoma names only the building, as a variant per draw.
