## Backstories
- **Sister Agnes Hallow**: kept the infirmary at Saint Ysolde and nursed a fevered traveler (the Count) who emptied it in four nights. He spared her because she prayed for his soul, as Ludmila once did.
- **Tomas Varga**: twenty years in the castle guard; carried his niece Marta over the wall and lost two fingers doing it. Marta is half-turned and keeps the castle's little gate.
- **Wren Kessel**: the gravedigger's daughter, who staked her own father. Her mother Ilka hid the infant Lenka at the church door, and the Count took Ilka for it. The "blank" bolt bears Ilka's name, scratched by Ilka herself.
- **Emeric Lutz**: apothecary's apprentice. His master Gerhardt's cipher, keyed to the Tome's first line, confesses that Gerhardt sold the Count Lenka's name.
- **Hanne**: keeper of the Lantern & Bell; cleaver in her apron, garlic at every window.
- **Father Oskar**: Vellmoor's priest; split the lair riddle into four letters; buried the morning the hunters came; rose.
- **The Count (Vlad)**: first son of the old house. He loved Ludmila, who loved his brother Sergiu; he made a bargain and killed Sergiu with his own sword, and she stepped from the window rather than open it for him.
- **Lenka Morosz**: nineteen, the burgomaster's foundling daughter; presses mountain violets. Taken by the Count the night before play begins (D-DRA-10). She is Ludmila reborn (confirmed by the Tome), and she refuses him every night.
- **Anton Morosz**: burgomaster for thirty years; died on the stair defending her door. Heard only in the maid's account.
- **Ludmila / Sergiu**: the original lovers; heard through the Tome, seen silently in the Rescued ending.
- **Old Vadoma**: a fortune-teller trapped by the mist forty years ago.
- **Dorka Halasz**: Lenka's friend; ran up the hill after her, and rose at the wake.
- **Jakob Brenner**: the castle's bookkeeper, now a self-committed fly-eater; wrote the shipping orders for the earth boxes.
- **Marta Varga**: Tomas's niece; pours the Count's wine; chooses to open the little gate.
- **Ilka Kessel**: Wren's mother, the third bride (shown in the app only).
- **Procession ghosts**: Mother Ursula (Agnes's abbess), Sergeant Dorn (Tomas's gate partner), Jozef Kessel (Wren's father), Gerhardt Albrecht (Emeric's master).
