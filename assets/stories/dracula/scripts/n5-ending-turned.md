# n5-ending-turned

- **Scene ID:** n5-ending-turned
- **Night:** any (loss: a hunter takes a third corruption; plays immediately, on whichever night it happens)
- **Kingdom slot / building:** Wherever it happens
- **Setting:** One of the hunters, standing very still, with eyes gone the wrong colour.
- **Mood:** Horror softened by tenderness; the cruelest version of the old story.
- **Cast:** Narrator, the turned hunter (one variant per hunter), The Count, Lenka Morosz (variant ~night5)
- **Targets:** untagged moments, lines and cues play on `storyboard` (the Story board: Mac screen and speakers, display only; tower cues fire from the Mac; all taps and votes are on the iPads). Moments tagged `ipad:<hunter>` play only on that hunter's iPad, never on the Story board. Variant moments (`scene.moment~variant`) replace the base moment of the same number when their condition holds.
- **Variant rule:** moments .02 and .03 play the variant for the hunter who took the third corruption. Private .04 lines play on every present iPad; the turned hunter's own iPad plays `.04.private.<hunter>~turned`.

## Moments

**n5-ending-turned.01** [clip]
[music: theme in minor, one violin] [sfx: a long exhale, wings settling] [tower: dim-all] [tower: heartbeat-fast] then [tower: heartbeat-half]
> **Narrator:** The third corruption is not the worst one. It is only the last. Somewhere a heartbeat stops, and another one starts, slower.

**n5-ending-turned.02~agnes** [clip]
> **Narrator:** Sister Agnes goes still. Then her reliquary drops from her hand, and the burn on her palm is gone.
> **Agnes:** I prayed for him for ten years. I should have prayed for myself.

**n5-ending-turned.02~tomas** [clip]
> **Narrator:** Tomas Varga goes still. Then he lowers the axe and turns to face the hunters, the way he used to face the gate.
> **Tomas:** I stood his gate three years. Looks like I'm standing it again.

**n5-ending-turned.02~wren** [clip]
> **Narrator:** Wren Kessel goes still. Then the crossbow drops, and she smells the air, and smiles at the smell.
> **Wren:** Mother's humming. I can hear it properly now.

**n5-ending-turned.02~emeric** [clip]
> **Narrator:** Emeric Lutz goes still. Then his spectacles fall, and he does not pick them up; he can see perfectly in the dark.
> **Emeric:** Page two hundred and twelve. I was wrong. It isn't frightening at all.

**n5-ending-turned.03** [clip]
[sfx: a window latch, wings]
> **The Count:** "Come freely. Go safely; and leave something of the happiness you bring!" *(Stoker)* The rest of you may go. This one stays.

## Outcome
Loss (base rule D-DRA-5: a third corruption loses the game). Lenka stays with him.

**n5-ending-turned.05** [still]
> **Narrator:** The mist comes down over the road again. In the castle, one more window is lit.
