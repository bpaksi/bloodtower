# n4-finding-the-tome

- **Scene ID:** n4-finding-the-tome
- **Night:** 4 (after midnight, before dawn), set piece 9, the key of K4
- **Kingdom slot / building:** K4 / the fortune's Tome building (variant per building)
- **Setting:** A heavy book bound in faded green leather, clasped with a brass lock shaped like a closed eye. The pages are in the Count's beautiful old hand. Between them, flowers pressed four hundred years ago.

## Outcome
The Tome is held (key 3). The ambiguity is resolved: Lenka is Ludmila reborn, and the soul has refused him every life, including this one. The party knows the win: free her, let her refuse him to his face, and strike with Sergiu's sword. Night 5 opens → `n5-drawbridge`.
