`script.yaml` is generated by `python3 scripts/dracula-pack-convert.py` from `assets/stories/dracula/scripts/*.md` (19 scenes + tokens-and-encounters.md); `cast.yaml` is hand-written from `cast.md`. 471 speaking moments, 657 lines, 27 cast ids. 146 moments carry `target: ipad:<hunter>`.

Conventions: line id = `<moment>.vo-NN` (matches manifest line ids); `(thought)`, `(far off)` etc. become `note`; `(whisper)` becomes a separate `<id>-whisper` cast entry (format 2.1); `(beat)` becomes `[pause 1]`; em dashes become `...` (Stoker's "you, with" keeps its comma); a mid-line stage direction splits the line there and becomes the second part's note; a trailing one becomes `note: after: ...`; an italic aside ending a thought line (nx-pressed-flower) becomes its own line voiced by `<hunter>-whisper`; Stoker tags become `note: source: ...`. Max 2 non-narrator speakers per moment: 0 violations. Longest line ~20.0 s est.

## Ambiguities
- `target` is not a script.yaml field in format 2.1; added so private lines stay targeted (the moment id also carries it).
- Trailing directions that are scene action, not delivery ("he is gone; the wolves howl again", "he shrugs off the light once; per mechanics the battle is harder"), sit in `note` as `after:`; they belong in the moment's cues/still, not the voice.
- Italic asides in nx-pressed-flower are read as the hunter's whispered inner thought, so 4 new cast ids (`agnes-whisper`, `tomas-whisper`, `wren-whisper`, `emeric-whisper`).
- Portraits/mouths are manifest ids (format says asset ids, not paths); anchors are placeholders.
