# Modifier effects

Status: **draft for Bob's review**, 2026-10-20. Cards: AREA-058, IDEA-027, DEC-067, Q-034, Q-035.

What the RtDT cards and virtues can do, and the effect kinds a Bloodtower card could have. This is the research Bob asked for before deciding between a digital hand and cards as props (Q-034).

## What we know about RtDT

Individual card text is not published online. The rulebook gives only examples, and none of Bob's box photos show card text. The rules below come from the RulesPal rulebook copy and [game-overview-and-rules.md](../return-to-dark-tower/game-overview-and-rules.md).

| Card | Count | Limit | How gained | When spent or lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gear | 18 (6 types × 3) | One of each | Bazaar reinforce | Back to its stack |
| Treasure | 22 | 4 | Market of 3, enhanced Bazaar (2 spirit) | Bottom of the deck |
| Potion | 18 | None | Citadel reinforce, haggle die | Bottom of the deck |
| Corruption | 12 | A 3rd loses | Can't pay a loss, a skull on a destroyed building, a home building lost, effects | Sanctuary (5 spirit) |
| Virtue | 16 (12 hero, 4 kingdom) | 3 active, 3 to unlock | Citadel (5 spirit) flips one | Can't be traded |
| Companion | 10 | | Companion quests, events, monthly quest | Can be traded |
| Quest item | | None | Quests | To the box |

**Advantages** are how most items help in a fight. There are six types that match foe and dungeon traits (Beast, Magic, Humanoid, Melee, Undead, Stealth), plus Wild, which counts as any type.

- **Automatic Advantages** (black background) are free and are counted before the fight.
- **Conditional Advantages** (grey background) cost something and can be spent mid-fight.
- In a battle, each Advantage moves a battle card's result one step better.
- In a dungeon, one Advantage improves a room, and each room can be improved once.
- A hero can use at most 10 Advantages per action, and unused ones vanish when the action ends.

Example cards from the rulebook:
- *Longswords* (gear): a Melee Advantage.
- *Dusky Cloaks* and *Leather Armor*: can be spent to meet a quest.
- *Potion of One Thousand Strides*: moves other heroes.
- *Generous* (virtue): after a Cleanse, remove a skull from another building.
- *Relic Hunter's Inventive* (virtue): triggered by spending 4 potions.
- *Hakan the Artificer*: recruited by spending 2 gear in the Ash Hills.
- *Letha the Dryad*: moves siege tree tokens.
- *Miras the Horselord*: uses caravan tokens.

**RtDT effect kinds:**
- Gain or lose warriors, spirit or items.
- Gain Advantages.
- Change movement or Reinforce.
- Remove skulls, foes, corruptions or seals.
- Spend items to trigger virtues or quests.
- Move heroes or tokens.
- Trade.

Most of these are paid in warriors and spirit, which Bloodtower has dropped (DEC-039).

## Effect kinds a Bloodtower card could have (proposed)

Each kind connects to a system we have already decided. A card's effect is one kind, plus a size and a timing.

| Kind | What it changes | Connects to |
|---|---|---|
| Fight strength | Adds to strength in every fight, or only against one foe trait (an Advantage) | DEC-014, Q-023 |
| Fight luck | A re-roll, take a hit for a present hero, or run without the horde growing | DEC-015, DEC-016 |
| Health | Max HP, heal now, or heal more on rest; the same for mana if magic is in | IDEA-009, Q-018 |
| Counter | Cancels one event type (healer against plague, scout against getting lost) | MEC-006, DEC-027 |
| Movement | An extra move option, a longer or shorter roll, or move another group | MEC-025, DEC-034 |
| Money | Earn more, pay less, or a better haggle | DEC-008, OPT-006 |
| Information | Shows whether a rumour is true, or a peek at the next event | DEC-026 |
| Board | Remove a skull, protect a building, or turn a drum to a glyph | DEC-064, DEC-006 |
| Penalty | The corruption side of any kind above: less strength, lose HP each day, a dearer purse | DEC-012 |

**Timing:**
- **Always on** while held: gear, treasure, virtues, companions, corruptions.
- **Spent once:** potions.
- **Once per chapter:** stronger effects.
- **Triggered** by an event: "when you fight Undead".

**Stacking:** RtDT caps Advantages at 10 per action, and R-FUN-4 asks for resistance to stacking. That is still open.

## Gaps

- **The full text of each card in Bob's box.** A photo of each card face (potions, gear, treasures, corruptions, virtues, companions; about 96 cards) would show which effects already fit these kinds and which need inventing. It matters only if the cards are used as props (Q-034).
- **Whether battle Advantages are kept** as trait matching (Q-035). DEC-014 already counts "matching Advantages" in party strength.
