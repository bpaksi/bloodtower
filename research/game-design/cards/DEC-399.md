Bob chose "Extra pick, once a day".

- **RtDT:** Reinforce twice at one building.
- **Bloodtower:** RtDT's Reinforce becomes a building pick (DEC-157). Once per day, when the group holding Fortunate takes a pick at a building, it may take a second pick at the same building for no extra move. The second pick can be any pick on that building's menu, Cleanse included (DEC-377), and it triggers Inspiring again (DEC-389).

Rejected: the same pick twice, and a second pick on every visit.
