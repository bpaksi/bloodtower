On the Mac's speakers, not the tower (DEC-767).

- **Start:** the base pulse begins after the horn (CUE-010).
- **Each round:** the Grim layer rises when the rough read of the odds (DEC-312) turns against the party, and the Good layer when it turns for them.
- **The clash:** silence while the round resolves (DEC-328).
- **End:** the victory or defeat ending; a retreat fades the pulse out.
- **Slot:** one engine track for every foe, plus the adversary's own. A story or chapter may replace either (DEC-535).
