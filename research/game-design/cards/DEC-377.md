Bob chose "A building pick".

- **Where:** Cleanse is a pick on the menu of every building that still stands (citadel, sanctuary, village, bazaar). A destroyed building has none (DEC-371).
- **What:** the group spends the pick and 1 skull comes off that building. Its menu gets back what that skull had taken (DEC-093, DEC-371).
- **Cards:** Generous, Lamp of Hope and Relic of Light hook onto this pick, and the Feverish corruption forbids it, close to their RtDT wording (Q-269).

This is the one pick every building type shares; DEC-157 still holds for each building's own Reinforce. Rejected: a Cleanse paid with gold or moves, skull removal only as a story reward, and no standing Cleanse at all.
