**Parked 2026-11-12 (Gamewright, DEC-777):** Bob's DEC-777 (2026-11-05, accepted in DEC-786) holds caravans out of 1.1 and says this card waits with AST-B25. The rules below stay as written for a story that asks. UI-064, drafted the day after and accepted in a bulk "accept all", is parked with it. If Bob wants caravans in 1.1 after all, un-park this card, UI-064 and AST-B25 together.

Each of the three caravans (N, W, E) follows its kingdom's trade route and moves along it at the end of each day, after the lieutenants (DEC-086, step 6b). A group that reaches a caravan gets an encounter, as at a building: trade, rumours that can be leads, hired help, at 1 move per pick (DEC-078). A group on a caravan's space when it leaves stays behind.

A story may switch on more: escort jobs (the caravan moves only when a group spends moves on it) or caravans as a kingdom's lifeline (a raided caravan raises that kingdom's prices).
