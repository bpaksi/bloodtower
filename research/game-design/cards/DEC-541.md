Bob chose "Hook". A slot the engine plays moments and effects at is a hook: a loop hook at a loop's start or end (DEC-540), or a trigger hook at one of the trigger nodes (DEC-290). "Story entry point" and "story hook" are retired, since the base may fill any hook. AREA-066 is now "Hooks". Older cards keep their wording until they are next edited. Rejected: renaming every old card now, and keeping "story hook".
