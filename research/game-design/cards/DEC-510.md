Bob: "This changes the whole dynamic of the game from a small part to nearly army size movements. For the stories I want to tell Hit Points make more sense." In both originals, warriors are an army you recruit and split. Bloodtower's health belongs to one hero, so it is a new concept and keeps its own name.

Recorded by Bob on 2026-10-23 in the design session on branch `chore/design-session-2026-10-22d`, which never merged; ported 2026-10-28 with a new ID. The renames were not applied to the other cards, which still use the old words: Q-328 asks whether to apply them.
