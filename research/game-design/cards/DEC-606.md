Claude proposed six traps from RtDT's tomb rooms. Bob: "For 1.0 let's go with 2 traps, 1 artwork each; Pit, falling rocks." On the costs he picked "Pit hits one, Rocks hit all".

| Trap | Loss (bold) | Painting |
|---|---|---|
| Pit | one hero, the group's pick, loses 4 hit points | 1 |
| Falling Rocks | each hero in the group loses 2 hit points | 1 |

- **Can't pay:** a hero who cannot pay the loss in full pays what they can and takes a corruption, as in RtDT.
- **On guard** halves the loss (DEC-607).
- **Which trap:** dealt with the tomb on first entry (DEC-602).
- **More traps** come with 1.1 and the other dungeon types.

The numbers are placeholders for the P-02 simulation.
