```
Group turn    spend moves until 0 or the group ends its turn
 └ Encounter  pick from the menu, 1 move each
    ├ Battle      rounds until won, ran or fallen
    └ Quest   its own steps or rooms until done or left
       └ Battle   rounds …
End of day: an event, which may start a battle
```
