DEC-370 makes the virtue tiles physical. The box has 4 hero boards, which have 8 starting virtues printed on them, plus 12 hero tiles and 4 kingdom tiles. Bloodtower starts with 6 heroes (DEC-099), each class with 6 virtues (DEC-156), so 36 virtues. Options: classes share tiles, classes have fewer virtues, the starting virtues stay app-only, or the virtues without a tile stay app-only. Spells have no RtDT tile at all; whether spells stay virtues (DEC-121) or become their own thing is also open here.
