Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 9, during the skull drop.
- **Played once sent:** a tower node (a cue, sound, light show or seal light) counts as played once the app sends it, even if the link drops part-way. After Reconnect and the tower check (MEC-018), play resumes at the next node.
- **Why:** the tower safety rules allow no automatic retry of a physical action. Tower nodes are cosmetic: no rule waits on one.
- **What the table needs is not a tower node.** The seal to break is shown on the board (MEC-033 .19), and a skull's landing is entered by hand (MEC-034 .14, .15). So nothing is lost.

Not picked: replay the cut-off node after Reconnect; ask the table each time.
