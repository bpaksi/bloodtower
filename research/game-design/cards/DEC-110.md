DEC-281 (Bob, 2026-10-26) moved steps 1 (the tower block) and 3 (lay out the board) to the session, once per evening (MEC-039). Noted under DEC-352, 2026-10-27; the title still holds.

**Engine core, in every game:**
1. The tower block: connect, calibrate, sync the drums, orient (DEC-104).
2. Party join (DEC-098).
3. Lay out the board.
4. Seals and skulls.
5. Place the heroes by the story's start option (DEC-112).
6. The starting purse.

**Then the story's extras,** from its manifest: its special tokens, NPC cards, quest markers and the like. A story cannot remove or reorder a core step. Today's test pack lists all its steps itself (bloodtower spec D31); that changes when this is built.
