Playtest PT-06 (2026-11-12): two groups roll two 2d3 and may each play a quest in a day, which makes splitting the strongest strategy and no probe assumed it.

- **Ruling.** `day.shifts.per_split_group: -1`, a placeholder in the tuning file. Each group's roll is shifted by -1 with a floor of 2 moves while the party is split.
- **It applies to every group** while split, a group of one included. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (Q4.3).
- DEC-640's "each group rolls its own" and DEC-821's "a quest pick ends the group's turn" stand.
- **Parked:** the P-008 probe that tunes the number. The placeholder ships.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
