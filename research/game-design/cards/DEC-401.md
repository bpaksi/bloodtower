Bob chose "Own asset, tied to its painting". A mask has its own ID, `<painting-id>.glow`: a greyscale WebP at the painting's exact size, painted in the studio or made in AI mode. The lint fails a mask whose size does not match its painting. A camera move belongs to the moment (DEC-205, DEC-400), so it is data in the job and never an asset. Rejected: baking the mask into the painting file, and masks that any painting may share.
