One railroad (DEC-276) that can fire between any two nodes of any loop (DEC-282): a press of Pause, an iPad dropping (DEC-154), or the tower dropping (DEC-301).

- **Owner:** engine. No entry points or trigger hooks.
- **Enter:** .1, the running loop holds at the node it stopped before.
- **Body:** .2 to .13, one interrupt per pass until all clear or Quit, so several at once are handled in turn.
- **Exit:** .13: resumed, every hero seated, and the tower back or switched off; or Quit.
- **Close:** .14 to .16: Quit keeps the autosave and hands "quit" up through every loop (DEC-208); otherwise play resumes at the held node.
- **Result up:** resume, or quit.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277).

Quit asks to confirm (DEC-230). A failed reconnect goes round again. Back and Replay (DEC-227) are not interrupts. The switch to the no-tower stand-ins waits on Q-176. The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277).

```railroad
enter
  .1  change  Hold the running loop at the node it stopped before   DEC-282
body
  repeat
    .2  check   Paused, an iPad gone, or the tower lost?     DEC-282
      track paused
        .3  board   Paused                                   SCR-017
        .4  prompt  Resume or Quit, from any iPad            SCR-017 DEC-230
      track ipad-gone
        .5  board   Waiting for the heroes without a device, and the join QR   DEC-154
        .6  prompt  A device claims a hero                   DEC-154
        .7  change  Seat the hero                            DEC-098
      track tower-lost
        .8  board   The tower is lost: Reconnect, Go on without it, or Quit   DEC-301
        .9  prompt  One of the three, confirmed              DEC-301 DEC-230
        .10 check   Which?                                   DEC-301
          track reconnect
            .11 call    Tower check                          MEC-018
          track go-on-without
            .12 change  Switch to the no-tower stand-ins     DEC-301 Q-176
          track quit
    .13 check   Quit, or all clear (resumed, every hero seated, tower back or off)? Neither: again   DEC-282 DEC-154
close
  .14 check   Quit?                                          DEC-208
    track quit
      .15 change  Keep the autosave; hand "quit" up through every loop   DEC-208 DEC-155
    track resume
      .16 change  Resume at the held node                    DEC-282
```
