**What:** The story's one villain, on the board, pressing the heroes the same way in every story; the finale is the fight against it.

**When:** Every story, exactly one. Give it a voice the table hears through the game, not only at the end.

**Why:** The villain is the face of the threat; heard early and often, the finale pays off.

**Pack:** the villain's cast entry and lines (§2, §2.1); the adversary card it uses (AST-B24).
