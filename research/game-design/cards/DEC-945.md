D6 brief (bloodtower #503). DEC-399 and DEC-913 give a group holding Fortunate a second pick at the same building, once per day, for no move, and end the pending pick when it is taken or the group says Leave. Three things were not said: two holders in one group, whether leaving without it spends the day's use, and whether the free pick still costs gold.

- **One a day per group.** A group gets one free pick a day however many of its heroes hold Fortunate, as Tempted's +2 is once however many hold it (DEC-477).
- **Spent only when taken.** Leaving without it, or a pick that ends the turn first (DEC-913), ends the pending pick but does not spend the day's use. The group's first pick at the next building it enters that turn makes it pending again.
- **No move, but its gold.** The free pick costs no move. A priced pick still costs its price (DEC-896, DEC-911); "free" in DEC-399 is the move.

Reason: the plainest reading of "once per day" is one free pick taken per day; DEC-399's rejected option was "a second pick on every visit", which this keeps out.

Not picked: two holders give two free picks (DEC-399 says "the group"); the day's use is spent by the first pick even if the free one is never taken (a Leave would then cost the group its virtue for nothing); the free pick is free of gold too (DEC-399 speaks only of the move).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
