An NPC in an encounter tells the party where a quest marker is, and the marker goes on the board. The location is valid for a number of turns, so the party can travel to it. When the window runs out the marker comes off the board, because it moved; another NPC can reveal it again. Leads are add-ons, like RtDT quests: they bring rewards but are never needed to finish the game (DEC-088).
