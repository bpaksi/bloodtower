**What:** The story's paintings for the kinds of place (village, citadel, sanctuary, bazaar, caravan) and their sub-locations, swapping the engine's.

**When:** When the story's world looks different from the engine's default: gothic villages, a ruined citadel.

**Why:** Buildings are visited every turn; their paintings set the story's look more than any other art.

**Pack:** place painting slots (§13). The count is kinds of place painted.
