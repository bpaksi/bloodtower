Bob chose Claude's draft. Numbers come at playtest.

| Goal | He hunts | A landed strike |
|---|---|---|
| Replace a hero | The hero with the most bites | Adds a bite |
| Win Lenka | Her hero | Lowers her loyalty, then takes her (DEC-746) |
| Destroy the blade | Its site, then its carrier | Weakens the blade |
| Darkness over the valley | The building with the most skulls | Adds a skull there |

Darkness also adds one extra drop at each chapter's end, not daily, since skulls are already the game's clock (DEC-686).

**Castle moment:** Bob saw only Replace a hero's (he takes that hero's place at the swap room). The other three are drafts on their goal cards.
