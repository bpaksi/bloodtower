`feel.lengthMs` is set only where Bob timed the sound in the review tool: SND-001…010, SND-087…089 and SND-093…113. SND-011…086 and SND-090…092 have none. The tower does not report when a sound ends, so a cue that waits for a sound to finish needs a length. Bob: "I didn't get the length of each sound, is that a problem?" It is not for now; the field is optional.

Options: measure each sound from the silence gaps in Bob's batch recordings (a measured length, not his Play-to-Stopped time, so not written to `feel:`), time each one on the tower when a cue first needs it, or leave them unset.
