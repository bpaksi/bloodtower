Bob: "Rivers separate kingdoms. When a space is adjacent to spaces in different kingdoms they are separated by rivers."

- **A river crossing** is any move between two neighbouring spaces whose kingdoms differ. The engine reads it from the board's neighbours and each space's kingdom (DEC-694), so the map tool marks nothing and DEC-808 stands.
- **One river per border:** the river between two kingdoms runs along every neighbour pair that joins them.
- **Amulet of the Marid** (DEC-445) stays as written. Claude's reading, for Bob to check in playtest: when the holder's group crosses from kingdom A to kingdom B, it may move to any space in A or B that neighbours the other kingdom, at no extra move.

Not picked: a river mark on each neighbour pair in the map tool, rewording the amulet for lakes only, and leaving the amulet out.
