Found by the first probe on day 1, as group A walks to *The Ambush* (DEC-636) on Muted Forest. DEC-089 marks a wild quest with a dungeon token, a special encounter whose only option is the quest. Dungeon tokens wait for 1.1 (DEC-502, DEC-633), and the Tomb "sits on a quest marker" (DEC-524). But the group turn offers Enter only on a building, caravan or story token (MEC-035), so no card says a quest marker in the wild can be entered.
