Bob chose "In the event, talk is lines only". The event's people track (MEC-072) draws the offer, calls the talk pick for the person's lines (MEC-073), then resolves the offer itself: a rumour is the talk's nugget, trade calls the buy pick (MEC-074), a companion asks which hero takes it (DEC-066, DEC-072), and a trick springs a hazard with two costs. The talk pick never resolves an offer, so the hazard nodes are not drawn twice. What a paid job is stays open (Q-191). Rejected: the talk pick taking an optional offer with a track per kind.
