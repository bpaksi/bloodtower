Bob: "I believe story beats are always tied to something happening. NPC, Building visit, ... I think we need to define these. I don't think story beats are tied to "time" specifically"

A story beat is a story hook (DEC-285, DEC-286) that fires on a trigger: something that happens in play. The chapter loop has no "beats due at the start of the day" step, and DEC-141's "on day 3" table beat goes. The list of triggers is Q-187. Where the hooks sit, given that only the game, chapters and quests have entry points (DEC-140), is Q-188.
