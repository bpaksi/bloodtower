Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. The finale desk walk of probe P-003 found the gap at the boss battle.
- **Traits:** Melee and Humanoid. He is a fallen knight, not a dead one (DEC-667), so not Undead. Weapon Master matches him (+1). His trait cards are Claws with +1 (P5).
- **Ability:** Crushing blow (DEC-553): a lost round's hit is 1 bigger. At phase 2 it is 2 bigger, and at phase 3 it is 3 bigger. A Rend in phase 3 is 2 × 5 + 3 = 13.
- **The engine rule** that this fills: DEC-567's "its ability grows" means +1 to the ability's number at each phase. A story's adversary names an ability that has a number.
- **Why:** it is a knight's ability, readable on the foe panel, and it makes the phases matter to a party without spells.

Not picked: Shield wall (the Oreks' garrison already has it); Ward (it only punishes spells, and this party has none).

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
