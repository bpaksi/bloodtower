Bob's answer to Q-146: the regions on the map are a few named areas and should be first-class objects, not text. Every space belongs to exactly one region. Bob can name them now. This changes DEC-225, which said a region label gets no ID; the label now becomes a region with its own ID, and the spaces point at it. The note in the entry tool (DEC-239) is then free text only.
