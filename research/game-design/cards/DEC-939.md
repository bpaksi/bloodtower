D2 brief (bloodtower #500), open question OQ-D2-2. Falling Rocks takes 2 from each hero (DEC-606). DEC-906 says a trap's third corruption loses the game "before anything else resolves", but not whether the other heroes still pay when one hero's fall is the third.

- **Ruling:** Falling Rocks is one effect. Every present hero pays the loss, and every hero at or below it falls (one corruption, back at 1 hit point, DEC-906). Then the loss check runs once. If any hero now holds a third corruption, the game is lost at once (DEC-676), with the reason "third corruption".
- **"Before anything else"** in DEC-906 means anything after the trap: no Tomb result, no room prompt, no quest result. It does not cut the trap itself in half.
- **No hero order.** The result never depends on seat or tap order (as DEC-655). The corruptions are drawn from the seed in the group's hero order only so a replay is the same.
- **The Pit** hits one hero, so it is unchanged.

Reason: rocks fall on everyone at once, so the table pays them at once; it needs no order and matches DEC-907's "the check runs after each effect".

Not picked: stop at the first hero whose fall is the third (needs a hero order that no card gives, and the record would depend on seat).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
