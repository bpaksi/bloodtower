D6 brief (bloodtower #503). DEC-912 says the band counts forward with the due-drop schedule, assuming no quest wins. Q-C3-04 (Bob approved 2026-10-31) counts the actual nights the pattern gives. Solo and pair schedules now have nights that drop nothing, and a quest won today means tonight drops nothing.

- **Ruling:** days left is the number of nights, from the next counted night (N + 1) on, until the due drops add up to the supply. A night that drops nothing counts as a day. A night whose drop is larger than what is left still counts.
- **A quest won today** (and the skip on): tonight drops nothing, so the band adds 1 for tonight, as a Cleanse's skull adds a day (DEC-686).
- **Examples:** supply 12 from N = 1: solo 23, pair 15, three or four heroes 8. Supply 23 from N = 2: pair 30.

Reason: Q-C3-04's "count the actual nights" carried over to the new schedules; the band then tells the table the true number of nights left.

Not picked: skip no-drop nights in the count (the band would understate the time); ignore today's won quest until tomorrow (the band would be one short all evening).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
