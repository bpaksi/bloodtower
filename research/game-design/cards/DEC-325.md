Partly replaced by DEC-369 (Bob, 2026-10-27): RtDT's 12 already include Brigands and the Dragon, so no 1981 types join; 1981 lends only their names and sounds. The two bullets below are kept as history.

Bob chose "RtDT 12 + DT81 classics". The engine ships RtDT's 12 foe types with their cards and tokens, plus 1981 classics as engine foes:

- **Brigands:** the 1981 foe fight, the plainest foe.
- **The Dragon:** in 1981 it struck and took a quarter of the gold and warriors; slain with the Dragonsword (dark-tower-1981/NOTES.md).
- **Plague:** a 1981 threat that costs warriors, with a Healer helping. It is not a battle, so it likely belongs to the event deck rather than the foe list (Q-216).

The classics have no tokens or cards in the box, so each borrows an RtDT token as a stand-in. Rejected: RtDT's 12 only, and an all-new Bloodtower foe list.
