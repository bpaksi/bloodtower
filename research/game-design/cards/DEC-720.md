Bob picked "3 visible steps" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session.

- **Shown:** on the hero's Look up, and announced when it changes. Few steps make every change an event.
- **Devoted:** strengthens the companion's one effect (DEC-070).
- **Steady:** the effect as written.
- **Wavering:** the companion risks walking out or turning (DEC-071).

Not picked: a visible 0 to 10 meter, a hidden value read from the companion's lines, and no loyalty at all.
