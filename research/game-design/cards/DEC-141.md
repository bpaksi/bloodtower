- **The table beat** is a chapter's timed beat. The chapter says when it plays ("on day 3", or "when flag X is set") and what it does: narrate, place or move story tokens. The engine checks the chapter's beats at the start of each day (beat 2 of DEC-077).
- **The group beat** belongs to the set piece on that spot. A group that starts its turn there hears it ("strange noises from beneath the tavern"); the set piece itself starts only when the group picks it (DEC-083).
