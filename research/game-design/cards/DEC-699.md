Bob chose "Won quest skips the drop".

- **The rule:** on a day when any group wins a quest, that night has no skull drop, at every length. With the tower, the app does not prompt the drop. Without it, the simulated drop (DEC-305) is skipped.
- **Whole night:** the skip covers the night's whole drop, so the extra drop of 3 or 4 heroes (DEC-564) is skipped too. Two quests won on one day still skip only one night.
- **On the board:** the day's effects panel shows "+1 day", as for a Cleanse (DEC-686). The days-left count is unchanged that night.
- **Why:** P-003's pair needed about 25 days at Standard and had 24. With its six quests skipping a night each, it would have had about 30, and reached the Tower with days to spare. A stalled party still runs out.

Claude added the whole-night and two-quests details; Bob to say if either is wrong. Rejected: a drop rate set per length, both together, and fewer quests at Standard.
