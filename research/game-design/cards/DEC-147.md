It mirrors Enter. The day's Close is the end of the day, DEC-086 steps 1 to 7; steps 8 and 9 are the chapter's exit check. A battle's Close is its reward (DEC-120). Most loops leave it empty.

Order: Enter, Start point, Body, Exit, Close, Result up, End point. The Close comes after the exit is met rather than before the check: the day's body (the groups' turns) stops when every group has played, and only then does the end of the day run.
