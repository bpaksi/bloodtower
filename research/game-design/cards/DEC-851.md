D-02 §5.

- Basement: Flooded Room, Swap, stair.
- Main: Banquet, Portrait, stair.
- Tower: Lift, Heart of Sorrow, lair.

At the Swap the Count speaks through the wight wearing the hunted hero's face, then leaves. The boss fight stays in the lair. In a solo Swap the companion vanishes. With no companion, the wight wears the hero's own face (OPEN RULING 12).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-10, under Bob's standing rule that design rulings are Gamewright's (relayed by Warden, 2026-11-10). Ruling 12: in a solo Swap the companion vanishes, or with no companion the wight wears the hero's own face.
