Bob chose "Armed ahead of time".

- **Arm:** in the use dialog (UI-038), the player arms a reactive card before its trigger, for example Blessed: "cancel the next hit to <hero>".
- **Fire:** the engine fires it when the trigger comes, and the board shows that it fired. Nothing waits for a tap.
- **Armed state:** the button's icon shows that something is armed. The player can disarm it in the dialog until it fires.

Rejected: a short window after the hit, and the engine firing Blessed by itself.
