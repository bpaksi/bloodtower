Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** Glyphs cost 1 more spirit.
- **Bloodtower:** Every buy the holder's group makes (MEC-074) costs a little more, after the haggle die. RtDT's glyph cost has no home, because seal glyphs are free boons (DEC-303), so Tempted pays more where Bloodtower spends gold. The amount belongs to the numbers pass. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).
