**What:** Where each quest starts: a highlighted first option in a building's menu, or a dungeon token in the wild. Main quest markers are fixed and never expire.

**When:** One for every quest. Spread them over kingdoms so the party travels.

**Why:** A quest marker is how the plot shows on the board; spreading them makes the map part of the story.

**Pack:** `objective.<chapter>` sites (§9).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 1 per quest | 1 per quest | 1 per quest |
| Target | 1 per quest, over 2 kingdoms | 1 per quest, over 3 kingdoms | 1 per quest, over 4 kingdoms |
