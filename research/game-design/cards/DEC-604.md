Bob first picked odds of 40 / 30 / 30 for nothing, battle and trap. Since the tomb is fixed on first entry (DEC-602), Claude proposed dealing that mix like a hand, so a seed cannot give one tomb four battles and the next none. Bob picked "Dealt mix from 40/30/30".

- **Deal:** the rooms other than the guardian's are split about 40 / 30 / 30, rounded, with at least 1 battle and 1 trap. The seed then places them.
- **Example:** a 6-room tomb has 5 rooms plus the guardian: 2 nothing, 2 battles and 1 trap, or 2 nothing, 1 battle and 2 traps.
- **Target:** the guardian room is always the guardian battle (DEC-491).

The split is a placeholder for the P-02 simulation (DEC-131). Not picked: per-room odds (streaky); dealt with battles capped at one per three rooms.
