Moves are the one currency of a group's turn. Walking one space costs 1 move. Each action inside a building (its base action, a sub-location, an NPC, a story point) costs 1 move, and a group may take several if it has the moves. Bob's example: 2 moves into a village, 1 for the tavern, 2 more into the wild is 5 moves.

Staying in town all turn is allowed; it costs the turn's travel.
