Bob: "I see it as it's own game loop. It is similar to dungeon encounter where a map is exposed with options to take, each room exposes more. A retreat keeps all game memory. The tower is multi-level. with a basement, main, and tower. sections. Base game artwork is very distinctive per level (including background sounds). Ample opportunity for Story to override look & feel. Battle with boss should feel special."

- **Its own loop** (MEC-076), shaped like a dungeon crawl (MEC-026): a map revealed as the group goes, with options to take; each room opens more.
- **Three levels:** basement, main and tower.
- **Memory:** a retreat keeps everything: rooms found and cleared, and the villain's damage (DEC-360).
- **Look and sound:** the engine's art and background sound are distinct for each level; a story may override any of it (DEC-199).
- **The boss battle** should feel special, beyond an ordinary battle (Q-265).

Rejected: approach, battle and ending only; a single trial before the battle; and the battle with hooks only.
