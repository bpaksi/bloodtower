Bob picked "At the chapter's end" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session.

A traitor nobody has unmasked springs the trick when the chapter ends, with the story's written effect, and is revealed. The party knows it has until then, and every story gets the same clock. This needs each set of suspects tied to one chapter. Only a won chapter runs the step, since a lost chapter has already ended the game.

Not picked: a story trigger beat (a trick might never fire), and a hidden seeded day (it can spring before the party has had any chance to find a clue).
