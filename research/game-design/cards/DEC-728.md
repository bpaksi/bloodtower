Bob asked Claude to draft all 10 in one pass, for his review.

- **RtDT:** +2 Beast. End your turn in a forest: gain 6 warriors.
- **Bloodtower:** When the holder's group ends its turn in a forest, the holder heals 3 hit points, up to their full hit points.
- **Why:** Warriors become hit points, as the item fit chart does. This is the one healing companion, so she stays distinct. The 3 is a placeholder for Q-404.

Accepted by Bob as written in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session; check it in the 1.1 playtest.
