- **Fighter:** fights and duels.
- **Healer:** cures and gives back hit points.
- **Magic user:** casts magic, and the shrine's glyph work.
- **Scout (rogue):** stealth, traps and the ruins.
- **Ranger:** hunts beasts and finds the way in the wild.
- **Scholar:** lore and riddles, and reads what others miss.

A party of up to 4 always leaves at least 2 roles out, so the picks at party join matter.
