Bob: "I think Glyphs is a whole subject that we haven't really thought out yet."

Glyphs needs a session of its own, high level first. What is decided so far: glyphs stay and a story gives them meaning (DEC-302), a broken seal reveals one as a boon (DEC-303), a game of chance runs on lights and sounds (DEC-304, IDEA-060), and they drop out without the tower (DEC-306). Not settled: whether the five glyphs (GLY-001 to GLY-005) have engine meanings tied to their RtDT names (Cleanse, Banner, Reinforce…), whether the kingdom a glyph faces matters (MEC-016 is parked), and how 1.0 uses them.
