Bob picked "Exact node, no replays". Probe P-002 found the gap on day 7, quitting mid-battle in the Barrow.
- **One engine rule:** on Continue, each loop in the saved call chain skips its enter and start steps and resumes its body at the saved node. That is MEC-033's and MEC-034's "Continuing?" check, made general. It covers the group turn, the place, the quest, the tomb, the battle, the scene and the Tower (MEC-035 to MEC-038, MEC-026, MEC-076, MEC-077).
- **What is saved:** everything the node needs. In a battle that is the foe's count and status, the round's revealed card, the shuffle left, each hero's hit points and spells used, and any hero sitting out. The seeded streams resume where they stopped.
- **Nothing fires twice:** no free cut, last attack, ability, turn-start virtue, cue or hook replays. Hooks are already once per game (DEC-291).
- **Diagrams:** the lower loops carry no extra check node. This card is the rule, and each loop links to it.

Not picked: back to the turn's start; a battle restarting from its foe panel.
