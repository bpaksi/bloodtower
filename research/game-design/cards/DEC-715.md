Bob, 2026-10-31 open-questions session: "In the c1 build there was no picking, just new story came up. I think that was because of other changes. Previous versions were really nice."

- **Design:** unchanged. Pick story is the W3 carousel as SCR-007 and UI-016 describe it: the focused cover large, the previous and next peeking, the choice made on an iPad.
- **The G-C1 problem:** at the playtest the table never saw the pick; a story came up on its own. That is a regression in the C1 flow, to be fixed test-first as a bug: Pick story always shows every loaded story and waits for a press before Game settings.
- **Where it lands:** C2 feature 14 becomes that bug fix (GC1-06).

Not picked: a redesign of Pick story.
