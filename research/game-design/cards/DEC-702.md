Bob chose "2 below the adversary".

- **The rule:** a Tower guard's level is the adversary's level − 2, in every room and on every Tower level. Under a level 5 Warden, every guard is level 3. Its count still follows the hero count (DEC-564).
- **The climb** is harder through the number of fights and traps, not the level. Retreat and come back with all memory kept stays (DEC-566).
- **Why:** with guards at +1 per Tower level, The Warden with the oath-blade was easier than his own stair guards (P-001, P-003).

Claude's simulator, one battle at full hit points with DEC-701's odds:

| Party | Guard, level 3 | Old stair guards, levels 4 and 5 | The Warden, level 5, oath-blade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo Ranger | 65% | 16%, 4% | 48% |
| Fighter and Bard | 61% | 9%, 1% | 50% |
| Four heroes | 96% | 65%, 23% | 71% |

The offset 2 is a placeholder for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131). Rejected: guards climbing to 1 below, guards only on the stairs, and more healing with the old levels.
