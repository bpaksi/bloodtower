Bob chose "Own loop card, called per group". The event loop (MEC-072) is a sibling of the set piece: it draws the event, shows it, the present heroes choose (DEC-019), it resolves and may call a battle (DEC-136). The day's close (MEC-034) repeats a call to it for each group that draws an event: one in the wild (DEC-080), or at a skulled building (DEC-093), oldest group first (DEC-148). It is drawn after the battle loop. Rejected: drawing the event's nodes inline in the day's close, and moving events into the group turn (which would reverse DEC-080).
