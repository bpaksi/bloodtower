Bob picked "Gold, hit points, corruption" in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review.

- **Gold:** taken from the group's purse, as a building price is (DEC-176). An option the group can't afford shows disabled, like a building pick (UI-043).
- **Hit points:** the amount is taken from each hero present.
- **Corruption:** one present hero the group picks takes it (DEC-019).
- **Items:** spending a held item is not a cost kind. It stays DEC-654's rule: the item is kept unless the option's effects say to spend it.

Not picked: gold and hit points only; adding a day off the skull clock (DEC-686) as a cost.

Open: what a hit-point cost does to a hero it would bring to 0 (Q-396).
