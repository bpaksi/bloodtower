**Recommendation.** What each card does is already ruled (DEC-411 to DEC-446, item-fit.md), and DEC-370 uses RtDT's treasures. Only the lines between DEC-120's piles were open.

| Foe level (after difficulty) | Pile |
|---|---|
| 1 | potion |
| 2 to 3 | gear |
| 4 and up | treasure |

- **Empty pile, or no present hero can hold the gear drawn:** draw from the next pile down. With no card left, pay 5 gold instead.
- **The draw** is seeded (DEC-067) and names one card. A fifth treasure still asks which to give back (DEC-424).

**Why:** on normal, a four-chapter story gives potions early and gear in the middle. Treasure comes from the last chapter, late guardians and hard games, so it stays the rarest. **Placeholder** until the 1.1 simulator run.

Accepted by Bob, 2026-11-05 (DEC-786), to check in the probes.
