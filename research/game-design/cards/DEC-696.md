Bob picked "A release field" in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review.

- **The field:** an optional `release:` in a card's front matter. `"1.1"` marks a ruling or deliverable that waits for 1.1 under DEC-502. A card with no field is 1.0. A parked card needs none.
- **The check:** the gaps report lists 1.0 gaps first and 1.1 gaps in their own section, so the 1.0 list is what the C2 plan must close.
- **Plans:** a C2 step's `Builds:` lists only 1.0 deliverables.
- **Build:** the same game-workbench change as DEC-695 adds the field to the card lint, the gaps check and `cards/README.md`. The cards are tagged after it merges.

Not picked: linking 1.1 rulings like any other, with the plan reading DEC-502 for scope; leaving 1.1 rulings unlinked in one mixed report.
