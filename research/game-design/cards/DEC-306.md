Bob chose "Seals and glyphs drop out". Without the tower the simulated drop never holds a skull back, no seal breaks at a chapter win, and no glyph is revealed. A story must not depend on a glyph boon to be winnable. Rejected: the app tracking seals, held skulls and glyphs.
