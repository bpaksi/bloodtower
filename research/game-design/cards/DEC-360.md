Bob chose "Retreat; damage kept".

- **Retreat:** retreating works as in any battle: a move left, one last attack, fall back a space (DEC-313, DEC-318). DEC-687's no-move exception inside the Tower is retired (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden): a Tower retreat costs that same move, including between Tower rooms.
- **Kept damage:** the adversary keeps what was cut from it, as RtDT's adversary battle keeps its improvements and a standing foe keeps its count (DEC-320). The next group, or the same one, picks up where it stopped.
- **Confirmed (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden):** the Warden's battle allows a retreat. Damage already taken is kept. A retreat ends the battle. It does not restore his wounds, and it is not a win.
- Groups can wear the adversary down in turns.

Rejected: the adversary back to full strength after a retreat, and no retreat from the final battle.
