Bob chose "Retreat; damage kept".

- **Retreat:** running works as in any battle: a move left, one last attack, fall back a space (DEC-313, DEC-318).
- **Kept damage:** the adversary keeps what was cut from it, as RtDT's adversary battle keeps its improvements and a standing horde keeps its count (DEC-320). The next group, or the same one, picks up where it stopped.
- Groups can wear the adversary down in turns.

Rejected: the adversary back to full strength after a retreat, and no retreat from the final battle.
