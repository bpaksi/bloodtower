- **Between attacks:** the ledge and base lights breathe (LFX-003); after the last pick they move to Breathe Fast (LFX-004, DEC-331). The doorway rings stay off.
- **A flash:** doorway ring lights On (LFX-002) for half a second, then back off. On differs from off, so the tower never stalls on an unchanged state (GAP-HW-010).
- **Won round:** the top ring flashes. **Lost round:** the bottom ring. **Spell:** all three rings.
- Flicker (LFX-006) is kept for the boss's phase changes (DEC-567).
