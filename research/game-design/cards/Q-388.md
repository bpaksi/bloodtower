Probe P-003, day 9. The table dropped the day's skull (MEC-034 .12), and the tower link dropped while the skull-dropped sound played (.13). MEC-071 holds the loop "at the node it stopped before" and resumes there (.16). It doesn't say whether a node that was cut off part-way counts as played. Playing it again after Reconnect would repeat a physical action on its own, which the tower safety rules forbid ("no automatic retry of a physical action"). Skipping it could lose something the table needs, such as the arrival show.
