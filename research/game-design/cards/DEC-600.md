Bob chose "Every time, passive".

While a glyph shows (DEC-599), every action of its name that any group takes in the kingdom it faces gets the boost (DEC-598) on its own. There is no button, no use limit and nothing to track. The players need to see it, and how is Q-342.

Rejected: the boost once per day per glyph for the first group, and a one-shot per chapter.
