Every space on the RtDT board has a printed name. The move map and the Story board show that name ("Howling Desert"), so players match it to the board without new labels. The data ID is the kingdom plus a slug of the name (`S-howling-desert`), stable and readable in events and tests. Region labels printed over several spaces ("Regal Run", "The Great Woods") are not spaces and get no ID.
