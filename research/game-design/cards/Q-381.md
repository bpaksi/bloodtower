Probe P-002 quit in round 3 of the Barrow's first battle and resumed the next evening. MEC-032, MEC-033 and MEC-034 each check "Continuing?" and skip their enter and start steps. MEC-035 to MEC-038, MEC-026, MEC-076 and MEC-077 have no such check. On resume, the battle's enter would take bold's free cut again (MEC-038 .37, .38), or give the foe a second last attack on guard, and the turn-start virtues would fire twice (MEC-035 .10 to .13).
