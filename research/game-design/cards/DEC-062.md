The build-time linter fails a story unless all four hold: every chapter has quests and the finale and adversary are present at short, standard and long; every quest, item, NPC, nugget, cue and asset it names exists; every image, clip, voice line and portrait exists, is 16:9 where needed and has captions; and content minimums are met (for example 100 nuggets and a mood line per terrain).
