Probe P-002 lost on day 18. The lone Ranger fell in the Barrow's first room for a third corruption, during the group's turn. MEC-033 .11 checks for a loss only after the day loop ends (DEC-086 step 8). Nothing said whether the turn and the day go on. A finale win ends the game at once (DEC-656). DEC-565 keeps the day's close from giving a third corruption, so a third comes only from a fight or a trap a group chose.
