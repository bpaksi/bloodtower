The sorted table is [catchup-gap-triage-2026-11-12.md](../catchup-gap-triage-2026-11-12.md). The rules it follows:

- A **parked** mechanic is closed: it was retired and has no surface to build.
- A **toolkit tool** (MEC-040 to MEC-070) that DEC-502 keeps in 1.0 gets its surface by a `needs` link from the screen or UI card that shows its result. One that DEC-502 puts in 1.1 moves to release 1.1.
- A **gameplay mechanic** gets the surface card that shows it. The C3 to C5 builds list that card.
- A **decided ruling with no deliverable** is reached by a `uses` or `needs` link from the mechanic or screen it governs.
- An **area or question** closes with the ruling named in the table.
