Bob chose "Most specific wins". The two fallbacks are two axes: how specific the piece is (DEC-501) and which layer supplies it (DEC-535, DEC-538). Specificity is the outer loop:

1. The variant on the entry (an angered vampire).
2. The entry's default (the vampire).
3. The variant, then the default, for each of the entry's type attributes, narrowest first (angered undead, then undead).
4. The engine default.

At each step the slot resolves as the active chapter, then the story, then the base; the first layer that fills it wins. So when the base has an angered vampire and the story has an angered undead, a vampire shows the base's angered vampire. A story that wants its own art there patches the entry (DEC-503). Rejected: walking the whole chain in the story layer before the base, so one story image reskins a whole type, and dropping the attribute fallback.
