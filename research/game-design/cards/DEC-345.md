Bob chose "Line tag, engine default". A story's line may tag its mood from the engine's list (DEC-344). An untagged line gets one from the moment: friendly for an offer, scared for a lead, angry at a wrong accusation, sly when a trick springs, and neutral otherwise. The default cast's lines come tagged. Rejected: requiring a tag on every line, and the engine always picking with no tags.
