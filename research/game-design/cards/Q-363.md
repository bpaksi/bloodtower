Found by the first probe when the Barrow's guardian fell and its reward, the story item *Barrow Key*, was paid (DEC-636). MEC-061 calls a story item "something the table holds". *The Lock* in chapter 3 needs the key, so it matters whether the key holder has to be in the group that goes there.
