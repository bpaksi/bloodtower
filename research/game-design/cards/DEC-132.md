Every level has the same shape: enter, repeat its body, exit on its condition, and hand a result to the level above. Each rule belongs to exactly one level, and the app runs the stack.

Session (DEC-152) → game → chapter → day → group turn → encounter → quest or battle (DEC-136). There are no phases (DEC-135).
