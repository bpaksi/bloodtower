Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden. This is a Tower battle only.

- **The case:** every hero present in the battle has fallen (0 hit points). The battle is inside the Tower, a guard, a stair guard, or the Warden.
- **The result:** the group loses the game. It does not return to the board to go on.
- **Not this rule:** a fall on the map, in a Tomb, or in any battle that is not in the Tower. Those stay DEC-119: a corruption, sit out, back at 1, and the battle ends fallen. They are not a game loss by this card.
