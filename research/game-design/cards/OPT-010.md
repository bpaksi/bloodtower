| Number | First guess |
|---|---|
| Horde | 4 per foe level, +1 per hero after the first |
| Fight number (DEC-130) | fighter 3, scout 2, ranger 2, healer 1, magic user 1, scholar 1 |
| Hit points | fighter 12, ranger 10, scout 9, healer 9, magic user 8, scholar 8 |
| A round's odds | strength ÷ (strength + 2 × level), kept between 10% and 90% |
| A won round cuts | half the round's strength, rounded up, at least 2 |
| A lost round | one random hero loses hit points equal to the foe's level |
| Automatic Advantage | +1 strength each round per match, at most 10 (RtDT's cap) |
| Conditional Advantage | +3 for the round it is spent |
| Spells (DEC-123) | Magic Missile, unlimited: −2. Lightning, once: −6. Fireball, once: halves the horde |
| Potion | +4 hit points |

Fighter and magic user, seed 2026: level 2 wins 100%, level 3 97%, level 4 46% (76% with 2 matching Advantages), level 5 17%. Four mixed heroes win level 5 81% of the time. A level-8 disaster is close to hopeless for any party.

**For:** level 4 is a real risk for two heroes, and Advantages swing it. **Against:** a lone hero with fight number 1 almost never wins, and the magic user's spells may be too strong.
