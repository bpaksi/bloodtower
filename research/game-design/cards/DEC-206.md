Bob made all four hard rules, so a story that breaks one fails the build (DEC-062). They hold for the engine's default drift too.
- **Size headroom:** the picture is 16:9 and large enough that the deepest zoom still fills the board at full resolution.
- **Camera inside:** no move shows past the picture's edge, and zoom has a maximum.
- **Slow moves:** pan and zoom speed are capped so nothing whips on a 65in TV. Reduced motion shows the still unmoved.
- **Caption band clear:** the picture's key detail stays out of the bottom third for the whole move. To check this, a picture's index entry (DEC-202) marks its focus box.
