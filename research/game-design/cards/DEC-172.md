Bob: "I'm not sure an errand does make sense. I like the idea of an NPC on the street and a tavern for rest similar to Citadel healing." Then he chose "Rest costs time, not money". The tavern's rest heals for free but uses up the group's time, while the citadel's rally (DEC-158) costs money and is quick. DEC-161 stands, because nothing else sells healing. A villager on the street is the village's NPC (DEC-162). Side jobs as errands to another space are in doubt (Q-081).
