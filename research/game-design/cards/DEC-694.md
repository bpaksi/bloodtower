Bob, in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review: "I think is a vocabulary issue. Every space belongs to a Kingdom. Optionally a space can belong to a region."

- **Kingdom:** every space has exactly one (DEC-579). This is the "every space is in one" rule DEC-242 meant.
- **Region:** optional. A space is in one of DEC-595's three regions (The Great Woods, Regal Run, Long Water) or in none, as the physical board prints them. Regions stay first-class objects with `REG-` IDs (DEC-242, DEC-243).
- **Settles** the conflict DEC-595 raised against DEC-242. The board map tool's "52 spaces with no region" errors are not errors.
