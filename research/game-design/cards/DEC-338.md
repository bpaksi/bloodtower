Bob chose "Neutral lines with blanks". The default cast's lines are plain and story-neutral, with blanks the engine fills from the story and the live game, such as place names, the adversary and the kingdom, the same way it fills nugget blanks (DEC-025). The story's narrator voice and style line read them, so the tone comes from the voice, not the words. Rejected: fixed lines with no blanks, and line sets per tone.
