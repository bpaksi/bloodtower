Bob chose "Eight fixed slots, rest in a list" over nearest-point stacking (OPT-013) and real-angle placement (OPT-014). The cross always draws the eight points N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW. A neighbour that does not fit one of them is shown in a short list under the cross, so the cross never moves around and a player always knows where a slot is.
