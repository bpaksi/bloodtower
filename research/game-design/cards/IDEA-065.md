Bob: "the only difference in RTDT between tomb, cave and fortress is the map artwork. We may do the same."

If adopted, the Cave and Fortress would replace their own games (the hunt, MEC-027, and the duel ladder, MEC-029) with the Tomb's room map (MEC-026), drawn in their own art. In RtDT's data each type also has its own 12 rooms and its own Advantage trait (Beast, Melee), so traps and the Advantage would change with the art ([app-dungeon-screen.md](../../return-to-dark-tower/app-dungeon-screen.md#the-rooms)). To be decided after the Tomb playtest (DEC-486).

**Parked 2026-10-29:** the Cave and Fortress wait for 1.1 (DEC-593). Ideas triage (DEC-612).
