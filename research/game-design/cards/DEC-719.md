Bob picked "5 mood stills, no mouths" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session. This settles the clash the 2026-10-27 sessions left between the talking default cast (DEC-333, DEC-344 to DEC-346) and silent generic lines (DEC-349).

- **The 22 pool faces** (DEC-341) are 5 stills each, one per mood: 110 stills and no mouth frames. The still changes with the line's mood. Nothing talks.
- **Mouth frames** go only to a story speaker who has a voiced one-off line (DEC-349). A story person with no voiced line needs only the stills.

Not picked: 1 neutral still per face (22 stills, but a default innkeeper looks the same friendly or scared), and keeping 110 talking avatars with 880 mouth frames for faces that never speak.
