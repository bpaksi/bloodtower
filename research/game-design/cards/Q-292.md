DEC-397 lets Inventive end any battle as a win. A finale boss (DEC-356) or the Tower's rooms (DEC-358) won with four potions could undo a story's climax, so they may need to be excluded or allowed only a smaller effect. A win with no fighting may also pay less than the full reward. The round step where the hero may use it (before the foe card is revealed, or after) and the count (4 in RtDT) also need settling.
