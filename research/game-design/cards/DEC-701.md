Bob chose "Foe strength per hero".

- **The odds:** a round's odds are strength ÷ (strength + level × heroes present ÷ 2), still kept between 10% and 90%. With four heroes this is OPT-010's 2 × level, so a full party is unchanged.
- **The count** stays OPT-010's 4 per level plus 1 per hero after the first (DEC-564, DEC-678). The solo +1 fight number and +2 hit points stay.
- **Hex** (Neuri) only works on a group of 3 or more heroes.
- **Shield wall** (Oreks) never cuts a won round below 2 for a group of 1 or 2 heroes.

Claude's simulator (OPT-010 battles with no round cards, spells, Advantages or retreat), share of battles won:

| Party | Level 2 now → new | Level 3 now → new | Level 4 now → new |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo Ranger | 59% → 99% | 5% → 65% | 0% → 17% |
| Fighter and Bard | 87% → 99% | 17% → 60% | 0% → 9% |
| Fighter, Healer, Scout | 100% → 100% | 79% → 90% | 34% → 53% |
| Four heroes | 100% → 100% | 96% → 96% | 64% → 65% |

The divisor 2 is a placeholder for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131). Rejected: lower foe levels for small parties, fixing the abilities only, and calling small parties a harder game.
