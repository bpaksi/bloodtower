Bob chose "Engine default, story swaps". Every slot (place paintings, UI, sound effects, tower cues, default scenes) has an engine asset, so a story with no art still runs. A story may replace any slot or add slots of its own (its scenes, NPCs, foes, set pieces). Hero portraits and voices stay engine-only, since the hero pool is the same in every story (DEC-045). Rejected: two fixed sets with no swapping, and a story supplying all art.
