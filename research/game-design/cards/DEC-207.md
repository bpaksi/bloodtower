Bob made all four hard rules, so a story that breaks one fails the build (DEC-062).
- **Frames match:** all 8 mouth frames are the portrait's exact pixel size, change only pixels inside the anchor box, and the box sits fully inside the portrait.
- **Chin above the caption:** the avatar's index entry (DEC-202) marks the chin line, and it must sit above the caption band.
- **Lines fit:** each spoken line's text fits in at most 3 caption lines on the board, about 120 characters. A longer line must be split into two lines.
- **Dark edges:** the portrait fades to dark at its edges, so the vignette into the void never shows a hard border.
