**What:** The quiet-event line for a terrain or time of day, shown with a soft tower cue before play moves on.

**When:** Every story, for each of the board's six terrains; add variants so the same road doesn't say the same thing twice.

**Why:** Quiet events are the most common thing in the wild; a line in the story's voice keeps the trip atmospheric.

**Pack:** mood lines per terrain (§4). The count is lines per terrain.

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 1 per terrain | 1 per terrain | 1 per terrain |
| Target | 3 per terrain | 3 per terrain | 3 per terrain |
