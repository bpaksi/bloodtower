Audit findings CB-06 (decision D2) and CB-01, with Gamewright's two confirmations to Wright on the F1 package (2026-11-13).

**The order.** After every effect that can change who stands, the battle checks, in this order: (1) a game loss already triggered (a third corruption, DEC-676; a skull loss, DEC-905); (2) the foe is gone: the battle is won; (3) every present hero has fallen: the battle is fallen. The first that is true ends the battle. Fight/Retreat, a round card and a roll never follow it.

**Where it runs:**
- **At entry.** MEC-038 alone applies the Tomb stance once, on a genuine entry (Bold cut or On guard attack, DEC-607). The gate runs after it, and again after a before-round-1 ability. Continue replays neither (DEC-675). This is Gamewright's ruling on the F1 package's gate routing, confirmed 2026-11-13.
- **A foe killed by Bold never uses its later pre-round ability** (Ambush, Fire breath or any other). This is Gamewright's ruling on TB-03, 2026-11-13, not only a reading of the card order.
- **After the spells** (DEC-643, as before) and **after a round** (the cut or the card).
- **After a retreat's last attack:** the foe is never gone there; a last attack that fells everyone is a fall (DEC-671).

**The final cut and Spines (CB-06).** A won round cuts first. If the cut leaves the foe gone, the battle is won at once and no won-round ability acts after the cut: Spines does not fire. An ability that changes the cut itself (Shield wall) still applies to it. If the cut leaves the foe standing, Spines fires; if it fells the last standing hero, the battle is fallen with the foe standing. So the final cut and the group's fall can never meet.

**If they ever do meet** (a later ability that acts after a cut), the foe's falling wins: the battle is won, the reward is paid once, the heroes who fell keep their corruption and come back at 1 hit point, and no gold is lost. A normal companion's swap (DEC-800, 1.1) happens before the fall counts.

Reward, fall penalties and the battle-ends hook each happen once, in the battle's close.

Not picked: Spines first, so a final cut can fell the group (a win that costs the hero the win); a mutual result with both the reward and the fall penalties.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
