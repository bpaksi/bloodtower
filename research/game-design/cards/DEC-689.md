Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. The finale desk walk of probe P-003 found the gap after The Warden's fall.
- **The node:** MEC-076 gains .20, a story node after .19: "End hook: the Tower's result (won, left or fallen)". The diagram is redrawn once Bob accepts.
- **The condition:** like the battle-ends hook (MEC-038 .26), the end hook gets the loop's result. A hook's moments may require it (DEC-290). The Ruined Keep's last choice requires "won", so a leave or a fall plays nothing.
- **The order on a win:** .18 the adversary falls, .19 the close, .20 the end hook plays the scene and sets its flag (DEC-664), then the game ends (MEC-032 .43). "At once" in DEC-656 means no other loop runs: the rest of the day, the night and the drop are all skipped.
- **Every loop's end hook** (DEC-540) gets its result the same way.

Not picked: the last choice in the win scene itself (DEC-664 already turned that down); a separate post-boss node only for the finale.
