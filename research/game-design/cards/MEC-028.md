"I was thinking this could be similar to a merchant in a village. However, you could anger the merchant and he would attack." **Proposal:** trade as at a merchant, but haggling hard, refusing an offer or stealing raises the camp's temper on the Story board. At full temper the camp attacks as a horde fight. A Humanoid Advantage calms it once.
