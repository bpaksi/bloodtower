**Proposal:** get in, take what you came for, get out. Each step choose sneak, bribe (money) or strike; every choice can raise an alarm meter shown on the Story board and the tower lights. At full alarm the camp musters into a horde fight. A Humanoid Advantage lowers the alarm once. **Alternatives:** a parley (talk your way through with nuggets and money); a raid (fight in waves, run between waves).
