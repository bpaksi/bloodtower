**What:** The rooms or beats a quest plays one per pass, each with its choice or fight.

**When:** Every quest. Use more steps for a quest that should feel like a journey (a crypt), fewer for a sharp scene.

**Why:** A one-step quest is just an encounter; steps give a scene a shape: build-up, turn, outcome.

**Pack:** the quest's moments (§4) or its room deck (§11).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Target | 3-5 | 3-5 | 3-5 |
