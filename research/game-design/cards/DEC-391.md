Bob chose "Rolled doubles → +1 Wild".

- **RtDT:** if you double your move, +1 Wild Advantage.
- **Bloodtower:** when the group's 2d4 move roll shows doubles (DEC-224, DEC-102), the hero holding Relentless has +1 Wild Advantage in every battle until the group's next move roll (DEC-324). That is about one roll in four.
- **Board:** the move roll already shows the dice, so the board marks the doubles and the Advantage it gives.
- **Dice:** 2d4 is set only for the Proving Grounds slice (DEC-224); the full game's dice are still open (DEC-102). Relentless needs a roll of two or more dice, so the numbers pass must keep that or give it a new trigger.

Rejected: a roll at or above a line, and a reward for walking most of the moves before a battle.
