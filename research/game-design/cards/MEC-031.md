Turn the drums so the right glyphs face the right kingdoms to wake the shrine. The heroes choose each turn on the iPad and the tower turns only after their press. Each wrong arrangement costs hit points or a corruption. A Magic Advantage reveals one glyph's place. Not picked: the light-sequence ritual; the offering.
