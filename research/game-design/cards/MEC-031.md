**Proposal:** a memory game on the tower. The tower lights a sequence of doorway lights; the heroes repeat it on the iPad. Each round the sequence grows; a mistake costs hit points or a corruption, and finishing the length set by the difficulty completes the ritual. A Magic Advantage replays the sequence once. **Alternatives:** a glyph puzzle (turn drums so the right glyphs face a kingdom); an offering (give items to restore the shrine step by step, from Covenant monuments).
