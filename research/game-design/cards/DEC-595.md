| ID | Region | Spaces |
|---|---|---|
| REG-01 | The Great Woods | W Delmsmire, W Akartus, W Yellowpike |
| REG-02 | Regal Run | S The Throne, S The Cloister, S Archmont |
| REG-03 | Long Water | N Dayside, N Fivepint |

Every other space has no region. Kingdom stays its own attribute (DEC-579), not a region level. The spelling is "Long Water", as in the board map file; board-map.md still says "Longwater".
