Bob chose "SCR/UI/CUE/MEC are deliverables".

- **Deliverable cards:** `SCR-` (screen), `UI-` (UI piece), `CUE-` (tower or sound cue) and `MEC-` (a loop or mechanic). One card per thing that gets built.
- **Reach:** every decided ruling is reached from at least one deliverable card by a `needs` or `uses` link, so building that card is where the ruling lands.
- **Plans:** each plan step lists the deliverable cards it builds, as `Builds: <IDs>`.
- **Check:** a gaps check reports decided rulings that no deliverable reaches (IDEA-062).

Missing deliverable cards are listed in Q-260. Rejected: a new DLV- card kind, and plan steps as the only list of deliverables.
