Bob asked Claude to rule the last 13 treasures and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** +1 Stealth; ignore the haggle die's cancel.
- **Bloodtower:** +1 Stealth Advantage while held. Bloodtower keeps the physical haggle die at merchants (DEC-091, MEC-074 .5). When the holder's group buys, it may roll the die again for each item and keep the better face, so a face that takes nothing off can be undone, as RtDT's Necklace ignores the cancel.
