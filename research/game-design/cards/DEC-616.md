One default per beat of DEC-327. Every tower slot is optional (DEC-317), and a story may override any slot (DEC-279). The sounds are picked from the inventory by name, because no feel notes are recorded yet. Bob checks each one in a tower review.

| Beat | Board slot | Tower slot |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Arrival | The foe panel slides in (UI-026) | SND-037 Battle Start, then LSQ-020 Whole Tower Breathing |
| 2 Foe reveal | The foe painting (Beginning) | The foe type's own sound (SND-067 to SND-079), else SND-065 Foe Event |
| 3 Card reveal | The round card's art slides in | A seeded pick of SND-028 to SND-030, Card Flip |
| 4 Picking | A Mac tension bed | LFX-003 Breathe, no sound |
| 5 Spell | The spell's flash on the foe | SND-034 Card Select |
| 6 Clash | Music falls silent (DEC-331) | LFX-004 Breathe Fast, no sound |
| 7 Round won | The foe's cut count drops | SND-019 to SND-023, Apply Advantage 01 to 05, rising with each round won; LSQ-002 Flare Then Fade |
| 8 Hero hit | The hero's hit points flash, with a Mac hit sound | LSQ-005 Angry Strobe 01, no sound |
| 9 Hero falls | The hero greys out, with a Mac sound | LSQ-008 Gloat 01, no sound |
| 10 Retreat | The retreat screen (UI-039) | LSQ-021 Slow Flare Then Fade, no sound |
| 11 Victory | The foe painting (Dead) | SND-038 Battle Victory, then LSQ-012 Victory |
| 12 Defeat | The foe painting (Victory) | SND-108 Quest Failed, then LSQ-011 Defeat |
| 13 Reward | The reward on a Reveal (UI-040) | SND-031 Card Flip Paper 01 |

Hits, falls and the retreat sound play on the Mac. This keeps the tower's stingers for the big moments, and the tower can't report when a sound ends. CUE-010 takes this table. 1981's 8-bit sounds are left out for tone.
