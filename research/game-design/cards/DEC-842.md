Bob chose "Only moods a voiced line uses". If the Count's voiced lines are only angry and sly, those two moods get a portrait and 8 mouths each, and his other three moods are stills.

- asset-studio's needs list works it out from the job's lines: one talking avatar (`portrait.<speaker>~<mood>`) per speaker and mood that a voiced line uses, one still for every other mood.
- A new voiced line in a new mood adds that mood's mouths to the needs list as `missing`. The still already made becomes that avatar's portrait draft.
- Each talking avatar still passes the avatar limits (DEC-207).

Rejected: mouths for all 5 moods of any voiced speaker, and mouths on the neutral portrait only.
