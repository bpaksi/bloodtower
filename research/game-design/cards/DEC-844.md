P2 was approved on 2026-10-28, before these rulings. Claude proposes that P2's revision takes them as they are:

- **Camera moves:** `push-in`, `pull-out`, `pan-left`, `pan-right`, `tilt-up`, `tilt-down` and `drift` (the default), at `slow`, `medium` and `fast`: 0.02, 0.035 and 0.05 picture widths a second, 20, 12 and 6 s by default (DEC-578). P2's `reveal` move goes, which also keeps it apart from the Reveal tool (DEC-342). The numbers are player constants, tuned on the TV.
- **IDs:** asset-studio takes IDs from the job as given and checks only the shape `<kind>.<entry>~<variant>`, lowercase kebab-case (DEC-622). It builds three kinds of ID itself: `portrait.<speaker>` for the neutral mood, `portrait.<speaker>~<mood>` for the others, and `glow.<entry>` for a painting's mask (`glow.n2-funeral` belongs to `painting.n2-funeral`). DEC-401's `<painting-id>.glow` would break the `kind.entry` shape.
- **Job:** `asset-job.json` (JSON, not YAML), with `schema`, `story`, `source`, `style`, `profiles`, `cast[]`, `lines[]`, `pictures[]`, `moments[]` (DEC-840) and `sounds[]` (DEC-623), and no `status` (DEC-839). A cast member with no `voice` gets mood stills only (DEC-719).
- **Kinds and moods are the consumer's labels:** a picture's `kind`, a sound's `kind` and a speaker's mood list are strings that asset-studio stores and filters by but does not check against a list. Bloodtower's lint checks them against its own lists: painting, portrait, foe, place, item, cover; effect, ambience; and DEC-344's five moods.
