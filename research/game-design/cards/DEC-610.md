Roadmap step 3, the old-doc conflicts (AREA-061). Bob chose "Banner + inline notes" over rewriting the docs, archiving them or deciding doc by doc. Each older document in `research/game-design/` (requirements.md, story-engine.md, core-mechanics.md, sessions.md and the rest) gets one line at the top: it is history, and where it disagrees with a card, the card wins. Each line known to conflict gets a short "Superseded by DEC-nnn" note. Nothing is rewritten, so the docs keep their history and a developer can't miss a stale passage.
