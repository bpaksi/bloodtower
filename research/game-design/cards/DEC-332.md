Bob: "Allow for 1-n Foe artwork. During the battle choose different images this will add variety. Thoughts to consider; each image represents a different state. beginning, losing, winning, dead, victory." He confirmed the states are named from the foe's side.

| State | Shown when |
|---|---|
| Beginning | arrival and the first round |
| Losing | the foe is losing: the horde is low or the odds read is Good |
| Winning | the foe is winning: a hero was hit or the odds read is Grim |
| Dead | the foe is slain, the heroes' victory |
| Victory | the foe triumphs: the group falls or runs |

- **1 to n:** each foe needs at least one painting. Each state may hold several, and the battle shows a seeded pick (DEC-011), so the same foe looks different from fight to fight.
- **Fallback:** a state with no painting shows a Beginning one.
- Story foes (DEC-326) and reskins (MEC-058) supply paintings the same way.

Rejected: states named from the heroes' side, and images tied to beats instead of states.
