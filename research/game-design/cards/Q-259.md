No card covers learning to play. The iPad Menu has a Look up group (DEC-229), but nothing says what it holds, and there is no first-game walkthrough or how-to-play screen.

The RtDT review design check (RV-C6, [design-check-rtdt-review.md](../design-check-rtdt-review.md)) shows why it matters: RtDT is easy to learn but hard to play well, and new players learn when to fight only by losing.
