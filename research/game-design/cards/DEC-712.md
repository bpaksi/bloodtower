Walkthrough scene 7b: an iPad goes to sleep during a long rules read, and a late player arrives between games.

- **Awake:** while an iPad is joined, the device page (SCR-016) asks the browser to keep the screen on. If that is refused or the iPad sleeps anyway, it is a drop, and DEC-154 applies: the game pauses until every hero has a device again. A refused request is logged and shown on that iPad.
- **Between games:** no heroes are claimed on the session screen, so a drop blocks nothing. The next claim step (SCR-010) is where the heroes need devices. If every iPad has dropped, the board shows the join QR (UI-010) until one joins (DEC-274).
- **Late player:** scans the QR at any time and claims heroes at the next claim step (DEC-153), or takes a dropped hero mid-game (DEC-154).

Rejected: a hard pause for a drop between games; leaving iPad sleep to the iPad's own settings.
