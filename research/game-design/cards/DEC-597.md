Bob chose "Engine boost, story reskins".

Each glyph (GLY-001 to GLY-005) has an engine meaning: once a seal break reveals it, it boosts the action it is named for (Cleanse, Quest, Battle, Reinforce, and Banner too, though Banner is not an action, DEC-532) in the kingdom it faces. Each glyph's effect is a slot (DEC-535), so a story may rename it, reskin it or replace it whole; with no story entry the engine default plays. A story still must not need a glyph to be winnable (DEC-306).

Rejected: glyphs as pure story boons (every story filling five slots), an engine rule for four glyphs with Banner left to stories, and glyphs as markers only.
