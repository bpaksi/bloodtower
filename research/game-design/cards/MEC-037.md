- **Owner:** story; the engine supplies the type's game (DEC-063).
- **Enter:** .1: a group picks the quest marker in an encounter (MEC-036 .11). Its progress loads (DEC-151). The board waits; no world time passes (DEC-084).
- **Start point:** .2 to .4, its cue, narration and track.
- **Body:** .5 and .6, one step of its type's game per pass, such as one room of the tomb (MEC-026). Each type gets its own step diagram card as it is designed (AREA-054); the tomb's is drawn in MEC-026. Go deeper or leave is a choice inside the step. A step may start a battle (DEC-136).
- **Exit:** .6: done (its target reached), left (the group walks out) or failed (the group falls).
- **Close:** .7 to .10: a done quest pays its rewards and counts toward the chapter (DEC-041); left or failed keeps its progress for the next visit (DEC-151).
- **Result up:** done, left or failed, to the encounter.
- **End point:** .11 and .12, its cue and closing narration for that outcome (DEC-142).
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art per quest: its opening scene, a painting per step or room, and a scene per outcome.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). .5 points at the quest types area until each type's step card exists; the tomb's diagram is MEC-026 and the plain scene's is MEC-077 (DEC-486: the other five wait for the playtest). Falling (the purse loss, DEC-177) belongs to the battle that caused it. Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
