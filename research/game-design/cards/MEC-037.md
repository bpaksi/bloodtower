- **Owner:** story; the engine supplies the type's game (DEC-063).
- **Enter:** a group picks the story point in an encounter (DEC-083). The board waits; no world time passes (DEC-084).
- **Start point:** the set piece's narration.
- **Body:** one pass is one step of its type's game, such as one room of the tomb (MEC-026). A step may start a battle (DEC-136).
- **Exit:** done (its target reached), left (the group walks out) or failed (the group falls).
- **Close:** a done set piece pays its rewards and counts toward the chapter (DEC-041).
- **Result up:** done, left or failed, to the encounter.
- **End point:** the set piece's closing narration, for that outcome.
- **Screen:** the Story board (SCR-014) and the group's iPads (SCR-016).
- **UI:** the set piece track (UI-025), panning stills (UI-003), pick cards (UI-007).
- **Art:** per set piece: its opening scene, a painting per step or room, and a scene per outcome.
- **Sound:** CUE-008 at the start, CUE-009 at the end.
