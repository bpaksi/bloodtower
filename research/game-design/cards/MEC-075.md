- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140); the pick's trigger hook sits in the encounter (MEC-036 .9).
- **Enter:** .1: called for 1 move from the encounter at a village tavern (MEC-036 .14, DEC-172).
- **Body:** .2 to .5, played once: the group picks how many moves to rest; the pick's move is the first. Every hero present heals a set amount per move, up to full (DEC-174).
- **Exit:** the rest is done, or Back. Back still spends the pick's move, taken before the rest opened.
- **Close:** none.
- **Result up:** the moves spent and the hit points healed.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: the tavern's painting (DEC-150).

Bob approved the diagram as drawn, 2026-10-27. Rejected: Back refunding the move, and one hero resting at a time. The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277).
