- **One step** moves a card one rung: Claw 4 to Claw 2, Sweep 2 to Sweep 1, Howl 2 to Howl 1. Soft is half, rounded down. A trait card's soft rung keeps the hit and drops the trait effect.
- **Stacking:** two conditional Advantages may soften the same card, from one hero or two. The second takes it to nothing. A third is wasted, and the use dialog (UI-038) says so.
- **Rend** softens to a Claw, then to nothing.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
