Walkthrough scene 7a: the table is on the session screen between games, and the tower drops (battery, Bluetooth).

- **On the board:** the session screen shows "Tower lost" in place of the tower's state. Nothing reconnects on its own.
- **Next game:** pressing New game, Play again or Continue first runs the session's tower block again (MEC-039 .5 to .25): Connect Tower or Play without Tower, calibrate, glyphs, facing and volume. Then the game starts.
- **Elsewhere, already covered:** a failed connect or calibrate in the session's Enter loops back to the choice (MEC-039 .11). A tower lost once a game has started, piece setup included, pauses that game (DEC-301).

Rejected: a Reconnect button on the session screen (a second way to do the same thing); ending the session.
