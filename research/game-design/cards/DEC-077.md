1. **Game:** roll each group's moves (DEC-079).
2. **Story (optional):** narration or a set piece, by location and story progress. Still to be worked out.
3. **Players:** each group in turn spends its moves until they run out or it ends its turn (DEC-078).
4. **Game:** events for each group where it stopped, then the world's turn (DEC-080).

A group's turn is spending moves, so there is no separate action and nothing replaces Reinforce as its own step.
