Bob: "I was considering only allow retreat if party has remaining moves. Foe, get 1 last attack. Party is placed on previous space. If encounter at building, party stay at building."

- **Only with a move left:** Retreat is offered only while the group has a move left in its turn (MEC-035). With none, the group fights on until the foe is gone or every present hero has fallen.
- **One last attack:** as the group leaves, the foe hits once, as in a lost round (DEC-015).
- **Where the group goes:** back to the space it came from. A battle at a building leaves the group at that building.
- **The foe:** stays and grows, as before (DEC-016, DEC-012).

This replaces the "for free" in DEC-016.
