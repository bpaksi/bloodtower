Bob: "The session screen should allow for reviewing the previous games with drill in to see statistics. From there they could play again. This would essentially bypass all story setup screens and put the user in at the start of the story."

Play again reuses the game's story, length, difficulty and seed, and skips Pick story and the setup screens.
