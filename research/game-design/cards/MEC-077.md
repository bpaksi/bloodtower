- **Owner:** engine; the story fills the narration and words the options (DEC-642).
- **Enter:** called by the quest loop as its one step (MEC-037 .5), after the quest's opening hook.
- **Body:** .1 to .10, played once.
- **Exit:** .7. The result is done, left (a retreat) or failed (a fall).
- **Result up:** to MEC-037 .6.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** UI-042. Art: the quest's Painting.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). The opening narration is MEC-037's .3 hook, so this step starts at the choice. Nodes .8 to .10 were added on 2026-10-30, after the first probe: an item goes to the hero the group picks, as a battle's card does (MEC-038 .21). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
