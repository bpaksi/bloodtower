- **Owner:** engine; the story fills the narration and words the options (DEC-642).
- **Enter:** called by the quest loop as its one step (MEC-037 .5), after the quest's opening hook.
- **Body:** .1 to .7, played once.
- **Exit:** .7. The result is done, left (a retreat) or failed (a fall).
- **Result up:** to MEC-037 .6.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** UI-042. Art: the quest's Painting.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). The opening narration is MEC-037's .3 hook, so this step starts at the choice. Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).

```railroad
body
  .1  board   The choice: the scene's Painting and its 2 or 3 options   UI-042 DEC-642
  .2  prompt  One option, the group together                 UI-042 DEC-019
  .3  check   Does the option hold a battle?                 DEC-642
    track yes
      .4  call    Battle loop: the option's foe and level    MEC-038 DEC-136
      .5  check   Won, retreated, or fell?                   DEC-642
        track won
        track retreated
        track fell
    track no
  .6  change  Won or no battle: apply the option's effects   DEC-642 DEC-054
  .7  change  Result: done; left on a retreat; failed on a fall   DEC-642 DEC-151
```
