D-02 §10 and G-4 to G-6. The only variant axes are the four goals, the four lairs and `~freed`, which `lenka.saved_by` selects. A line whose speaker is absent is skipped, so there are no `~no-<companion>`, length or private variants. The old `.vo-NN` lines move to `~vo-NN` when each scene is rewritten.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-10, under Bob's standing rule that design rulings are Gamewright's (relayed by Warden, 2026-11-10).

**G-9 (Gamewright, 2026-11-10):** the guard and Lantern lines are event moments, `count-strike.guarded~vo-NN` and `count-strike.lantern~vo-NN`, not state variants. One shared guard line covers both the two-bite guard and the blade at +1.

**D-04 (Gamewright, 2026-11-10):** every event moment uses `<scene>.<event>~vo-NN`. This includes room outcomes (`n5-banquet.won~vo-01`), Long Night outcomes and the traitor (`traitor.jakob-sprung~vo-01`).

**G-17 (Gamewright, 2026-11-10):** clue lines are `<scene>.clue-<suspect>~vo-NN`. **G-18:** no line names or counts the hunters.
