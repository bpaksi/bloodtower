**Recommendation.**

- **Layers:** a base pulse, a Grim layer (drums and low strings) and a Good layer (a lift). Victory and defeat endings. Silence for the clash, as DEC-328 says.
- **Tracks:** one engine battle track for all foes, plus the adversary's own (DEC-567). It's a slot, so a story or a chapter can replace it (DEC-535).
- **Source:** generated or licensed, with the licence recorded in the asset index. Never RtDT's music.

**Why:** a track per foe type means 12 or more tracks for a difference few players would notice.

**Not picked:** one track per foe type, one per chapter, and commissioning music for 1.1.
