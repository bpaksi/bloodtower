Two small brakes on the spiral, with no new system:

- **Cure price:** the first corruption a hero carries is cured at the base price. Every later one costs one higher price, and the price rises no further (this would trim DEC-170 to a single step once Bob agrees).
- **Free rest after a fall:** when every hero in a group falls (DEC-175), the group's next building visit gives one rest at no cost.

The foe still keeps its cut count (DEC-314), so ground won before a retreat is not lost.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
