Each tomb gets its own room layout, drawn from the game's seed. The target room is hidden behind one of the doorways, and luck in picking paths matters, as in RtDT. Entering it starts a battle with the tomb's guardian (MEC-038). Winning finishes the tomb (done). Losing is a fall, so the tomb is failed (MEC-037 .6). Retreating from the guardian is leaving. The layout and the cleared rooms are kept for the next visit (DEC-151). Not picked: a target room shown from the start; a hidden target with no guardian fight.
