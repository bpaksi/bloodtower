Bob chose "Holder takes less per hit".

- **RtDT:** prevent 2 warrior losses per battle card.
- **Bloodtower:** passive armor. Whenever a hit lands on the holder, in a battle round, on a retreat's last attack or in a dungeon room, it is a little smaller. It protects only the holder. The amount belongs to the numbers pass.
- **Held:** gear lasts until it is lost (DEC-370).

Rejected: letting the holder guard like a guard virtue (DEC-319), and making every lost round's hit smaller for the whole group (item-fit.md's proposal).
