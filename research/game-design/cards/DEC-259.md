Bob chose "By what it does for the story". Six groups, each a sub-area of AREA-081:

- **Plot** (AREA-082): chapters, set pieces, timed beats, win and loss scenes.
- **People** (AREA-083): NPCs, companions, the villain, lieutenants, traitors.
- **Places** (AREA-084): buildings, story points, leads.
- **Threats** (AREA-085): foes, villain quests, corruption.
- **Rewards** (AREA-086): story items, clues.
- **Voice and look** (AREA-087): narration, nuggets, mood lines, avatars, tower cues, art.
