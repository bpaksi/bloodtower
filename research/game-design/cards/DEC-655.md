Bob picked "Flat cuts first, then halving".

- At MEC-038 .8 the engine first applies every flat cut (Lightning, Magic Missile), then any halving (Fireball).
- The result never depends on who tapped first or where they sit.
- Foe abilities that change spells, such as the Striga's Ward and the Titan's Colossus (DEC-553), still apply to each spell.

Not picked: halving first; seat order.
