Bob chose "Spends 1 move, may go on". The fall back to the previous space costs 1 move. The group turn (MEC-035) then goes on with what is left, so the group can go around the horde another way or head elsewhere. At a building, where the group stays put (DEC-313), the move is still spent. Rejected: the turn ends, and all moves are lost.
