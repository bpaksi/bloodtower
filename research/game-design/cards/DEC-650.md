Bob picked "The next card lands".

- **When:** a group retreats (MEC-038 .32, DEC-318), or enters a Tomb battle room on guard and takes the foe's last attack before round 1 (DEC-607).
- **What:** the next card from the battle's shuffle (DEC-649) is revealed and lands in full, as on a lost round. It counts as one of the 8.
- **Unchanged:** a guard picked for the round still blocks it (DEC-318), and foe abilities that change lost rounds apply.

Not picked: a flat Claw from no deck; landing the card already revealed this round.
