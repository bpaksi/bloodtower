Probe P-003, day 1. The two heroes split at the citadel (DEC-640): the Fighter took *The Ambush* alone, and the Bard took *The Herald*. DEC-564 scales by "the hero count": a party of 2 gets foe count +1, and a party of 1 gets +1 fight number and +2 full hit points. It does not say whether a battle reads the party or the heroes in it. With 4 heroes split 2 and 2, every battle would face a 4-hero foe.
