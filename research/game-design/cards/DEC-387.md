Bob chose "Heavy losses → treasure".

- **RtDT:** after a battle in which you lose 10 or more warriors, gain a treasure.
- **Bloodtower:** after a battle in which the hero holding Callous lost a lot of hit points, that hero gains a treasure (DEC-039, DEC-370). Only this hero's losses count, not the group's.
- **Any battle:** won, lost or retreated from (DEC-313), as long as the losses reach the line.

The line itself is left to the numbers pass (Q-288). Rejected: gold after any battle where the hero was hurt, and counting the whole group's losses.
