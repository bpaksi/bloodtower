Bob chose "Content yes, rules no". Foes, gear, virtues, places, items and every other content entry live in base manifests, and a story may add, change or remove any of them. Rules stay engine code that a story picks from but cannot change: the counters (DEC-054), the effect kinds (DEC-069), the loops, and the core setup steps (DEC-110). Heroes stay engine-owned (DEC-045, DEC-199). Rejected: letting a story change heroes, and making rules manifest data too.
