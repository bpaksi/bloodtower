Bob: "The art work could/would change based on options or even story." He picked "Story sets the tomb style" and "Story events".

- **Tomb style:** one set of the six section paintings (DEC-585), such as a crypt, catacomb or flooded vault. The engine ships a default style, and a story pack may supply its own (DEC-199). Each tomb uses one style.
- **Story events:** a story may swap the style, or lay an overlay on it, partway through a tomb or from one chapter to the next: a tomb that floods, burns or wakes after an event.

Not picked: a setup option that picks the style; the view darkening with depth or as hit points fall.
