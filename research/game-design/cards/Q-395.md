Probe P-003, the finale desk walk. The tower link dropped at The Warden's arrival show (MEC-076 .14), Reconnect failed twice, and the table chose Go on without (MEC-071 .12). Seven skulls were still held inside the tower. DEC-306 says that without the tower "the simulated drop never holds a skull back", but it was written for a game that starts without one. Nothing says whether the held skulls come out, stay counted somewhere, or matter to the supply loss (DEC-043, DEC-686).
