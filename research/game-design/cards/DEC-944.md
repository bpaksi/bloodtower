D5 brief (bloodtower #502). DEC-911 says any gold in the pool can pay "a buy", and that a buy is greyed only if the pool cannot cover it. The game has other gold moves: an event's "Pay them off", a quest option with a gold price, the quest toll (a short group pays all its gold and hit points, Q-C3-22, Bob approved 2026-10-31), and losses. Nothing said which of them the absent heroes' gold may pay.

- **Ruling:** DEC-911's pool and payment order apply to every **priced choice**, one the iPad greys when the gold is short: a building pick (MEC-074), an event's pay-off (DEC-023), and a quest option's gold price. Each is enabled when the pool covers it and paid in DEC-911's order.
- **The quest toll is not a priced choice.** It is never greyed, and a short group pays what it holds (Q-C3-22). The heroes present pay it in equal shares and cover each other (DEC-178). Absent heroes never pay it. "Short" means the heroes present hold less than the toll.
- **Losses stay where they fall:** a fallen group's loss (DEC-177) and own-share losses (DEC-896: Cruel, Greedy, Feral, Lost) are taken as those cards say. They never reach an absent hero and are never covered by the pool.
- **Gains** go to the heroes present (DEC-178), odd coins by DEC-646.

Reason: DEC-911's line is "a buy is greyed only if the pool cannot cover it"; the choices that grey are the ones it speaks of. The toll's short rule is Bob's and names the group's own gold, so it stays as he approved it.

Not picked: the pool pays the toll too (it would rewrite Q-C3-22's "a short group pays all its gold"); only building picks use the pool (a pay-off would then grey while the board shows enough gold).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
