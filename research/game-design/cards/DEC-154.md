Bob: "If an iPad drops, game pauses until all hunters are claimed."

Whatever loop is running waits. The dropped device rejoins and takes its heroes back, or another iPad claims them. Play goes on only when every hero has a device. This replaces the R2 rule that a dropped device's heroes are let go after 60 seconds.
