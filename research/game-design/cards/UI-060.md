- **Reveal:** the first time a hero uses a cursed item, the board shows it on a Reveal, darkened, with "Cursed" (DEC-522, DEC-168). The hero takes a corruption card (DEC-169).
- **Held:** the hero panel marks the item and its corruption as linked.
- **Shedding:** the fence's menu shows Sell back, and the sanctuary's shows Lift curse, each with its price. Either removes the corruption.
- **A third corruption** (DEC-804): instead of the corruption the Reveal shows the item crumbling, and the hero loses 2 hit points, stopping at 1. The card goes back to the box.
