Bob chose "iPad lights, board sums". On each hero's iPad (UI-027), the cards that match the foe's traits are highlighted; a matching conditional Advantage shows as a pickable round action. On the Story board, the horde panel (UI-026) shows each present hero's number of matches next to their name. The odds themselves stay a rough read (DEC-312). Players learn which gear pays off against which foe. Rejected: the horde panel listing every matching card, and only a silent total.
