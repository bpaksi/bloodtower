Bob chose "Required inside its owner". Every slot belongs to an owner: the game, an optional tool, or a kind of entry (a chapter, a quest, a foe). A required slot counts only where its owner exists, so a story that never uses the dungeon tool fills none of its slots, and every quest the story has must fill its own required slots. The schema carries no other condition. Game length is handled by the lint checking each length on its own (DEC-062). Rejected: free conditions on required, and required in every story regardless of tools.
