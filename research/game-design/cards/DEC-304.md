Bob chose "Chance by lights, not glyphs". Every game's setup puts all 12 seals on (MEC-032 .21). A game of chance uses the tower's lights and sounds, as the purse spin does (DEC-114), not the glyphs behind the seals. A glyph matters only once a chapter win breaks its seal and reveals it (DEC-303). A drum turn shows only at the doorways already open, and Bob accepts that it is a smaller effect. A sync after the seals are on (Continue or reconnect) can't see the glyphs; whether it can trust calibration instead is Q-033's tower test. Rejected: starting with the top drum unsealed, and glyph chance growing with each open doorway.
