Bob chose Claude's recommendation, "plus the RTDT advantage apply to reduce effects of card" (Q-212).

- **Reveal:** after Fight is chosen and before heroes pick actions, the foe flips one card from its deck onto the foe panel. Examples: "Claw: 4 to one hero", "Sweep: 1 to everyone", "Howl: the horde regrows 2".
- **Respond:** heroes pick actions knowing what is coming: guard a big single hit (DEC-319), drink a potion before a sweep, or go all out to win the round.
- **Draw:** the seeded draw of strength against level (DEC-014) still decides the round. Won: the foe is cut and the card does nothing. Lost: the card's effect plays.

This brings RtDT's battle-card detail into the round loop without replacing the foe or the rounds. Rejected: RtDT's level-many cards with no rounds, and keeping the single draw with only a richer display.
