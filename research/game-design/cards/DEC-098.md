Every device sees the whole pool and picks heroes from it. Once a device picks a hero, the others see that hero greyed out. A story can mark a few pool heroes as suggested for its plot, but it does not supply heroes of its own.

The table seats N heroes from that pool, not always 4. N is the story's party-size setting. Proving Grounds defaults N to 4, from the pool of 6 (DEC-099). Continue stays off until N are picked. The button and the help are DEC-232.
