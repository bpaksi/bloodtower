From 1981. Clues found in the ruins hint at the answer; the heroes set a position for each drum on the iPad, and the tower turns to it only after their press. A right answer opens the vault; a wrong one costs hit points or a corruption. A Stealth Advantage gives one more clue. Not picked: the collapse; a trap gauntlet.
