**Proposal:** a treasure hunt against time. The ruins hold a relic in one of several spots; each search reveals the spot or a clue to it, but every search shakes the ruins (a collapse meter, drum rumble on the tower). Leave before it falls or lose hit points. A Stealth Advantage searches without a shake. **Alternatives:** a trap gauntlet (pick the safe path step by step); a riddle of the keys (1981: answer by drum position).
