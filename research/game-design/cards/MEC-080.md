A hero starts fresh with their class's two intrinsic virtues (spells among them, no mana), full hit points and no corruption. Virtue tiles are drawn at the citadel or earned, up to 4. Each corruption is one of RtDT's 12 cards with its own drawback.

Proposed by Claude in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review as the deliverable for rulings no other card builds (DEC-355). Its `uses` links list them.
