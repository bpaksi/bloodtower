Bob: "One thing I don't like about RTDT is the concept of months. That is why I changed to chapters (and being story driven). … Each chapter/kingdom will increase in difficulty. … I think difficulty will always be associated to chapter."

The story sets the chapters; every story's challenge steps up with each one. The difficulty picked at setup (DEC-059) is a different knob, set once for the whole game.
