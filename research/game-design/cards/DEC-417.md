Bob chose "Run from a battle for free" over moving a standing horde's token.

- **RtDT:** move any foe up to 2 spaces.
- **Bloodtower:** offered only in battle, as a hero's round action (DEC-411). The whole group runs: the foe gets no last attack (DEC-318), the group needs no move left (DEC-313), and the step back spends none (DEC-316). Otherwise it is a run: the group falls back to the space it came from, and the horde is left standing (DEC-314). Whether the horde still may rise a status step is open (Q-300).
- **Not at the turn start:** it does nothing outside a battle.

Rejected: moving a standing horde's token up to 2 spaces (item-fit.md's proposal, often useless), and moving a horde or skipping the next wild battle.
