Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 25, when the Fighter guarded against the Oreks in the Tower's basement.
- **The rule:** party strength is each attacking hero's fight number plus that hero's own automatic Advantages. A hero who guards, casts, softens or spends a card adds neither.
- **Party-wide Advantages:** an Advantage that names the party, such as a story boon, counts every round.
- **Example:** the Fighter guards, so his +1 Weapon Master is lost too. The party's strength falls from 6 to 2 (the Bard's 1 and his Rally's 1).
- **Why:** the Advantage is how that hero fights. It keeps one meaning for "gives up the round" across DEC-129, DEC-324 and DEC-484, and the odds read stays easy to follow.

Not picked: Advantages always count (guarding would cost the Fighter only his 3); a guard keeps Advantages, but a spender loses them.
