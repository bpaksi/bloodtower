Probe P-003 lost on day 25, the same night the Tower unlocked: the day's drop took the last skull from the supply. DEC-043 says the game is lost on "an empty skull supply", and that there is "no round limit: the heroes are pressed by skulls and fallen buildings, not a clock". But every day's drop takes 1 skull from the supply (DEC-564) whatever the heroes do. Skulls held inside the tower never come back, and only a Cleanse returns one (DEC-377). So 24 skulls is a hidden limit: about 24 days for one or two heroes, and about 16 for three or four. Nothing on the board shows it.
