**What:** The villain's mid-bosses. The engine places, moves and fights them; the story says how many, where and when, and what beating or ignoring one changes.

**When:** In a longer game, to give the middle chapters a face and a fight. Beating one never changes the villain's strength; a story may keep the Tower shut until a lieutenant dies (DEC-362, trimmed under DEC-352, 2026-10-27).

**Why:** A lieutenant turns 'the villain is strong' into something the heroes can act on before the end.

**Pack:** lieutenant entries with placement and outcome (§2).
