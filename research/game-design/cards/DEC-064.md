"Skulls, like RTDT, are the end of a day (every group moves). This will need to be prompted and we need to record where the skull lands and what building." After the last group moves, the Story board and iPads prompt the drop; the tower senses it; a player then taps which kingdom each released skull landed in and which building it was placed on, or that it stayed inside.
