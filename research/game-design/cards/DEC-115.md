"This will only show the effect of events. No recap or statistics."

When a day ends (beat 4, DEC-077), the Story board shows what that day's events did, such as a threat moving, a skull's effect or a countdown ticking. It shows no summary of the day and no stats. The numbers are kept for the end of the game (DEC-116).
