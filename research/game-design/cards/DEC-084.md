Like RtDT: once a party starts a quest (DEC-083), the whole scene is played on the spot, start to finish, and the board waits. No world time passes while it plays. With one party this plays the same as a quest spread over turns; with several, the other parties watch the scene. Rejected: spending moves inside the quest across turns, and charging world time for its length.
