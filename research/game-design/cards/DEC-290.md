Bob chose "Five, flags as conditions". The engine offers five trigger nodes (DEC-288):

| Trigger | Loop | Fires when |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive on a space | group turn (MEC-035) | a group steps onto a space |
| Enter a place | encounter (MEC-036) | a group enters a building, caravan or story token |
| Pick from a menu | encounter (MEC-036) | a group picks a menu item, an NPC included |
| Battle ends | battle (MEC-038) | the battle ends won, ran or fallen |
| Set piece ends | set piece (MEC-037) | the set piece ends done, left or failed |

A story flag or lead never fires a beat by itself. Any trigger may require one ("enter the inn once flag X is set"), so a beat cannot set off another beat. Each trigger's exact node number is fixed when its loop is drawn. Rejected: flags firing as a sixth trigger, and meeting an NPC as a seventh apart from other picks.
