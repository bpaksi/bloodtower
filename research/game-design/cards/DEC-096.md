Three or four attributes (for example Might, Stealth, Magic, perhaps Wits). A fight or test uses the one that fits the challenge: a fortress duel Might, the ruins Stealth, a shrine Magic. Party strength is the group's total in that attribute, plus gear and virtues, so a mixed party matters. Which attributes, and each hero's values, are Q-030.
