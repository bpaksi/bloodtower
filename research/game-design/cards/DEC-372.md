Bob: "Yes, please move forward with 'game uses the physical cards, skulls, companions and the trait effects'". On RtDT's foe cards, each of a foe's two traits has a fixed battle effect. Each foe card also has one event, and DEC-311 already gives each foe type one ability.

| Trait | RtDT | Bloodtower |
|---|---|---|
| Melee | Lose gear | Lose a gear card |
| Magic | Manipulates the Tower | Open (Q-271) |
| Stealth | Lose potions | Lose a potion card |
| Humanoid | Adds skulls to buildings | Adds a skull to a building (the table picks, DEC-092) |
| Beast | Lose extra warriors | A harder hit (more hit points lost) |
| Undead | Lose spirit | Lose gold |

When an effect fires, how much it takes and what Magic does are Q-271. Rejected: the one ability only, with traits used just to match Advantages.
