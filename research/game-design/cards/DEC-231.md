Bob chose "Silent; haptic tap only". The table hears only the Story board and the tower. Every press shows a clear press state; a light haptic is added only where the device and browser support it. Most iPads and Safari do not, so the press state must carry the feedback alone. Rejected: soft tap sounds on by default, and no haptics at all.

Bob, 2026-10-25: if an iPad still shows a video, that video's sound stays muted. Do not add iPad audio.
