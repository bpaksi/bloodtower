Playtest PT-02 (paper playtest, 2026-11-12): DEC-044 said all 4 buildings of one kingdom, the C3 plan's Q-C3-05 (Bob, approved 2026-10-31) said 4 fallen buildings lose the game, and the day loop's `fallenLimit` counts them board-wide.

- **Ruling.** The game is lost when 4 buildings are destroyed anywhere on the board (16 exist). They need not share a kingdom.
- **Timing** is DEC-905's: the check runs the moment the fourth building falls, with DEC-676's stop.
- **Replaces** the one-kingdom wording of DEC-044 (Bob, 2026-10-20). Bob's 2026-10-31 approval of Q-C3-05 is the later word. The code already works this way.
- **Dracula (1.2)** keeps its own "four houses" ending (DEC-885).
- **Loss scene.** The fallen-buildings scene is plain text with no new art until a table reaches it (PT-11). Its wording is Loremaster's (LQ-6, default A).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
