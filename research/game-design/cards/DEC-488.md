Asked for the Tomb's meter, Bob answered with what a room does: "I think it is more that each room as a chance for 1) nothing 2) battle 3) trap (this is new, something I'd like to consider)".

This is a change from RtDT, where every room is a flat loss that one Advantage turns into a gain, and no room starts a fight ([app-dungeon-screen.md](../../return-to-dark-tower/app-dungeon-screen.md#the-rooms)). In the Tomb, entering a room reveals nothing, a battle (MEC-038) or a trap. There is no separate meter: hit points lost in battles and traps are the pressure, and the party leaves before it falls.
