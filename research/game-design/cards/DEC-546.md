The line is half the holder's full hit points, rounded up: 6 for a Fighter (12), 4 for a Magic user (8). A share keeps the line fair across classes, where RtDT's fixed 10 would not. Hit points lost to a guarded hit, a retreat's last attack and a card effect all count. Falling to 0 always reaches it.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
