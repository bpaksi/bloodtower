These areas list what the tower can do, one card per sound, seal, light, effect, sequence or drum. They are facts of the hardware, not rulings, and nothing in them is waiting for a design call. 1.0 uses the library as catalogued (SND-, SEAL-, LGT-, LFX-, LSQ- and DRM- cards), and each cue (CUE-001 to CUE-012) picks from it by DEC-055's defaults. A new sound or light needs its own ruling when a cue asks for it.

This card records that the 19 areas are settled so `pnpm graph gaps` can close them.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
