Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 6, at the first rally with the Bard present.
- **1.0:** a quarter off every gold price the Bard's group pays at a building, rounded down, at least 1 off. That covers the citadel's rally (5 → 4), a virtue draw (10 → 8) and the sanctuary's cure (10 → 8). It does not apply to tolls or pay-offs in scenes and events.
- **1.1:** DEC-545's village and bazaar wording comes back when those buildings sell again. It could keep the 1.0 reach as well; that choice waits for 1.1.
- **Why:** every class should bring something in 1.0. Gold is the Bard pair's tightest resource: in the walk it paid for 5 rallies and ran out on day 15.

Not picked: a 1.0 stand-in of another kind, such as +1 gold per won battle; leaving it dead in 1.0.
