The review: losing a battle costs resources, recovering costs turns, and the unattended enemies grow meanwhile. A fallen hero comes back at 1 hit point (DEC-119) and costs no gold (DEC-175), but a retreat leaves the foe growing (DEC-016) and the sanctuary charges more for each corruption a hero already carries (DEC-170), so the hero who most needs a cure pays most.
