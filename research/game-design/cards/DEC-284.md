Bob chose "A short sequence of nodes". In a loop diagram a story point is one node. Inside it the story writes its own small railroad from the same kinds (board, tower, prompt, check, change…), for example narration, a tower cue, then a choice. The story point's home card holds the engine's default content, such as the game start cue (CUE-001), which a story can keep, change or replace. Rejected: board and tower nodes only, and one board moment plus one tower cue.
