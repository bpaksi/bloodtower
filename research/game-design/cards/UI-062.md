The round action picker (UI-027) and the use button (UI-038) gain these 1.1 choices.

- **Inventive** (DEC-549): a round action after the foe's card shows, costing 4 potions. It is greyed out in the boss battle and in Tower rooms, with the reason.
- **Siren's Song** (DEC-485): on Retreat, the potion is offered. The retreat result (UI-039) then says the foe was lured and does not rise.
- **Callous** (DEC-546): when it fires, the board shows a line at the battle's end naming the hero and the hit points lost against their line.
- **Before a round:** Grigor's "Pay 1 gold: +1 Wild" with uses left (DEC-726), and Zaida's "Spend a treasure: +3 Wild" once a battle (DEC-734), which says it costs the fight number.
