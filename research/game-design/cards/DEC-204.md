Bob chose "One moment, plays through", and asked to "also allow for a transition between dialogs". A conversation is one moment whose lines may have any speakers, the narrator over a panning still included (DEC-056). The lines play back to back on their own, and the board changes to each line's speaker with a transition. The table can skip or replay it like any visual, and a press comes only at the end. It needs no new pack data: a moment's `lines` already name a speaker each (story-pack-format §2.1). Rejected: a press per line, and a chain of one-line moments.
