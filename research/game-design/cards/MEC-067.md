**What:** A talking portrait with 8 mouth frames and an anchor box for each character who speaks.

**When:** For every story character with a voiced line (DEC-349, DEC-719): named NPCs, companions, the adversary. A silent speaker needs only mood stills.

**Why:** The talking avatar is how the story's people speak; a line with no face fails the asset check (DEC-062).

**Pack:** `cast[]` portrait and mouth frames (§2.1). The count is avatars per speaker.

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 1 per speaker | 1 per speaker | 1 per speaker |
| Target | 1 per speaker | 1 per speaker | 1 per speaker |
