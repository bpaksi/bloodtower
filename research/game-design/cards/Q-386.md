Probe P-003, day 6. The party rallied at the Inner Kinghills citadel with the Bard present. Silver Tongue takes "a quarter off every village and bazaar price" (DEC-545). In 1.0 the bazaar's menu is only Cleanse, and the village's rest is free (DEC-377, DEC-502, DEC-174). Merchants, potions and gear wait for 1.1. So the Bard's first virtue has no effect anywhere in 1.0.
