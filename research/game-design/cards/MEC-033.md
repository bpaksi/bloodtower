- **Owner:** story.
- **Enter:** .1 to .4: the challenge step (DEC-133) and board bounds (DEC-134, DEC-146) take effect, and the set piece tokens go on the board. Everything else carries over (DEC-145). Continue skips it (DEC-283).
- **Start point:** .5 to .9, the chapter's narration and its card.
- **Body:** .10 and .11, one day per pass (MEC-034).
- **Exit:** .11: "won" when every set piece is done (DEC-041), "lost" when a loss check is true (DEC-086 step 8). A loss on the same day as a finish is a loss.
- **Close:** .17 to .21: with the tower in play, a won chapter breaks one seal; the app picks it, and a story may name it. The glyph behind it is revealed and its story boon takes effect (DEC-303). Without the tower, seals drop out (DEC-306). Then .22 to .30: any skulls the break freed are placed one at a time, as after a drop (DEC-409).
- **Result up:** won or lost, to the game (MEC-032).
- **End point:** .12 to .16. A won chapter plays its cue, its closing hook and the stamped card. A lost chapter plays nothing here; the game's defeat cue and outcome hook tell it (MEC-032 .46, .47).
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: one title painting per chapter, on the chapter card (UI-020).

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). Story beats inside the days fire on the engine's trigger nodes (DEC-290), not on a day count (DEC-287). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower are not drawn; they use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
