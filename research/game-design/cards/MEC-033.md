- **Owner:** story.
- **Enter:** the chapter's challenge step (DEC-133) and board bounds (DEC-134) take effect; its set piece tokens go on the board.
- **Start point:** the chapter's narration.
- **Body:** one pass is one day. At each day's start the engine plays any of the chapter's timed beats that are due (DEC-141).
- **Exit:** "won" when its set pieces are done (DEC-041); "lost" when a loss check is true (DEC-086, step 8). Checked at the end of each day.
- **Result up:** won or lost.
- **End point:** the chapter's closing narration, with the result.
- **Screen:** the Story board (SCR-014).
- **UI:** the chapter card (UI-020).
- **Art:** one title painting per chapter.
- **Sound:** CUE-004 at the start, CUE-005 when won.
