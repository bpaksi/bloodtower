Bob chose "Turn start + battle rounds" for when the iPad offers a held potion.

- **Turn start:** every held potion is offered in the group's turn-start step (UI-037), next to the virtues.
- **Battle:** the potions that help in a fight, Dragon Teeth (heal) and Fortune's Favor (+1 Wild), are also offered as a hero's round action. Spending one costs that hero's fight number for the round, like a conditional Advantage (DEC-324).
- **Not offered elsewhere:** there is no potion button between moves or encounters.

Rejected: offering potions between every step of a turn (closest to RtDT's "any time on your turn"), turn start only, and a free potion in battle.
