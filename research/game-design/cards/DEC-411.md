Bob chose "Turn start + battle rounds" for when the iPad offers a held potion.

- **Turn start:** every held potion except Siren's Song and Fortune's Favor (DEC-483) is offered in the group's turn-start step (UI-037), next to the virtues.
- **Battle:** the potions that help in a fight, Dragon Teeth (heal), Fortune's Favor (+1 Wild) and Siren's Song (a free run, DEC-417, added later the same day), are also offered in battle, before each round, on the use button. Spending one costs no round action (DEC-484).
- **Not offered elsewhere:** there is no potion button between moves or encounters.

Rejected: offering potions between every step of a turn (closest to RtDT's "any time on your turn"), turn start only, and a free potion in battle.

DEC-483 and DEC-484 (Bob, 2026-10-28) replaced "Spending one costs that hero's fight number" and took Fortune's Favor out of the turn start. Trimmed under DEC-352.
