Bob chose "Anyone can finish it". Any group that arrives meets the same horde, with its cut count and its status, and gets the usual choice (DEC-023). The group that wins takes the full reward (DEC-120); the group that softened it gets nothing. Groups can wear a foe down for each other. Rejected: only the group that ran may finish it, and a fresh full horde for every other group.
