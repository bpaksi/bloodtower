Audit finding LC-01 (decision D1). DEC-676 says an empty supply and a fallen kingdom "can only happen when skulls land, at the day's close or the chapter's close", and are checked at MEC-033 .11. That was never true: DEC-559 drops a skull in the middle of a battle, and the chapter close places freed skulls (.22 to .30) after .11 has already run.

- **Checked after each skull:** the moment a skull leaves the supply (the day's drop, real or simulated; a Magic trait drop, DEC-559; any other skull a rule drops), and the moment a skull lands on a building (the day's placement, the seal break's freed skulls, a Humanoid trait skull).
- **Lost if either is true:** the supply is 0, or every building of one kingdom is destroyed (DEC-044).
- **Then it is DEC-676's stop:** every open loop ends (battle, tomb, quest, place, group turn, day, chapter); no other group plays; the game loop's close and end run (MEC-032 .43 to .49) with the loss scene and its reason. No later step of the interrupted loop plays: no battle round, no placement prompt, no reward.
- **If several are true at once:** the reason line names the first of: a third corruption, an empty supply, a fallen kingdom. It is one loss.
- **MEC-033 .11** stays the exit check for "every quest done" and "the Tower won". It is now a backstop for the losses, not their only home. A loss on the same day as a finish is still a loss: the drop's loss fires before .11.
- **A won chapter can still be lost:** if the seal break's freed skulls destroy a kingdom's last building (.27), the game is lost then. The chapter's won cue, closing hook and stamp (.13 to .16) do not play; the loss scene does. The chapter never finished closing.
- **Dracula (1.2):** DEC-885 stands as written. Its order (Turned, then the Valley Falls, then the Houses Fall) is Dracula's own; this card does not generalize it.
- **Trims DEC-676** (the other-losses bullet; its premise was false) and the "checked at .11, after the day's drop" line of DEC-686. Bob's choices stand: DEC-676's "at once" for the third corruption, and DEC-686's own title, "the loss is checked when a drop empties it".

Fixtures for R03: (a) a Magic card drops the last supply skull in a battle round: lost at once, no later round; (b) a seal break's freed skull destroys a kingdom's last building: lost at .27, no chapter stamp; (c) a day drop of two skulls with one left: lost at the first; (d) a third corruption and the last skull on one node: one loss, reason "third corruption".

Not picked: check only at .11 plus a second check after the chapter close (a mid-battle drop would let the group win a battle after the game was lost); queue the loss until the open loop ends (a lost game that keeps playing); lose only when a drop is due and the supply is empty (DEC-686 already refused it).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
