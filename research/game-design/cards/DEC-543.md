Bob chose "Same slot, as a rule". Wherever a trigger node (DEC-290) fires at a loop's start or end, it and the loop hook (DEC-540) are one hook with one place to fill. The battle's end hook is the "battle ends" trigger, as the set piece's end hook is "set piece ends" (DEC-297). Rejected: merging only the battle case, and two slots played one after the other.
