Bob picked "Hommlet: the ruined keep". The goal, in his words: "I don't want to rush the Dracula story. So for the v1 release I want to continue and turn 'Proving Grounds' into a 'real' story. It will serve [two] purposes, 1) test the game, 2) deliver something playable." It must use at least one 1.0 element and feel complete from beginning to end.

- **The shape:** a village lives in the shadow of a ruined keep. Chapter 1 is trouble on the roads around the village. Chapter 2 goes down into the ruins under the keep, and the Tomb is there. Chapter 3 is the evil behind it all, in the Tower.
- **Why it fits:** it maps almost one to one onto DEC-636's Gate, Barrow and Tower chapters. It needs nothing that 1.0 cuts (DEC-502), because nobody in the village has to be questioned or unmasked.

Not picked: Hommlet with a Saltmarsh twist (the haunting is a front); N1's village cult (its "who is in the cult" play waits for 1.1 NPCs); U1's haunted house mystery (it leans hardest on clues and NPCs, which 1.0 cuts).
