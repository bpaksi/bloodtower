Probe P-003, day 6. The party went back into the Barrow after a retreat. The guardian still stood at count 13 (DEC-651). DEC-607 gives bold a free cut and on guard a last attack "before round 1" when a group enters a battle room. It doesn't say whether walking back into a room the group already fought in counts as entering. DEC-675's "nothing fires twice" covers Continue, not a new visit. If it counts, a bold re-entry takes 1 off on every return.
