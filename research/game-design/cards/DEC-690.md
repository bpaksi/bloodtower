Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. The finale desk walk of probe P-003 found the gap when the tower was lost for good at the arrival show.
- **Held skulls stay held.** The app keeps their count, but they are not in the supply and not on any building. The table doesn't open the tower to get them.
- **The clock is unchanged:** the simulated drop (DEC-305) takes 1 a day from the supply, as the real drop did. Going on without the tower neither speeds the clock nor slows it.
- **Reconnect later** (DEC-229) puts the tower back, with its held count, and the next seal break may free them again (DEC-409).
- **Why:** no physical step that the table can't do, and no change to the loss the players were already watching.

Not picked: the held skulls fall at once onto buildings (a sudden spike no one caused); they go back to the supply (it would buy free days for losing the tower).

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
