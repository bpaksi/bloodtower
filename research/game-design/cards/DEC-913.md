Audit finding BE-02 (decision D6). The encounter loop takes 1 move at .8 and exits at .15 with no moves left, so a group that spends its last move on a pick would lose DEC-399's free second pick. DEC-399 also says the second pick triggers Inspiring again, which DEC-548 (heal once per visit) overrides. Forced turn-ends (EE-01) were not ranked against it.

- **The entitlement:** once per day, a group holding Fortunate that takes a pick at a building may take a second pick at the same building for no move (DEC-399). It needs a first pick, so a group with no moves left cannot start it.
- **No move gate on the free pick.** While the entitlement is pending and the menu still has a pick, .15 does not end the visit even with 0 moves left, and .8 takes no move for that pick. When it is taken, or the group says Leave, the entitlement ends.
- **A forced turn-end comes first.** A quest pick ends the turn once it resolves (DEC-821). Uncertain ends the turn after the pick (DEC-468). Either one ends the turn right then; the free pick is lost. A group that wants both takes the quest as its second pick. The quest, taken second, then ends the turn as usual.
- **Any pick is legal as the second,** Cleanse included, but Cleanse stays once per building per visit (DEC-572), and a skull-shrunk menu offers only what it still holds (DEC-093).
- **Inspiring heals once per visit** (DEC-548). The free pick does not heal again. This trims DEC-399's "triggers Inspiring again".

Fixtures for R02: the last move on a rest, then a free pick at 0 moves; a quest taken first (no free pick); a quest taken second; Uncertain held (no free pick); a Cleanse second after a Cleanse first (refused); the free pick does not heal twice.

Not picked: let a quest pick be followed by the free pick (DEC-821's "the moves left are lost" would stop meaning it); the free pick costs a move (it would never be free).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
