Mirrors RtDT's two kinds. Automatic Advantages (gear, passive virtues) that match the foe's trait count toward strength in every round. Conditional ones (potions, some virtues) are spent by a hero as their round action and boost that one round. How much each adds is detail for Q-023.

Refined by DEC-324 (Bob, 2026-10-27): a spent conditional Advantage now softens the foe's revealed card one step, instead of a one-round strength boost.
