Bob picked "Keep as the 1.1 values". In the script each one moves win rates by 3 points or less (normal, every cell):

| Lever | Tried | Biggest change |
|---|---|---|
| Lieutenant level | chapter level instead of +1 | up to +2 points |
| Strike | a flat 5 instead of 3 + chapter | under 1 point |
| Hire | 30 gold instead of 20 | under 1 point |

They become 1.1's tuning values, still to check in the playtest. The strike stops at 1 and never fells (DEC-775), and a party heals it off, which is why it barely matters. Its feel at the table is for the playtest.

Not picked: keeping them but making the strike a flat 5; and leaving all three as placeholders.
