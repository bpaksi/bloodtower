Corruption is a loss condition (DEC-043) and comes from falling in a fight (DEC-119), a curse (DEC-169) and events, and the sanctuary removes it (DEC-170). But no card says what kinds there are or what each does beyond the move roll (DEC-102). AREA-009 has nothing decided. The toolkit lets a story rename them (MEC-059).
