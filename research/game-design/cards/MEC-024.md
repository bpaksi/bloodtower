**Proposal:** the tower plays the battle horn and the Story board shows the horde. Each round the present heroes pick Fight or Run on an iPad. A seeded draw from party strength against foe level picks the round's winner: the horde count drops, or a random present hero loses hit points, with the tower's hit sound for that side. It ends when the horde is gone (bounty: loot and money, IDEA-002, DEC-008), the group runs (the horde stays and grows), or a hero reaches 0 hit points and takes a corruption.
