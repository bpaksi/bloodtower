The tower plays the battle horn and the Story board shows the horde. Each round:
1. The present heroes pick Fight or Run together (DEC-118). Run ends the fight; the horde stays and grows (DEC-016).
2. Each present hero picks one action on their iPad (DEC-129). Picking nothing means a normal attack.
3. Spells cut the horde first (DEC-122, DEC-123).
4. A seeded draw (DEC-011) weighs party strength against the foe's level: the fight numbers of heroes making a normal attack (DEC-130) plus matching Advantages (DEC-128).
5. A won round cuts the horde, with the tower's hit sound. A lost round hits a random present hero (DEC-015), unless a guard virtue cancels it.
6. A hero at 0 hit points takes a corruption and sits out the rest of the fight (DEC-119).

It ends when the horde is gone (gold and one card by foe level, DEC-120), the group runs, or every present hero has fallen, which counts as a run.
