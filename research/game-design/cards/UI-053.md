The adversary's token sits on the Story board's map from the day he starts roaming (DEC-791; The Warden after The Barrow). A faint path runs to the building he heads for (DEC-802), and that building is ringed.

- **Threat bar** (UI-047): "The Warden: 2 days to Ostvale" beside the skull counts, so a group can dodge him.
- **Day's end:** the board draws his move of up to 2 spaces (DEC-775). On arrival a skull drops onto the building, and the path redraws to the next target.
- **Last chapter** (DEC-738): at its start the token walks back to the Tower, the path is gone, and the bar says "Waits in the Tower".
