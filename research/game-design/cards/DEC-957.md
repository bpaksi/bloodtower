Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden. A group with 0 present heroes is an illegal state.

- It cannot start or finish a quest.
- It is not paid the quest's gold, and it is not paid 0 as a payout. There is no split to perform.
- The engine refuses the step. It does not treat the empty group as a normal group of none.

Whether a real game can reach an empty group is not decided here. The guard stands either way.
