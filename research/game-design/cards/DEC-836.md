Bob picked "Every [COPY] slot". The C3 to C5 plans and briefs hold 95 distinct `[COPY: …]` tags. About half are story lines: scenes, chapter names and goals, seal breaks, battle and boss board lines, the finale and the endings. The rest are help text (`[COPY: help <screen>]`) and UI labels. The script writes all of them, so C5's swap from placeholders to copy needs no writing.

Help text and labels follow the tone (DEC-834) only as far as plain instructions allow: they say what to tap, in short sentences.

Where a plan already proposes a line (`[COPY: tag = "…"]`), the script starts from it and may change it.

Not picked: story and in-world lines only, with help text left to the screens; and scene Scroll lines only.
