Playtest PT-15 (2026-11-12): a first-time player picked a class by its letter, because an iPad shows no art (DEC-201).

- **Ruling.** While heroes are picked, the Story board's pool shows a card per hero: portrait, class, fight number, hit points and one line. A seated hero greys out.
- **The line** is the pack's `ability`. Loremaster can give each ability word a line in the Copy table (Q8.1).
- **iPad copy unchanged:** "Pick Hero A", "N of N picked", "Who this iPad speaks for at the table". "Picked" and "Open" stay; Loremaster may reword in step 45 (Q8.3).
- **No cap** on heroes per iPad. "Another iPad is waiting for a hero" is **not built in 1.0** (Q8.2).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
