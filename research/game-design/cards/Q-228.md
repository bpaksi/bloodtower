Bob asked: "npc's; are they solely defined by story." Today the engine owns building roles, their offers and trust (MEC-047, DEC-026), and the story names them and writes their lines. Named NPCs are the story's own (MEC-048). People met in the wild are drawn from the engine's offers and renamed by the story (DEC-024).
