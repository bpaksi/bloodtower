Two effects, together:
- **The menu shrinks**, as in OPT-005: 1–2 skulls and the rumours turn bad, 3 (shadowed) and the merchant closes, 4 (destroyed) and every place and person is gone. A story point there moves; a main story point is never lost (DEC-088).
- **It gets dangerous:** a group that ends its day at a skulled building can draw an event, more likely the more skulls it has.

The exact steps and chances are numbers for later.
