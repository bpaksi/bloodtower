Bob picked "A floor, cap at ~95%". The pass only lifts the cells below about 80%. A big party is meant to feel strong, so 3 or 4 heroes at Short and Standard stay at 89–96% in the script, and 1.0's numbers for them don't change.

**Why it came up:** pulling those cells down to 80% would take about 2 skulls a night. Their ceiling is about 95%, set by corruption losses and bad luck, not the clock.

Not picked: a target band of 75–85% for every cell; and a floor with a cap of about 90%.
