Bob: a tavern and a merchant. The tavern offers **rest and a meal**, **rumours** (a hint at the next encounter, a story point or a threat) and **side jobs** (Bob's picks). The merchant sells everyday goods, leaving rare goods to the bazaar; what it takes as payment is Q-019. Every feature is one a story switches on or off (IDEA-008), and a story may add NPCs such as an elder. Base Reinforce (6 warriors) is under review in Q-020. Skulls close it step by step (OPT-005).

**Parked 2026-10-27:** retired by the village rulings (DEC-164, DEC-172 to DEC-174) (DEC-308).
