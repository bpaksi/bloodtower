Bob asked Claude to draft all 10 in one pass, for his review.

- **RtDT:** +2 Melee. Spend 1 spirit for +1 Wild, up to 3 times per turn.
- **Bloodtower:** Before any battle round, the holder may pay 1 gold from their share of the purse for +1 Wild Advantage for the rest of that battle, up to 3 times a battle. Paying gold is not spending a held card, so the holder keeps the fight number (DEC-484).
- **Why:** Keeps the printed trade, with gold standing in for spirit as the item fit chart does. It gives gold a use in battle.
