- **Might:** fights, duels, forcing a way through. The fortress.
- **Stealth:** sneaking, traps, picking locks. The ruins.
- **Magic:** spells, wards, glyphs. The shrine.
- **Wits:** lore, riddles, tracking and finding the way. The cave hunt and the riddles.

The fighter leads with Might, the scout with Stealth, the magic user with Magic, and the scholar and ranger with Wits. The healer's strength is an ability (healing) rather than an attribute. The values are numbers for later.

**Parked 2026-10-22:** retired by DEC-125: heroes have no attributes.
