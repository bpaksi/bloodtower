The last to pick is the one the table is waiting on, so the tap follows naturally. If no one taps in 5 seconds, every standing hero's iPad shows Strike, and the first tap resolves the round. A solo player or a dropped iPad is covered the same way, with no Story board button, since the board is never input (DEC-278).
