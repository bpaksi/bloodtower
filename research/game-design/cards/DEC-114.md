In the core setup step for the purse (DEC-110), a player presses Spin. The tower's lights chase round, slow, and stop on a side with a coin sound, and the Story board shows the amount. The draw is seeded, so a replay matches. The tower plays lights and sound only; nothing mechanical moves. The amount never goes below a floor, so no party starts broke. The range, the floor, and whether length or player count scale it are numbers for later.
