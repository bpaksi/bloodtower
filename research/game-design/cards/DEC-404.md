- **RtDT:** at month end, gain 15 warriors.
- **Bloodtower:** at the end of each day (MEC-034), the hero holding Resourceful heals a small amount (DEC-039). Only the holder heals. It is a steady trickle rather than RtDT's once-a-month burst.

The amount is left to the numbers pass (Q-296). Rejected: a full heal at chapter start, and a heal for the whole group at chapter start.
