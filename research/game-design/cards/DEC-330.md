Bob chose "Foe painting has its own". There is no terrain or place backdrop layer by default: each foe painting is a full picture with its own setting, so foes need no cut-outs. For a set-piece battle a story may replace the foe painting with its own picture from its scene pictures (MEC-066), such as the foe in a castle hall or a crypt. Putting a backdrop behind the standard painting would need cut-outs, so a story swaps the whole picture. Rejected: 6 terrain backdrops, and 3 or 4 place-type backdrops.
