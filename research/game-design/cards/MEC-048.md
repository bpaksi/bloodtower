**What:** The story's own people, met in a quest or an encounter. Once met they stay on the place's menu for the chapter, and the clues the party holds unlock hero actions with them.

**When:** For anyone the plot wants the heroes to come back to: a witness, a suspect, a guide.

**Why:** Named people turn rumours and clues into choices (ask, accuse) and make the board feel inhabited.

**Pack:** cast entries and their lines (§2, §2.1); clue tags (DEC-182).
