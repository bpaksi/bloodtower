Probe P-003, day 11. The party retreated from *The Last Stand* (*Hold the line*) with the Neuri still standing. MEC-038 .35 puts a foe token on the space for any standing foe outside a tomb (DEC-321). MEC-037 keeps the quest open with "its progress" (DEC-151). That leaves two foes for one fight: a token any group may finish, and a quest that would deal a fresh battle at the full count from MEC-077 .1. DEC-651 settled the same split for the Tomb, but not for scenes.
