Bob chose "Spell, guard, Advantage, perk".

- **Spell:** cast as a round action, once for big damage or without limit per battle or dungeon (DEC-122, DEC-123).
- **Guard:** a round action that takes the foe's hit for the party (DEC-129, DEC-319).
- **Advantage:** counts as a matching foe trait in battle, automatic every round or conditional when spent (DEC-324).
- **Perk:** a board effect outside battle, such as +1 to the move roll (DEC-102), a cheaper price or more from rest.

Each class's six (DEC-156) mix the kinds; a magic user's are mostly spells, a fighter's few or none (DEC-121). The lists themselves stay open in Q-248. Rejected: battle kinds only, and RtDT's 16 tiles re-fitted.
