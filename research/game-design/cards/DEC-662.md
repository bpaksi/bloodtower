Bob picked "The reveal". The heroes think they face only raiders. In the Tomb under the keep they learn the raiders serve The Warden, a lord everyone thought long dead. Chapter 3 is the hunt for him. The turn is told through scenes, so it needs nothing that 1.0 cuts.

- **Chapter 1, The Roads (Arisilon):**
  - *The Herald*, at the village: the herald begs for help. Choice: take the job, or ask for pay first.
  - *The Ambush*, on a wild space: raiders. Fight them, or pay the toll.
- **Chapter 2, The Keep (Brynthia opens):**
  - *The Barrow*, the Tomb under the ruined keep. Its guardian is the keep's dead garrison. **The reveal:** the raiders wear The Warden's crest. The guardian's fall gives the *Warden's Signet*, the story item DEC-636 called the Barrow Key.
  - *The Shrine*, the sanctuary where The Warden swore his oath. It grants his *oath-blade*, a quest item.
- **Chapter 3, The Tower (the whole board):**
  - *The Lock*: the Signet opens the keep's sealed gate and the road to the Tower.
  - *The Last Stand*: The Warden's oathsworn guard, a level 3 battle.
  - **Finale:** The Warden in the Tower.

Not picked: a straight road (the herald names The Warden from the start); the village under threat (it loses something each chapter, and the last stand is fought at its gate).
