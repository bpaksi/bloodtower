Bob chose "Hides, returns after". When a visual or cut scene zooms to full screen, the art gets the whole screen. The frame slides back when the visual ends, and a number that changed meanwhile pulses once. Rejected: the frame staying on top, and fading it to low opacity. Which edge it sits on is left as Q-095.
