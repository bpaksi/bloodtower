Bob chose "Intrinsic virtues only".

Spells stay virtues (DEC-121), but only a class's 2 intrinsic virtues (DEC-373) can be spells. They live in the app with no physical piece; RtDT has no spell tile. The 16 virtue tiles in the pool never grant a spell, so a fighter never learns Fireball. Which classes have which of the three spells (DEC-123) is Q-248.

Rejected: a spellbook apart from virtues, spells from item cards, and intrinsic spells plus Scroll-like cards.
