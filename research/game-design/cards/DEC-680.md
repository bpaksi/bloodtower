Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 6, retreating from the Barrow's guardian.
- **The step:** on the retreat track, before the last attack (MEC-038 .32), the iPad asks: does a standing hero with a guard virtue cover the retreat? It is skipped when none is present. This is a new prompt node, numbered .42 and up when the diagram is redrawn.
- **The hit:** a guarded last attack is a guarded hit (DEC-556) on the guard, whoever the card would have hit. A Sweep becomes one guarded hit on the guard, as in a round.
- **No cost:** the group is leaving, so the guard gives up no fight number.
- **In the walk:** the Fighter covered five retreats and took 1 to 2 each time. No retreat felled the Bard after day 6.

Not picked: the guard picked for the last round carries over (a retreat comes before that round's actions); no guard on a retreat, with DEC-319 trimmed.
