Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 6, retreating from the Barrow's guardian.
- **The step:** on the retreat track, before the last attack (MEC-038 .32), the iPad asks: does a standing hero with a guard virtue cover the retreat? It is skipped when none is present. This is a new prompt node, numbered .42 and up when the diagram is redrawn.
- **Where the check lives (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden):** the guard-prompt check is the battle-end brief (C4/03). The Tower part of that check, a Tower retreat still asking it and still costing a move, is the Tower loop (C5/01). The prompt itself is an iPad state of the round picker (C4/10).
- **The hit:** a guarded last attack is a guarded hit (DEC-556) on the guard, whoever the card would have hit: a seeded quarter to half of the normal hit, rounded up, at least 1. It is not 0 (Gamewright, 2026-11-27, under Bob's delegation via Warden). A Sweep becomes one guarded hit on the guard, as in a round. The guard can drop to 0. With two guards, the one with more hit points takes it; a tie is the first in seat order (DEC-556).
- **No cost:** the group is leaving, so the guard gives up no fight number.
- **In the walk:** the Fighter covered four retreats and took 1 to 2 each time. No retreat felled the Bard after day 6.

Not picked: the guard picked for the last round carries over (a retreat comes before that round's actions); no guard on a retreat, with DEC-319 trimmed.
