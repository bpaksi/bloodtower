**Recommendation.** All three, each small:

- **Story board:** the glyph's icon sits on the kingdom it faces, always visible.
- **iPad:** the boosted action shows its new value, with the glyph icon beside it ("Cleanse: 2 skulls"). The move roll says "+1 Banner".
- **Moved or newly shown:** the board shows the change, with the tower's glyph sound (SND-099) when the tower is in play.

**Why:** a passive boost nobody notices is worth nothing, and the table never does sums. It keeps to the board for pictures and the iPad for text (DEC-199 to DEC-207).

**Next:** this needs a UI card when 1.1's deliverables are written (DEC-784).
