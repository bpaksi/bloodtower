Probe P-003, day 6. The party retreated from the Barrow's guardian with the Bard at 2 hit points. DEC-319 says the guard "also covers the last attack on a retreat", and DEC-650 says "a guard picked for the round still blocks it". But MEC-038's retreat track (.32, .33) comes before any round action (.6, .7), so no guard is ever picked. As drawn, the last attack always hits a random hero. In P-002 that is how a retreat felled the Ranger.
