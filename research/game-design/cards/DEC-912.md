Audit finding LC-03 (decision D6). DEC-824 says a won-quest night "doesn't move the count" for a pair; DEC-822 for solo and DEC-564 for 3 and 4 heroes say "every other night" with no word on it. DEC-826 (Long, 1.1) already excludes won-quest nights. The day diagram enters the real and the simulated drop with no due count.

- **One counter, N, the counted nights.** It starts at 0 and is saved with the game (Continue restores it). At a day's close, if any group won a quest that day, there is no drop, N does not change, and the panel shows "+1 day" (DEC-699). Otherwise N goes up by 1, and the due drop is read from N.
- **Due drop by party size (1.0):** 1 hero: 1 skull on an odd N, none on an even N (DEC-822). 2 heroes: 1 skull, none when N is a multiple of 4 (DEC-824). 3 or 4 heroes: 1 skull on an odd N, 2 on an even N (DEC-564, DEC-703).
- **One due count, three users:** a real tower drops the due skulls into the tower (MEC-034 .11); the no-tower game simulates that many, each with its own seeded kingdom (DEC-305); the days-left band counts forward with the same schedule, assuming no quest wins, plus 1 for a Cleanse (DEC-686).
- **A won-quest night skips the whole night's drop**, the second skull of a 3 or 4 hero night too (DEC-699).
- **Long (1.1, parked):** DEC-826 sits on top, counting the nights whose due drop is above 0. It uses the same N. It waits for 1.1.

Example, a pair: night 1 N=1 drops; night 2 a quest is won, no drop, N stays 1; night 3 N=2 drops; night 4 N=3 drops; night 5 N=4, no drop. A calendar count would have skipped night 4.

Fixtures for R03: zero, one and two drops; a won-quest night mid-cycle for each party size; Continue across the counter; days left equals the nights actually played when no quest is won. Check P-006's script on the next probe run for the solo and 3 or 4 hero counts; if a cell moves more than about 3 points, Bob picks the count (DEC-817).

Not picked: a calendar count (it hands the quest-win night's skip back to the pair's fourth night); a separate counter per rule.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
