Bob: "Freely shared. The amount of money displayed is always the pool. But the game tracks per player." Players see and spend one purse, as in DEC-007. Underneath, the app knows how much of the pool each hero holds, so a loss can hit heroes one by one (DEC-177). Rejected: fully separate gold per hero, and a purse with no per-hero tracking.
