The review's main complaint about RtDT: solo needed the harder setting, four players were too hard even on the base one. Nothing in Bloodtower scales with the number of heroes. Every group acts once before the tower acts (DEC-033), so four heroes get four times the actions against the same skull drop, which may tilt it the other way: easy at four, hard solo.
