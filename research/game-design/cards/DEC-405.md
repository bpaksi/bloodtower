Bob chose "Block a hit, once a battle, free".

- **RtDT:** spend spirit to prevent 6 warrior losses.
- **Bloodtower:** once per battle (MEC-038), after a lost round, the hero holding Blessed may cancel that round's hit on one hero in the group, the holder included. Nothing is paid, so gold stays out of the battle loop.
- **With a guard:** a guard takes the hit for the party at reduced damage (DEC-319). Blessed can cancel the guard's reduced hit too.

Rejected: paying gold per blocked hit, and paying gold to heal after a battle.
