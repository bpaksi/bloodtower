Bob chose "Split equally across the group". A gain is shared equally by the heroes in the group that earned it. A cost is paid equally by the heroes in the group that spends it, and when one hero's part runs short the others cover the gap. The starting purse is split equally too. Nobody picks whose money moves. Unlike a purchase, a group's loss in DEC-177 is capped per hero and not covered by the others. Rejected: the buyer pays first, and picking on the iPad.
