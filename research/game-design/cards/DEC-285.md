Bob, reviewing MEC-032: "For story touch points (which we haven't seen yet). These need to be game-engine provided optional hooks. They can't be open ended. i.e. narrative needs to be 1 or more dialogs, panning stills and/or talking avatars stringed together with optional transactions. The flow isn't defined at the story level"

A story point is a hook the engine places in a loop diagram, on a bypass track, so a story may fill it or leave it empty. What a story puts there is content only: one or more dialogs, panning stills (UI-003) or talking avatars, played in order. A story adds no prompts, checks or branches. The flow belongs to the engine's diagrams. What "optional transactions" means is Q-185. This replaces DEC-284, which let a story write its own railroad.
