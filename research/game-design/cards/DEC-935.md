Loremaster (step 45) asked whether Fix my position, in a battle, a scene or a building menu, is greyed with a reason or enabled and refused with an error. CU/05 Q5.3 (Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted) says it runs only on the move map or between loops; its EV-30 pins the refusal; no card says how the control looks.

- **The gear row stays enabled.** Game board setup is always enabled (C2/04), and the piece check inside it is useful in any state.
- **The control is greyed with a reason line** in those three states, as DEC-227 and the gear's other greyed rows do ("Only in a game.", "C3+"). The reason is a Loremaster line, proposed as "Only on the move map or between loops."
- **A press that still gets through is refused** (a forced press, a stale iPad, the host). That is CU/05 EV-30: no state change, one logged error with its code, shown as a Story board toast and on the iPad. EV-08's refusal of a space or group that does not exist is the same path with another code.
- **Not in the eval.** EV-30 sends the press directly, so it passes either way. The greyed state is a Copy-table slot and a screenshot item for the step that builds it.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-15, under Bob's delegation. No card decided this before; it follows DEC-227 and Q5.3. Revise in playtest.
