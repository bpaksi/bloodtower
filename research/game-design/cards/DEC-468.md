Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** After you Reinforce, your turn ends.
- **Bloodtower:** After the holder's group makes a pick at a building, the group's turn ends (MEC-035). The rest of its moves are lost. Item-fit.md proposed ending the day, but a Bloodtower day belongs to every group, so the turn is the closer match to RtDT. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).
