Bob: "Via the iPad maybe the party could listen down each open path." He picked "A hint that can lie".

- **The listen:** the acting group's iPad plays a sound for one open corridor. It hints at the kind of room behind it (DEC-488): still air for nothing, a click or drip for a trap, shuffling or breathing for a battle.
- **Usually right:** the hint matches what is behind the door most of the time. Sometimes it is wrong, as when an echo carries sound from another room. The roll is seeded like the room (DEC-011).
- **What it does not say:** which foe or which trap.
- **Why:** bold or on guard (DEC-492) becomes a guess with something behind it, and the guess can still go wrong.

Not picked: a hint that is always right; ambient sound for mood only; a hint about the guardian only.
