Playtest PT-03 (2026-11-12): a quit writes a record, yet Continue resumes the same game and it may then be won.

- **One record per game id.** Quit stamps it `quit`. Continue reopens that record. The final outcome (won or lost) overwrites it in the projection the stats read.
- **Append-only.** The earlier `quit` row stays in the event log (DEC-107). "Overwrite" is a projection rule only.
- **It stays `quit`** only if a New game or Play again replaces the autosave (DEC-208, DEC-709).
- **Totals and averages** count won and lost records only. A plain quit counts neither as played nor as won. Quit records show in the list.
- Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted: the append-only and not-counted lines above (QA.12).
