A group rolls its moves (seeded), then adds 1 for each virtue that helps movement and takes 1 away for each corruption. The result never goes below a floor, so a group can always do something, such as one building action. The board shows the roll, each modifier and the total, so players see why they got what they got.

Still open: the dice, the floor value, and which virtues count. These are tuned with the move-roll simulator.
