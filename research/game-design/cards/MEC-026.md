The Tomb is the one quest type designed before the build and playtest (DEC-486). It starts from the RtDT app's dungeon screen (DEC-487).

- **Picture:** a top-down floor plan on the Story board that grows room by room. The current room glows, open doorways are marked, and Exit and the group's Undead Advantage count are always shown (DEC-487, DEC-493).
- **Two halves:** the map sits beside a first-person view of the current room (DEC-583). A room has up to 3 corridors, left, ahead and right, and the view shows a wall where there is none (DEC-584). The view is one wide room joined from three blended sections that it pans across (DEC-585), in a tomb style the story supplies and a story event may change (DEC-588).
- **Listening:** the acting group may listen down any open corridor on its iPad for a hint of what is behind it, usually right but sometimes wrong (DEC-586). A corridor it listened down can only be entered on guard (DEC-587). The iPad shows three direction cards, Left, Ahead and Right, with Leave below (DEC-591). A trap's painting or a battle replaces the view half until it is resolved (DEC-592).
- **Meter:** none of its own. Hit points lost in battles and traps are the pressure (DEC-488).
- **Choices:** after each room, the acting group may listen down corridors, then picks an open doorway, bold or on guard, or leaves (DEC-490, DEC-492, DEC-586).
- **A room:** nothing, a battle or a trap (DEC-488). A trap is a random trap type with its own painting and a flat loss (DEC-489). Bold strikes first in a battle but takes a trap in full; on guard cuts a trap but lets the foe strike first (DEC-492). Matching Advantages work as in battle, traps too (DEC-493). A loss the party cannot pay is paid in part plus a corruption (DEC-496).
- **In and out:** the layout is drawn fresh for each tomb, and the target is a hidden guardian room. A story may give its door a threshold scene; without one it is a surprise, and its battle looks like any other (DEC-589, DEC-590). Beating the guardian is done, a fall is failed, and leaving or retreating is left (DEC-497). Progress is kept (DEC-491, DEC-151).
- **Rewards:** won battles pay as battles do; finishing pays the quest's reward (DEC-494).
- **Tower:** a door cue, then a cue for what is behind it (DEC-495).
