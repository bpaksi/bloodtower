The Tomb is the one set piece type designed before the build and playtest (DEC-486). It starts from the RtDT app's dungeon screen (DEC-487).

- **Picture:** a top-down floor plan on the Story board that grows room by room. The current room glows, open doorways are marked, and Exit and the group's Undead Advantage count are always shown (DEC-487, DEC-493).
- **Meter:** none of its own. Hit points lost in battles and traps are the pressure (DEC-488).
- **Choices:** after each room, the acting group picks an open doorway, bold or on guard, or leaves (DEC-490, DEC-492).
- **A room:** nothing, a battle or a trap (DEC-488). A trap is a random trap type with its own painting and a flat loss (DEC-489). Bold strikes first in a battle but takes a trap in full; on guard cuts a trap but lets the foe strike first (DEC-492). Matching Advantages work as in battle, traps too (DEC-493). A loss the party cannot pay is paid in part plus a corruption (DEC-496).
- **In and out:** the layout is drawn fresh for each tomb, and the target is a hidden guardian room. Beating the guardian is done, a fall is failed, and leaving or running is left. Progress is kept (DEC-491, DEC-151).
- **Rewards:** won battles pay as battles do; finishing pays the set piece's reward (DEC-494).
- **Tower:** a door cue, then a cue for what is behind it (DEC-495).

This is the step MEC-037 .5 calls: one pass is one room. MEC-037 .6 reads the result each pass sets. The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277).

**Cut** (DEC-496): flat costs on every room (rooms are now nothing, battle or trap); a straight line of 5 rooms (the map branches); money the party cannot pay coming out of hit points (RtDT's shortfall rule instead).
