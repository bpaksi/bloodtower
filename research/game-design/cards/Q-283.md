Raised by DEC-383. `@talking-avatar/formats` and `@talking-avatar/player` 0.1.0 are on npm and pinned in apps/bloodtower/src/web. Choices include a new `@asset-studio/*` scope with the old packages deprecated, or keeping the `@talking-avatar/*` names as one part of the new repo. A rename touches `scripts/pinned-versions.json`, which only Bob changes.
