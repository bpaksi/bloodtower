Bob chose Claude's recommendation: RtDT's 12 cards, each with its own drawback.

- **The cards:** the base box's 12 corruption cards (DEC-037). Each is one corruption.
- **Drawback:** each card has its own lasting drawback, built from the engine's fixed effect kinds (DEC-069), on top of the -1 to the move roll (DEC-102).
- **Unchanged:** a hero's 3rd loses the game (DEC-043); the sanctuary removes one at a time (DEC-170); a story may rename them (MEC-059).
- With at most 4 heroes and 2 corruptions each before a loss, 12 cards never run out.

What each card's drawback is, is Q-266. Rejected: every corruption the same, and a few kinds tied to how it was gained.
