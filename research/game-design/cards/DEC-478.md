Bob, after the item rulings: "We will need artwork for all of these. Please add cards to track the artwork. Only need 1 image per. We have source images as inspiration, however, all images should follow the existing look and feel we've already established."

- **One picture each:** every potion, gear, treasure, quest item, corruption and virtue tile the game names (DEC-370) gets one image. Cards with several copies (the three of each potion, the four Amulets of Hope) share it. Each image is one `ART-` card.
- **Look:** the game's established look, the oil-painting look the Portrait and Painting tools are named for (DEC-335), not the RtDT card style.
- **Sources:** the RtDT card scans in the backup folder (`reference/return-to-dark-tower/cards/`) are inspiration for the subject only. They stay out of the repo, and no image copies them.
