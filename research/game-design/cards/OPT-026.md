Bob: "1 image for each permutation of corridor options that is panning left to right and then back."

**For:** one seamless room; the slow pan feels like the party looking around, which is immersive.

**Against:** 7 paintings per tomb style (every mix of left, ahead and right, plus the dead end makes 8); a change to one corridor (listened to, cleared) needs an overlay on the painting; while panning, only part of the room is on screen at once.

Not picked: DEC-585 uses blended panels in one pan (OPT-027). Parked in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review.
