Bob asked: "What is the railway diagram for an NPC interaction." It is the talk pick (MEC-073). Bob chose "Add a face node". Before the NPC speaks, a roll node picks the face: the story's own face if it gave one, otherwise the default cast's face for the role (DEC-333), otherwise, for a stranger, one drawn from the shared pool (DEC-334). The nodes after it move up by one (.5 to .20). Rejected: leaving the face as a lookup inside the speaking node, and redrawing the diagram.
