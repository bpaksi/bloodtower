Bob picked "Quest foe, guardian +1".

- **Battle rooms:** the quest's foe at the quest's level.
- **Guardian:** the same foe, one level up.
- **Story:** the guardian is an optional slot (DEC-535). A story may fill it with its own foe.
- **Fixed:** all set with the tomb on first entry (DEC-602).

Not picked: random base foes in the battle rooms; a guardian always at lethal.
