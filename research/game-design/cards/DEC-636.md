Claude drafted it, and Bob accepted it as written. Each piece is there to reach one engine path, with as little story as possible.

- **Start:** all the heroes together at the Arisilon (N) citadel (DEC-112, "all same" plus a named place). Groups may split and join (DEC-031). This replaces DEC-223's one hero per citadel.
- **Chapter 1, The Gate:** the board is Arisilon only (DEC-134). It has two quests. *The Herald* is a plain scene at a building: narration and a choice, with no battle. *The Ambush* is a plain scene on a wild space, with a battle against a level 1 base foe.
- **Chapter 2, The Barrow:** Brynthia (E) opens, and Arisilon stays walkable (DEC-146). It has two quests. *The Barrow* is a Tomb (MEC-026, DEC-593) on a space, and its guardian's fall gives the story item *Barrow Key* (DEC-054). *The Shrine* is a plain scene at a sanctuary that grants a quest item (DEC-447).
- **Chapter 3, The Tower (finale):** the whole board opens. *The Lock* is a plain scene that needs the Barrow Key. *The Last Stand* is a plain scene with a battle against a level 3 foe. Together they unlock the Tower: the story's trigger is "chapter 3's quests are all done" (DEC-645). The finale place is the Tower (DEC-357, MEC-076).
- **Adversary:** *The Warden*, with an engine boss deck (DEC-567). It has no Portrait, and its lines are Scroll text.
- **Scenes:** the opening, each chapter's start, the win and the loss are each one Scroll line (DEC-626). There is no voice, and Paintings use the engine defaults (DEC-500).
- **Lengths:** Short and Standard have the same six quests. Standard gets bigger tombs and more encounters (DEC-669).

**Changed by DEC-669 (Bob, 2026-10-30):** length no longer changes foe strength. Standard gets bigger tombs and a higher event chance in the wild.
