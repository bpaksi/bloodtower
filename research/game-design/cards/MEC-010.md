Length sets the number of chapters, encounter frequency and dungeon size. The opening, the quests the win needs and the finale exist at every length. Play times are measured, not guessed.

Confirmed by Bob, 2026-10-27 (DEC-310).

**Refined by DEC-669 (Bob, 2026-10-30):** in 1.0, Standard gets bigger tombs and a higher event chance in the wild. Length never changes foe strength.
