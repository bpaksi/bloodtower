Bob chose "One interrupt diagram". Pause (SCR-017), a dropped iPad (DEC-154) and a lost tower (DEC-155) are not drawn in the loop diagrams. They share one diagram (MEC-071) that can fire between any two nodes of any loop and resumes at the node it stopped before. Rejected: marking in each loop where an interrupt may fire, and drawing a pause track at every prompt.
