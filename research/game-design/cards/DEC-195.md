Bob chose "Game, chapter, day, turn". Outermost first: the game loop (MEC-032) adds each hero's health and the purse, the chapter loop (MEC-033) its name, the day loop (MEC-034) "Day N", and the group turn (MEC-035) the acting group and its moves left. The session loop adds nothing. The encounter, quest and battle loops add nothing either, since they own the middle when they're innermost. Rejected: only day and turn, and a segment from every loop.
