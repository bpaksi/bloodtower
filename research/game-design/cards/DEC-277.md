Bob chose "Each node names one home card". Every node points at exactly one card, written as the first ID on its line:

- **board, prompt, show:** its SCR- or UI- card.
- **tower:** its CUE- card or tower output (SND-, LSQ-…).
- **check, roll, change:** the DEC- or MEC- card that holds the rule.
- **call:** the inner loop's MEC- card.

A node with no card yet gets a new one. Art, sounds and tower links go on the home card, and one card can be home to several nodes. Rejected: a new card type for every node, and home cards for player-facing nodes only.
