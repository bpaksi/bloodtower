"Right after calibrate, include both sync drums and orient to board."

Connect, calibrate, sync the drums from the visible glyphs, then orient the tower to the board: one block, each step started by a press, before Pick Story. So the tower and the board's kingdom edges have to be on the table by then, even though the rest of the board pieces go down later in setup. Seals and skulls stay in setup.
