- **Rooms:** each level has 3 rooms. The last is the stair room: a guard battle the group must win to go up.
- **Room kinds:** a guard (a battle, foe level +1 per Tower level), a trap (a flat loss, as the Tomb's, DEC-489) or a shrine (each hero heals 2, once).
- **Levels:** basement, main, tower (DEC-364). The group goes up only through a won stair room.
- **The adversary** always waits at the top, in the tower level's last room (DEC-356).
- **Hooks:** a story may put a hook on any room's start or end (DEC-540).

One picture: the level's room map. One meter: the group's hit points. The choices each room: open the next door, rest at a found shrine, or retreat (all memory kept, DEC-360).
