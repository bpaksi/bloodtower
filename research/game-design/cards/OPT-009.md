Updated 2026-10-27 (DEC-378): the Bard replaces the Scholar; the other five roles stand. DEC-380 sets the intrinsic virtues; Shield Wall, Mend, Slip Past and Pathfinder carry over, and Arcane Bolt and Lore are dropped.

A first pass, used as placeholders by the prototypes and Bloodtower until Bob rules after playing.

Updated 2026-10-27 (card audit, DEC-352): heroes have no attributes (DEC-125), so the Might, Stealth, Magic and Wits columns are gone. Spells and abilities are virtues (DEC-121), so each old innate ability is now a candidate for one of that class's two starting virtues (DEC-126, DEC-156). There is no mana (DEC-124). The full lists are Q-248.

| Role | Name | Candidate starting virtue |
|---|---|---|
| Fighter | Bram Ironhand | **Shield Wall:** once per fight, cancel one hit on a hero |
| Healer | Sister Ilsa | **Mend:** once a day, give 2 hit points back, or cure a sickness, for a hero on her space |
| Magic user | Orren the Grey | **Arcane Bolt:** add 2 to the party's strength in a battle (was: spend 1 mana) |
| Scout | Vex | **Slip Past:** once per quest, avoid one trap or one room's cost |
| Ranger | Kael Thornwood | **Pathfinder:** their group gets +1 move each turn it ends in the wild |
| Scholar | Maren Ashby | **Lore:** once per encounter, reveal one hidden outcome or read one sign for sure |

**For:** every role is distinct, so the picks at party join matter (DEC-100). **Against:** the virtues are a first guess, and hit points per hero are in OPT-010.
