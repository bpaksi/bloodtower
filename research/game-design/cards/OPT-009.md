A first pass, used as placeholders by the prototypes and Bloodtower until Bob rules after playing. Every hero has 8 attribute points, with a lead of 4 (DEC-101). The healer's strength is her ability.

| Role | Name | Might | Stealth | Magic | Wits | Innate ability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fighter | Bram Ironhand | 4 | 1 | 1 | 2 | **Shield Wall:** once per fight, cancel one hit on a hero |
| Healer | Sister Ilsa | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 | **Mend:** once a day, give 2 hit points back, or cure a sickness, for a hero on her space |
| Magic user | Orren the Grey | 1 | 1 | 4 | 2 | **Arcane Bolt:** spend 1 mana to add 2 to the party's strength in a fight or a Magic test |
| Scout | Vex | 2 | 4 | 1 | 1 | **Slip Past:** once per set piece, avoid one trap or one room's cost |
| Ranger | Kael Thornwood | 2 | 1 | 1 | 4 | **Pathfinder:** their group gets +1 move each turn it ends in the wild |
| Scholar | Maren Ashby | 1 | 1 | 2 | 4 | **Lore:** once per encounter, reveal one hidden outcome or read one sign for sure |

**For:** every role is distinct, and every hero is good at one thing and poor at another, so the picks at party join matter (DEC-100). **Against:** the numbers are a first guess. Hit points and mana per hero are still open.
