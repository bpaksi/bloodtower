Bob chose "Named beats, each with 2 slots". The beats:

1. Arrival (the horn)
2. Foe reveal (the painting and its traits)
3. Card reveal (the foe's round card, DEC-323)
4. Picking (heroes choose; a tension bed)
5. Spell (a cast, before the draw)
6. Clash (the draw resolving)
7. Round won
8. Hero hit
9. Hero falls
10. Run
11. Victory
12. Defeat (the group falls)
13. Reward

Each beat has a **board slot** (picture, motion, Mac sound) and a **tower slot** (sound, lights). The engine fills every slot with a default so any battle plays well with no story work; a story may override any slot (DEC-279). Nothing physical runs outside a tower slot. Rejected: only a few big moments, and stories presenting battles themselves.
