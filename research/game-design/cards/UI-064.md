Each caravan (N, W, E) has a token on the Story board's map, drawn on its kingdom's trade route. At the day's end, after the lieutenants, the board draws its step along the route (DEC-090).

A group that reaches a caravan's space gets the building menu (UI-043) with the caravan's picks: trade, rumours and hired help, at 1 move each. A group left on the space when the caravan moves on stays there.
