D6 brief (bloodtower #503). A Rest is one pick that spends a number of moves, +3 hit points each (MEC-075). DEC-913 makes Fortunate's second pick cost no move, and keeps Cleanse once per building per visit (DEC-572). Not said: what a free Rest buys, and what the iPad shows.

- **A free Rest waives one move.** Taken as the free pick, a Rest of N moves costs N − 1 moves and heals for N. With 0 moves left the group can rest for 1 (+3 hit points). The Rest step offers 1 to (moves left + 1).
- **The cost line** of every pick reads "0 moves" while the free pick is pending (the existing "N moves" words, N = 0); gold shows as now. No new copy and no new screen state.
- **A Cleanse already used this visit** is greyed with the existing fallback line "Cleanse is not possible now."; pressing it is refused with the same words and changes nothing. The use is kept per group and building for the turn: leaving and entering again in the same turn is the same visit for Cleanse. It is offered again on a later turn (DEC-572: "it may come back on a later turn").

Reason: the plain reading of "no extra move" on a pick that is priced in moves; the iPad shows the true cost with the words it already has.

Not picked: a Leave and re-Enter in one turn opens a new Cleanse (one visit would no longer remove one skull); a free Rest heals for all moves left at no cost (it would make Fortunate a full heal); hide Cleanse after one use (C3/13 shows closed picks greyed, not hidden).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
