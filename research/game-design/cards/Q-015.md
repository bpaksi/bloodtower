Base RtDT allows one Reinforce per turn at a building. If sub-locations and NPCs are free extras, every visit is a long menu and turns slow down. Options: one pick per visit (Reinforce or one sub-location or one NPC), the menu plus Reinforce, or a visit costs a clock tick.
