Bob chose "One generic job per story". The BT adapter reads the pack and writes the job. It grows talking-avatar's cast and script shape:

- **Cast:** each speaker's voice (provider, voice, style), a portrait set per mood (DEC-346) with 8 mouth frames and an anchor, and reference sheets for AI art (R-AST-ORG-5).
- **Lines:** id, speaker, text and mood. Only lines DEC-349 voices are in the job.
- **Pictures:** id, size per screen (`@dashboard`, `@ipad`), an art prompt or a source file, and for a Painting its camera move and glow mask (DEC-343).
- **Sounds:** music and sound effects, by id.

The pack (packs/<story>/cast.yaml, script.yaml) keeps BT's format, so the outside repo never owns it. Rejected: packs adopting asset-studio's format, and asset-studio parsing the script markdown.
