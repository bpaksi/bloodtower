**What:** The stills or clips behind narration on the Story board. A still with no camera track drifts by engine default.

**When:** At least one per set piece; ideally one per moment, so each beat has its own picture.

**Why:** The board is the table's window into the story; a new picture marks a new beat.

**Pack:** picture and clip IDs on moments (§4, §13), optional camera tracks.

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 1 per set piece | 1 per set piece | 1 per set piece |
| Target | 1 per moment | 1 per moment | 1 per moment |
