Probe P-003, the finale desk walk, at the boss battle (MEC-076 .15). DEC-567 gives the adversary its own deck and says "its ability grows" at each phase. DEC-326 says a story foe names its traits and one ability from the engine's list. The Ruined Keep names neither for The Warden (DEC-661, DEC-636). So the Fighter's Weapon Master (+1 Melee) can't be checked, and the phases have no ability to grow.
