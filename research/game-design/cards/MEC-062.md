**What:** Story items tagged to a named NPC. Holding one unlocks that NPC's matching hero action, such as 'Ask about the ring' or 'Accuse'.

**When:** When the story has a mystery or a traitor: who did it, who lies, where the true coffin is.

**Why:** Clues turn talking into deduction and give named NPCs a reason to be visited again.

**Pack:** clue items with their NPC tag (§7).
