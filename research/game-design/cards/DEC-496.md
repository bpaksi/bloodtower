The P-02 prototype (`apps/prototypes/src/web/app/p/tomb`) has three things the Tomb no longer uses, and they are cut:

- **Flat costs on every room:** rooms are now nothing, a battle or a trap (DEC-488).
- **A straight line of 5 rooms:** the map branches (DEC-490, DEC-491).
- **Gold the party cannot pay comes out of hit points:** replaced by RtDT's rule below.

**Shortfall, as in RtDT:** a trap loss the party cannot pay in full (a potion or item it doesn't hold, or too little gold) is paid as far as it can, and the party gains a corruption.
