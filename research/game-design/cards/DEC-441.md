Bob asked Claude to rule the last 13 treasures and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** +1 Humanoid; spend instead of dropping a skull: take another turn.
- **Bloodtower:** +1 Humanoid Advantage while held. Spend it at the end of the day, when the skull drop comes (MEC-034), and no skull drops that day. The RtDT "take another turn" part is dropped, because a Bloodtower day belongs to every group, not to one player.
