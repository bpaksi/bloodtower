Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-30 rulings review, as built in the POC.

**Changed by DEC-639 (Bob, 2026-10-30):** every story now shows the engine's Length (only the lengths the story supports), Difficulty and Party size, and then its own options. A story no longer declares those three.
