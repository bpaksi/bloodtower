- **Owner:** story.
- **Enter:** .1 to .37: new game, Play again (DEC-213) or Continue (DEC-283).
- **Start point:** .38 to .40, the opening (DEC-111).
- **Body:** .41 and .42, one chapter per pass (DEC-133).
- **Exit:** .42: the finale chapter hands up "won", or any chapter hands up "lost".
- **Close:** .43, the game's record (DEC-116, DEC-117).
- **Result up:** won or lost, to the session (MEC-039).
- **End point:** .44 to .49, the outcome and the stats.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). The story's opening, win and loss scenes (DEC-144) fill its story hooks (DEC-285).

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). Story nodes are optional engine hooks that hold only dialogs, panning stills and talking avatars, joined by optional transitions (DEC-285). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower are not drawn; they use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071). Without the tower, tower nodes wait on Q-176.
