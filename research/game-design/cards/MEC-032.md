- **Owner:** story.
- **Enter:** setup: story, length and difficulty, party join, pieces placed, Begin (SCR-001). All heroes start as one group (DEC-109).
- **Start point:** the opening narration (DEC-111).
- **Body:** one pass is one chapter (DEC-133).
- **Exit:** the finale chapter hands up "won", or any chapter hands up "lost".
- **Result up:** none; the game writes its record (DEC-116, DEC-117).
- **End point:** the outcome scene, won or lost with the reason (SCR-018).
- **Screen:** setup screens SCR-001 to SCR-012; End SCR-018, Game stats SCR-019, Goodbye SCR-020.
- **UI:** a text card or panning still for the narration (UI-006, UI-003).
- **Art:** the story's opening scene, its win scene and its loss scene(s).
- **Sound:** CUE-001 at the start, CUE-002 or CUE-003 at the end.
