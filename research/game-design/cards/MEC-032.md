- **Owner:** story.
- **Enter:** from the session (MEC-039): Pick game (SCR-007), length and difficulty (SCR-008), party join (SCR-010), the story's pieces placed (SCR-011), Begin (SCR-012). All heroes start as one group (DEC-109).
- **Start point:** the opening narration (DEC-111).
- **Body:** one pass is one chapter (DEC-133).
- **Exit:** the finale chapter hands up "won", or any chapter hands up "lost".
- **Close:** the game writes its record (DEC-116, DEC-117).
- **Result up:** won or lost, to the session (MEC-039).
- **End point:** the outcome scene, won or lost with the reason (SCR-018), then the stats (SCR-019). The session then loops back to Pick game.
- **Screen:** SCR-007 to SCR-012 at the start; End SCR-018 and Game stats SCR-019 at the end.
- **UI:** a text card or panning still for the narration (UI-006, UI-003).
- **Art:** the story's opening scene, one win scene, and one loss scene for each way it can be lost (DEC-144).
- **Sound:** CUE-001 at the start, CUE-002 or CUE-003 at the end.
