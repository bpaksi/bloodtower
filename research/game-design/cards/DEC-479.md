Bob: "When prompting to use/apply during start of turn or battle. The iPad should just display a button that expands into a dialog. We don't want to require the player to click through screen every time. The icon should give some indication when there is something applicable to use."

- **One button:** at the turn start and in battle, the acting group's iPad shows one button (UI-038). It does not put up a screen of offers.
- **Dialog:** pressing it opens a dialog listing the cards and virtues the group can use now (DEC-410, DEC-411). The player uses one or closes it.
- **No click-through:** play never waits for this button. A player who ignores it simply goes on. This replaces UI-037's "One Done on the iPad moves on".
- **Indicator:** the button's icon shows when something can be used now, and looks quiet when nothing does.

Still open: what happens to an automatic virtue that needs a choice, such as Infused's building, when the player never opens the dialog (Q-298).
