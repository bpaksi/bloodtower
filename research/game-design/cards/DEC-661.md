Bob picked "The Warden, a fallen knight".

- **Who:** the ruined keep's last lord. He swore to guard what lies sealed under the keep. Over the years it turned him, and now he serves it from the Tower.
- **Why this one:** it keeps DEC-636's name, and it ties the keep (chapter 2) to the Tower (chapter 3).
- **Art:** one Portrait, a ruined knight. It is the one adversary Portrait DEC-502 allows, so The Warden is no longer text only.
- **The hook:** raiders have closed the roads around the village. The village's herald begs the heroes for help. This is DEC-636's *The Herald* scene.

Not picked: a cult rebuilding its temple (the closest to Hommlet); an old horror waking in the Tower, with no mastermind until the end.
