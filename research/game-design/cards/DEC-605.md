Bob, from an RtDT cave screenshot: "Maybe a cave can have a max of 1 or 2 out corridors where a tomb can have 1, 2 or 3 outs." Asked where the number lives, he picked "Fixed in the engine".

- **Tomb:** each room has 1 to 3 ways out, so its map branches.
- **Cave (1.1):** 1 to 2, so it runs narrow with few forks.
- **Locked:** each dungeon type carries the limit as a closed attribute (DEC-499). It is not a slot, so no story patches it (DEC-536).

Not picked: a per-type setting a story can patch; a limit carried by the art style.
