Found by the first probe in *The Ambush*'s first round. With OPT-010's first-guess numbers, a level 1 foe facing two heroes has a count of 5, and the Healer's Lightning cuts 6 (DEC-123). The battle loop has no check after the spells (MEC-038 .8). It goes on to Strike and the roll, and a lost roll lets the revealed card land.
