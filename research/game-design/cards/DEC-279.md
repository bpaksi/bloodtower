Bob: "I made a mistake, I think they are the same. These nodes can be optional and story driven."

There is no new kind. The tower kind (DEC-268) is the node that marks where commands can be sent to the tower. A tower node may be optional, drawn on a bypass track (DEC-269), and story driven: a story can choose its commands or leave it empty. Its home card (DEC-277) holds the default commands. Nothing physical happens automatically: a tower node sends commands only where the diagram puts one.
