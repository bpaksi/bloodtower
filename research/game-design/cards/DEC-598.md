Bob chose "Yes, boost only there".

A revealed glyph boosts its action only in the kingdom it faces (MEC-017 knows which side faces which kingdom). When a drum turns (DEC-006), its glyphs face new kingdoms and the boon moves with them. MEC-016 comes back in this form, with no spirit cost (there is no spirit, DEC-308).

Rejected: the boost in every kingdom, and the boost only for the group whose home kingdom the glyph faces.
