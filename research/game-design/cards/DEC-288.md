Bob chose "Engine trigger nodes". The engine places named trigger nodes in its inner loops where things happen, for example "arrive at a building" or "meet an NPC". Each is an optional story hook. The active chapter may fill it with moments and effects (DEC-285, DEC-286), or leave it empty. The story still owns no inner loop and adds no flow; it only fills the hooks the engine offers. Rejected: beats only through quests, and queueing beats to the day's end.
