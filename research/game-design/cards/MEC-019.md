**Proposal:** the engine gives every building type the same frame and a story fills it. Arriving opens the menu on the iPad: the base action (Reinforce, Cleanse), the sub-locations (each a small encounter table or shop), the NPCs (each a dialog with a few offers), and, when the story marks the place, a story-point entry shown first. The engine owns the four building types and the features each can offer; a story switches features on or off (IDEA-008), renames them, adds sub-locations and NPCs, or locks some until a flag.
