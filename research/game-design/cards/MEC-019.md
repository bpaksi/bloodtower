**Proposal:** the engine gives every building type the same frame and a story fills it. Entering opens the menu (MEC-036 .1): each building type's own Reinforce actions (DEC-157), the sub-locations (each a small event table or shop), the NPCs (each a dialog with a few offers), and, when the story marks the place, a story-point entry shown first. The engine owns the four building types and the features each can offer; a story switches features on or off (IDEA-008), renames them, adds sub-locations and NPCs, or locks some until a flag.

Confirmed by Bob, 2026-10-27 (DEC-309).
