Found by the first probe in the Barrow's guardian battle. Each round the foe reveals one card from its 8-card deck (DEC-323, DEC-557): Claw ×2, Sweep, Howl, 2 trait cards, Rend and Falter. No card says whether a revealed card is gone until the deck runs out, or whether the same card can come up two rounds running.
