Each present hero picks one before the draw. Any action other than a normal attack replaces that hero's attack: they add no fight number that round (Bob, 2026-10-22).
- **Normal attack:** the hero adds to the round as usual. A hero who picks nothing makes a normal attack.
- **Cast a spell:** Lightning, Fireball or Magic Missile cuts the horde before the draw, win or lose (DEC-123).
- **Spend a conditional Advantage:** softens the foe's revealed card one step (DEC-324 replaced "boosts this round's odds", 2026-10-27; trimmed under DEC-352).
- **Drink or give a potion:** a present hero gets hit points back.
- **Use a guard virtue:** if the foe wins this round, no one loses hit points.

How much each one does is detail for Q-023.

Refined by DEC-319 (Bob, 2026-10-27): the guard takes the hit for the party at reduced damage, rather than no one losing hit points.
