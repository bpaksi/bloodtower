- **Picture:** the Tomb's two halves, in shrine art, with 3 altar marks on the map.
- **Meter:** none. Hit points are the pressure.
- **Choices:** the Tomb's. Finding an altar lights it, and lighting all 3 finishes the quest with no guardian battle.
- **Magic Advantage:** makes one listened hint certain, and says if an altar lies that way.

**Not picked:** the glyph puzzle with seals on (it can't be seen), and a light-sequence memory game (already not picked in MEC-031).
