Bob chose "Yes, a lost round". The last attack follows the lost-round rules. A guard virtue picked that round blocks it, a hero brought to 0 hit points takes a corruption and comes back at 1 (DEC-119), and a foe ability that changes lost rounds (DEC-311) applies too.

Rejected: a plain hit with no guard and no ability, and a last attack that cannot drop a hero below 1.
