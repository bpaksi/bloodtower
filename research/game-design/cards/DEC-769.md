**Recommendation, held by DEC-486.** Adopt IDEA-065. Revisit once the Tomb has been played, and build nothing before then.

- **Picture:** the Tomb's two halves, with cave art and RtDT's cave rooms.
- **Meter:** none, as the Tomb. Hit points are the pressure.
- **Choices:** listen, pick a passage bold or on guard, or leave. Listening already is reading signs, so the hunt needs no game of its own.
- **Twist:** a Beast Advantage makes one listened hint certain. A group that reaches the lair having taken no trap this visit strikes first.
- **Deal:** 30% nothing, 40% battle, 30% trap.
- **Cut:** the light meter. In P-03's simulation a party following the signs found the lair 93% of the time, so it rarely bit.

**Caveat:** Bob hasn't played the prototype; those are simulation numbers only.

Accepted by Bob, 2026-11-05 (DEC-786), to check in the probes.
