Bob: "No money is lost unless the entire group loses. Then each member of the group loses equally." A hero who falls takes only DEC-119's corruption and sits out. When every hero in the group falls, the group has lost and gold is lost. How "each member loses equally" works with one shared purse (DEC-007) is Q-083. Rejected: a share or a fixed sum per fallen hero, and no gold loss at all.
