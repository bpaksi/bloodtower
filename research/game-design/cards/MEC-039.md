- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140).
- **Enter:** splash (SCR-004), the landing painting (SCR-002) and the sound dialog (SCR-003); tower connect and calibrate, only on a press (SCR-005); the volume test (SCR-006); the iPads join (DEC-153); the base board set up (DEC-152).
- **Body:** one pass is one game (MEC-032), from Pick game.
- **Exit:** after each game, the session screen (SCR-021, DEC-214): "New game" loops back to Pick game, a past game can be played again (DEC-213), "Done for tonight" goes to Goodbye (SCR-020) and ends the session.
- **Close:** the teardown checklist, and the tower to idle (SCR-020).
- **Result up:** none (top level).
- **Screen:** SCR-002 to SCR-006, SCR-020, SCR-021.
- **UI:** the status strip (UI-008, debug only).
- **Art:** the landing painting (SCR-002), shared by every story.
- **Sound:** the volume test's clip (SCR-006); no cue of its own otherwise.
