Bob: "I'm thinking no voice and no motion is a good idea for base game." He chose Claude's follow-up, "Still, except battle feedback".

The base game's own pictures are plain stills: no voiced lines, no drift, no push-in, no glow. Motion and voice then mark the story speaking, and engine default art needs only 1080p, not the headroom a camera move needs (DEC-206). The one exception is the battle's hit shake and spell flash (DEC-329): feedback on a result, like a sound effect, taking no time.

What changes:
- **DEC-205:** the default drift runs only in a story's Painting. A base-game still does not move.
- **DEC-329:** the battle's slow push-in while heroes pick goes. The shake on a hit and the flash on a spell stay.
- **DEC-343:** the glow layer is for a story's Paintings only, not base-game stills.

Rejected: no voice with motion kept, fully still with battle included, and still with glow allowed.
