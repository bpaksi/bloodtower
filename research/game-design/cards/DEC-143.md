Bob: "I want where possible to keep the loops consistent. Additionally for each [loop] I want to identify UI, art work, sounds required."

Each loop card fills these rows in this order:
- **Owner:** story or engine.
- **Enter:** what sets it up.
- **Start point:** its story entry point (story loops only, DEC-140).
- **Body:** what one pass is; it repeats until the exit.
- **Exit:** its conditions, checked after a pass (DEC-137).
- **Close:** the engine's work once the exit is met, before the result goes up (DEC-147). Often empty.
- **Result up:** what the parent receives.
- **End point:** its story entry point, with the result (DEC-142).
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** what it needs at enter, each pass and exit. Missing ones become open UI-, CUE- or Q- cards.
