- **Best** is the shortest active length of a won game, keyed by story, length, difficulty and party size. The average uses the same key and counts won and lost games.
- **First win under the key** shows "Best: this game".
- **A loss shows the earlier best** when a win exists under its key. "No win yet to compare" shows only when no win exists under the key. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (QA.8); it keeps C3/17 EV-01.
