Bob took the 2026-10-23 draft as written:

- a **band** of Brigands
- a **horde** of Oreks
- a **pack** of Shadow Wolves
- a **brood** of Spine Fiends
- a **gang** of Frost Trolls
- a **swarm** of Lemures
- the **Clan** of Neuri
- a **nest** of Widowmade Spiders
- a **coven** of Mormos, and a **coven** of Striga
- a **Dragon** and a **Titan** always come alone, with wounds instead of a head count (DEC-533)

The group noun is on-screen text. A story's foe reskin may change it (DEC-326). Rejected: changing nouns now, and parking the list for the content pass.
