Beat 2 of the turn (DEC-077), optional, driven by story progress and location:
1. **The table:** one beat for everyone on the TV. Narration, and set piece tokens placed or moved ("strange noises from beneath the tavern").
2. **Each group:** a group standing on a story location gets its own beat before its loop starts.

The action itself happens later, when a group chooses the story point in an encounter (DEC-083).
