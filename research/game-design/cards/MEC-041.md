**What:** The plot's acts. Each holds a few set pieces in any order; finishing them opens the next; the last is the finale.

**When:** Always. Use more chapters for a longer game, so each act stays short and the table feels the plot move.

**Why:** Chapters pace the story and gate what is on the board; a chapter change is the clearest 'the story moved on' moment.

**Pack:** `nights` / chapters (§3.1), `by_length.<len>` (§3.2).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Target | 3 | 4 | 5 |
