CU/07's Q7.1 (Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted) builds the turn-start choice list generic, tests it with Infused, and flagged the 1.0 list for Gamewright.

- **The 1.0 deck is the 8 pool tiles** (DEC-581 takes Crafty, Inventive, Callous and Lucky out for 1.1): Inspiring (DEC-389), Relentless (DEC-391), Fortunate (DEC-399), Unseen (DEC-402), Resourceful (DEC-404), Blessed (DEC-405), Infused (DEC-406), Anointed (DEC-407). The 4 kingdom tiles are out (DEC-381).
- **Choice-asking virtues at a turn start: Infused only.** It asks for a building. No other tile or intrinsic virtue asks a choice at a turn start. The list stays generic (Q7.1), and the fixture virtue of CU/07 EV-03 still proves it.
- **The other tiles that ask a choice ask it elsewhere,** and CU/07 does not build those: Fortunate's second pick at the building menu (DEC-399, DEC-913); Unseen's trade when a quest is finished (DEC-402); Blessed in the battle round (DEC-405, C4 10).
- **Lines the panel can show** (UI-037, DEC-408): Infused (when its kingdom has a skull to remove), Relentless (after a doubles roll), Fortunate (a second pick today), and the intrinsic Mend (a heal today, DEC-545). Inspiring, Resourceful and Anointed fire on other moments, not the turn start, so they show no line.
- **Infused.** The iPad lists the standing buildings of the group's kingdom with their skull counts (DEC-482). A building with 0 skulls is shown but cannot be pressed. If no building has a skull, no Infused line shows. The pick never blocks the move map (DEC-479), and an unused Infused is gone when the turn ends (DEC-482).
- **Mend** shows as a line only. Using it is not part of CU/07.
- **The citadel draw** (C3-06) draws from this deck. Code today holds six placeholder names (`VIRTUE_TILES` in `encounter/picks.ts`: courage, mercy, wisdom, honor, fortitude, grace); the build step that replaces them is Wright's to queue.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-15, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
