Bob chose "Reconnect, Go on without, or Quit". When the tower drops mid-game, the pause offers three buttons. Go on without the tower asks to confirm, then switches the rest of the game to the no-tower stand-ins (Q-176). Reconnect from the Menu stays possible later (DEC-229); the tower check (MEC-018) then runs before play goes on. Reconnect still starts only on a press. Rejected: keeping only Reconnect or Quit, and switching to the stand-ins on its own with no pause.
