Bob chose "Seeded, no repeats, kept". When a stranger met in the wild has no face from the story or the default cast, node .4 of the talk pick (MEC-073) draws one from the shared pool (DEC-334) with the game seed. No face is drawn twice in a chapter. The stranger keeps that face for as long as they stay in the game, for example a companion who joins. Rejected: a fresh draw at every meeting, and faces tied to offer kinds, which could give a trick away.
