| Virtue | Amount | Resets |
|---|---|---|
| Shield Wall | the guarded hit of DEC-556 | every round it is used |
| Mend | 3 hit points to each hero in the group, never above full | once a day |
| Magic Missile | cuts the foe 2 (OPT-010) | unlimited |
| Lightning | cuts the foe 6 (OPT-010) | once a battle (a dungeon counts as one, DEC-122) |
| Fireball | halves the foe, rounded down (OPT-010) | once a battle |
| Silver Tongue | a quarter off every village and bazaar price, rounded down, at least 1 off | always |
| Slip Past | skips one trap or one room's cost | once per quest |
| Weapon Master, Shadow, Beast Hunter, Rally | +1 strength per match (DEC-324) | always |
| Pathfinder | +1 to the move roll in the wild | always |

Mend resets per day, not per chapter, so the Healer matters on every day.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
