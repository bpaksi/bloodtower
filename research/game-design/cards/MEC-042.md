**What:** The story's real scenes, each at a quest marker on the board, each a small game of its engine type.

**When:** For every scene the plot needs the heroes to play through, not just hear. The win must depend on them (IDEA-013).

**Why:** Encounters are random and belong to the engine; quests are the only place the plot is played.

**Pack:** `set_pieces` (§3.3), kept per length in `by_length.<len>.set_pieces` (§3.2).

|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Target | 8 | 12 | 14 |
