D-02 §2. Dracula's values: `parishes`, `count-goal`, `blade-site`, `lantern-site`, `tome-site`, `lair` and `traitor`. All of them are drawn at Begin so that a restore replays the same game. Each one shows nowhere until its reveal (the reading, the Tome or the lair). At the reveal the board names the site and the players place the physical marker, with a Done prompt. Nothing is drawn as a map.
