Bob chose "Building pick heals group".

- **RtDT:** after you Reinforce, gain 6 warriors.
- **Bloodtower:** RtDT's Reinforce becomes a building pick (DEC-157). After the group takes any pick at a building, Cleanse included (DEC-377), every hero in the group heals a little (DEC-039). The holder's whole group benefits, as a Warlord rallying the troops.

How much it heals, and whether it fires on every pick or once per visit, is left to the numbers pass (Q-289). Rejected: healing only the holder, and healing after a won battle instead.
