**What:** Short pieces of word the NPCs hand out at random, each once: rumours, lore and fortunes. Blanks are filled from the live game, and the NPC's trust sets how often one is true. Not all need to be true or about the plot.

**When:** Every story. Write more for longer games, so no talk repeats and rumours stay worth chasing.

**Why:** Nuggets are the world talking back between quests; they feed leads, clues and traitors' lies.

**Pack:** the nugget list with blanks (§4). The count is nuggets.

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Target | 150 | 250 | 400 |
