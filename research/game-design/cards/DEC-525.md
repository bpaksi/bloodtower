Bob chose "Lead = the tip, it opens a side quest". A lead is the NPC's tip, a concept neither original has. What it reveals is a side quest, shown by its own quest marker and open for a few turns (DEC-180). Main quests never expire (DEC-088). Rejected: "lead" for both the tip and the quest, and folding leads into companion quests.
