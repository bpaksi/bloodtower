Bob approved the talk pick as drawn (MEC-073). One talk allows one hero action (ask, accuse, or the lord's quests), and the 1 move the talk costs covers it; a second action means talking again. A right accusation may start a battle (DEC-184), so a talk can call the battle loop. The fortune teller is paid in gold (DEC-166), so her reading is a buy pick (MEC-074), not a talk. Rejected: accusing for a second move, and several actions in one talk.
