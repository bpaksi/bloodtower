After the chapter card (UI-020), the board shows "The tower shifts" and which drum will turn. Any iPad gets Turn and Skip (DEC-762).

- **Turn:** the tower turns that drum at a quiet beat (DEC-006). Then the board shows any glyph that moved (UI-056) with CUE-015.
- **Skip:** the game goes on and nothing turns.
- **No tower:** the step is not shown (DEC-306).

**Tower safety:** nothing turns without the press.
