Bob asked Claude to draft all 10 in one pass, for his review.

- **RtDT:** Once per turn, spend 1 treasure for +3 Wild. No other ability.
- **Bloodtower:** Before any battle round, once a battle, the holder may spend 1 treasure for +3 Wild Advantage for the rest of that battle. Spending a held card costs the fight number that round (DEC-484). The treasure goes back (DEC-370).
- **Why:** Keeps the printed trade, using the battle moments (DEC-481, DEC-483).
