Playtest PT-24 (paper playtest, 2026-11-12): C2 feature 05 had each iPad tap "Enable sound" to unlock its own browser audio and then run a volume test that played sound on the iPad, while DEC-201 puts all sound on the Story board.

- **Ruling.** No iPad ever plays sound, tests or story. All sound plays on the Story board (DEC-201). The per-iPad unlock step and its locked copy (Bob, 2026-10-31 10:33 AM ET) are removed.
- **The board's audio** is unlocked by the first click or key on the landing page's Sound control (SCR-003, C2 feature 02), which carries to the Story board. Nothing else on an iPad is needed.
- **Tower volume** (SCR-006) stays as it was: Pick story opens with Volume 0, 1 and 2, Keep and Try another on the iPad, each press plays Dungeon Complete on the tower, and Keep is disabled until one has played. It runs only on the connect track.
- **Play without Tower:** no volume step and no volume line. The board's line "How loud should the tower be? Press one to hear it." is not shown. Pick story opens straight on the story list.
- **Why.** A tap per iPad bought nothing once no iPad plays sound, and the no-tower volume step tested an iPad level that no longer exists.

Replaces the 2026-10-31 locked copy and the 6:15 PM board-line rule for the no-tower track. The removed strings are listed in the catch-up rulings note.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
