Bob picked "Listening costs Bold".

- **Free and unlimited:** the acting group may listen down any open corridor, as often as it likes, before it picks.
- **The cost:** a corridor the group listened down can only be entered on guard (DEC-492). The party lingered there, so it lost surprise.
- **Corridors not listened to** can still be entered bold or on guard.
- **The trade:** listen and take a trap's cut but give the foe its first strike, or go in blind and keep the first strike. A wrong hint (DEC-586) can still put the party on guard for nothing.

Not picked: one free listen per room; every corridor free with no cost; a chance of drawing a wandering foe with each listen.
