Bob chose Claude's recommendation, "but damage is a variable but largely reduced from normal hit".

When the foe wins a round in which a hero used a guard virtue, the hit goes to that guard instead of a random present hero (DEC-015). The guard loses hit points, but much less than a normal hit, and the amount varies. This makes the guard a protector role: it shields a weak hero, but still bleeds. It also covers the last attack on a run (DEC-318). Rejected: no one loses hit points (DEC-129's first wording), and a guard that protects only itself.
