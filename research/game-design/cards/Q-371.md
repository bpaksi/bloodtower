DEC-636 gives six quests by engine path: *The Herald* and *The Ambush* (chapter 1), *The Barrow* Tomb and *The Shrine* (chapter 2), *The Lock* and *The Last Stand* (chapter 3). Each needs a place in the plot, so that the chapters read as one story from the village to The Warden.
