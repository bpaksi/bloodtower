Bob chose "Replace or silence". A story that writes a hook replaces the base's moments and effects whole. An explicit empty marker silences an optional hook the base fills, so leaving a hook out still means "keep the base". A required hook cannot be silenced; the lint fails it. A story cannot play its moments before or after the base's. Rejected: also appending before or after, and replace only.
