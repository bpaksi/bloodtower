Bob, on how matching Advantages work in a dungeon: "In RTDT and in the screen shot, This dungeon is Humanoid and 1 Advantage was applied. The when a battle occurs and the results are presented, advantages can be spent to reduce the effect. In BT we are doing battle a bit different." Then: "I think we need to fold BT battle behavior into how traps work. Maybe the part can be on guard walking into a room. On guard loses initiative in a battle, but for a trap lessons the effects?"

Bloodtower's battle has no initiative step today (MEC-038), so "initiative" needs a meaning before this can be ruled.
