Probe P-003, day 22. Both heroes fell at *The Last Stand* on day 21, so each carried one corruption. The group rolls once (DEC-108), and DEC-102 takes "1 away for each corruption". P-001 and P-002 never had two corrupted heroes in one group. Summed, a pair with one corruption each loses 2 moves a day, and a party of four could lose 4. Taking the most corrupted hero's count, the pair loses 1.
