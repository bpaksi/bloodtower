Bob picked "One hero, picked".

- **Taking it:** when a quest, scene or battle gives a story item, the group picks which present hero takes it, as with quest items and virtue tiles (DEC-370). The quest's rewards (MEC-037 .13 to .15) and a scene's items (MEC-077 .8 to .10) ask.
- **Carrying it:** the item stays with that hero, so it moves with the hero's group.
- **A check** for an item, like *The Lock* needing the Barrow Key, looks only at the heroes present.

Not picked: a party pool that any group can use anywhere; a per-item hero or party flag in the pack.
