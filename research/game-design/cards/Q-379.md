Probe P-002 found it on day 1. The night's event was foes: Brigands at level 2. After round 1 the lone Ranger was at 2 hit points, but MEC-038 .3 offered no Retreat, because MEC-035 .9 had already dropped the turn's last move. The Ranger fell in round 4. DEC-028 says the heroes can still retreat after any round, and DEC-313 says only with a move left.
