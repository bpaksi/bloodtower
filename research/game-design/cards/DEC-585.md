Bob picked "Panels as one pan" (OPT-027).

- **Sections:** left, ahead and right (DEC-584). Each is painted as a corridor and as a wall, with edges that blend into the next section.
- **Joined:** the three sections make one wide room, so there are no seams.
- **Pan:** the view drifts slowly left to right and back, as if the party is looking around.
- **Art count:** 6 paintings per tomb style. Any room is a mix of them, and one direction can change without the others.

Not picked: three framed panels (OPT-025); one finished painting per corridor permutation, 8 per style (OPT-026).
