Bob picked "Opening is the story's hook" in the 2026-10-30 rulings review.

- **Begin:** any joined iPad presses Begin once every setup step is Done (SCR-011), and then the story's opening hook plays on the Story board (DEC-351).
- **The tools are the story's:** the hook names what it uses, a Portrait, a Painting or a Scroll (DEC-335). SCR-012 no longer fixes the talking avatar and panning stills.
- **No hook:** the game goes straight to the first move. The engine has no opening of its own (DEC-340, DEC-347).
- **The grey-stair stills** from the Oct 17 playthrough become Proving Grounds' own opening, not an engine default.

Not picked: an engine title still when there is no hook; keeping SCR-012 as built.
