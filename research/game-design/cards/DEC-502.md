Bob accepted Claude's first cut as written (asked for in IDEA-068). Rule: keep every loop a game needs to be fun (move, events, battle, skulls, corruption, the Tower finale), cut variety inside them.

**Keep in 1.0:** movement and the group turn; base wild events (engine names only); battle against the base foe set with levels and difficulty; skulls and the loss conditions (DEC-043); corruption (RtDT's 12, engine names); the 6 pool heroes with their class virtues and intrinsic spells; money for rest, cures and virtues; tavern (rest, rumours) and sanctuary (cures); story items and quest rewards; 3 chapters; quests as plain scenes (narration, a choice, maybe a fight); opening, win and loss scenes; nuggets; the villain as the one story Portrait, a narrator voice; Paintings (panning) for scenes; default tower cues (DEC-055).

**Wait for 1.1:** the six dungeon quest types and dungeon tokens (DEC-063, DEC-089); gear, treasure and potion decks and booty; magic and cursed items, the fence; merchant, bazaar and the haggle die; building NPCs, named NPCs, companions, lieutenants, traitor tricks, clues, leads and villain quests; foe reskins, corruption and event renames; place paintings (engine defaults only), music; Long game length (Short and Standard ship).

**Art left for 1.0:** 6 hero Portraits, the villain Portrait, a narrator, one image per base foe type (state variants optional, DEC-500), default place paintings per terrain and building type, and the story's scene Paintings.
