Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found it on day 25, when the drop emptied the supply.
- **Keep the rule:** an empty supply loses the game. It is checked at MEC-033 .11, after the day's drop. It is RtDT's pressure, and it ends a stalled game.
- **Say what it is:** it is a clock. The board shows the skulls left as days left, at the party's drop rate (DEC-564). The day's effects panel shows a Cleanse as "+1 day".
- **DEC-043** would be trimmed of its line "No round limit… not a clock" once this is accepted. Fallen buildings stay a separate loss.
- **For the numbers pass:** the story's lengths must fit the clock. In this walk, Standard with two heroes needed 25 days for six quests, and the Tower still waited. The numbers pass should set the supply or the drop rate per length, so a party playing well reaches the finale with days to spare.

Not picked: lost only when a drop is due and the supply is empty (one more day, with the board showing 0); count the skulls held in the tower as still in the supply (no physical skull is left to drop).

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
