Bob chose "Yes, keep ART". Art gets built too, so an `ART-` card is a deliverable card: a decided ruling it reaches by `needs` or `uses` is not a no-deliverable gap, and `pnpm graph drift` stops its walk at an `ART-` card a step did not build. The gaps and drift tooling already counted ART (IDEA-062, #318); this records it. Rejected: only SCR, UI, CUE and MEC, as DEC-355 first listed them.
