Bob picked "At day start, before the roll". The first probe found the gap at day 1's group turn (MEC-035).

- **When:** a Regroup step opens each day (MEC-034 .38 to .42), before the moves are rolled. It is skipped when every hero stands alone on their space.
- **What:** heroes on one space may split off into a new group, or join a group on their space.
- **Order:** a split-off group is the newest and plays last. A merged group keeps the older place (DEC-148).
- **Moves:** every group then rolls its own moves (.2). Nothing changes mid-day, so each hero moves once a day and no moves are divided.

Not picked: splitting at a group's turn start, with both parts keeping the full roll; splitting at any time, with the moves left carried over.
