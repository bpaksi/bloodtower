When his move reaches a group, or a group enters his space, once a day (DEC-775):

1. **Story board:** the adversary's portrait on a Reveal with "The Warden strikes" and the hit-point loss for this chapter (3 + the chapter number).
2. **iPad:** each hero there gets two buttons, "Take a corruption" or "Lose N hit points". A hero who already holds 2 corruptions sees only the second (MEC-013). The loss stops at 1.
3. **Board:** one line per hero with what they took. The tower plays CUE-014.
