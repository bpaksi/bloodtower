Probe P-003, the finale desk walk, after the boss battle (MEC-076 .18). DEC-664 plays The Ruined Keep's last choice in the Tower loop's end hook. But MEC-076 draws no end hook: its close is only .19. DEC-656 says a won Tower "ends the game at once", which could skip the close and any hook. And an end hook fires on every exit (DEC-540), so as written the choice would also play when a group leaves the Tower or falls in it.
