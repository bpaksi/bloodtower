Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 11, at *The Last Stand*.
- **No token:** like a tomb room's foe (DEC-651), a standing foe from a quest's battle gets no foe token (MEC-038 .35 skips it). The quest marker shows it instead, with its count.
- **Progress:** the scene's progress is the option the group chose and its foe. That foe keeps its cut count and status, and may step up (DEC-314).
- **The next pick:** skips the choice (MEC-077 .1, .2) and resumes at the battle (.4). The foe panel shows the kept count. In the walk the Neuri went 13, 9, 7, then 1, and fell on the fifth visit.
- **Events and wild battles** are unchanged: their standing foe still gets a token (DEC-321).

Not picked: a fresh choice and a full-count foe on every visit (a retreat would undo the progress); a token on the space as well as the quest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
