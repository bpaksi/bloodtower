The Tower stands in the board's centre and has no space of its own in RtDT. For the Tower encounter (DEC-358) a group needs a way in: from any space touching the centre, from a citadel, from the last chapter's kingdom, or something the story sets. In 1981 a hero needed all three keys to enter.
