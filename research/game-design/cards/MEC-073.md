Every building has NPCs (DEC-162): the lord's audience (DEC-158), sanctuary lore (DEC-171), street rumours that may open a lead (DEC-173), the fortune teller (DEC-165) and named NPCs, who stay on the menu once met (DEC-181). The NPC's lines play on the board as talking avatars; the group may then take a hero action the party's clues unlock, such as Accuse (DEC-182, DEC-184).
