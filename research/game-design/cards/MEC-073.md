- **Owner:** engine; a story writes its NPCs, lines and clues. No entry points (DEC-140); the pick's trigger hook sits in the encounter (MEC-036 .9).
- **Enter:** .1 and .2: called for 1 move from the encounter (MEC-036 .12, DEC-078), or for a person met in the wild (MEC-072 .21). A named NPC met for the first time joins the place's menu for the chapter (DEC-181).
- **Body:** .3 to .20, played once: the nugget is filled true or false by trust (DEC-025, DEC-026), the speaker's face is picked (DEC-336), the NPC speaks, what was learned is noted, then one open hero action if any (DEC-182, DEC-299).
- **Exit:** the action's track ends, or Leave.
- **Close:** none.
- **Result up:** what was learned, and any battle's result.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: the NPC's talking avatar, from the story or the shared pool (DEC-334).

Every building has NPCs (DEC-162): the lord's audience gives, takes back and pays quests (DEC-158), sanctuary lore is one true piece per visit (DEC-166, DEC-171), the street NPC gives rumours (DEC-173), and named NPCs stay on the menu once met. Offers from people met in the wild are resolved in the event (DEC-298); the fortune teller's paid reading is a buy pick (DEC-299). The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277).
