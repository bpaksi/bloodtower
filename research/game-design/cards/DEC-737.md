Bob picked "Story picks guard or hunter" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session.

- **Guard:** stays on its space until beaten, so a story can use it to hold a road or a place.
- **Hunter:** moves 1 space a day toward the nearest group, and meeting it starts a battle.

Not picked: always a guard, always a hunter, and moving like the adversary (it would speed up the skull clock and repeat the adversary's job).
