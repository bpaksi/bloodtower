Bob picked "Boost solo in both". A solo hero was the weakest cell by far, and neither strength nor the clock alone lifted it to DEC-816's 80%.

| Solo | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Fight number | +1 | +2 |
| Full hit points | +2 | +4 |
| Skull drops | 1 a night | 1 every other night |

Script, solo at normal (P-006, DEC-821 in play):

| Solo | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 30% | 21% | 2% |
| +2 fight, +4 hit points | 63% | 53% | 20% |
| Both, with drops every other night | 81% | 80% | 43% |

Long's clock is a separate question for the pass. **No `release` field:** this applies to 1.0, so `packs/base/tuning.yaml` (`heroes.solo`, `skulls.drops_a_night`) and its tests change in their own code session, as DEC-801's did. Its night drop is skipped on even nights, counted from the first.

Not picked: the same boost for 1.1 only; solo as a hard mode outside the target; and no solo games.
