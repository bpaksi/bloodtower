Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** You cannot double your move.
- **Bloodtower:** The holder's virtues and gear (Swift, Trusted Maps) add nothing to the group's move roll (DEC-102). Item-fit.md proposed "cannot pay for extra moves". Bloodtower has no paid moves, and a second move loss on top of DEC-365's -1 would be too harsh, so Feeble takes away the bonus instead. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).
