Bob picked "Replaces the view".

- **Trap:** the trap's painting and its loss (DEC-489) take over the view half while the group softens or takes it (MEC-026 .13, .14).
- **Battle:** the battle screen (MEC-038) takes over the view half, the guardian's battle too (DEC-590).
- **Map half:** stays as it is, with the current room glowing.
- **After:** the room's panning view comes back with its corridors (DEC-585), ready for the next pick.

Not picked: the trap or foe laid over the room as an overlay; a pop-up over both halves, as in RtDT.
