**What:** Optional side quests an NPC reveals: a quest marker on the board, open for a few turns, with a reward. Never needed to win.

**When:** When the story wants the world to feel bigger than its plot, and to reward talking to people.

**Why:** Leads give rumours a payoff and the party a choice between the plot and a detour.

**Pack:** lead entries with marker, window and reward (§9).
