Playtest PT-17 (2026-11-12): a third corruption ends the game at once (DEC-676), and nothing showed which hero was one fall away.

- **Mark.** A hero at the corruption limit minus one gets a mark on the edge band (UI-028), inside the lead's span, as an icon with an aria-label. The limit is one exported engine constant, 3, which C3-08 reuses; the mark threshold is that constant minus one. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (Q10.1, Q10.2, Q10.4).
- **Warning.** The Fight/Retreat board line (C4-09) gains one line naming every marked hero. A sat-out hero at 0 hit points is not named (QA.11; Gamewright to confirm). The words are Loremaster's (LQ-2, default A).
- **Display only.** DEC-676 is untouched. A narrow test-only seed for corruption counts is added in this step (Q10.3).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
