Bob: "a story board UI that has 2 halves. 1st half is very similar to RTDT (top down view of progress). The 2nd half I'm thinking might be a 1st person view to create a more immersive feel." He picked "Side by side, always".

- **Map half:** RtDT's top-down floor plan as DEC-487 set it: found rooms, the current one glowing, open doorways, Exit and the Undead Advantage count.
- **View half:** what the party sees standing in the current room, looking at its doorways.
- Both stay on screen the whole time. The map says where the party is; the view says what it feels like.

DEC-487 turned down a full-screen room painting; this keeps the map as the picture and adds the view beside it. Not picked: the view big with the map as a corner inset; the map big with the view as a small window; swapping which half is big by moment.
