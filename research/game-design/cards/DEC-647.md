Bob: "Every round of a battle is against the same foe. On start of a battle, when story hasn't specified, a random Foe from the deck matching the level is chosen."

- **The story names it:** a quest, a Tomb (DEC-608) or a story beat may name its foe.
- **Otherwise:** when the battle starts, the engine draws a seeded random foe of the battle's level from the foe set (DEC-325, AST-B23).
- **One foe** for the whole battle: every round is against it.
