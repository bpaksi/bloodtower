**Recommendation.** Q-012: both. The potion, gear and treasure decks stay in play beside story items, and a story may rename or remove any entry (DEC-498). Q-034: DEC-370 already keeps the physical card. Only the app's wording counts, so printed text never applies.

- The Story board and iPad show the card's printed name beside any story name, so a player can find it in the deck.
- A story item with no card of its own is app-only, as a replaced companion is (DEC-723).

**Why:** this matches DEC-370, DEC-752 and item-fit.md. Q-034's old "design as if digital" note predates DEC-370.

**Not picked:** story items that replace the decks, and printing our own cards.
