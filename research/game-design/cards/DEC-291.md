Bob chose "Once by default, story may say every time". A filled trigger hook plays the first time it matches in a game, then stays quiet. The story may mark a hook "every time", for example a shopkeeper's greeting. Which hooks have played is saved with the game (DEC-208), so Continue keeps it. Rejected: every time with flags to stop it, and once with no exceptions.
