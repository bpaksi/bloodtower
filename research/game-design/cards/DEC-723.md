Bob picked "RtDT's 10, story may swap" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session, settling DEC-072 against DEC-370.

- **Defaults:** the engine ships the 10 RtDT companions. Each keeps its physical card, which the hero holds (DEC-370), and has one effect (DEC-070). Their lines are silent captions.
- **A story** may keep, rename or replace any of them. A replacement lives only in the app, with no card on the table. Only a story's own companion can have voiced lines (DEC-349).

Not picked: the cards as props for story characters (the picture may not match), story-only companions with the cards left in the box, and only RtDT's 10 with no story versions.
