Claude's proposal, to close Q-374 for Bob's review.
- **What it is:** a story item (DEC-054), not a quest item, so it has no engine effect.
- **Its one use:** *The Lock*'s *Turn the Signet* option needs it (DEC-654). That option is disabled while no present hero holds it (MEC-077 .1). It is kept after use.
- **Who holds it:** the hero the group picks when the Barrow's guardian falls (DEC-653).
- *Force it*, the level-up battle, stays the way through for a party that lost it or split from its holder.

Not picked: a Signet that also buys something, such as a free rally at the keep's citadel. It would be a second effect to tune for one story.
