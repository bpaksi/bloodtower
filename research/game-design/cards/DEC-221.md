The RtDT board is one fixed graph in the base content (`packs-v2/base`), shared by every story: each space with its kingdom, terrain and building, the spaces it touches, the rivers between kingdoms, and the spaces next to the tower. Bob supplies a top-down photo of the board; Claude drafts the graph from it, and Bob checks it on the workbench before the slice uses it.
