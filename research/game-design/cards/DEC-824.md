Bob picked "3 nights in 4". A pair was below DEC-823's floor at Standard. It now drops 1 skull on three nights out of four and none on every fourth night, counted from the first.

| Pair, normal | Short | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Every night (before) | 81% | 64% |
| 3 nights in 4 | 88% | 81% |

A night with a won quest still has no drop (DEC-699) and doesn't move the count. **No `release` field:** this applies to 1.0, so `packs/base/tuning.yaml` (`skulls.drops_a_night`) and its tests change in their own code session, together with DEC-822.

Not picked: 2 nights in 3 (Standard 85%), and the skip at Standard only.
