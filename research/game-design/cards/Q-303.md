RtDT's app shows upcoming foe events only behind an eye icon, and the review found players decided badly without knowing it was there. The horde panel shows a foe before a fight (DEC-311), but nothing says where players see which places are about to fall, what the villain will do, or which hordes are growing, before they spend their moves.
