- **Owner:** engine; a story renames hazards and adds flavour (DEC-022). No entry points (DEC-140), and no trigger hooks: events fire no story beats (DEC-289).
- **Enter:** .1 and .2: called from the day's close (MEC-034 .9) for one group that ended in the wild (DEC-017, DEC-080) or at a skulled building (DEC-093). The engine draws the event, seeded, and the board shows it.
- **Body:** .3 to .30, played once, with one track per type (DEC-022). The present heroes choose together (DEC-019). Foes and disasters may call a battle (DEC-028, DEC-136); meeting people calls the talk pick for the lines, then resolves the drawn offer in the event (DEC-024, DEC-298).
- **Exit:** the type's track ends.
- **Close:** none.
- **Result up:** what changed, for the day's effects panel (UI-022, DEC-115).
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: one painting per event card; a story may swap them.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). A quiet event's mood line is the .2 board. A rumour is the talk's nugget, so its track is empty; a trick is a hazard with no counter. DEC-004's hunter token is not drawn. Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
