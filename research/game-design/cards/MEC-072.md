Not drawn yet (DEC-294). Called from the day's close (MEC-034) once for each group that draws an event: one that ended in the wild (DEC-017, DEC-080) or at a skulled building (DEC-093). The engine draws the event, seeded, from its types (DEC-022), and the board shows it. The present heroes choose together (DEC-019); every event can be gone around at a cost (DEC-018). It resolves, and may call a battle (DEC-136). It hands up what changed for the day's effects panel (UI-022).

- **Owner:** engine; a story renames hazards and adds flavour (DEC-022). No entry points (DEC-140).
