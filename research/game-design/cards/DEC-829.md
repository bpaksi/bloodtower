| Hard, % won | 1 hero | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short | 53 | 64 | 66 | 82 |
| Standard | 51 | 46 | 47 | 71 |
| Long | 47 | 50 | 82 | 86 |

Under the band: Standard with 2 or 3 heroes, and solo Long. **Claude's reading, for Bob to check:** easy keeps DEC-819's 90% as a floor, as DEC-823 reads normal's 80%. Easy wins 99 to 100% in every cell.

Not picked: hard offsets by party size so every cell lands near 65%; and accepting whatever hard gives.
