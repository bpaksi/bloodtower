Bob chose "Up to 4; 5th: pick one to return", as RtDT has it.

- **Limit:** each hero holds at most 4 treasures.
- **A fifth:** when the engine names a fifth treasure for a hero, the hero's iPad asks which of the five to give back, the new one included. The board names the card to return (DEC-370).

Rejected: no limit, and refusing the fifth treasure.
