**Recommendation.** Each applies only while the glyph shows and only in the kingdom it faces (DEC-598 to DEC-600).

| Glyph | Boost in that kingdom |
|---|---|
| Cleanse (GLY-001) | The Cleanse pick removes 2 skulls, not 1 |
| Quest (GLY-002) | A won quest pays double gold, 10 to each hero |
| Battle (GLY-003) | Each hero present has +1 strength in the battle, beyond the cap |
| Banner (GLY-004) | A group starting its turn there adds 1 to its move roll |
| Reinforce (GLY-005) | Any paid building pick costs 2 gold less, at least 1 |

**Banner:** there is no Banner action (DEC-532), so it boosts the one thing every group does: marching.

**Why:** all five are one number, so they're easy to show and need no button (DEC-600). **Placeholder** until the simulator run.
