Bob's example: two heroes hold 25 and 75 gold. They lose a battle and both end at 1 hit point with a corruption (DEC-119). If the app sets the loss at 50, each owes 25, so they end with 0 and 50. If it sets 100, each owes 50: hero 1 has only 25 and drops to 0, hero 2 drops to 25. A hero's shortfall is not passed to the others. Heroes outside the fallen group lose nothing.
