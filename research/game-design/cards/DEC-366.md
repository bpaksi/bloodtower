Bob asked whether the player should draw and tell the app, or the app tell the player, seeing pros and cons in both, then chose "App names it".

- The app picks the card from a seeded draw (R-ENG-8), as it deals every card (DEC-067). The Story board shows its art, name and drawback, and the player takes that physical card for the hero board.
- No input step and no wrong taps; the game always knows each hero's cards.
- When a corruption is removed (DEC-170), the app names which card goes back.

Rejected: the player draws face down and taps which card it was, and the physical card as an optional reminder.
