Bob chose "Finer split". Every node in a loop diagram is one operation of one kind:

- **Quest marker:** an entry point the story may fill or leave empty (DEC-138, DEC-140).
- **Roll:** the engine draws a chance result.
- **Check:** a rule decides which branch to take.
- **Change:** the engine writes game state.
- **Board paint:** what the Story board shows (DEC-193).
- **iPad prompt:** asks the players something and waits for the answer.
- **iPad show:** shows the players something without waiting.
- **Tower output:** one sound, light, drum or seal.
- **Call:** runs an inner loop's diagram (DEC-267).

Rejected: Bob's four kinds alone (tower cues and calls as notes on other nodes), and six kinds with one Engine step and one iPad kind.
