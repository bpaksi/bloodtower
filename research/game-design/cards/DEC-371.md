Bob: "Yes, please move forward with 'game uses the physical cards, skulls, companions and the trait effects'". Until now Bloodtower had no way to take a skull off a building. Skulls only piled up until the 4th destroyed it (DEC-093).

- **Cards remove skulls:** Purifying Breath, Acorns of the White Oak, Hallowed Reliquary, Blessed Sceptres and Infused work as their cards say, once the wording is settled (Q-269).
- **The menu recovers:** a building that loses skulls gets back what those skulls had taken from its menu (DEC-093).
- **A destroyed building stays destroyed.** Removal only helps a building that is still standing.

Whether a group also has its own way to clear a building, like RtDT's Cleanse, is Q-270. Generous, Lamp of Hope, Relic of Light and the Feverish corruption depend on that answer. Rejected: no skull removal, with those nine cards dropped or reworded.
