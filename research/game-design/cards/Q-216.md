Narrowed by DEC-369 (Bob, 2026-10-27): Brigands and the Dragon are RtDT foe types with their own cards and tokens, so only Plague is left. The original question:

Brigands and the Dragon each need a level range, RtDT traits (DEC-068), one ability (DEC-311), a card deck (DEC-323) and an RtDT token to stand in on the board (DEC-321). Is the Dragon a foe you fight, or a strike that comes to you, with the Dragonsword as its Advantage? Plague probably belongs with the events.

**Answered** by DEC-022 (Plague is a hazard, not its own type) and DEC-027 (a hazard is cancelled by its counter, the healer; without one the heroes pick between two costs). The cost sizes go in the numbers pass. Found in the 2026-10-29 ideas triage (DEC-612).
