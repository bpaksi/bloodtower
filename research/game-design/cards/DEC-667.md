Bob picked "A shard of the Tower".

- **The shard:** long ago a shard broke off the Tower. The keep was built to hold it, and its lords swore to guard it.
- **The fall:** guarding it, The Warden heard the Tower call. He left the keep to serve the Tower, and his raiders now work for it.
- **Why it holds together:** it explains why the hunt ends in the Tower. It also sharpens DEC-663's last choice: shatter the shard for good, or guard it as he once swore to.

Not picked: a buried king whose whispers turned him; a demon bound under the keep.
