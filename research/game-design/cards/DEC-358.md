Bob: "Since the Tower is so special to the game I want a custom Encounter game loop for it. Providing additional hooks for the story."

- The Tower encounter is its own loop card (MEC-076), not the place menu every building uses (MEC-036).
- It calls the boss battle (DEC-356) and gives the story more hooks than an ordinary encounter's five triggers (DEC-290).
- What it holds, its hooks and who takes part are Q-262; how a group reaches the Tower on the board is Q-263.
