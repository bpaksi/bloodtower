- **Owner:** engine; a story fills the menu (MEC-019). No entry points (DEC-140); its enter and pick trigger hooks (.4, .9) are ones the active chapter may fill (DEC-288).
- **Enter:** .1 to .4: the group enters a building, a caravan or a story token from its turn (MEC-035). The menu is built, shrunk by its skulls (DEC-093), with the quest marker first (DEC-083) and met NPCs added (DEC-181).
- **Body:** .5 to .15, one pick per pass for 1 move (DEC-078). The pick calls the diagram for its kind (DEC-296): the quest (MEC-037), talk (MEC-073), buy (MEC-074) or rest (MEC-075).
- **Exit:** .15, the group picks Leave or has no moves left.
- **Close:** none.
- **Result up:** the moves spent; the group is still at the place.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: one painting per kind of place and per sub-location; a story may swap or add (DEC-150).

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). The pick hook plays before the pick resolves, so a story can set the scene. Rest counts the move from .8 as its first resting move. A talk can start a battle when a right accusation unmasks a traitor who fights (MEC-073 .16, DEC-184); other battles come through quest steps and events (DEC-136). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
