- **Owner:** engine; a story fills the menu (MEC-019). No entry points (DEC-140).
- **Enter:** the group reaches a building, a caravan or a story token. The place's menu opens, shrunk by its skulls (DEC-093).
- **Body:** one pass is one pick, 1 move each (DEC-078). A pick resolves on the spot (MEC-006), or starts a battle or a set piece (DEC-136).
- **Exit:** the group picks Leave, or has no moves left.
- **Close:** none.
- **Result up:** the moves spent; the group is still at the place.
- **Screen:** the Story board (SCR-014) and the group's iPads (SCR-016).
- **UI:** the place menu (UI-024), pick cards (UI-007).
- **Art:** one painting per kind of place and per sub-location; a story may swap or add (DEC-150).
- **Sound:** CUE-007 on arrival.
