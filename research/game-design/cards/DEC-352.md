Bob chose "Park it; trim partial".

- **Fully replaced:** the old card's status becomes `parked`. Its first body line reads "Replaced by DEC-x (Bob, date)." The rest of the body stays as the record of what was ruled.
- **Partly replaced:** the old card stays `decided`. Its title is cut to the part that still holds, and its body says what DEC-x replaced.
- **The newer card** links `refines` to the old one, with a note saying what it replaces.

This uses the fields the cards already have. A dedicated `replaces` verb can come later as its own chore. Rejected: adding that verb and status now, and leaving replaced cards `decided` with only a "Refined by" note.
