Bob chose "Shared pool, story filter". The engine ships one pool of neutral talking avatars in `shared/cast/`: the default cast for each role (DEC-333) plus extra faces for crowds and strangers met in the wild. Each story's filter preset (palette, grain, duotone) is always laid over them, so they match the story's look. A story can replace any face with its own. This is the one exception to R-AST-ORG-6. Rejected: pools per art style, no shared art, and the physical RtDT card art.
