Answers Q-452 and Q-453.

- **Q-452:** `feel.lengthMs` stays optional. In 1.0 a cue that must wait for a sound to finish uses only a timed sound (SND-001 to 010, 087 to 089, 093 to 113). A sound with no length is timed on the tower the first time a cue needs it, and the length goes in its card then.
- **Q-453:** the workbench `/sounds` page stays Bob's review view, filtered by his tone. No `aiTone` field and no code change; the AI tone is read from the card by cue picks only.

Neither touches the game, so neither blocks a milestone.
