Bob chose "One shared shape". Every tool takes the same inputs:

- **Art:** one asset by ID (DEC-202). A Portrait's art is its portrait art, which carries the 8 mouth frames, the anchor and the character's voice (DEC-203). A Painting's art is a picture.
- **Lines:** each a speaker and its text. The voice audio is made from the text with the speaker's voice. A Painting's speaker is the narrator (DEC-056).
- **Sound:** optional music and sound effects.
- **How it ends:** after the lines, or after a set number of seconds.
- **Transition in:** how the board changes to this tool from the one before (Q-185, DEC-204).

A Painting adds one optional input, the camera move. Without it the engine drift runs (DEC-205). Rejected: a separate input list per tool, and art and lines only with the engine choosing the rest.
