Bob: "for v1 the adversary is at the tower. The tower is locked until the story unlocks it. This is usually based on a chapter, i.e. chapter 5 = The Tower. Until unlocked the group is unable to move to the tower."

- **Where:** in 1.0 the adversary is in the Tower from the start. It has no token on the board and is never placed at setup.
- **No roaming in 1.0:** it doesn't hunt buildings or add skulls (DEC-052), and it doesn't strike heroes before the finale (DEC-053). Those wait for 1.1. The day's adversary move (MEC-034 .23) is skipped.
- **Locked:** the Tower is shut until the story unlocks it, usually by chapter. Until then no group can move into it from a Tower-adjacent space (DEC-363, MEC-076 .1).
- **Still open:** how the story's unlock fits with DEC-356, where the finale chapter's quests unlock the adversary (Q-355).
