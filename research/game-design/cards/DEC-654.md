Bob picked "Needs, shown disabled, kept". The first probe found the gap at *The Lock* (DEC-636), which needs the Barrow Key.

- **The need:** an option may need a story or quest item held by a hero present (DEC-653).
- **Without it:** the option shows, disabled, like a building pick the group can't afford (UI-043).
- **The item:** using the option keeps it, unless the option's effects say to spend it.
- **Lint:** every scene keeps at least one option with no need, so a group can never be locked in.

Not picked: the item always spent; hiding an option the group can't take.
