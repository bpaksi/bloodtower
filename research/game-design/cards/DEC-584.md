Bob: "A room can have up to 3 outgoing corridors. I was thinking a view in 3 parts each with a panel for the corridor. if there is no corridor in the direction, a solid wall is shown."

The map layout (DEC-491) gives each room up to three ways on, read from the way the party came in: left, ahead and right. The first-person view (DEC-583) looks at those three directions. An open corridor shows as a way on; a direction with no corridor shows a solid wall. A room with no way on is a dead end. How the three directions are painted is Q-333.
