Bob: "I think this is story driven, so any group can. The story may bring all groups together first."

- **Engine default:** any group that reaches the Tower may enter it.
- **Story option:** a story may require every living hero to gather at the Tower before it opens, so the whole party storms it as one group.

Q-262 still holds the encounter's stages and hooks. Rejected: always gathering first, and one group inside with the others helping through hooks as the engine's rule.
