Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 6, going back to the Barrow's guardian.
- **A new visit:** walking into the room on a later visit is an entry. The group picks bold or on guard, and the free cut or the last attack applies before round 1 (MEC-038 .37 to .39).
- **Not a new visit:** Continue from a save (DEC-675). The battle resumes at its node, and nothing fires twice.
- **Why it isn't an exploit:** a bold return takes 1 off, but leaving again costs a last attack (DEC-650) and a day or two to heal. In the walk the guardian took three visits; the free cuts took 3 of its 13.
- It matches the Tower, where each visit brings the spells back (DEC-657).

Not picked: only the first entry picks a stance; a return is always on guard.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
