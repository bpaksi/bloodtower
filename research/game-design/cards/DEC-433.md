Bob asked Claude to use good judgement on the three Scrolls and to revise them in playtest. Claude chose the offensive burst, which keeps RtDT's "+4".

- **RtDT:** spend: +4 Melee.
- **Bloodtower:** offered in a battle round after the foe's card is revealed (DEC-323). Spending it adds 4 Melee Advantage to the holder's strength for that round, and only against a foe with the Melee trait, like any Advantage (DEC-324). The holder still fights that round, so the Scroll is a free burst, unlike a potion (DEC-411).
- **Why:** it keeps RtDT's number and feel. Softening the card instead (item-fit.md's proposal) would make the Scrolls a second kind of conditional Advantage. Lasting the whole battle would be too strong.
- **Playtest:** revisit if a Scroll wins battles on its own.

Not chosen: softening the foe's card up to 4 steps, and +4 for the whole battle.
