1. Events for each group that ended in the wild (DEC-080).
2. Skull drop: the app prompts it, players record where it landed and on which building.
3. Skulls go on buildings (Q-040).
4. Places close or fall.
5. The adversary strikes and moves.
6. Lieutenants move.
   6b. Caravans move along their trade routes (DEC-090).
7. Countdowns: the adversary's quest deadlines, and lead windows; an expired lead's marker comes off (DEC-087).
8. Check for a loss.
9. Check for a chapter end.

Quest markers do not move as a step of their own: leads expire in step 7, and the story beat places or removes story tokens at the start of the turn (DEC-082). A loss on the same day as a chapter end is a loss.
