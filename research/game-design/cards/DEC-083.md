A story point is never a place of its own. It is one more pick in an encounter's menu, shown first and highlighted. Bob's example: in the East Kingdom village, the party enters the tavern, and the top option is "head to the basement", which starts a dungeon. An RtDT token on the board shows which building has a story point. Choosing it costs 1 move, like any pick (DEC-078).
