Bob: "I like the option of both your recommendation and AI end to end."

- **Needs list:** the BT adapter reads a pack and lists every asset ID it needs, each missing, draft or final (R-AST-ORG-7, R-AST-ORG-8). Both modes start from it.
- **Studio mode:** Bob brings art (portrait, 8 mouth frames, paintings) into the studio, which checks sizes; he sets the anchor, camera move and glow mask. Claude runs `render` through a skill (voice, lip-sync, screen sizes) to make drafts. Bob reviews each one as it plays on the board, then approves it to final or rejects it with a note. BT `pack` lints and copies the finals in.
- **AI end-to-end mode:** Claude makes every missing asset as a draft, art included, renders and packs it, with no approval screen. Bob judges in the game itself and sends notes back.

Proposed by Claude, for Bob to confirm: a release still needs finals (R-AST-ORG-7), so only Bob moves an asset to final, in either mode.

Rejected: the studio as the only hub, where Bob presses every button.
