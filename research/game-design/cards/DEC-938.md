D1 brief (bloodtower #499), open question OQ-D1-2. Under DEC-929 the next chapter's card and opening play before the freed-skull picks, so a fatal freed skull lands after the table has seen the next chapter named.

- **Ruling:** the record names the chapter that was closing (the one whose seal broke and freed the skull). The game is lost in that chapter.
- **Reason:** DEC-933 says the next chapter's quest tokens and first day never start, and MEC-033's enter (the challenge step, the quest tokens) is where a chapter begins. The card and opening are only a preview. Naming the closing chapter needs no new rule and matches what the chapter loop already hands up.
- **Without the tower** there are no freed skulls (DEC-306); nothing here runs.

Not picked: the next chapter (it never entered); a separate "between chapters" value (a new record field for one rare case).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
