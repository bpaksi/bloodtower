Bob chose "Money per corruption". Each corruption is removed for its own price, and the price rises with the number of corruptions the hero carries, so a badly corrupted hero pays more. A curse from a fence item (DEC-169) is lifted the same way. The prices are numbers for later. Rejected: one price for all corruptions, a penance task, and a choice of gold or penance.
