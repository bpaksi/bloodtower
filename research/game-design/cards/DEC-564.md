Not a setup choice: the engine reads the hero count.

| Heroes | Foe count | Hero | Skull drops a day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | base | +2 fight number, +4 full hit points (DEC-822) | 1 every other night (DEC-822) |
| 2 | +1 | — | 1 on three nights in four (DEC-824) |
| 3 | +2 | — | 1, and 2 every other day |
| 4 | +3 | — | 1, and 2 every other day |

The foe column is OPT-010's +1 per hero after the first. The skull column answers DEC-033's tilt: more groups act before the tower does. The combat simulator (DEC-131) tunes the rows.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.

**Counted nights (DEC-912, Gamewright, 2026-11-13):** the nights this counts are the nights with no won quest; a won-quest night does not move the count.
