Not a setup choice: the engine reads the hero count.

| Heroes | Foe count | Hero | Skull drops a day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | base | +1 fight number, +2 full hit points | 1 |
| 2 | +1 | — | 1 |
| 3 | +2 | — | 1, and 2 every other day |
| 4 | +3 | — | 1, and 2 every other day |

The foe column is OPT-010's +1 per hero after the first. The skull column answers DEC-033's tilt: more groups act before the tower does. The combat simulator (DEC-131) tunes the rows.
