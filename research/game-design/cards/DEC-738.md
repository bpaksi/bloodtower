Bob picked "Roams, then back to Tower" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session. DEC-052's last-chapter hunt across the board could not fit a finale won only in the Tower (DEC-656).

- **Earlier chapters:** it walks toward the most-skulled building, adds skulls and strikes heroes it reaches (DEC-052, DEC-053).
- **Last chapter:** when the chapter starts it returns to the Tower and waits, so the day's adversary move is skipped.

Not picked: hunting until a group enters the Tower (the piece jumps, and it can wear the party down before the climb), and never leaving the Tower (gives up the adversary moving).
