- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140).
- **Enter:** .1 to .4: the Regroup step (.38 to .42, DEC-640), then the app rolls each group's moves with no press (DEC-108). Continue skips it (DEC-283).
- **Body:** .5 and .6, one group's turn per pass, oldest group first (DEC-148).
- **Exit:** .6, every group has played.
- **Close:** .7 to .34, DEC-086 steps 1 to 7: an event loop for each group that draws one (DEC-294), the skull drop and skulls on buildings (step 4 is .19), the world's moves on one board (DEC-295), countdowns, then the day's effects panel (DEC-115).
- **Result up:** the day is done; the chapter then runs its exit check (MEC-033 .11, steps 8 and 9).
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). No art of its own; events bring theirs.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower are not drawn; they use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071). In 1.0 the adversary waits in the Tower, so .23 is skipped (DEC-644). Without the tower the drop is simulated (.36, .37, DEC-305): no skull is held back (DEC-306), and tower nodes are skipped.
