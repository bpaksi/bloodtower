- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140).
- **Enter:** the app rolls each group's moves (DEC-108), then plays any of the chapter's timed beats that are due (DEC-141).
- **Body:** one pass is one group's turn, in order.
- **Exit:** every group has played.
- **Close:** the end of the day, DEC-086 steps 1 to 7: events, the skull drop, skulls on buildings, places close or fall, the villain, lieutenants and caravans move, countdowns.
- **Result up:** the day is done; the chapter then runs its exit check (steps 8 and 9).
- **Screen:** the Story board (SCR-014).
- **UI:** the day roll panel (UI-021), the whose-turn banner (UI-019), the day's effects panel (UI-022).
- **Art:** none of its own; events bring theirs.
- **Sound:** CUE-006 at the roll; the skull drop (SND-113) in the Close.
