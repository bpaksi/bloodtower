Bob picked "Yes, once per visit".

- The Tower counts as a dungeon, as the Tomb does (DEC-652).
- Each hero casts Lightning or Fireball at most once per visit, across guard, stair and adversary battles. The party chooses whether to spend them on the way up or save them for the adversary.
- They come back when the group leaves and enters again.

Not picked: once per battle in the Tower; a fresh set for the adversary's battle only.
