D-02 §4. She joins at the Burgomaster's House and the group picks her hero. A battle, walking out, turning or the wavering strike sets `lenka-taken` and sends her to the castle. She never becomes a foe (OPEN RULING 9). If her hero falls, she passes to another present hero, or she is taken.

She is veiled until `n4-finding-the-tome` (Bob's Oct 16 lock; a change is pending with Bob). `lenka.saved_by` is `party` or `lantern` (G-5).
