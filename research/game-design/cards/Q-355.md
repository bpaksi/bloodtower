Bob (DEC-644): the story unlocks the Tower, "usually based on a chapter, i.e. chapter 5 = The Tower". DEC-356 and MEC-076 .1 open it once the finale chapter's quests are done. Proving Grounds v1.0's chapter 3 has two quests, *The Lock* and *The Last Stand*, before the Tower (DEC-636).
