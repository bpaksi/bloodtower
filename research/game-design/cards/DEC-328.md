Bob chose "Layered track that follows the fight".

- **Arrival:** the chapter music (MEC-070) fades out under the horn, and the battle track starts.
- **Layers:** layers come in and drop out with the odds read (DEC-312) and as the foe shrinks: Grim adds drums and low strings, Good adds a lift.
- **Clash:** the music cuts to silence while the draw resolves, then a hit lands on the result (beat 6, DEC-327).
- **End:** a victory or defeat ending, then the chapter music fades back in after the reward.

The music never plays over a tower sound it would clash with (MEC-070's rule). Rejected: one looping track with stings, and no music.
