Walkthrough scene 4: mid-game, an iPad picks Quit story and confirms (DEC-230), and the table lands on the session screen (DEC-218). Someone says "let's keep going".

- **Continue on the hub:** while the autosave exists, the session screen shows Continue first, above New game and the list of tonight's games. It enters the game loop on DEC-283's Continue track. Pick story keeps its Continue too.
- **Replacing the save:** New game and Play again both start a new game, which replaces the autosave (DEC-208). While one exists, the press asks "This replaces the saved game. Are you sure?" first.
- **The quit game in the list:** it shows as quit (DEC-218), with its stats so far. Its Play again starts a new game, as for any other.

Rejected: Continue only on Pick story (one more step back into a game just quit); a quit that drops the autosave (would change DEC-208).
