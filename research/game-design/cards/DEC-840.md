Bob chose "Work folder, packed back".

- The job lists `moments[]`: the moment ID, its picture ID and its camera (a named move, focus box and speed, or a drawn track) as the pack has it. A move belongs to the moment, not the picture (DEC-401), so one painting in two moments can move two ways.
- The studio saves a changed move in the work folder (`camera.json`, keyed by moment ID), never in the job.
- Export carries those moves. Bloodtower's `assets:pack` writes each one into its moment in the pack's YAML. From then on the pack holds it.
- If the pack's move changed after the job was written (the job's `source` hash no longer matches), the pack wins: `assets:pack` keeps it, names the moment in a warning, and leaves the studio's move in the work folder.

Rejected: the studio only previewing while moves are copied into the pack by hand, and the studio editing the pack directly (against DEC-385).
