**What:** Where the heroes may go in each chapter. Bounds open forward and never close behind; 'anywhere' is a valid answer.

**When:** Every chapter. Narrow bounds walk the party through the world in order; open bounds let them roam.

**Why:** Bounds turn the board into the story's map: each chapter a new kingdom, harder than the last.

**Pack:** each chapter's bounds (§3.1).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 1 per chapter | 1 per chapter | 1 per chapter |
| Target | 1 per chapter | 1 per chapter | 1 per chapter |
