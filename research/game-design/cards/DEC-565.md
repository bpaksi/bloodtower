- **One a day:** after the first corruption in a day's end (DEC-086), any more for that hero go to the fallback (MEC-013): the story's, or by default 2 hit points, or 2 gold when the hero has 1 hit point.
- **Never the third:** a day's end cannot give a hero their third corruption. It goes to the same fallback. A hero's third corruption, the loss (DEC-043), comes only in the players' own turn, when they could have chosen otherwise.

This extends MEC-013's guard to the whole day's end. Skulls and fallen buildings can still lose the game at the day's end.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.
