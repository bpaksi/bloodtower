Bob chose "Not in the schema". A slot is either required or optional. Anything a story must never change (the hero pool, core rules, the effect kinds) is engine code or engine-only data, not a slot. The story lint rejects a story that writes a key the schema does not declare, which also catches typos. Rejected: a third "locked" slot kind, and marking whole manifest kinds base-only.
