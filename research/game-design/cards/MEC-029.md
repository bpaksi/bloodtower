**Proposal:** a multi-visit assault. The fortress has walls, a gate and a keep; each visit the heroes fight one layer, and broken layers stay broken. Left alone, the defenders rebuild one layer each round. The keep's fight ends it. A Melee Advantage breaks a layer outright. From RtDT's adversary battle (retreat allowed, progress kept). **Alternatives:** a duel ladder (beat champions in order); a rescue (reach a prisoner before the alarm).
