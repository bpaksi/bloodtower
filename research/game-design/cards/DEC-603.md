Bob: "I believe the grid is or should be 4x4. So that is 16 possible spaces." Asked how many cells hold rooms: "I think length of game will help determine. … 4-5, 6-7 & 8-10 for long games. The goal should be a minimum number of rooms deep. Say no less than 2, no more than 5."

- **Grid:** 4×4, 16 cells. The entrance is on an edge cell. Cells with no room are solid rock.
- **Rooms, guardian's included:** short 4–5, standard 6–7, long 8–10, seeded within the range. Long ships with 1.1 (DEC-502).
- **Depth:** the guardian room is 2 to 5 doors from the entrance, on the shortest path.

Claude pushed back on filling all 16 cells: a battle takes several rounds, and a full grid at 30% battles is about five battles plus the guardian in one tomb. Not picked: all 16 cells as a maze; a fixed 6–7 rooms.
