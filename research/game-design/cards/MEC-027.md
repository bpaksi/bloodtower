Track a beast through branching tunnels. Each round pick a passage from the signs (tracks, bones, a sound from the tower); a wrong turn costs light, and when the light runs out the beast ambushes (a hard fight). Finding the lair first gives a surprise round. A Beast Advantage reads one sign for sure. Not picked: a dark-navigation crawl (a light meter instead of rooms); a lair siege (smoke it out over several visits).
