"I believe that every story will start with a Narration to set the scene, then it will start Turn #1 in 1 to many locations."

After Begin, the story's opening narration plays on the Story board. Then turn 1 starts with the roll (DEC-108), with the heroes wherever the story's start option put them (DEC-112). Turn 1's story beat (DEC-077, beat 2) is then a normal beat, not the opening. Every story must supply an opening narration; a story without one fails the pack lint.
