Bob chose "Yes, same loop owns both". The innermost loop paints the TV's middle and gives the acting group's iPads their controls together. For example, the battle loop gives the horde panel (UI-026) and the round action picker (UI-027). Outer loops add nothing to an iPad beyond the idle card (UI-029) and the menu (UI-018). Rejected: a generic iPad choice list, and a mini band on each iPad.
