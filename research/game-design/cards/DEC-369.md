Bob: "DEC-325: the engine has RtDT's 12 foe types. Brigands and the Dragon are already among them; the 1981 part is their names and sounds."

RtDT's foe cards already include Brigands (level 2, Stealth Humanoid) and the Dragon (level 4, Melee Beast) ([foes-adversaries-companions.md](../../return-to-dark-tower/foes-adversaries-companions.md)). So the engine has 12 foe types, all with RtDT cards and tokens, and no stand-in tokens are needed. The 1981 game gives Brigands and the Dragon their names and their sounds.
