**Recommendation. Placeholders until the simulator run.**

- **Start:** steady.
- **Devoted:** +1 on the effect's number. Grigor gets 4 uses. **Two exceptions:** Tomas keeps +1 and also sets the roll's floor to 3, since +2 on a 2d3 is too much; Vasa's cures cost a quarter.
- **Hire:** 20 gold, one seeded offer per tavern per chapter. A hero holds one (DEC-072).
- **Turned foe:** level = the chapter's foe level +1, counted like any foe, with the trait of its Advantage (Wild becomes Melee). Beating it pays as a battle and its card goes back.
- **Return:** a hunter (DEC-737) from a Tower-adjacent space at the next chapter's start, or the next day's start in the last chapter.
- **Art:** a story need not supply any. By default the companion's card picture plays as the foe (DEC-500).
