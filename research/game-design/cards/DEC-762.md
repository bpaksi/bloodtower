**Recommendation.** At each chapter's start the board says the tower is shifting and asks any iPad to press Turn. The seed picks the drum and the direction. It avoids a turn that would hide the only showing glyph, if another turn is possible. The game can also go on with Skip, and without the tower the step drops out (DEC-306).

- **Other turns:** a story effect, a card, or the Ruins quest (DEC-772).
- **Not at day's end:** a nightly turn would move the boons too often to plan around, and adds a wait and a check on Continue.

**Tower safety:** nothing turns without the press, and a move runs at a quiet beat (DEC-006).

**Not picked:** never turning in the base game (DEC-598's moving boon would never happen), and a turn every day.
