Bob picked "Size and encounters". Probe P-002 found the gap at MEC-032 .14.
- **Tombs:** Standard tombs have 6 or 7 rooms, Short ones 4 or 5 (DEC-603).
- **Encounters:** the event chance in the wild is higher at Standard (DEC-017). The two numbers are for the numbers pass.
- **Foes:** length does not change foe strength. Foe levels come only from the chapter step and the difficulty offset (DEC-185), so difficulty stays one knob.
- **Quests:** the story still says which quests each length keeps (DEC-050).
- DEC-636 and DEC-508 are trimmed: their "stronger foes" at Standard is gone.

Not picked: Standard also adds +1 foe level; each story sets its encounter rate and foe levels per length; tombs only.
