Every roll, move, choice, fight, purchase, hero claim and tower cue is one row, never changed or deleted, carrying the game's seed and pack version. The stats screens compute their numbers from the log, so a stat thought of later can still be counted for past games. No summary tables for now; one can be added later if a screen is slow.

Bloodtower's existing timing marks and control presses (spec D6) already work this way and become kinds of event.
