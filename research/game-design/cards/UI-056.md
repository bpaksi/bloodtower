This is DEC-764 drawn out.

- **Story board:** each glyph that shows through an open window (DEC-599) puts its icon on the kingdom it faces, at the kingdom's edge on the map. It stays as long as the glyph shows.
- **iPad:** the boosted action shows its new value with the glyph icon beside it: "Cleanse: 2 skulls", "Quest pays 10 each", "+1 strength", "Reinforce: 2 gold less", or "+1 Banner" on the move roll (DEC-761). There is no button to press; the boost is passive (DEC-600).
- **A story reskin** (DEC-597) changes the icon's name and line, not where they show.
