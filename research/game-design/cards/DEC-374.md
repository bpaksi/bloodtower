Bob chose "Only during play".

- **Start:** a hero has only their class's 2 intrinsic virtues (DEC-373). No tile is dealt at setup.
- **Citadel:** buying a virtue draws one at random from the tiles still in the pool (DEC-127, DEC-067). The app names it, and the player takes the tile (DEC-370).
- **Rewards:** a story or set piece can grant a tile, drawn the same way unless the story names one. A kingdom tile is never drawn; only a story can grant one (DEC-381).
- **Pool:** a tile held by one hero is out of the pool until it is lost.

Rejected: 1 dealt per hero at setup with the rest in play, all dealt at setup, and a random subset picked per game.
