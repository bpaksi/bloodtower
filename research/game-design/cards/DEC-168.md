Bob chose "Hidden, but easy to shed". The app reveals a curse only when the hero first uses the item. The party can then sell it back to the fence at a loss, or have the sanctuary lift the curse and keep the item. A curse is a surprise that never traps the party. Rejected: telling the curse up front, a fortune-teller warning, and a curse that sticks until the sanctuary lifts it.
