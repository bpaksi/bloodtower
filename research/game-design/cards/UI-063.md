In the talk pick (MEC-073) the Story board shows the NPC's face as a mood still (DEC-719) under the story's filter (DEC-334), with their line as a silent caption under it (DEC-338). The engine fills the line's blanks with live names.

- **A role's NPC:** the story's cast, or the engine's default for that role (DEC-333).
- **A stranger:** a face drawn from the shared pool with the seed, never repeated in a chapter, and kept while they stay in the game (DEC-339). A pool that runs out repeats a face and writes a warning to the log, not a toast (DEC-341).
- **The story lint** renders every shared face under the story's filter and reports any that fail (DEC-348). That is a check in the pack tooling, not a screen.
