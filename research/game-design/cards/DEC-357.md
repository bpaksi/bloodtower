Bob: "No default, story is required to identify. But I would think it will always be Tower." Before that: "Doesn't DT81 always end at Tower? I think we need a special tower encounter. The story should determine where the final battle occurs."

- A story pack names its finale place; the pack lint fails a story that does not.
- Bob expects every story to pick the Tower encounter (DEC-358), as the 1981 game always ended at the Dark Tower.

Rejected: the Tower as the engine's default, a fixed lair or a hunting villain chosen by the engine, and the Tower with no other choice.
