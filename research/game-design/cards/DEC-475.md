Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** You cannot Cleanse a building with 3 skulls.
- **Bloodtower:** The holder's group gets no Cleanse pick (DEC-377) at a building with 3 skulls. Other skull removal, from cards or virtues, still works. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 open-questions session: "Accept all ten". Revise in playtest.
