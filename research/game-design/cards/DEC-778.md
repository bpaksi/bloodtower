**Clash found in the review.** DEC-053 says the adversary is only fought in the finale. DEC-743 and IDEA-126 have the Count fight a short battle and withdraw if beaten. A story can't change a rule (DEC-498), so one has to move.

**Recommendation.** Add an engine setting, a slot on the adversary: `strike` or `skirmish`. The default stays DEC-053's strike.

- **Skirmish:** up to 3 rounds of the normal battle. Win a round and he leaves the board for the day; his wounds don't carry to the finale.
- **If no round is won:** the strike lands (DEC-775).

**Guard:** a landed strike follows DEC-775, so it can never give a third corruption on its own. That trims IDEA-122, whose strike adds a bite, under MEC-013.

**Why:** Bob ruled DEC-743 for Dracula, and this keeps both rulings. No finale strength changes (DEC-361).
