CU/16 brief (bloodtower). DEC-154 says another iPad may claim a dropped hero, but the pause shows no Pick buttons today: the iPad offers Pick only on the Pick screen and on Begin. After DEC-952 the same pause follows Continue, so it needs a way to pick.

- **Ruling:** while the game is paused because a seated hero has no iPad (DEC-154, after a drop or after Continue), every joined iPad shows the Pick screen's hero rows for the seated party only: the same "Pick Hero A" buttons, the same "this iPad", "another iPad" and "unclaimed" words, and the counter "N of N picked". N counts the seated heroes, not the whole roster. The board shows the same counter.
- **A pick** takes the hero as on the Pick screen; pressing an own pick lets it go. An unseated pool hero is not listed and cannot be picked mid-game.
- **Play goes on** by itself when the last seated hero is picked, as DEC-154 says. There is no Continue press. If the tower is lost, its pause (DEC-155, DEC-301) shows after the picks, as it does today.
- **No new words.** The title stays "Paused".

Reason: it gives DEC-154's claim a control using the locked Pick words, and it changes no game state.

Not picked: send the table back to the Pick screen itself (it is a lobby screen; its Continue goes on to the piece setup and a new game, and it would reopen the whole roster). A separate Reassign panel in the gear (new words, more taps).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-20, under Bob's delegation. Bob confirms with the CU/16 eval approval.
