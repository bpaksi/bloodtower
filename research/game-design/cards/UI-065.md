Each effect from DEC-796 draws its own board step after the scene's choice resolves, in the order the option lists them.

- **A companion joins / a loyalty step:** the companion moments (UI-050).
- **A skull on a named building:** the board pans to the building and the skull lands, with SND-113 or the real drop. The threat bar updates.
- **A standing foe on a space:** the foe's token appears on its space with its name and level. Meeting it starts a battle.
