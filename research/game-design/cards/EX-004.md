Two heroes, 5 moves, 2 spent walking into the East village. Sanctuary lore had said "the villain's spy in the east wears a silver ring". On the street, the peddler Wren gave a rumour (a lead at the old bridge, a 2-turn window) and wore a silver ring. Bob talked on the street again (1 move) and drew a different villager, Marta, with another rumour. He then ended the turn with 2 moves unused.

Found: the street draws a villager at random, so the party cannot go back to Wren. Once an offer ends, nothing lets the heroes act on the NPC. The clue matched Wren, but no button used it. This led to DEC-181.
