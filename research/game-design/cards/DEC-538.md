DEC-542 replaced its hooks-only limit: the chapter may override any slot.

Bob chose "Base → story → chapter". A slot resolves as the active chapter, then the story, then the base, then empty. The chapter layer applies only while that chapter is active. A set piece's own hooks (start, end, steps) are slots on the set piece entry, not a fourth layer. Rejected: two layers with chapter hooks as conditioned story data, and a fourth set piece layer over every slot.
