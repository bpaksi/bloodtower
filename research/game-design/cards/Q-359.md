Raised in the first probe's Barrow battle, when Bob compared the battle with RtDT's. In RtDT a foe's level is the number of cards dealt: each card is resolved, Advantages lessen it, there are no dice, and you may leave after any card. Bloodtower has a foe count (DEC-013), a seeded roll each round (DEC-014, DEC-130), and a revealed card that lands only on a lost round (DEC-323).
