Bob chose "Met NPCs stay on the menu" after playing EX-004. Once the party meets a named NPC, such as Wren, that NPC gets an entry on the place's menu for the rest of the chapter. Talking to them again costs 1 move. It offers their own talk plus hero actions the app unlocks from what the party knows, such as "Ask about the ring" or "Accuse". Rejected: a one-off button when a clue matches, a fixed ask/bribe/threaten/accuse menu on every talk, and playing another scene first.
