Bob: "Base game has no rules for them. Allow to optionally be used by Story. I could see where defeating a "champion" the the "North" might provide a virtue."

- **The tiles:** Champion of the North, East, South and West, each +2 Wild Advantages in one terrain (mountains, hills, deserts, forests). In RtDT each hero takes their home kingdom's tile; Bloodtower has no home kingdoms (DEC-092).
- **Base game:** no rule uses them. They are not part of any class's six virtues (DEC-156).
- **Story:** a story may grant one as a reward (DEC-127), for example for defeating a "Champion of the North". The app names the tile and the hero takes it (DEC-370), and the app counts its Advantage in battles in that terrain.

Rejected: each hero takes the tile of their starting kingdom, a tile in a class's six, and leaving them in the box.
