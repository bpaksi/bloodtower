Bob took the five direct gear cards as written.

- **RtDT:** after you Reinforce, remove 1 skull from the building on your space.
- **Bloodtower:** After the holder's group makes a pick at a building, that building loses 1 skull.
- **Held:** gear lasts until it is lost (DEC-370); a hero holds one of each type.
