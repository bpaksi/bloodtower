Bob picked "Stops at 1" in the 2026-10-31 open-questions session.

- **Rule:** a plain scene's hit-point cost takes each present hero down to 1 hit point at most. A story choice alone never fells a hero; heroes fall only in battle.
- **Why:** it keeps the spirit of the instant-loss guard (MEC-013) for a choice the group picks, and no option can trap the group.

Not picked: greying an option whose cost would fell a present hero (every option could end up greyed); taking the cost in full, so a choice can fell a hero.
