Bob chose "Story-only: stories grant them".

- **Who grants:** only a story, through one of its hooks. The story says why the hero gets it (DEC-054). The base game's reward draws never deal a quest item.
- **Effect:** the engine holds each quest item's Bloodtower effect, as for every card (DEC-370), so a story only names the card.
- **Story fills in:** Bezoar, Herbal Remedy and Tools of the Saboteur name a villain, a curse or a target. The story granting one fills that in.
- **Held:** any number, as in RtDT.

Rejected: quest items in the base reward draws as well, and leaving them in the box.
