Bob picked "Half, and one free cut".

- **On guard, trap:** the loss is halved, rounded down: the Pit 4 → 2, Falling Rocks 2 → 1 each (DEC-606).
- **Bold, battle:** before round 1 the foe loses 1 head, or 1 wound for a lone foe (DEC-533). No card is drawn.
- **On guard, battle:** before round 1 the foe makes its last attack (DEC-318) once.
- **The guardian room** is a battle room, so both apply there too.

MEC-038 gains one step before round 1 for these. Not picked: on guard cancels a trap and bold wins round 1; bold hits with every hero's fight number.
