DEC-371 lets cards remove skulls. In RtDT, any hero can Cleanse a building on their space as their heroic action. Four cards depend on an action like that: Generous and Lamp of Hope widen a Cleanse, Relic of Light rewards one, and the Feverish corruption forbids one. Options: a building pick that clears skulls (DEC-157), a cost in moves or gold, a story reward only, or no action at all, with those four cards reworded.
