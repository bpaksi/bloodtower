**What:** The narration that plays after Begin and sets the scene before turn 1.

**When:** Every story, once. It names the goal, the adversary and where the heroes start.

**Why:** The table needs to know what the game is about before the first roll; a pack without one fails the lint (DEC-111).

**Pack:** the game's start moment (§4), with its tower cue (§6).
