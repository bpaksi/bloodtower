Bob, G-C1 playtest: "iPad settings menu only allow quit and no way to cancel."

- **Close:** the Menu dialog always ends with a button named "Close", under the three groups.
- **Also closes it:** a tap outside the dialog, and a tap on the gear (UI-032).
- **What closing does:** nothing changes, and nothing is sent to the host.
- **Not only Quit:** the Menu shows all three groups of DEC-229, Look up, Table and Leave. An item that cannot be used yet is greyed, never left out (DEC-227).

Accepted by Bob, 2026-10-31: "looks good".
