Audit finding QD-06 (decision D4). DEC-093 says a destroyed building's quest marker "moves". DEC-613 (Bob, later and narrower) says a 1.0 quest stays on the one site its chapter gives it, and DEC-777 parks moving quests. Moving a marker would need a destination rule, bounds and a confirmation that nothing defines. Staying needs none.

- **The rule:** when the 4th skull destroys the building holding an unfinished quest marker, the marker stays. The space stays a place the group can Enter (DEC-641). Its menu holds only the quest, then Leave. Every other place and person is gone, as DEC-093 says.
- **The quest** keeps its identity and progress (DEC-151). A group can come back and pick it for 1 move. Early quest credit is paid once, as for any quest.
- **Reachable:** a space is never removed, so the quest stays reachable. A required quest is never lost (DEC-088).
- **The kingdom count** is unchanged: the destroyed building still counts toward the fallen kingdom (DEC-044, DEC-905).
- **Cleanse:** none; a destroyed building has none (DEC-377).
- **1.1 (parked):** if moving quests open (MEC-025, DEC-020), a destination rule is designed then. DEC-777 keeps them out of 1.0.

Trims DEC-093's line "A quest marker there moves". This resolves two Bob locks, DEC-093 (2026-10-21) and DEC-613 (2026-10-29), in favor of the later one; DEC-088 holds in both.

Fixture for R05: destroy a building holding a required quest; the space offers Enter; the menu is the quest and Leave; progress kept; quest done once.

Not picked: a seeded move to a legal site (a destination algorithm, and it breaks DEC-613); the table picks a new site (a bounds and confirmation rule to design, and the quest leaves its story place).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
