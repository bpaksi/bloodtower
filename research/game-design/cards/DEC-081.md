- **Encounters** are every static RtDT building, plus story-driven locations the story marks on the board with RtDT special tokens. A group chooses them during its loop, at 1 move per action (DEC-078).
- **Events** are random things that happen in the wild, at the end of the turn (DEC-080).

The older documents (story-engine.md, core-mechanics.md) still call the random draw an "encounter"; that is for the doc-conflicts pass.
