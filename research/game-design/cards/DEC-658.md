Bob picked "Every phase fires, in order".

- Each phase line crossed changes the phase: its painting, one more Rend in the deck, and its tower show (DEC-567).
- When one round crosses two lines, phase 2 and then phase 3 both play, with the shows back to back.
- A big blow can't skip the boss's escalation, and the phases add no wounds of their own.

Not picked: cuts that stop at the next phase line; only the deepest phase firing.
