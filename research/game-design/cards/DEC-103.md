"The calibrate step for BT will include syncing the drums. This will require prompting the user which glyphs are visible. We will want to prototype this with the Tower POC."

So after the tower calibrates, the app does not trust the library's fixed glyph table (research/tower/setup-slice-findings-2026-10-21.md, PR #148). Players tell it which glyphs they can see, and the app sets its picture of the drums from that. The prompts, their order, and how orientation fits in are worked out in the tower-poc prototype before Bloodtower builds the step.
