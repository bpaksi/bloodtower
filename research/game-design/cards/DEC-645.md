Bob picked "Story names the trigger".

- **The trigger kinds:**
  - a chapter opens (Bob's example: "chapter 5 = The Tower")
  - a chapter's quests are all done (DEC-356's old rule)
  - a named quest is done
  - a story item is held (DEC-054)
- **Required:** a story names exactly one. The pack lint fails a story that names none, because its Tower would never open and the game could not be won (DEC-062).
- **Until then** the Tower is locked (DEC-644). When the trigger fires, MEC-076 .1 starts offering the Tower from a Tower-adjacent space.
- **Proving Grounds v1.0** uses "chapter 3's quests are all done" (DEC-636).

Not picked: always the finale chapter's quests; always the finale chapter opening.
