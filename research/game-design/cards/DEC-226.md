Bob: "for the kingdom names use both direction (N,S,E,W) and the DT81 names." The DT81 names go clockwise from North in name order: North is Arisilon, East Brynthia, South Durnin, West Zenon. Wherever a kingdom is named to players it shows both, for example "Arisilon (N)". Data IDs keep the direction letter (`S-howling-desert`, DEC-225).
