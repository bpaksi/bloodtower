Bob recorded all 113 tower sounds in batches of five and had an AI listen to each recording, giving each one a tone (good, bad, neutral), a short description and a confidence. The recordings and results sit in Bob's Desktop `audio` folder, outside the repo (the sounds are RtDT content). Asked where that AI text goes, Bob chose: "Body, marked AI".

- **`feel:`** keeps only what Bob recorded in the sound review tool (`sound-feel.json`): his words, his tone, his timed length. 37 cards have one (SND-001…010 and SND-087…113).
- **Card body:** every SND- card ends with one line, "AI listen, not approved (2026-11-09, DEC-845)", giving the AI's tone, its confidence and its description. The AI's confidence is self-reported, not approval.
- Where Bob and the AI disagree on tone, the card shows both. No code change: the workbench `/sounds` filter still reads `feel.tone`, so it shows only the sounds Bob has toned.
