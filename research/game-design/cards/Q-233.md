Bob asked which inputs each Story board tool takes. Today a Portrait takes the portrait art, 8 mouth frames with an anchor, a voice and the lines (UI-002, DEC-203). A Painting takes the picture, an optional camera track and narrator lines (UI-003, DEC-205). Q-103 is still open on whether a camera track is an asset of its own.
