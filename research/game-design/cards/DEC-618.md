- **Wrong hints:** 1 in 5, seeded like the room (DEC-011). Nothing changes the odds in 1.0. An Advantage or virtue that sharpens hearing can come in 1.1.
- **Where the sound plays:** the group taps Listen on its iPad, and the sound plays on the Story board's Mac speakers. DEC-586 said the iPad plays it, which clashes with DEC-201 (all sound on the board) and DEC-057 (nothing private). This trims DEC-586 to match.
- **Sounds:** three engine-default sounds, one per room kind, each 2.5 s at matched loudness. All three share one cavern ambience bed and one music motif, each in its own variation. Nothing: the calm motif, a faint drip-drip and a very slight breeze. Trap: the ambience drains away, leaving only the dread music, with no mechanism sound. Battle: low muffled talking and a fire crackle over a tenser motif. A wrong hint sounds the same as a true one. A story may swap them (DEC-199). They are not tower sounds, since Tomb tower cues wait for 1.1 (DEC-609). The bed and music live inside each 2.5 s file; there are no separate 1.0 ambience tracks.
