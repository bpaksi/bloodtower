- **Wrong hints:** 1 in 5, seeded like the room (DEC-011). Nothing changes the odds in 1.0. An Advantage or virtue that sharpens hearing can come in 1.1.
- **Where the sound plays:** the group taps Listen on its iPad, and the sound plays on the Story board's Mac speakers. DEC-586 said the iPad plays it, which clashes with DEC-201 (all sound on the board) and DEC-057 (nothing private). This trims DEC-586 to match.
- **Sounds:** three engine-default Mac sounds, one per room kind. Still air for nothing, a click and a drip for a trap, shuffling and breathing for a battle. A story may swap them (DEC-199). They are not tower sounds, since Tomb tower cues wait for 1.1 (DEC-609).
