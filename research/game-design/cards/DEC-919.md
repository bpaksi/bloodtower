Playtest PT-07 (2026-11-12): the simulated drop (DEC-305, `simulated_drop: kingdom_die d4`) named a kingdom and no rule picked the building.

- **Kingdom** by the d4. **Building:** the standing building with the fewest skulls in it, ties by seed.
- **Counts are re-read after each skull** in a night. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (Q3.2).
- **None stands:** reroll the kingdom once. If the new kingdom has none either, **no skull is dropped and an error is logged** (Q3.1). A kingdom has 4 buildings, so the game is already lost under DEC-905; the night also stops at that loss (DEC-931).
- **The board** shows one line per skull, "A skull falls on <building>.", stacked, never cut short. The line is as Bob approved it.
- **Old logs.** The change to the seed stream for old logs is accepted (Q3.3).
