Built in the bloodtower POC. The landing route `/` has its own Sound control: music, ambient and voice, a short test clip, then Continue. It is not only a dialog that appears on the way to Pick Story. The first click or key on `/` is the gesture that unlocks browser autoplay; until that gesture the browser will not start audio. Levels are remembered on that Mac.
