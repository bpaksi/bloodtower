Bob chose "No step on a Song run".

- **Rule:** on a normal retreat, the foe left standing has a seeded chance to rise one RtDT status step (DEC-314, MEC-038 .22). On a retreat made with Siren's Song (DEC-417), the foe was lured, so it skips that chance.
- **What the potion saves:** the foe's last attack, the spent move and the status step.
- **Kept:** the foe still keeps its cut count and its foe token (DEC-314, DEC-321).

Rejected: keeping the status step chance, so the potion saves only the last attack and the move.
