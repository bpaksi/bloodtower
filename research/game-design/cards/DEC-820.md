Bob picked "All play as Tombs". DEC-812 moved Cave, Fortress, Encampment, Ruins and Shrine to 1.2, but the feature map (DEC-792) used them for five quests. In 1.1 each of the five is a Tomb quest (MEC-026) at its length's room count (DEC-603), with the story's own name and lines:

- The Raiders' Market (Encampment), Standard and Long
- The Moat Lair (Cave), Standard and Long
- The Oathsworn Hold (Fortress), Long
- The Warden's Vault (Ruins), Long
- The Three Altars (Shrine), Long. As a Tomb it has a guardian, which DEC-773's Shrine didn't.

Quest counts stay 6, 10 and 15 (14 required). In 1.2 each quest swaps in its real type. P-006 plays all five as Tombs.

Not picked: four Tombs with the Market as a plain scene with a shop; cutting the five until 1.2; and five plain scenes with one battle each.
