Bob chose "Visible now, engine aims breaks".

Only 5 of the 12 drum faces hold a glyph (GLY-001 to GLY-005), so a seal break could show a blank face. While any glyph is still hidden, the engine picks the chapter's seal (DEC-303, MEC-033) so that the break shows one. A glyph acts only while it shows through an open window. If a drum turn (DEC-006) slides it behind a seal, it stops acting; if a turn brings one into an open window, it starts.

Rejected: a glyph that stays active for the game once shown, and a random seal break that may show nothing.
