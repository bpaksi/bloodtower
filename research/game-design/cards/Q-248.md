Narrowed by DEC-373 (Bob, 2026-10-27): a class has 2 intrinsic virtues, and the rest come from the shared tile pool, so only the two per class are open here.

DEC-156 sets 6 virtues per class (2 active at the start, 4 to unlock), DEC-121 makes spells virtues and DEC-123 names three spells, but no card lists the virtues. OPT-009's old innate abilities are the only candidates so far. The hero panel (UI-030), the round actions (DEC-129) and the move roll (DEC-102) all need the lists.
