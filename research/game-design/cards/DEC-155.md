Replaced by DEC-275 and DEC-301 (Bob, 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-27; parked under DEC-352, 2026-10-27): play without the tower is allowed again, and a lost tower pauses with Reconnect, Go on without the tower, or Quit.

Bob asked whether to allow play without the tower, since that "would require fallback options for Tower required features". Ruled no.

Whatever loop is running waits. Reconnect happens only on a press, never on its own; then the players confirm what the tower can't sense (MEC-018). Quit leaves the autosave, so the table can Continue (DEC-208). The app builds no stand-ins for the skull drop, drums, glyphs, seals or tower cues.
