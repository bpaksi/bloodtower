Bob asked Claude to draft all 10 in one pass, for his review.

- **RtDT:** +2 Undead. Glyphs facing you cost no spirit; after an action matching such a glyph, gain 1 spirit. Her event removes corruptions.
- **Bloodtower:** A sanctuary cure the holder pays for costs half, rounded up. The free first cure each chapter (DEC-700) still applies.
- **Why:** Glyph costs do not exist here, since glyphs are passive (DEC-600). Her event removes corruptions, so she helps with cures. It also matters for loyalty, since a corruption on her hero lowers it (DEC-721).

Accepted by Bob as written in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session; check it in the 1.1 playtest.
