Bob chose "Yes, Tomb only". The Tomb (MEC-026, with its screens in AREA-094) is built and ships in 1.0. The Cave, Encampment, Fortress, Ruins and Shrine (MEC-027 to MEC-031) still wait for 1.1, as DEC-502 and DEC-580 say. Its open questions, Q-307 and Q-310 to Q-313, are now 1.0 work, answered in the build and playtest that DEC-486 asks for. Its art and cues join the 1.0 art list: the story's tomb style paintings (DEC-585, DEC-588) and the door, nothing, trap and guardian cues (Q-313). Rejected: parking the Tomb for 1.1, and building it without shipping it.
