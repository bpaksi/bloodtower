Audit finding BE-05 (decision D5). DEC-176 (Bob): one pool, freely shared, and the board shows the pool. DEC-178 (Bob): a cost is paid equally by the heroes in the group that spends it, and the others cover a short hero. Nothing said what happens when the acting group holds less than the price and the heroes who are not there hold the rest.

- **Affordable means the pool.** The board shows the party's pool (DEC-176). A buy is greyed only if the pool cannot cover it.
- **Who pays, in order:** (1) the heroes present, equal shares, odd coins by DEC-646 (a hero holding the most); (2) if one present hero is short, the other present heroes cover it, a coin at a time from the hero holding the most, ties by seat; (3) only if the present heroes together are short, the rest comes from the heroes not there, the same way.
- **Conservation:** the pool falls by exactly the price. No hero goes below 0.
- **Costs that name a hero's own gold stay own-share:** Cruel, Greedy, Feral and Lost (DEC-896), and the holder's gold in a companion's price (DEC-726, 1.1). They take only from that hero's part, are never covered by anyone, and never touch an absent hero. A fallen group's loss (DEC-177) stays capped per hero within the group.
- **A gain** is still split across the heroes present (DEC-178).
- **Presence (MEC-003)** says who takes part and who is hit. Gold is not a hero's body, so an absent hero's gold may pay a present group's buy.

Example: the acting group holds 0, the absent group holds 10, and the acting group takes a 5-gold rally. It is allowed; the absent heroes pay 5, by (3).

Fixtures for R06: remote funding; a short present hero covered by the present; a pool that is 1 short; the odd coin; no negative balance; the pool's change equals the price.

For Bob via Warden, no action needed: the two Bob cards can be read the other way (only the present group's gold pays; the pool shown would then be a total the acting group cannot spend). I read DEC-176's "freely shared" as the stronger line. If Bob prefers the other reading, this is the one card to flip.

Not picked: only the present heroes' gold can pay; the buyer picks who pays (DEC-178 refused it).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
