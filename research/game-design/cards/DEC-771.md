**Recommendation, held by DEC-486.** This one keeps its own game: Bob's idea of a merchant you can anger has no match in the Tomb.

- **Picture:** the camp at its fire, with the temper meter across it.
- **Meter:** temper, 6 steps. Progress is kept between visits.
- **Choices:** Buy, Haggle hard or Leave. The quest's prize is one of the goods.
- **Haggle hard:** roll the die for a bigger discount (DEC-760); temper +1, or +2 on the hooded figure.
- **Full temper:** a battle at the quest's foe, one level up. Winning takes the prize for free, and the camp's goods are gone.
- **Humanoid Advantage:** lowers temper by 2, once.
- **Cut:** Refuse and Steal, to stay at three choices. Steal was never simulated.

**Numbers:** P-04 found temper 4 attacks every time, and 6 gives a 1-in-8 risk for about 16% off. **Caveat:** simulation only.
