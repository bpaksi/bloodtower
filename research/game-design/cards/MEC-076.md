The deliverable card for DEC-358's custom Tower encounter: a crawl through the basement, main and tower levels, revealed room by room, ending in the boss battle (DEC-364). A group enters from a Tower-adjacent space (DEC-363); a retreat keeps all progress (DEC-360).

- **Owner:** engine. Start and end hook slots (DEC-540), plus a hook on any room's start or end (DEC-566).
- **Enter:** .1 to .4. It's offered only once the story's unlock trigger has fired (DEC-645, DEC-644), from a Tower-adjacent space, for 1 move (DEC-363). The first visit deals the rooms. Later visits resume where the party stopped.
- **Body:** .5 to .18, one room per pass. Each level has two dealt rooms (a guard, a trap or a shrine), then its stair room, a guard battle the group must win to go up. The tower level's last room is the adversary (DEC-566).
- **Exit:** .18. The adversary falls, every present hero falls, or the group leaves or retreats.
- **Close:** .19. Cleared rooms, the level reached and the adversary's cut wounds are kept for the next group (DEC-360, DEC-566).
- **Result up:** won, left or fallen, to the group turn. A won Tower ends the game in the win scene (DEC-356, DEC-144).

Drafted by Claude (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Bob accepted it as drafted, 2026-10-29. Numbers are placeholders in the tuning file (DEC-500).
