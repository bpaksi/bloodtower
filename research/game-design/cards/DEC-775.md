**Recommendation. Placeholders until the simulator run.**

- **Start:** the Tower. The first move is the first day's close.
- **Move:** 2 spaces a day toward the most-skulled building, so a group (about 4 a day) can outrun it.
- **Arrival:** 1 skull from the supply, then it picks a new target.
- **Strike:** when its move reaches a group or a group enters its space, once a day. Each hero there picks a corruption or loses 3 + the chapter number in hit points, stopping at 1. A hero already holding 2 corruptions can't pick the corruption, so no strike alone loses the game (MEC-013).
- **Lieutenant:** the chapter's foe level +1, counted like any foe.
- **Hunter's battle:** a hunter reaching a group at day's close fights at the start of that group's next turn, before the roll. Retreat there needs no move, as in DEC-670.
