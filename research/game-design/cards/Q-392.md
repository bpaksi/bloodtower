Probe P-003, day 25. The party entered the Tower with 2 moves and spent 1 to go in (DEC-363). The rooms cost no moves, so a group may fight a guard with 0 left. DEC-360 says a retreat from the adversary works "as in any battle: a move left". The Tomb lets a group retreat with no move and leaves it on its space (DEC-497), and the Tower counts as a dungeon (DEC-657). Without a move a group inside the Tower can't retreat at all, and MEC-076 .18 lists "retreated" as an exit.
