Bob picked "Enter the marker". The first probe found the gap on day 1, at *The Ambush* (DEC-636).

- **The place:** a quest marker (AST-B08, DEC-524) on a wild space is a place, like a building. The move map offers Enter there (MEC-035).
- **The menu:** only the marker's quest, then Leave (MEC-036). Enter is free and the pick costs 1 move (DEC-078), as at a building.
- **1.0 and after:** this is DEC-089's special encounter, with the quest marker standing in for the dungeon token. In 1.0 it is the only wild site, so the Tomb works this way too (DEC-593).

Not picked: arriving on the marker starts the quest at once; no wild quests in 1.0.
