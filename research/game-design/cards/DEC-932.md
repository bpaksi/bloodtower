CU/04's Q4.1 (Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted) read the PT-18 wording one way and flagged it for Gamewright, because the playtest text says both "the board shows the groups for 4 seconds every day" and "the prompt appears only when something is queued".

- **Confirmed: the second reading.** The groups card (the day's groups and their heroes, no button) shows for 4 seconds on a day whose start applied at least one queued change. On any other day, a split party's quiet days included, nothing shows and the roll follows the day start. It is never a question.
- **Why this one:** it is the only reading that keeps Bob's happy path free of exceptions (DEC-926). A card every day would be the daily Regroup step again, without the button.
- **Mixed days:** if one queued action applied and another was dropped as impossible (Q4.2), the card shows the groups as they now stand, and the dropped one is logged and toasted as its own error. A day where every queued action was dropped shows no card, only the error.
- **Length:** the one exported 4 s constant (Q4.4). PT-26's longer panel is not ruled for 1.0 and does not apply.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-15, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
