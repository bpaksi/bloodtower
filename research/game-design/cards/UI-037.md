Shown on the Story board at the start of a group's turn, after the whose-turn banner (UI-019). One line per virtue: the tile's name, its art, and what it does now ("Infused: remove 1 skull in Brynthia", "Relentless: +1 Wild until the next roll", "Fortunate: a second pick today"). A virtue that needs a choice shows it on the acting group's iPad, for example the buildings in the kingdom with skulls for Infused. One Done on the iPad moves on. Not shown when nothing applies.
