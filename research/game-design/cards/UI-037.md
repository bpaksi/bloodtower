Shown on the Story board at the start of a group's turn, after the whose-turn banner (UI-019). One line per virtue: the tile's name, its art, and what it does now ("Infused: remove 1 skull in Brynthia", "Relentless: +1 Wild until the next roll", "Fortunate: a second pick today"). Anything usable is offered through the iPad's use button (UI-038), and the board does not wait for it (DEC-479). Not shown when nothing applies.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-30 rulings review.
