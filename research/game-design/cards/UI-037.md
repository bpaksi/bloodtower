Shown on the Story board at the start of a group's turn, after the whose-turn banner (UI-019). One line per virtue: the tile's name, its art, and what it does now ("Infused: remove 1 skull in Brynthia", "Relentless: +1 Wild until the next roll", "Fortunate: a second pick today"). Anything usable is offered through the gear's "Use a potion or item" (DEC-922; the use button UI-038 is retired), and the board does not wait for it (DEC-479). Not shown when nothing applies.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-30 rulings review.

**Kept by DEC-922 (Gamewright, 2026-11-12):** built in C3. A virtue that fires at turn start is automatic and shown here; the iPad needs a press only when the virtue asks a choice. **The 1.0 list is set by DEC-934 (2026-11-15):** Infused is the only turn-start choice.
