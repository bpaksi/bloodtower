Found by the first probe at the day 1 group turn (MEC-035). DEC-031 lets a hero split off or join "at any time", and DEC-148 has a split-off group play last. But the group turn has no split or join node: the move map (UI-023) offers only a space, Enter or End turn. The moves are rolled per group at day start (MEC-034 .2), so no card says what moves a group that splits mid-day has.
