Bob picked "The cause decides" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session.

- **A story moment lowers a wavering companion:** it walks out.
- **Its hero's corruption lowers a wavering companion:** it turns, and returns later as a named foe.

Players can see it coming, and corruption gets a real threat.

Not picked: a seeded roll, always walking out, and each story writing the outcome per companion.
