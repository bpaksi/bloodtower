- **Objective:** marks the site of the night's next quest.
- **Lure:** optional; the first hero to reach it gets a story item or finding.
- **Hunter:** an enemy token that moves toward the nearest hero.

Parked by Bob, 2026-10-21: the quest tokens are only lead markers, placed when an NPC reveals a lead (DEC-087). None are placed at setup or at dusk. The hunter token (MEC-001) stays open on its own.
