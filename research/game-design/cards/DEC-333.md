Bob chose "Engine roles, story cast". The engine owns the roles: innkeeper, priest, merchant, lord, street villager and the stranger met in the wild, each with its offers and trust (DEC-026). For each role the engine also ships a default cast member: a name, lines and an avatar. A story uses that default as is or replaces it with its own. Named NPCs, companions and the adversary stay the story's own (MEC-048). Rejected: the story defining every NPC, and building and wild NPCs as fixed, engine-only extras.
