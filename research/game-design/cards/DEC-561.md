- **When:** at arrival, at each round's result (MEC-038 .15) and at the end. Not while heroes pick, so the painting does not flicker with the odds read.
- **Within a state:** the painting holds. Variety comes from the seeded pick in each new battle (DEC-332).
- **Motion:** a straight cut. Base-game pictures have no motion (DEC-347); the hit shake still plays on a hit.
