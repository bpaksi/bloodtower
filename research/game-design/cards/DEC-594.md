Bob chose "Named per kind". A foe carries attributes such as rank, traits and size; a place carries terrain and building. Each attribute is a closed list in the engine schema, and the engine branches only on these (DEC-499). Type, Subtype, Class and SubClass stay words for the idea, not fields. Rejected: four generic levels on every entry, where "Class" would mean something different per kind, and one ordered path per entry (foe › undead › vampire), which is easy to fall back through but hard to branch on one property.
