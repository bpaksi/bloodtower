Bob chose "A: two kinds, two jobs", after Claude walked through a two-round battle (a Fighter with an automatic Blessed Blade and a Magic User with a conditional Holy Water against a level-2 Undead foe).

- **Automatic** matches (gear, passive virtues) add strength in every round, as before (DEC-128): they help win rounds.
- **Conditional** matches are spent as a hero's round action, after the foe's card is revealed (DEC-323). Each softens that card one step, RtDT's arrow: "Claw: 4 to one hero" to "Claw: 2", "Sweep: 2 to everyone" to "Sweep: 1". The hero adds no fight number that round (DEC-129).
- Softening only matters if the round is lost, so it is a defensive bet; adding strength is the offensive one.

This replaces DEC-128's "+3 for the round it is spent". Rejected: every match softening the card, and choosing per match each round.
