Bob chose "Named moves, track as escape". The job names a move from a short engine list (push in, pull out, pan left, pan right, reveal the focus box) plus a focus box on the picture. The studio previews it, and AI mode can pick one. A drawn camera-track JSON (UI-003's contract) is still allowed for the odd shot. Named moves are easy to lint (DEC-206). With no move named, the engine drift runs (DEC-205). Rejected: drawn tracks only, and named moves only.
