**What:** The engine's people in each building type: the innkeeper, the priest, the merchant, the lord and the rest. The engine owns the role and its offers; the story names them and writes what they say.

**When:** Every story. Write enough lines per role that a second and third visit sound different.

**Why:** Buildings are where the heroes spend most turns between set pieces; a role that repeats one line makes the world feel empty.

**Pack:** cast entries per role and their lines (§2, §2.1). The count is lines per role.

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Target | 6 | 8 | 10 |
