In RtDT a player can take three corruptions, the loss threshold, before their next turn. Here a hero's 3rd corruption loses the game (DEC-043), and no single random effect can lose it (MEC-013). But the end of the day runs seven threat steps before the loss check (DEC-086). Several of them could each hand the same hero a corruption, with no turn for the players in between.
