Bob: "I don't think the count should refill, that makes the player feel like no progress was made. I say there is % chance that the foe 1 RTDT status step. It status increases, the retreat screen should indicate it."

- **The count stays cut:** the rounds the group won still count; the next fight starts from what is left.
- **A chance to step up:** when the group retreats (DEC-313), or falls, which counts as a retreat (DEC-119), a seeded draw (DEC-011) decides whether the foe rises one RtDT status step: panicked, aggressive, savage, lethal.
- **Shown:** the retreat screen (MEC-038 .24) says when the foe stepped up.

Rejected: refilling the count, and a step up that always happens.
