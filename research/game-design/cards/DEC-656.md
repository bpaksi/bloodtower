Bob picked "Only when the Tower is won".

- **The finale chapter's last quest** is the Tower encounter (MEC-076). Its other quests only unlock it, through the story's trigger (DEC-645).
- **Days go on** while the Tower is unlocked and not yet won, so the skulls keep pressing.
- **The win:** the adversary falling in the Tower wins the chapter and the game. As MEC-076 already says, it ends the game at once, in the win scene, without running the rest of the day.
- **Other chapters** are unchanged: won when every quest is done (MEC-033 .11).

Not picked: an engine-added Tower chapter after the story's last one; the story listing the Tower as a quest, with a lint check.
