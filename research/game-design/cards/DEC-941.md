D2 brief (bloodtower #500), open question OQ-D2-4. DEC-553's Spines hits "the hero with the highest fight number" on a won round. No card says who it hits when two or more heroes tie.

- **Ruling:** among the standing heroes tied for the highest fight number that round, the app picks one from the battle's seeded stream (DEC-011). That hero takes the 1. There is no prompt and no table choice.
- **Never seat or tap order** (as DEC-655). The same seed and the same round give the same hero, so a replay or a Continue hits the same hero.
- **Unchanged:** Spines fires only if the foe still stands after the cut (DEC-907), and only one hero takes it.

Reason: it is the rule the base foes already use for a random hero (Ambush, Hex), costs the table nothing mid-round, and keeps the round seat-independent.

Not picked: the group picks (a prompt in the middle of a round, for 1 hit point); every tied hero takes 1 (a bigger Spines than the card).
