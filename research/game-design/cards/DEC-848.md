D-02 §1. New effects: `loyalty: ±1`, `skull: 1 on <building>` (named, seeded or most-skulled), `weaken: <story item>` (one step, never below its floor) and `vanish: <hero>` (Tower rooms only; the hero sits out with their held cards set aside until the room's battle ends).

Reworded: taking a companion is the walk-out plus the flag `lenka-taken`; Darkness's extra drop is a `skull` effect in the chapter-end hook; the foe strikes first is a `foe-first` flag on the battle effect. A choice in a hook is dropped: those rooms become traps with DEC-489's two-loss choice.
