Each change to a companion is an event on the Story board, so the table never has to notice it alone.

| Moment | Story board | iPad |
|---|---|---|
| Joins (hire, scene or reward, DEC-796) | Reveal of the companion with its name and effect | The hero's Look up adds it |
| Loyalty up or down (DEC-721) | A short line under the companion's portrait: "Letha is now devoted", with the cause ("after the Shrine", "Mara's corruption") | The step mark changes |
| Walks out (DEC-722, a story moment) | Reveal of the portrait turning away, with the cause | The card goes back to the box |
| Turns (DEC-722, a corruption) | Reveal with a darkened portrait: "Grigor has turned. He will return." | The card goes back; the board names when he returns (DEC-774) |
| Falls in its hero's place (DEC-800) | On the fall result (UI-039): the companion falls, the hero stays at 1 | The card goes back |

The companion's own tower sound plays where one exists (CUE-013).
