Bob: "No banner action in BT. However, I think Glyphs is a whole subject that we haven't really thought out yet."

RtDT's banner action is a once-a-turn power on the hero boards, which stay in the box (DEC-376). Bloodtower adds no such power. What the Banner glyph (GLY-004), and every other glyph, does is left to Q-329.

Rejected: a Banner action as a new once-a-turn hero power. Not chosen yet: the story-boon and engine-default answers for the glyph, which Q-329 takes up.
