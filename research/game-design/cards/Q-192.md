Notes: the session's tower block (DEC-104) runs before each game's setup places the seals (MEC-032 .21, DEC-281), so the first sync sees bare glyphs. The awkward case is a sync after the seals are on: Continue or a reconnect (MEC-018). If calibration puts each glyph where the library says (Q-033), the sync becomes a check and may need no glyph seen at all.
