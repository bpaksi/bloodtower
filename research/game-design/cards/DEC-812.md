Bob: "let's move to v1.2 and also move other dungeon types to v1.2 also."

**What moves to 1.2:**
- **Dracula:** every card in AREA-080 and its sub-areas AREA-101 to AREA-113 that says `release: "1.1"`, plus Dracula's open list: Q-424 to Q-431, DEC-778, DEC-780, IDEA-094, IDEA-095 and IDEA-097. Dracula's drop after 1.1 (DEC-788) is 1.2.
- **The five other dungeon types:** Cave, Fortress, Encampment, Ruins and Shrine, so DEC-769 to DEC-773 and MEC-027 to MEC-031. The Tomb (MEC-026) stays as it is.

**Why:** 1.1's gaps then list only 1.1 work, so "coding ready" can be read straight off the list.

**Tools first:** the card lint and the gaps check accept only `"1.1"` (DEC-696). A small workbench change adds `"1.2"` on its own branch: the lint takes it, and the gaps check and its workbench page report 1.2 after 1.1. The retag lands after that change is on `main`.

DEC-811 is replaced by this ruling.
