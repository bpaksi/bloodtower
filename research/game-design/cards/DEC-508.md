Bob accepted Claude's proposal as written. The full version is kept in Dracula v2 (AREA-092).

**Chapters (3, DEC-502):** 1. The village (K1): `n1-lantern-and-bell`, `n1-burgomasters-house`, `n1-fortune-reading`, which seeds the sites. 2. The hunt (the fortune's kingdoms): `n23-sergius-blade`, `n23-catacombs-of-saint-ysolde`, `n2-burgomasters-funeral`, `n4-earth-boxes`, `n4-finding-the-tome`; the blade, the holy symbol and the Tome are the three keys. 3. The castle: `n5-drawbridge`, `n5-final-confrontation`. Ten quests at both lengths; Standard gets bigger tombs and more encounters, not more scenes (DEC-669).

**Keeps:** the goal (rescue Lenka, D-DRA-10) and the four endings (Rescued, Endures without the blade, Taken, Turned on a 3rd corruption); corruption cured by holy water, the host or a sanctuary rest (D-DRA-6); story items holy water, the host, stakes, Sergiu's blade and the three keys (the hunter's kit becomes story items, not gear); the investigation advantages as story item effects; base foes.

**Voices and art:** the Count is the one story Portrait; a narrator speaks for Lenka, Anton, Jakob and the mourners. One scene Painting per quest (10) plus the cover.

**Waits for v2:** the 5-night structure; the crypt crawl (D-DRA-14); `n1-open-grave`, `n3-fly-eaters-cell` and `n5-portrait-gallery`; the pressed-flower and midnight-procession beats and their clues; the lookalike; the finale guard; garlic, silver and the crucifix; foe reskins; the Long length.

**Changed by DEC-669 (Bob, 2026-10-30):** length no longer changes foe strength. Standard gets bigger tombs and a higher event chance in the wild.
