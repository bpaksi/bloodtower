A group makes one move for all its heroes. A hero can split off or join a group on the same space. Four solo heroes are four groups.

**Changed by DEC-640 (Bob, 2026-10-30):** "at any time" is replaced. Heroes split and join only in the Regroup step at the start of each day, before the moves are rolled.
