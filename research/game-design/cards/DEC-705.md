Bob, G-C1 playtest, of the connect dialog on the Samsung TV: "it literally fills the entire vertical space. There always need to be some border."

- **Rule:** every dialog on the Story board, the connect dialog included, leaves the landing painting showing above, below and to both sides. No dialog fills the height or the width of the board.
- **Size:** at least 5% of the board's height above and below, and 5% of its width at each side. This is a placeholder number Claude proposed, to tune on the TV.
- **Where it is checked:** the Samsung at 1920×1080 and at 3840×2160.
- **When the content is long:** the dialog's content is cut down or set smaller to fit inside the border. The board still never scrolls or clips text (SCR-013).

Accepted by Bob, 2026-10-31: "looks good".
