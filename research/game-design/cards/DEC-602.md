Bob: "I would like every tomb to be 'determined' on first entry and remain the same for the remainder of the game."

On the first entry the engine builds the whole tomb from the game's seed: the room layout (DEC-603), what each room holds (DEC-604), which trap is in each trap room (DEC-606) and the foe in each battle room (DEC-608). Nothing is rolled as a room is entered; entering reveals what is already there. A group that leaves and comes back, or another group, finds the same tomb, with cleared rooms still cleared (DEC-491).
