DEC-808 drops river crossings from the base pack, since the map tool does not export them and no 1.0 rule reads them. DEC-445 (Amulet of the Marid, a treasure, so 1.1) needs to know where the rivers are. The parked MEC-001 also names river rules.

Options: the board map tool marks rivers per neighbour pair and exports them in 1.1; the amulet is reworded to need no rivers (for example, lakes only); or the amulet stays out.
