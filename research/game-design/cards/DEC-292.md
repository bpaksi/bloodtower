Bob chose "Park it, group beat becomes arrive". DEC-141 had two beats. The chapter's timed table beat is gone (DEC-287). The quest's group beat ("strange noises from beneath the tavern") becomes a story hook on the "arrive on a space" trigger for its spot (DEC-290), played once unless marked every time (DEC-291). A group that ends one turn on the spot and starts the next there hears nothing new. MEC-034, MEC-035 and the other cards that cite DEC-141 are fixed as their diagrams are drawn. Rejected: a sixth trigger for starting a turn on a space, and keeping the group beat as its own engine step.
