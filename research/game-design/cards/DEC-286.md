Bob chose "Yes, as engine effects". Besides its moments (DEC-285), a story hook may list effects from a fixed engine menu: set a story flag, open a lead, or place, move or remove a story token. The engine plays them in order. For a physical change it draws its own board node ("place the token on X", DEC-278) and a Done prompt. No effect branches or asks for a choice. Rejected: moments only, and flags only.
