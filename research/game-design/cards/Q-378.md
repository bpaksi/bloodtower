Probe P-002 found it at MEC-032 .14. MEC-010 says length sets the chapters, the encounter rate and the dungeon size. DEC-603 sizes the tomb by length. DEC-636 and DEC-508 say Standard gets "more encounters and stronger foes", but no engine rule says how. Stronger foes would also make length a second difficulty knob, against DEC-185.
