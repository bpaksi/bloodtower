Bob chose "Quest gold + free cure".

- **Quest gold:** when a group wins a quest, each hero in that group gets 5 gold, on top of any battle gold and the quest's own reward.
- **Free cure:** each hero's first cure at a sanctuary in a chapter costs no gold. It still costs the pick and its move. Later cures in that chapter cost the normal price, which still rises as DEC-170 and DEC-552 say.
- **Why:** P-003's pair ran out of gold for rallies by day 15. P-002's solo Ranger never had the 10 gold for a cure, so corruption cut its move roll until it lost. Quest gold pays for progress, and the free cure stops the corruption snowball.
- **Tuning:** 5 gold and once a chapter are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131).

Rejected: quest gold only, the free cure only, and lower prices.
