Bob chose "Set piece → clear a horde".

- **RtDT:** finish a monthly quest and remove a foe instead of gaining spirit.
- **Bloodtower:** RtDT's monthly quest becomes a set piece, and spirit becomes money. When the group holding Unseen finishes a set piece, the holder may give up the set piece's money reward to remove one standing horde (DEC-320) in the group's current kingdom. Its foe token goes back. It is a trade, not a free gift.

What Unseen trades when a set piece pays no money is open (Q-294). Rejected: removing the horde on top of the reward, and slipping past a battle once per chapter.
