Bob, after the hero tiles: "We need to keep the player in the loop when virtues can/are applied per day. This will require a UI at the start of every turn." He then chose "Each group's turn start".

- **When:** at the start of each group's turn (MEC-035 .10 to .13), after the whose-turn banner.
- **What:** the board lists that group's virtues that fire now (Infused's skull, Relentless's +1 Wild after a doubles roll) or can be used today (Fortunate's second pick, Lucky's swap), one line each (UI-037).
- **Choices:** a virtue that needs a choice, such as Infused's building, is asked on the acting group's iPad.
- **Skipped:** when no virtue applies, the step does not show, so turns stay quick.

Rejected: a virtue line per group in the day roll panel (UI-021), and both the panel and the turn start.
