Bob: "This loop is until the enemy is defeated, group is defeated or party runs."

- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140).
- **Enter:** started from an encounter, an event or a set piece step (DEC-136). The tower plays the horn; the board shows the horde.
- **Body:** one pass is one round (MEC-024).
- **Exit:** the horde is gone, every present hero has fallen, or the group runs.
- **Close:** a won battle pays money and one card by foe level (DEC-120); a run leaves the horde to grow (DEC-016).
- **Result up:** won, ran or fallen, to the loop that started it.
- **Screen:** the Story board (SCR-014) and each present hero's iPad (SCR-016).
- **UI:** the horde panel (UI-026), the round action picker (UI-027).
- **Art:** one painting per foe.
- **Sound:** CUE-010: the horn, a hit each won round, victory.
