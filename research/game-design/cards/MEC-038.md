Bob: "This loop is until the enemy is defeated, group is defeated or party runs."

- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140); its battle-ends trigger hook (.26) is one the active chapter may fill (DEC-288).
- **Enter:** .1 and .2: started from an encounter, an event or a set piece step (DEC-136). The horn, then the horde panel.
- **Body:** .3 to .16, one round per pass (MEC-024): the group picks Fight or Run (Run needs a move left, DEC-313), the foe reveals its round card (DEC-323), every standing hero picks an action at once (DEC-118, DEC-129, DEC-324), a hero taps Strike (DEC-331), then the seeded draw.
- **Exit:** .16: the horde is gone, every present hero has fallen, or the group runs.
- **Close:** .17 to .26: a won battle pays money and one card by foe level (DEC-120); a run takes the foe's last attack and falls back a space (DEC-318, DEC-313, DEC-316); a standing horde keeps its cut count, may step up, and gets a foe token any group may finish (DEC-314, DEC-320, DEC-321); a fallen group also loses money (DEC-177, DEC-179). Fallen heroes come back at 1 hit point (DEC-119). The battle-ends hook plays last, so a story can match on the result.
- **Result up:** won, ran or fallen, to the loop that started it.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Battle beats with a board and a tower slot each (DEC-327), layered music (DEC-328), 1 to n foe paintings by state (DEC-332, DEC-329, DEC-330).

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). Nodes added on 2026-10-27 (.27 to .35) take the next free numbers, so older references to .1 to .26 stay valid. Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
