Bob: "This loop is until the enemy is defeated, group is defeated or party runs."

- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140); its battle-ends trigger hook (.26) is one the active chapter may fill (DEC-288).
- **Enter:** .1 and .2: started from an encounter, an event or a set piece step (DEC-136). The horn, then the horde panel.
- **Body:** .3 to .16, one round per pass (MEC-024): the group picks Fight or Run, then every standing hero picks an action at once (DEC-118, DEC-129), then the seeded draw.
- **Exit:** .16: the horde is gone, every present hero has fallen, or the group runs.
- **Close:** .17 to .26: a won battle pays money and one card by foe level (DEC-120); a run leaves the horde to grow (DEC-016); a fallen group also loses money (DEC-177, DEC-179). Fallen heroes come back at 1 hit point (DEC-119). The battle-ends hook plays last, so a story can match on the result.
- **Result up:** won, ran or fallen, to the loop that started it.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: one painting per foe.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
