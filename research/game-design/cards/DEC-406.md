Bob chose "Day start: 1 skull, group's kingdom".

- **RtDT:** at your turn start, remove 1 skull in your home kingdom.
- **Bloodtower:** heroes have no home kingdom, so the group's current kingdom stands in. At the start of its turn each day (MEC-035, DEC-408), the group holding Infused removes 1 skull from one building in that kingdom. The players pick the building on the iPad, and the board shows the skull to take off. That building's menu gets back what the skull had taken, as with Cleanse (DEC-371, DEC-377).
- **No skull:** if no building in the kingdom has a skull, nothing happens.

Rejected: once per chapter anywhere on the board, and a free Cleanse pick once a day.
