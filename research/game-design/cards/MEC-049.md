**What:** Story characters who join one hero: one effect, a loyalty level, and lines at key moments through the talking avatar. At most one per hero.

**When:** When the story wants allies with a voice: someone the heroes win over, protect or lose.

**Why:** A companion gives the plot a person the table cares about, and a loss that hurts.

**Pack:** cast entries with effect kind, join condition and lines (§2); the physical companion cards (AST-B18).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Target | 4 | 4 | 6 |

Lines each: target 5 (join, loyalty up, loyalty down, leaving, the finale).
