DEC-527 keeps RtDT's action names, but no decided card says what Cleanse does in Bloodtower. It decides what the Cleanse glyph (GLY-001) changes.

Answered 2026-10-29 by DEC-377, decided on 2026-10-27 before this question was ported: the group at any standing building spends one menu pick (one move, MEC-036) to remove 1 skull from it.
