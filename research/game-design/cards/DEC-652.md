Bob picked "Once per visit".

- **On Enter:** every present hero's once-per-dungeon spells (Lightning, Fireball, DEC-379) come back.
- **Inside:** each is cast at most once until the group leaves.
- **Why it's fair:** leaving and coming back costs the party a day and moves, which prices in the reset.

Not picked: once per tomb across visits; once per battle only.
