Live-quests brief (F1/03). On main the engine can run quests and chapters, but in a real game nothing loads them: no quest marker is ever open, the chapter loop has no plan, so a chapter is never won and a lost day never ends the game.

- **When.** At the start of play (the first time the board shows the heroes on their citadels), the bound story's quests and its chapter plan are loaded. Chapter 1 is current and its quest markers are open, as C3-19 says.
- **From where.** The quests, their places and their plain scenes come from the story pack (C3-18). The chapter plan reads the pack's chapters (index, kingdoms, quests) and the base tuning file's `c3_end_after_chapter_1`, `setup.foe_level_by_chapter` and `day.seal_break.min` (DEC-703). Seals: none until the pack declares them (C4-07). In C3 a won chapter 1 ends the game before any seal breaks (Q-C3-01).
- **A quest with no place** is not on the board, so it is not part of its chapter's goal. A quest with a place but no plain scene (a Tomb) is part of the goal; its game is C4.
- **Continue** restores the saved quests and chapters whole. Nothing is loaded again, and no chapter enter or start runs twice (DEC-675).
- **A save from before this step** has no quests or chapters. It plays on as it was saved: no quest markers, and no chapter end. A New game gets them.

Reason: the pack already says everything; loading it once at the start is the plainest reading of MEC-033 and C3-19. An old save cannot know which quests are already done, so loading quests into it would invent progress.

Not picked: loading quests into an old save at chapter 1 with nothing done (it invents a state the table never played); counting an unplaced quest toward the chapter's goal (nobody can ever do it, so the chapter could never be won).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
