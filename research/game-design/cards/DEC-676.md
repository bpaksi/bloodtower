Bob picked "At once". Probe P-002 found the gap on day 18, in the Barrow.
- **When:** the moment a hero takes a third corruption (DEC-043), in a battle, a trap or any effect.
- **What stops:** every open loop: the battle, the tomb, the quest, the place, the group turn, the day and the chapter. No other group plays its turn.
- **Then:** the game loop's close and end run as for any loss (MEC-032 .43 to .49): the record, the defeat cue and the loss scene with its reason.
- **The other losses:** an empty skull supply and a kingdom with every building fallen can only happen when skulls land, at the day's close or the chapter's close. They are still checked at MEC-033 .11.
- This mirrors DEC-656, where winning the Tower ends the game at once.

Not picked: the loss at the day's close; the loss at the end of the turn.
