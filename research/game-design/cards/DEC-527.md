Bob chose "Action, with RtDT's names". Whatever a group picks from a place's menu is an action, at 1 move each (DEC-078). Actions that match RtDT's keep its names (Battle, Quest, Cleanse, Reinforce), so each tower glyph names the action it changes. There is no "heroic" limit of one per turn. Rejected: "heroic action" for every pick, and keeping "pick".
