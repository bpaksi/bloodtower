Bob chose "Everything". The engine schema declares every slot: hooks, settings, content entries and asset slots, each marked required or optional. The base config may fill any slot, and the story's config merges on top by the DEC-503 field patch. A slot resolves as story, then base, then empty. The story lint (DEC-062) fails a required slot that is still empty after the merge. Rejected: only hooks and settings, and only hooks.
