Bob chose "Places, people, outcomes". A trigger node (DEC-288) sits where a group does something or where a result lands:

- **Places:** a group arrives on a space, or enters a place (building, caravan, story token).
- **People:** a group meets an NPC, or picks an item from a place's menu.
- **Outcomes:** a battle ends won, ran or fallen; a quest ends done, left or failed. A story flag set or a lead opened also counts.

World happenings at the day's end (an event drawn, the skull drop, a building falling, the villain moving, a countdown) fire no beats. Rejected: places and people only, and everything including the world.
