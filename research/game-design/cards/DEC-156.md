Partly replaced by DEC-373 (Bob, 2026-10-27): a class has 2 intrinsic virtues, and the 16 virtue tiles are one pool dealt at random, so "6 per class, 2 active and 4 to unlock" no longer holds. Whether the citadel still sells virtues is Q-272. The rest stands.

Bob chose "6, citadel only". A class has 6 virtues: the 2 it starts with (DEC-126) and 4 more to unlock. A hero unlocks them by buying at the citadel or as a story or quest reward (DEC-127). No other building is tied to a virtue, so a party does not need to travel to a particular building type to grow. Rejected: each extra virtue at its own building, 4 in all like RtDT, and an open pool per class.
