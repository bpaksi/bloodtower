Bob chose "Separate concepts" over making kingdoms the top region level and over making kingdoms the regions. A kingdom stays the chapter unlock and the letter on each space (DEC-226). A region is a smaller named area, such as The Great Woods (REG-, DEC-242, DEC-243), and it never replaces a kingdom. AST-B01's wording about "regions chapters unlock" should be read as kingdoms; fixing that text is a separate edit.
