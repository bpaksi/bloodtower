Every win pays some gold. On top, one card is drawn from the pile that fits the foe's level: a potion for low foes, gear for middle foes, treasure for the toughest. The present heroes decide together who takes the card.

**Gold split (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden):** the reward gold is split evenly across the heroes present. An uneven remainder follows DEC-646.

**Level 3 in 1.0 (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden):** the level-3 reward deck is gold only. No card is drawn for a level-3 win. This does not say what any other level's deck holds.

**One hero (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden):** with one hero present, the card goes to that hero. No one picks. With two or more, the group still decides.
