Playtest PT-13 (2026-11-12): UI-037, UI-038, UI-013 and UI-045 sat in no plan, and the gear's "Use a potion or item" (Bob approved 2026-10-31) did the use button's job.

- **UI-037 stays.** A virtue that fires at turn start is automatic and shows on the board. The iPad needs a press only when the virtue asks a choice. The choice list is built generic and tested with Infused (DEC-406, asks for a building). Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (Q7.1); the 1.0 deck list is confirmed by DEC-934 (Gamewright, 2026-11-15): the 8 pool tiles, and Infused is the only turn-start choice.
- **UI-038 is retired.** The gear is the single route for exceptions (Bob's gear rule); in battle the round picker already offers Spend and Drink.
- **Build.** UI-037, UI-013 and UI-045 join the C3 `Builds:`. UI-013 opens from "Use a potion or item"; "Current party" keeps HP, gold and the item count (Q7.3).
- **Host action.** The use-item host action is built with the items panel, behind the gear item "Use a potion or item". Bob ruled this on 2026-11-14 (Q7.2). The host split goes with DEC-926.
- **Game of chance (UI-045).** With no tower the spin shows its result at once on the board and the coin sound plays on the board (DEC-201, DEC-903). With the tower the chase command is sent only on the press (Q7.4).

Supersedes the Oct 28 button of DEC-479 (Bob, accepted 2026-10-30) for the use button only.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
