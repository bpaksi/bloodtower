- **Joins:** the companion's own ally sound, SND-009 to SND-018, with LFX-003 Breathe on the doorway facing its hero's kingdom. A story's own companion uses its story slot, or no tower sound.
- **Walks out:** no sound, the doorway light fades (LFX-001).
- **Turns:** SND-096 Tower Gloat 1, with LSQ-008 Gloat 01.
- **Loyalty step:** no tower cue; the board line is enough.

Without the tower these drop out (DEC-305).
