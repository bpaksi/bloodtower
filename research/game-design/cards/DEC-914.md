Hammer's status check of the project audit (items 1b, "rules that hit a win rate") asked that DEC-821, 822, 824 and 826 be treated as provisional.

- **What they are:** rules and numbers Bob picked from the scripted party's win rates in P-006 (a party that never splits, takes the nearest quest, and spends).
- **Provisional means:** they stay in force and are built as written. The first human 1.0 playtest (the audit's P01, and Bob's Nov 16 tower run) is their check. P01 notes whether each one changed what the table did.
- **How they change:** only with Bob, by a new card that refines the old one. The probe's numbers are evidence about a script, not about tables. Nobody retunes them from a probe alone (DEC-817).
- **Each card** gets one line saying so. This card adds no other rule.

Also in the same class, not edited here: DEC-825 (the adversary's pace) and DEC-827 (the quest's pay), which carry the same note in their notes of origin.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
