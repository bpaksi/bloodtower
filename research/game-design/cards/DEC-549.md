- **Not allowed:** the adversary's boss battle (DEC-356) and every battle inside the Tower (DEC-358). The climax is always fought.
- **Allowed:** any other battle, a disaster or a lieutenant included.
- **Reward:** the full reward. Four potions is already a high price.
- **When:** as the holder's round action, after the foe's card is revealed (MEC-038 .7). The holder adds no fight number that round.
- **Count:** 4 potions, as in RtDT.

In 1.0 there are no potions (DEC-502), so Inventive is out of the 1.0 pool (DEC-581).

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
