Bob picked "Every length × party size". The batch has 12 cells: Short, Standard and Long, each with 1, 2, 3 and 4 heroes. The seed picks each hero's class, so casters and no-caster parties both turn up. Each cell aims for about 80% at normal on its own, so no length or party size ends up the hard one by accident.

Not picked: only the overall average at 80%; tuning P-005's Long game with 4 heroes and trusting the rest; and tuning the weakest party (solo, no caster) to 80%.
