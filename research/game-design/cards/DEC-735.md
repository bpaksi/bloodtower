Bob picked "Story suspects, seed picks" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session. DEC-076's open draw and DEC-182's written clues could not both hold: a clue written for one NPC would point at the wrong person if the draw named another.

- **The story** names 2 to 4 suspects and writes clues and a trick for each.
- **The seed** picks the real traitor each game. Clues against the others become false leads.
- **Default NPCs** are never drawn unless a story names one as a suspect.

The mystery changes between games, at the cost of more writing.

Not picked: a story naming one fixed traitor (no mystery on a replay), and any NPC with engine-made clues (generic, and it loses written clues).
