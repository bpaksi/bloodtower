C3/15 and C4/13 carry "the freed-skull picks come after Set the table's Done" as answered by Bob (PT-21, 2026-11-14, adopted), with Gamewright to confirm. DEC-929 said the same (QA.3).

- **Confirmed.** Order: the stamped card, the close Scroll, the next chapter's card and opening; then Set the table with its rows (the lit seal, the freed skulls count, the quest tokens) and one Done; then the freed-skull picks. The glyph and boon Reveal stays right after the seal break (QA.4).
- **Bob's older lock.** DEC-409 (Bob, 2026-10-28) asked "right after the break". Bob's own PT-21 adoption of 2026-11-14 is the later word, so this reverses no Bob lock.
- **Pick order.** One skull at a time, in the order the break freed them. For each: a kingdom, then a building (DEC-409, DEC-092), on the acting iPad. It is a choice of its own, never a row. The counts the building menu shows are read again after each skull (as DEC-919).
- **Loss check per landing (DEC-905).** After each skull lands, the loss check runs. If the fourth building is destroyed, or the supply is 0, DEC-676's stop runs at once: the skulls not yet picked are never asked, and the loss scene plays.
- **Amends DEC-905's won-chapter clause.** DEC-905 said the won cue, closing hook and stamp do not play when a freed skull loses the game. Under DEC-929 the picks come after those beats, so they have already played. What holds: the game is lost; the next chapter's quest tokens and first day never start; the record says lost.
- **Without the tower** there is no seal break and no freed skulls (DEC-306). Nothing here runs.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-15, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
