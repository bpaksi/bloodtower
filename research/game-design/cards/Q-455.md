Hammer's status check (item 1b): the tower is never load-bearing. DEC-305 skips every tower node without it, and battle uses the tower only for cues. One option offered: make the tower the visible draw in a battle round, as the purse wheel does.

**Parked with P-008 (bloodtower #475, parked to 1.1).** No 1.0 rule changes here. DEC-305 and DEC-306 stand: a game must be winnable without the tower.
- **1.0:** the tower already does two real things, the skull clock and the held-skull seal breaks. The only 1.0 addition on the table is the paper playtest's PT-29, a small days-left cue at 5, 3 and 1 days, which is polish and unrelated to this question.
- **1.1:** a tower-decided battle draw would be a new mechanic with its own balance (a tower cell in P-008, PT-05). It is Bob's call when 1.1 opens.
