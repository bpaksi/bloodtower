**Clash found in the review.** The Portrait, Flooded Room and Lift use bold and on guard, but those are the Tomb's (DEC-492). The Tower only opens the next door (DEC-566). Also, DEC-566's rooms are dealt at random, while the Banquet is always first and the Swap needs a room type it doesn't have.

**Recommendation.**

- **Stances:** the Tower gets none. Each of these rooms' start hook offers a plain-scene choice with the same result (DEC-692), such as ride the Lift or study it first.
- **Fixed rooms:** add an engine setting so a story may name a level's rooms by position. Dealt rooms remain the default.
- **Harmless rooms:** a shrine with no heal, played through a hook.
- **The Swap:** a guard battle with its start and end hooks. The missing hero is an effect the engine must add; this one needs a closer look when the script is written.

**Gamewright, D-02 (2026-11-10):** fixed rooms by position, yes, and no stance. The choice moves out of the hook, so DEC-286 holds. The Portrait and the Lift become traps with DEC-489's choice of two losses. The Flooded Room is a fixed guard with `foe-first` (DEC-848), and the Swap uses the `vanish` start hook. The layout is DEC-851.
