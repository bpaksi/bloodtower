CU/16 brief (bloodtower). Anvil found that after Save and quit, or after a host restart, Continue brings back the saved game's holds and its saved list of joined iPads. Those name iPads that are gone, and no drop ever fires for them. Every hero still reads as held, so a new iPad's Pick is refused and play never pauses for the missing seats.

- **Ruling:** when Continue loads a saved game into the running session (after Save and quit, or after a host restart), the joined iPads are the ones joined now, not the saved list. A saved hold stays only if its iPad is joined at that moment. Every other hold is cleared.
- **Kept as saved:** the party (which heroes are seated, in which groups), gold, items, hit points, the chapter, the day and its clock, quests, positions, skulls, buildings. Only who holds whom changes.
- **Then:** if any seated hero has no holder, the game is in DEC-154's pause and the iPads joined now pick them (DEC-953).
- **Not this rule:** the Pick screen restored after a restart before the game starts (the lobby resume). There, the saved holds stay as they are so the iPad that picked can rejoin and keep its hero (approved W1 test, C2/04 EV-17, CU/08 EV-10).

Reason: the saved holds point at iPads that may not be at the table any more. Keeping a hold whose iPad is joined matches Play again (DEC-708) and keeps C2/04 EV-12 ("still the new holder after a host restart and Continue") passing. Clearing the rest frees every hero a gone iPad held.

Not picked: clear every saved hold on Continue (the suggested default). It makes every iPad pick again, and it breaks C2/04 EV-12, an approved eval. Keep every saved hold and wait for drops (the old iPad ids never drop, which is the bug).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-20, under Bob's delegation. Bob confirms with the CU/16 eval approval.
