- **Pan:** one slow sweep left to right takes 20 seconds, then it sweeps back, easing at each end.
- **Picked corridor:** when the group picks a doorway, the pan eases to that section within a second and holds there until the room resolves.
- **Template:** each of the three sections is a third of the room's width, plus a 10% feathered overlap at every inner edge. The horizon and floor lines sit at fixed heights. A corridor opening sits inside its section's middle 60%, so it never falls on a seam. Every corridor and wall painting of a style is painted to this template, so any mix fits (DEC-585).
