Bob chose the recommendation, and added: "cards in BT are 'selected' by the engine. So this would be a feature built into the UI."

- **RtDT:** spend a treasure to gain the top treasure.
- **Bloodtower:** once per chapter, outside battle, the hero holding Lucky may give back a treasure they hold. The engine names the new treasure from its seeded draw (DEC-067, DEC-370), and the player swaps the physical cards.
- **UI:** the player never draws from the deck. Using Lucky is an action on the iPad, so a hero needs a place to use a held card (Q-290).

Rejected: no limit on how often, and drawing two treasures to keep one.
