**Clue log.** The iPad's Look up gains a Clues page: one row per suspect the story names (DEC-735), each with the clues heard about them and where. A clue checked at a sanctuary is marked true or false (DEC-183, DEC-166); an unchecked one shows a question mark.

**Accuse.** At a suspect's building the building menu (UI-043) shows Accuse once at least one clue points at them. The iPad asks for a confirm that names the stake: a right accusation unmasks and pays the story's reward, a wrong one loses that NPC for the chapter (DEC-184).

**Board.** A right accusation plays the unmasking on a Reveal. A wrong one shows the NPC leaving. A shop NPC who leaves is replaced by a nameless hand (DEC-805), and the menu keeps its buy pick.
