Bob chose "From any Tower-adjacent space".

- **Way in:** a group on a space that borders the Tower may enter the Tower encounter. The board map lists six: Tower Scar Desert (N), Forest of Shades and Bleak Wastes (E), Irontops and The Throne (S), The Grass Sea (W).
- **Cost:** 1 move, like a building action (DEC-078).
- **Story gates:** a story may close some sides, or keep the Tower shut until a condition is met, such as keys as in 1981 or a lieutenant's death (DEC-362).

Rejected: only the finale chapter's kingdom, and a story-named list of spaces with no engine rule.
