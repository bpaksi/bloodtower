When a doorway is picked, the tower plays a door cue (a sound and a light). Then it plays a cue for the room: a quiet one for nothing, a sharp one for a trap, the battle's own cues for a battle room, and a cue of its own for the guardian room. The table hears what is behind the door before reading it. The drums never turn, and nothing physical happens on its own (CLAUDE.md, Tower safety). Without the tower the cues are skipped (DEC-305). Not picked: lights that step down a level as the party goes deeper; cues in battle rooms only.
