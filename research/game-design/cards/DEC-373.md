Bob: "I want to separate from RTDT classes and go more traditional class. I say the virtues are a pools and randomly get distributed at game time. Each class should have 2 intrinsic virtues similar to the RTDT ones."

- **Classes:** traditional roles (fighter, healer, magic user and so on, as in OPT-009), not RtDT's Warlord, Scion, Hunter and Spymaster. The pool stays at six heroes (DEC-099).
- **Intrinsic virtues:** each class has 2 of its own, always on, like the 2 printed on an RtDT hero board. They live in the app; they are not tiles. Which two each class has is Q-248.
- **Virtue pool:** RtDT's 12 hero virtue tiles are not tied to any class. They form one pool, and the app deals them at random at game time (seeded, DEC-067). How and when they are dealt is Q-272. The 4 kingdom tiles stay out of the pool; a story may grant one (DEC-381).

This replaces DEC-156's six fixed virtues per class. Rejected: RtDT's 4 heroes as the 4 classes (2 board virtues + 3 tiles + 1 kingdom tile = 6), 6 classes with mixed tile and app-only virtues, tiles shared between classes' lists, and virtues back in the app only.
