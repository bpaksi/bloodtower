Audit finding CB-04 (decision D3). DEC-553: "the first spell cast in the battle does nothing". DEC-655: flat cuts first, never by tap order or seat. With Lightning (4) and Magic Missile (2) in one round, neither tells which is first.

- **Order at MEC-038 .8:** (1) the Ward cancels one spell, if it has not yet; (2) the surviving flat cuts apply (DEC-655); (3) any halving applies (the Titan's Colossus, DEC-553), to each surviving spell.
- **Which:** of the spells cast in the round it first meets, the one with the smallest cut. If two have the same cut the result is the same; the app names the first in seat order on the caption only.
- **A cancelled spell is cast and used up.** A once-a-battle spell (Lightning, Fireball, DEC-801) is spent. The Ward costs the group that spell.
- **The Ward is used once.** It does not come back each round, and a spell cast in a later round meets nothing.
- **Example:** Lightning and Magic Missile in the same round: the Missile is cancelled, the foe takes 4, not 2. Magic Missile alone in round 1 draws the Ward, and Lightning in round 2 lands in full. Lead with the small spell is a table skill.

Not picked: cancel the strongest (a Ward that punishes casting two spells at once); the table picks (the same result, with one more prompt); tap order or seat order (DEC-655 refuses them).
