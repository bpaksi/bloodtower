Bob: "At the start of the turn, during the groups move, the button should display "available" status. When selected, dialog popups with all available "effects" that can be applied. Player chooses, "infused", dialog should all buildings in kingdom with skull count and allows the player to select which building to remove skull from. If the player doesn't execute, then it is lost."

- **Available:** from the group's turn start (MEC-035 .11) through every move (.3), the use button (UI-038) shows that something is available.
- **Pick:** the dialog lists every effect the group can apply now. The player chooses Infused, and the dialog lists every building in the group's current kingdom with its skull count. The player picks one, and 1 skull comes off it (DEC-406).
- **Lost:** if the player never uses it, Infused does nothing that day. It is gone when the group's turn ends. The engine never picks a building.
- **Kingdom:** the kingdom the group stands in when the player uses it, so a group may walk into another kingdom first.

Rejected: the engine picks the building with the most skulls and the player may change it, and the engine always picks.
