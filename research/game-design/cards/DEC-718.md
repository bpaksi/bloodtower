Bob picked "Move both to 1.1" in the 2026-10-31 open-questions session, reviewing the corruption rulings.

- **Why:** Suspicious (DEC-467) limits potions and Weak (DEC-469) limits treasures. Both are cut from 1.0, so in 1.0 these two cards would do nothing.
- **1.0:** both cards leave the deck at setup; the corruption deck has the other 10. With at most 4 heroes and 2 corruptions each before a loss, 10 cards still never run out (DEC-365).
- **1.1:** both return with potions and treasures, as written.

Not picked: new 1.0 effects for the two cards; keeping them as draws that only cost the -1 move.
