D4 brief (bloodtower #504). DEC-910 covers a building that falls while it holds a quest marker. A later chapter's quest can name a building that fell in an earlier chapter, before its marker was ever placed.

- **Ruling:** the marker is placed on its space as usual, at the chapter's enter (and the "Set the table" quest tokens row, DEC-929). The space is then a place to Enter with only the quest, then Leave, on its menu, exactly as DEC-910 says.
- **Reason:** DEC-613 keeps a 1.0 quest on its one site and DEC-088 says a required quest is never lost. It is DEC-910's case, reached in a different order; it needs no destination and no new screen.
- **Nothing moves:** no other site is chosen, and the table is not asked.
- **The fallen-buildings count** is unchanged (DEC-915).

Not picked: skip the quest (breaks DEC-088); move it to a standing building (needs the destination rule DEC-910 parked with moving quests, DEC-777).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
