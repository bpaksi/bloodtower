Region names, as Bob gave them on 2026-10-25:

- **The Great Woods** (W). The west outer forest belt near Yellowpike, Akartus and Delmsmire on the photo.
- **Regal Run** (S). A mountain range over S The Throne, S The Cloister and S Archmont.
- **Long Water** (N), spelled that way by Bob on 2026-10-25 (board-map.md still says Longwater). The lake across N Dayside and N Fivepint.

The spaces each region covers are still to be entered in the neighbour entry tool (IDEA-032). Every space must end in exactly one region (DEC-242). Whether the kingdoms are a region level is open in Q-149.
