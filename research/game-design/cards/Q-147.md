Names known so far, from the board map's region labels (board-map.md, "Region labels"):

- **Regal Run**: a mountain range over S The Throne, S The Cloister and S Archmont.
- **The Great Woods**: the west outer forest belt near Yellowpike, Akartus and Delmsmire. The spaces it covers are not yet listed.
- **Longwater**: the lake across N Dayside and N Fivepint.

Bob will name the missing regions, and enter the spaces each one covers in the neighbour entry tool (IDEA-032). Every space must end in exactly one region (DEC-242).
