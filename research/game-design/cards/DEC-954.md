Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden. A silent seat, or an absent seat, in the finale vote gets a "Vote for <hero>" offer. It follows Pick for (DEC-928, PT-20).

- **Where it shows.** On the other iPads of the acting group, sat-out heroes' iPads included, and not on the silent seat's own iPad. The wait is the same 30 seconds, the tuning placeholder on DEC-928.
- **Solo.** If that seat is the only hero, the offer shows on the Story board, as Pick for does (DEC-928).
- **It never votes by itself.** A press casts that hero's vote. This ruling does not name which of the two options the press selects.

Not picked here: an automatic vote, and a different wait from Pick for.
