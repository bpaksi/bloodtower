Bob chose "Fit to distance". The app picks a lead's location a few spaces from the group that heard it. It sets the window from the distance and an average move roll, plus one turn of slack, so every lead can be reached. Distance, slack and the average roll are numbers for later. Rejected: one fixed window for every lead, and far leads that pay more.
