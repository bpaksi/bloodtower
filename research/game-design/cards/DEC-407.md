Bob chose "As written".

- **RtDT:** +1 Wild Advantage per skull-free building on or next to your space.
- **Bloodtower:** in a battle, the hero holding Anointed has +1 Wild Advantage (DEC-324) for each building on or next to the group's space that still stands and has no skull (DEC-371). The app counts them, and the board marks the buildings that count.
- **No cap:** it rewards fighting near buildings the group has kept clean, and pairs with Cleanse (DEC-377) and Infused (DEC-406).

Rejected: a cap of +2, and counting every skull-free building in the kingdom.
