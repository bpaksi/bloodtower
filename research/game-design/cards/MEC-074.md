- **Owner:** engine; a story may rename goods and set what a place sells. No entry points (DEC-140); the pick's trigger hook sits in the encounter (MEC-036 .9).
- **Enter:** .1: called for 1 move from the encounter (MEC-036 .13, DEC-078) or for a trade offer met in the wild (MEC-072 .23). The board shows the goods, their prices and the purse.
- **Body:** .2 to .16, one buy or sell per pass until Leave; the move covers the whole trip (DEC-300).
- **Exit:** .15, Leave.
- **Close:** none.
- **Result up:** what was bought and sold.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). Art: the sub-location's painting (DEC-150).

Paid services and goods: the citadel's rally and potions (DEC-159, DEC-161), the sanctuary's corruption removal (DEC-170), the bazaar's trade, the fence and the fortune teller's reading (DEC-165, DEC-167, DEC-299), and selling a cursed item back (DEC-168). At a merchant a player rolls the physical haggle die for each item (DEC-091). Money is split across the heroes present (DEC-178). The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277).

```railroad
enter
  .1  board   The goods and services, each with its price, and the purse   DEC-159 DEC-176
body
  repeat
    .2  prompt  Buy (an item and the hero it's for), Sell, or Leave   DEC-159 DEC-168
    .3  check   Buy, Sell, or Leave?                         DEC-159
      track buy
        .4  check   At a merchant?                           DEC-091
          track yes
            .5  board   Roll the haggle die                  DEC-091
            .6  prompt  The face rolled                      DEC-091
            .7  change  Price down by the face               DEC-091
          track no
        .8  check   Can the purse pay?                       DEC-176
          track yes
            .9  change  Pay, split across the heroes present; the hero gets it   DEC-178
            .10 board   What was bought                      DEC-159
          track no
            .11 board   Not enough money                     DEC-176
      track sell
        .12 board   What the heroes can sell here, and the price   DEC-168
        .13 prompt  An item, or Back                         DEC-168
        .16 check   An item, or Back?                        DEC-168
          track item
            .14 change  The item goes; money to the purse    DEC-168 DEC-178
          track back
      track leave
    .15 check   Left? No: again                              DEC-159
```
