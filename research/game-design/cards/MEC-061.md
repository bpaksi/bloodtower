**What:** Named items the story adds (keys, relics, holy water). Set pieces give and check them; each picks one engine effect kind, a size and a timing.

**When:** For anything the plot needs the heroes to find, carry or spend, and for the win's keys.

**Why:** An item makes progress something the table holds; a set piece that checks for it ties the plot together.

**Pack:** `items` (§7) with effect kind, size and timing (§0.2).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | what the win needs | what the win needs | what the win needs |
| Target | 4 | 6 | 8 |
