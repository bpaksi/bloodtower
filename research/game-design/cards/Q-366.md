Found by the first probe at the Tower's first guard battle. Lightning and Fireball are once per battle or dungeon (DEC-379), and DEC-652 resets them on each tomb visit. The Tower is a crawl through rooms (DEC-364, DEC-566), but no card says whether it counts.
