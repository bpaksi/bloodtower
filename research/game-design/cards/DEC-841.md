Bob chose "Asset contract v3".

- The avatar's `manifest.json` gains an optional `chin`: the chin's height from the top of the portrait, as a fraction 0 to 1. Every v2 folder is a valid v3 folder. The player ignores the field.
- Bob sets it in the studio by dragging a line on the portrait. AI mode proposes one, and it stays a draft until Bob approves the asset.
- A mood still with no mouths (DEC-719) carries the same `chin` in its export index entry.
- asset-studio only records it. Bloodtower's lint checks it against the caption band (DEC-207, DEC-576), and fails a Portrait that has none.

Rejected: keeping the chin in Bloodtower's index only, and measuring it from the anchor box.
