Bob: "Drop that idea, lights are used with sounds for battle feels. flash during battle move, breath between attacks."

The lights carry feel, not information. Each battle move (a round resolving, a spell, the last attack on a run) flashes with its sound from CUE-010; between attacks, while heroes pick actions, the lights breathe. The odds read (DEC-312) stays on the Story board only. Rejected: lights that show the odds read, and lights that count the horde. Claude first offered coloured lights; the LEDs are single-colour, so that was never possible.
