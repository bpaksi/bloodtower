Bob, G-C1 playtest: "Sounds should be playing all the time. From the root the sound menu works and I hear the sounds but I don't hear sounds anywhere else."

- **Where:** the Mac plays the ambient bed with the Story board (R-DEV-6) on every screen: the landing page, setup, play, every pause and the session screen (SCR-021). It stops at Goodbye (SCR-020).
- **No second gesture:** the first click or key on the landing page unlocks audio (SCR-003). Going from the landing page to the Story board does not stop the bed, and it does not need another gesture.
- **Levels:** the music, ambient and voice levels set on the landing page apply everywhere. Music and voice play over the bed.
- **iPads:** play nothing (DEC-231).
- **Which bed:** until a story or a screen names its own, the landing page's bed plays throughout (DEC-500).

Accepted by Bob, 2026-10-31: "looks good".
