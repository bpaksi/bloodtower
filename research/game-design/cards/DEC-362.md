Bob: "I would think that if a story wanted this they could block access to the Tower on the death of the lieutenant."

- Beating or ignoring a lieutenant does not make the villain weaker or stronger (DEC-361).
- A story may make a lieutenant's death a gate: the Tower encounter (MEC-076) stays shut until that lieutenant is beaten. How a group reaches the Tower is Q-263.
- A lieutenant's other rewards and penalties land during play, as the story sets them (DEC-074).

Rejected: lieutenants as the one exception to DEC-361, and story hooks inside the Tower encounter as the only effect.
