Bob chose "As written".

- **RtDT:** a spent potion's number doubles.
- **Bloodtower:** when the hero holding Crafty spends a potion, its number doubles: a heal of 3 heals 6, and +2 moves becomes +4. The app applies it and the board shows the doubled number (DEC-370).
- **Whose potion:** any potion the holder spends, whoever it helps.

Where a hero spends a potion on the iPad is Q-290. Rejected: doubling only potions spent on the holder, and keeping a spent potion once per chapter instead.
