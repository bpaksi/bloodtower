Bob: "Set pieces are story driven locations where events can happen?" The cards so far make a quest a scene (trigger, steps, outcome) and a quest marker the place a story marks. **Proposal:** keep both words: the quest marker is where, the quest is what plays there, and its steps can include events (a battle, a plague).
