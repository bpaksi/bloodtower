Bob: "Set pieces are story driven locations where events can happen?" The cards so far make a set piece a scene (trigger, steps, outcome) and a story point the place a story marks. **Proposal:** keep both words: the story point is where, the set piece is what plays there, and its steps can include events (a fight, a plague).
