A quest with a window carries a small day count on its board marker: "3 days". Main quests never show one.

- **A lead** (DEC-525): when an NPC gives the tip, the board places the side quest's marker at the spot the app picked (DEC-180), on the quest's one movement option (DEC-020), with its window.
- **An adversary quest** (DEC-799): the same marker in the adversary's colour.
- **Last day:** the count turns red.
- **Missed:** the marker fades, and a line on the day's effects panel (UI-022) names what happened. A missed adversary quest names its penalty ("2 skulls on Ostvale") and the skulls drop. Either way it closes, and its chapter goes on.
