Bob: "Effects should be divided into 2 patterns, turn and battle. Battle are presented before every battle. Fortune's Favor is a battle effect. I believe battle effects apply to every battle, so if during a turn 2 battles take place the effect is available to both unless it is associated to a consumable in which case when executed the consumable is used."

- **Two patterns:** every held card and virtue effect is either a turn effect or a battle effect.
- **Turn effects** are offered at the group's turn start and through its moves (DEC-481 moments 1 and 2, DEC-482).
- **Battle effects** are offered on the use button before every battle (MEC-038, at its start).
- **Kept effects:** a battle effect from something that is not spent, such as a virtue or gear, is available in every battle, so two battles in one turn each get it.
- **Spent cards:** a battle effect from a card that is spent, such as a potion or a scroll, is used up when the player applies it. It lasts for that battle only.
- **Fortune's Favor** is a battle effect. Its +1 Wild lasts the battle it is spent in. It is not offered at turn start, which trims DEC-411 for this potion.

Rejected: until the next move roll, like Relentless (DEC-391), and the rest of the turn.

Still open: whether battle effects are offered only before the battle, or also as a round action, as DEC-324 and DEC-481 moment 3 have it now (Q-301).
