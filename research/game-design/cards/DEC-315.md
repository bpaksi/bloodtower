Bob chose "Short: 2 to 4 rounds". A game has many battles and other groups wait, so each one is quick and each round's choice matters, close to 1981's brigand fights. "Even foe" means a foe whose level fits the chapter (DEC-185) against the group that meets it. Bob tunes OPT-010's foe count and round cut to this with the simulator. Rejected: 4 to 6 rounds, and lengths that vary by foe.
