Bob picked "Evens out the shares".

- **A gain:** after the equal split (DEC-178), each leftover coin goes to a hero holding the least.
- **A cost:** each leftover coin is paid by a hero holding the most.
- **Ties** go by seat order.
- The pooled total on the board (DEC-176) is never changed by the rounding.

Not picked: rotating the odd coin by seat; losing it on a gain and rounding up a cost.
