Bob picked "Evens out the shares". Amended by Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden: the whole remainder goes to one hero. The old "each leftover coin" pass is retired.

- **Equal split first.** The amount is divided by the heroes in the split. Each gets the whole-gold share. What is left is the remainder.
- **One hero takes all of it.** On a gain, the poorest hero. On a loss, the richest hero. "Poorest" and "richest" are read after the equal shares. If more than one gold remains, all of it goes to that one hero. There is no second pass that hands one gold to the next hero.
- **A tie.** If two or more heroes hold the same gold, so they tie for poorest or richest, the remainder goes to the first of them in seat order. That is the same tie-break as DEC-556. Bob did not specify a tie-break; this default is recorded so a test can name the hero.
- **Seat order** is the order of the start assignments. The first hero in that list among those tied takes the remainder. A hero with no start assignment comes after every hero who has one, in the order the play already lists them.
- The pooled total on the board (DEC-176) is never changed by the rounding.

A loss of 5 from heroes holding 7, 7 and 4, in that seat order, leaves them at 4, 6 and 3: shares of 1, then both leftover gold from the first of the two richest. A gain of 5 to heroes holding 3 and 0 leaves them at 5 and 3: shares of 2, then the 1 to the poorer.

Not picked: rotating the odd coin by seat; losing it on a gain and rounding up a cost; handing leftover coins out one each.
