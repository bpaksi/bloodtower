- **Chance:** 1 in 3 per retreat or fall, seeded (DEC-011). The same at every difficulty, so difficulty stays one knob (DEC-185).
- **A step:** the foe's level rises by 1. Its count, card and odds follow from the level.
- **Lethal:** the fourth and last step. A lethal foe does not step again.
- **Waiting:** a foe nobody fights does not grow. The skull drop and the adversary already press a group that stalls.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
