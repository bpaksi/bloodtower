Bob's idea (IDEA-064), as Claude proposed it and Bob picked "Bold strikes first":

- **Bold:** in a battle room the heroes get a free first strike before round 1. A trap hits in full.
- **On guard:** a trap's loss is cut. In a battle room the foe gets a free first strike before round 1.
- A nothing room plays the same either way.

So the acting group's pick after each room (DEC-490) is a doorway and a stance, or Leave. Every doorway becomes a small gamble on what is behind it. The guardian room (DEC-491) is a battle room, so the stance applies there too. Not picked: on guard as the only stance with an effect; no stance, with matching Advantages acting as an automatic on guard.
