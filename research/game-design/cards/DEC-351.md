Bob: "I believe Opening, Chapter & Villain is all the Story responsibility. Outcome of a base game piece should just be a still with text."

The engine itself never plays a Portrait or a Painting. The game's opening, a chapter's start and the villain's lines are story hooks (DEC-285): a story fills them with its own tools, or leaves them empty. When the base game shows the outcome of one of its own pieces (a game won or lost, a battle, a quest), it shows a still with text and no voice or motion (DEC-347). This closes DEC-340's "few places": there are none. Rejected: the engine voicing the opening, the outcome, chapter starts or its own villain.
