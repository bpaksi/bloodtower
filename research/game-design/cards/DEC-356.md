Bob chose "Last chapter, boss battle".

- **The last chapter** is the finale chapter (DEC-041). Its set pieces are played like any chapter's.
- **Unlock:** finishing them unlocks the villain. Before that, the villain only strikes and moves on (DEC-053).
- **The fight** runs in the normal battle loop (MEC-038), with the villain as a boss foe. Its beats get their own board and tower content through the battle's beat slots (DEC-327).
- Like RtDT: finish the main goal, then beat the adversary.

Rejected: the villain's lair as a set piece of its own, and a boss battle wherever a group meets the hunting villain with no fixed place.
