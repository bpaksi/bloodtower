Bob chose "Last chapter, boss battle".

- **The last chapter** is the finale chapter (DEC-041). Its quests are played like any chapter's.
- **Unlock:** finishing them unlocks the adversary. Before that, the adversary only strikes and moves on (DEC-053).
- **The battle** runs in the normal battle loop (MEC-038), with the adversary as a boss foe. Its beats get their own board and tower content through the battle's beat slots (DEC-327).
- Like RtDT: finish the main goal, then beat the adversary.

Rejected: the adversary's lair as a quest of its own, and a boss battle wherever a group meets the hunting adversary with no fixed place.

**Changed by DEC-645 (Bob, 2026-10-30):** the story now names what unlocks the Tower: a chapter opening, a chapter's quests done, a named quest done, or a story item held. In 1.0 the adversary waits in the Tower and does not strike or move (DEC-644).
