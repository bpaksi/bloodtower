Bob picked "Claude scripts, Bob rules".

- **The walk:** Claude plays one whole 1.0 game turn by turn, using only what the cards say, with seeded dice and draws. It follows the loop diagrams, game (MEC-032) down to battle (MEC-038), plus MEC-072 to MEC-076.
- **The record:** a probe doc in `research/game-design/probes/`, which cites the card behind every step.
- **A gap:** where no card says what happens, or two cards disagree, the walk stops. Bob gets one question, and his answer becomes a `Q-` and a `DEC-` in AREA-096. Then the walk goes on.

Not picked: Bob playing live with Claude as the engine; one short pass per loop card.
