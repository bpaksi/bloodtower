Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** Carry at most 2 treasures.
- **Bloodtower:** The holder's treasure limit is 2, not 4 (DEC-424). When the corruption arrives with more, the iPad asks which to give back. Turquoise Urn (DEC-463) adds its 2 on top. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).
