Audit finding QD-05 (decision D2). DEC-606 pays what a hero can and adds a corruption; DEC-119 gives a hero at 0 a corruption and a seat out; DEC-714's floor of 1 belongs to plain scenes. Nothing said what an exact zero is, whether a shortfall also falls, or when a fallen hero comes back in a Tomb that has no battle to sit out.

- **The loss** is the trap's number after on guard halves it (DEC-607) and Advantages soften it (DEC-493): the Pit takes 4 from one hero, the group's pick (shown with each hero's hit points); Falling Rocks take 2 from each.
- **A hero whose hit points are at or below the loss falls:** one corruption (DEC-119, DEC-496) and 1 hit point, at once. Exact zero and a shortfall are the same case. Never two corruptions, never a hero left at 0.
- **No sit-out in the Tomb.** Sitting out is a battle rule. The fallen hero stands at 1 hit point and plays the next room.
- **Every hero in the group falls** (all present heroes were at or below the loss): the Tomb result is failed, the quest fails (DEC-642), the group leaves the Tomb to its board space. No gold is lost: DEC-177's loss is set by a foe's level, and a trap has none. The corruptions stay.
- **A third corruption** from the trap loses the game at once (DEC-676), before anything else resolves.
- **Companions (1.1):** DEC-800's would-fall swap applies to a trap fall as to a battle fall. Nothing to design here; it waits for companions.
- **Not a plain scene.** DEC-714's floor of 1 with no corruption stays for plain scenes only.

Examples, Pit of 4: a hero at 5 goes to 1, no corruption; a hero at 4 goes to 1 with one corruption; a hero at 3 goes to 1 with one corruption. On guard, Pit of 2: a hero at 2 falls the same way.

Not picked: a hero left at 0 who sits out to the Tomb's end (a seat at the table with nothing to do); corruption for the shortfall and another for the zero; a floor of 1 with no corruption (DEC-606 is Bob's).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
