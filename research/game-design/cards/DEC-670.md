Bob picked "Yes, free at night". Probe P-002 found the gap on day 1, in an event battle at day close.
- **When:** a battle called from the day's close, an event's foes or a disaster (MEC-072 .17).
- **Retreat:** offered after any round, with no move needed, since every group's moves are gone by then (MEC-035 .9).
- **Cost:** the foe's last attack, the next card landing in full (DEC-650). The group falls back to the space it came from, or stays at a building (DEC-313). The foe stands as usual (DEC-314).
- **In a group's turn:** DEC-313 is unchanged. A retreat needs a move left and spends it.
- MEC-038 .3 and .33 now say so.

Not picked: retreat costs a move off the next day's roll; no retreat at night; retreat at night with no fall-back.
