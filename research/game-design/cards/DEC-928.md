Playtest PT-20 (2026-11-12): one silent seat stalled a four-hero battle.

- **Ruling.** The waiting line names the hero (UI-031). After 30 seconds, any iPad of the acting group, sat-out heroes' iPads included, shows "Pick for <hero>", only for standing heroes who have not picked.
- **The pick** is Guard if the hero can Guard, otherwise a normal attack (DEC-129). The offer never picks automatically: Q-C4-11 (a), no round timer, stands.
- **Round pick only.** Fight or Retreat is first press wins already. The clock restarts at 0 on Continue.
- **30 seconds** is a tuning placeholder; the key name is left to the PR. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (QA.6, QA.7).
- The gear's Change who holds whom stays the permanent fix.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
