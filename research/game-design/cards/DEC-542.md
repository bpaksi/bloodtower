Bob chose "Any slot". While a chapter is active, its config may override any slot the story or base fills, settings (clock length, encounter odds) as well as hooks. So a story can make a later chapter harsher with no extra mechanism. Rejected: hooks only, and a schema list of the settings a chapter may override.
