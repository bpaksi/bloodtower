Battle behaviour folded into traps, as Bob asked (IDEA-064). The Tomb is an Undead dungeon, so the group's held Undead and Wild Advantages count, from gear and virtues (DEC-068). The board shows the count beside the map, like RtDT's "1 Advantage Applied".

- **Battle rooms**, the guardian's included, use Advantages as any battle does (DEC-324).
- **Traps:** each automatic Advantage cuts the trap's loss a little. The group may spend one conditional Advantage to soften the trap one step, as softening a foe card. This stacks with entering on guard (DEC-492).

How much a cut and a step are goes with the battle's softening step (Q-214) and the on-guard cut (Q-312). Not picked: Advantages in battles only; a spent Advantage that cancels a trap outright.
