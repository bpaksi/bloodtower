Bob asked Claude to rule the 17 quest items and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** Spend: +5 Wild in a dungeon.
- **Bloodtower:** Offered in a battle round inside a quest or a Tower room (DEC-359). Spending it adds 5 Wild Advantage that round, like the Scrolls (DEC-431). The hero who spends it adds no fight number that round (DEC-484).

DEC-484 (Bob, 2026-10-28) replaced "and the holder still fights": every held card spent in a battle, Tomas's Map by name, costs the spender that round's fight number. Probe P-003 found the drift. Trimmed under DEC-352.
