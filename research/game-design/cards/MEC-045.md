**What:** A chapter's beat that plays at a day's start, on a day number or when a story flag is set: it narrates and places or moves story tokens.

**When:** When the world should move without the heroes: the villain acts, a new story point opens, a rumour comes true.

**Why:** Without beats the board only changes when the heroes act; a beat makes the clock felt.

**Pack:** the chapter's beats (§3.1), conditions (§0.1).

| Count | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Target | 1 per chapter | 1 per chapter | 1 per chapter |
