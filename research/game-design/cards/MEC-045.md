**What:** A story hook on one of the engine's five triggers (DEC-290): arrive on a space, enter a place, pick from a menu, battle ends, quest ends. It may require a story flag or lead, and plays once unless marked every time (DEC-291). It holds moments and story effects only (DEC-285, DEC-286).

**When:** When the board should answer what the heroes do: a hint on a quest's spot, a rumour when they enter the inn, a token placed after a battle.

**Why:** Without beats the story only speaks at chapter and quest starts and ends; trigger beats make the places the heroes go feel part of the plot. The world still moves on its own at each day's end (DEC-086).

**Pack:** the chapter's beats (§3.1), conditions (§0.1).
