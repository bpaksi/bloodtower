Bob chose "Park glyphs for 1.1".

In 1.0 one seal still breaks at each chapter win (DEC-303), so the tower opens and holds back fewer skulls, but no glyph has an effect. The glyph rulings made today (DEC-597 to DEC-600, MEC-016) stand and ship in 1.1. Their open questions (Q-339 to Q-342, and Q-340's drum turns) are parked until the 1.1 pass, as DEC-580 does for other 1.1 content. A 1.0 story has no glyph slot to fill.

Rejected: shipping the boosts in 1.0 without drum turns, and shipping all of it with drum turns.
