Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 25, entering the Tower with 1 move left.
- **Retired (Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden):** the no-move exception, and the Tower free exit that was bundled with TP-01. Retreat costs the same everywhere, including a retreat between Tower rooms. It is offered only with a move left, and the step back spends 1 move (DEC-313, DEC-316). A retreat with no move left is not offered.
- **Still:** the foe's last attack lands, and a guard may cover it (DEC-680). The group leaves the Tower and stands on the Tower-adjacent space it entered from (MEC-076 .19).
- **Kept:** the cleared rooms, the level reached and the adversary's wounds (DEC-360). A standing guard keeps its count, with no board token (DEC-651).
- The Tomb's retreat and a retreat at day close still need no move (DEC-497, DEC-670). This ruling does not change them.

Not picked: a retreat that stays in the Tower and only leaves the room.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
