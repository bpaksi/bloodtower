Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 25, entering the Tower with 1 move left.
- **The rule:** inside the Tower, Retreat is always offered (MEC-038 .3), with no move. The foe's last attack lands, and a guard may cover it (DEC-680). The group leaves the Tower and stands on the Tower-adjacent space it entered from (MEC-076 .19).
- **Kept:** the cleared rooms, the level reached and the adversary's wounds (DEC-360). A standing guard keeps its count, with no board token (DEC-651).
- **Why:** the Tower is a dungeon in every other rule (DEC-657). A group that spent its moves walking in can't be locked into a stair guard it can't beat.
- DEC-360's "a move left" stays true outside dungeons.

Not picked: a move needed, as DEC-360 reads; a retreat that stays in the Tower and only leaves the room.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review ("move any proposed items to accepted and we will review in v1 game play"). To be checked in the v1 playtest.
