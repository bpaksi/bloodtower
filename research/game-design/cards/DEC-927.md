Playtest PT-19 (2026-11-12): a mis-tap on a 60-space board cost a move, and on a trigger space it fired a story beat.

- **Back** on the move map reverts the last tap until a trigger plays. A trigger is any arrival that starts an encounter, event, foe or quest scene. Enter and End turn also close the window. Spending the last move does not (Back gives it back). Each press undoes one tap, back to the turn start or the last trigger. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (Q5.1, Q5.2).
- **Fix my position** is a gear item under Game board setup: any space, charges nothing, fires nothing, logged, with a line on the board. It works on the move map or between loops only, never in a battle, scene or building menu, with no limit (Q5.3).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
