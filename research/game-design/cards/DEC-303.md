Bob: "I like your recommendation, and it feels like RTDT."

Seals in place do not stop skulls; they hold some back, and a held skull may come out on a later drop with others. So a seal is a dam. One seal breaks at each chapter win, in the chapter's close (MEC-033). The break is mixed news: the glyph behind it is revealed, and the story makes it a boon (DEC-302), but the tower is more open, so later drops run freer and held skulls may pour out. The tower gets harsher as the story goes on, matching the challenge that rises each chapter (DEC-133). How much a seal holds back is Q-032's tower test.

Bob raised three problems with seals covering the glyphs; they are Q-192. Rejected: seals as a reward the heroes win back, and waiting for the tower test.
