- **Limit:** 4 tiles per hero, on top of the 2 intrinsic virtues (RtDT heroes end with at most 4 unlocked).
- **A fifth:** as with treasures (DEC-424), the iPad asks which of the five to give back, the new one included. The returned tile goes back to the pool (DEC-374).
- **Price:** a citadel draw costs 10 gold, the same every time. About five won level-1 battles or two and a half level-2 ones (DEC-547).
- **No trades:** heroes do not swap tiles. The give-back is the only way to shed one.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
