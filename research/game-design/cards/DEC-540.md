Bob chose "Yes, every loop". The day, the group turn, the encounter and the battle start and end with a hook slot, like the game, chapters and set pieces. The base fills the inner ones with its defaults (for example a dawn still, or a battle's end text, DEC-340), and a story or chapter may replace or silence them (DEC-537). The trigger nodes (DEC-288, DEC-290) stay as extra hooks inside the loops. Rejected: inner hooks the base alone may fill, and keeping DEC-140.
