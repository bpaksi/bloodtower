While a revealed glyph faces a kingdom, it boosts its named action there (DEC-597, DEC-598). A drum turn (DEC-006) moves the boost with it. A story may reskin or replace the boost through its slot.

**Reopened 2026-10-29 by DEC-598.** It was parked on 2026-10-27, when glyphs became story-only boons and the RtDT rule ("that action costs 1 spirit there") lost its spirit (DEC-308). The spirit cost stays gone.
