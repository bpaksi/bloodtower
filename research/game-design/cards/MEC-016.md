Base rule kept: while a glyph faces a kingdom, that action there costs 1 spirit. A story's own glyph effects cost story items, story tracks or corruption, never warriors or spirit (DEC-003). Since drums may now turn in play (DEC-006), a story can also turn a glyph to a kingdom (Dracula: holy water turns Cleanse to that parish).
