Bob chose "Offered at its moments" for Q-290.

- **Rule:** the engine knows what each hero holds and when each card can be used (DEC-370). It offers a card on the acting group's iPad only at those moments, as one more button beside the usual choices.
- **Moments:** a card usable today is offered in the turn-start step (UI-037), for example Lucky's swap. A card used in battle is offered as a round action (DEC-324), for example a potion or Inventive. A card that answers something is offered right when that happens, for example Blessed when a round's hit lands.
- **Nothing to hunt for:** the Menu's Look up › Items (UI-033) only shows what is held. It has no Use button, and the iPad has no held-cards row.

Rejected: a Use button in Menu › Items, a held-cards row always on screen, and moments plus the Menu.
