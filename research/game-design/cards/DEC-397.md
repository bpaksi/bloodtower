Bob chose "Win a battle outright".

- **RtDT:** spend 4 potions to remove a foe on your space.
- **Bloodtower:** during a battle (MEC-038), the hero holding Inventive may spend 4 potions. The battle ends at once as a win, against a fresh foe or a standing one (DEC-320). The potions go back as usual (DEC-370).

Still open (Q-292): whether a boss battle (DEC-356) or the Tower (DEC-358) can be won this way, whether such a win pays the full reward, when in a round it may be used, and the potion count for the numbers pass. Rejected: clearing only a standing foe outside battle, and cutting a foe one step per potion.
