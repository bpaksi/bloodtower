The hero panel (UI-030) gains a companion row: the companion's portrait, its name with the printed card's name beside a story name (DEC-723), its one effect in a line, and its loyalty step as one of three marks: devoted, steady or wavering (DEC-720). Devoted shows the effect's raised number (DEC-774).

- **Where it shows:** on the Story board's hero panel and the iPad's Look up. A companion helps only where its hero is present (DEC-066), so it is dimmed on the idle group card (UI-029) when its hero is elsewhere.
- **Wavering** is drawn in the warning colour, so the risk is visible before the next drop (DEC-722).
- **The 10 defaults** (DEC-725 to DEC-734) each show their effect line from the card's ruling.
