Bob picked "A last choice in the Tower".

- **The choice:** when The Warden falls in the Tower, the heroes choose one of two things. They can break what is sealed under the keep for good (Q-372). Or they can swear his oath and guard it in his place.
- **Two win endings:** each choice plays its own closing Scroll line. The second one echoes The Warden's fall: the heroes take up the oath that turned him.
- **The loss:** one Scroll line, as in DEC-636.
- **Fit for 1.0:** it is one choice with two outcomes, the same shape as a plain scene (MEC-077), so it needs nothing that 1.0 cuts. Where the engine plays it is Q-375.

Not picked: a bookend at the village (the herald rings the bells); a plain victory with one win line.
