Bob chose "Sized for one sitting".

- **One sitting:** stories are sized to finish in one session (DEC-047), and there is no Save button or save slot (DEC-208).
- **Continue:** the running autosave is offered until a new game replaces it, whether the table comes back the same night or another evening. Continue runs the board and tower check (MEC-018, DEC-283).
- Resuming another evening is allowed, not designed for: no feature is built just to support it.

This replaces DEC-047's "there is no save and resume". Rejected: Continue as same-night crash recovery only, and making multi-evening play a feature.
