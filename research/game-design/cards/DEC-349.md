Bob weighed three options: NPCs break the voice rule (OPT-022), "silent for generic text, use dialog for one off dialog" (OPT-023), and voice the first time, silent after (OPT-024). He worried that voice "limits the text and we couldn't do text substitution" of names. He chose OPT-023 with Claude's addition.

- **The rule:** a line is voiced only when its text is fixed and plays once. Voice is made ahead of play from fixed text (DEC-337), so a line with names filled in at the table stays silent.
- **Generic NPC lines** (a building NPC's greeting and offers, repeated each visit) are silent. The board shows the NPC's still with the line as a caption, and the acting iPads show the same line as text next to the picks (DEC-201 allows text).
- **One-off dialog** a story writes for a moment plays as a voiced Portrait.

This changes MEC-073, where the talk pick showed the NPC's talking avatar.
