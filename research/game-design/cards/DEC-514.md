Bob: "For the design they are Foe. A foe has a name (Brigand) and what a group is called (Horde)."

- **Foe** is the design word for any enemy type, as in RtDT. Rules say "the foe's level", "the foe's count".
- A foe has a name (singular and plural: Brigand, Brigands), a group noun (band, pack, horde, swarm…), a level and traits (DEC-068).
- A battle is against one foe with a count: "a pack of 8 Shadow Wolves". Won rounds shrink the count (DEC-013). At a count of 1 the screen shows only the name: "a Dragon".
- A story can add its own foes with their own name and group noun (Dracula: "a swarm of 20 Rats").
