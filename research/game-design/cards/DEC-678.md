Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 1, at *The Ambush*.
- **Foe count:** +1 per hero present in the battle after the first. A lone Fighter at *The Ambush* faces Brigands at count 4, not 5. The foe panel (MEC-038 .2) shows the count for the heroes there.
- **Skull drops:** read the party, as DEC-564 says. Splitting doesn't slow the tower.
- **The solo row:** +1 fight number and +2 full hit points are only for a party of one. Full hit points are a setup number, and a split group can join again the next morning (DEC-640).
- **Why:** a split group fights with fewer hands, so its foe should be smaller. The skull clock stays the price of splitting.

Not picked: everything reads the party (a split group faces a foe sized for heroes who aren't there); everything reads the group (splitting would also earn the solo bonus).
