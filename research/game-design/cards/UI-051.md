A lieutenant has a token on the Story board's map with his portrait and name.

- **Guard** (DEC-737): a shield mark, and he never moves.
- **Hunter:** a footprint mark, and at the day's end the board draws his one-space step toward the nearest group.
- **Tap on the iPad's Look up:** his name, level (the chapter's foe level +1, DEC-775), his trait and the quest his fall completes (DEC-798), even if that quest's chapter hasn't started.
- **Battle:** the foe panel (UI-026) shows his portrait instead of the foe type's, with "Lieutenant" above the name.
- **His fall:** the battle reward (UI-040) adds the quest's reward and a line naming the quest it closes.
