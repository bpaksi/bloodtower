Bob chose "Token on board + map detail". When a battle ends with the horde standing (a run or a fall), the app prompts the players to put a foe token (AST-B05) on that space. The Story board map marks the space too; a tap shows the foe, its count left and its status (DEC-314). Every group can see it before moving there, so softening a foe for another group (DEC-320) is a choice the table can plan. When a group finishes the horde, the app prompts the token off. Rejected: the Story board map only, and nothing shown until a group arrives.
