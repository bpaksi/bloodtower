| Cards | What |
|---|---|
| 4 base | Claw (the level to one hero) ×2, Sweep (half the level, rounded up, to everyone), Howl (regrow 2) |
| 2 trait | one per trait, with the trait's effect (DEC-372, DEC-559) on top of a Claw |
| 1 big | Rend: twice the level to one hero, RtDT's critical hit |
| 1 weak | Falter: nothing |

- **Level** sets the sizes, not the mix, so one deck shape covers every foe and story foe (DEC-326).
- **A status step** (DEC-555) takes out Falter and puts in a second Rend.
- **Help:** no card helps the heroes beyond Falter.
- **Trait effects** fire only when the trait card is the revealed card and the round is lost (Q-271).

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.
