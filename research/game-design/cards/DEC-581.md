The four tiles use potions or treasures, which DEC-502 cuts from 1.0 (punch list item 5). In 1.0 the citadel draws from the other 8 hero tiles. Their rulings (DEC-387, DEC-393, DEC-395, DEC-397) stand for 1.1.

Proposed by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Bob chose "Out of the 1.0 pool" the same day. Rejected: 1.0 stand-ins on gold or story items for all four, and gold versions of Callous and Lucky only. Both would mean 1.0-only rulings to design now and redo in 1.1.
