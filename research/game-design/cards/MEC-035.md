- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140); the arrive trigger hook (.6) is one the active chapter may fill (DEC-288).
- **Enter:** .1, the whose-turn banner names the group (UI-019).
- **Body:** .2 to .8, one step per pass: walk (1 move a space, one tap for the space where the minis stop, DEC-035), or Enter the place the group stands on (MEC-036), which is free; each pick inside costs 1 move (DEC-078).
- **Exit:** .8, no moves left or the group ends its turn.
- **Close:** .9, moves left are lost (DEC-149). Events wait for the end of the day (DEC-080).
- **Result up:** where the group stopped: in the wild or at a place. The day's Close uses it for events.
- **Screen, UI, art, sound:** on each node's home card (DEC-277). No art of its own; a pass is quiet so walking stays quick.

The first ID on each line is the node's home card (DEC-277). The arrive hook fires only on the space where the group stops, never on spaces it walks through. Enter shows only on a building, caravan or story token (DEC-089). Pause, a dropped iPad and a lost tower use the interrupt diagram (MEC-071).
