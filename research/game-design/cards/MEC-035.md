- **Owner:** engine. No entry points (DEC-140).
- **Enter:** the whose-turn banner names the group (UI-019). A group standing on a set piece's spot hears its beat (DEC-141).
- **Body:** one pass is one step: walk to a space (1 move a space, tapped on the move map, DEC-035), or enter the encounter at a building or caravan (1 move a pick, DEC-078).
- **Exit:** no moves left, or the group ends its turn; moves left are lost (DEC-149).
- **Close:** none. Events wait for the end of the day (DEC-080).
- **Result up:** where the group stopped: in the wild or at a place. The day's Close uses it for events.
- **Screen:** the Story board (SCR-014) and the group's iPads (SCR-016).
- **UI:** the whose-turn banner (UI-019), the move map (UI-023).
- **Art:** none of its own.
- **Sound:** none by default; a pass is quiet so walking stays quick.
