Bob picked "He enters at the reveal". The adversary's start is a setting on its slot. By default it roams from the first day's close (DEC-775).

**The Ruined Keep:** The Warden's token first appears when The Barrow is done. Until then the threat is the raiders, and the reveal lands harder because he then walks onto the board.

**Check in the probes:** a game that skips ahead can't start him late, because The Barrow is on every length's spine.
