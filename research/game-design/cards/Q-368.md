Bob does not want to rush Dracula, so for the 1.0 release Proving Grounds grows from a bare test story into a real one. It still tests the game, and it also gives players something whole to play from start to end. Bob offered three old D&D starter modules to draw from: T1 The Village of Hommlet, U1 The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh, and N1 Against the Cult of the Reptile God.
