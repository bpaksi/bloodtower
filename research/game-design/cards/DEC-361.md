Bob chose "No escalation".

- The villain's strength in the final battle is set by the story and the setup difficulty (DEC-059), not by what the heroes left undone.
- Fallen buildings, standing hordes and failed villain quests keep their own costs during play (DEC-044, DEC-075, DEC-320); they do not add to the villain.
- MEC-012 is parked.

Rejected: escalation both ways (failures stronger, beaten lieutenants weaker), and leaving it to each story.
