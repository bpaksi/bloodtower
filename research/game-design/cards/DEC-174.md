Bob chose "Moves for hit points". At the tavern a group spends as many of its moves as it likes on rest, and each move heals a set amount. The group decides how long to stay. Resting costs no money, so the citadel's paid rally (DEC-158) is the quick way to heal. How much one move heals is a number for later. Rejected: a full or half heal that ends the turn, and a full heal that costs the next turn.
