Bob chose "Nugget inside, named by source". "Nugget" is the design word for one piece of story information. Players see it by where it came from: a rumour (village, may be false), lore (sanctuary, always true) or a prophecy (fortune teller). A nugget may carry a clue, which unlocks an NPC's action (DEC-182), or a lead, which opens a side quest (DEC-525). Rejected: dropping "nugget".

Recorded by Bob on 2026-10-23 in the design session on branch `chore/design-session-2026-10-22d`, which never merged; ported 2026-10-28 with a new ID. The renames were not applied to the other cards, which still use the old words: Q-328 asks whether to apply them.
