Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** Spend at most 4 Advantages per turn.
- **Bloodtower:** In each battle round, at most 4 of the holder's Advantages count toward their strength (DEC-324). The app picks the 4 that help most. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).
