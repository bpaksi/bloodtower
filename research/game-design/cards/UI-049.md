At a village tavern the building menu (UI-043) shows a Hire pick when the chapter's seeded companion is on offer (DEC-724). It opens the buy pick (MEC-074) with one line: the companion's portrait, name, effect line and price (20 gold, DEC-774).

- **Who takes it:** the iPad asks which present hero it joins. A hero who already holds one is greyed out with the reason.
- **Board:** the companion shows on a Reveal (UI-036) as it joins, with its cue (CUE-013).
- **Gone:** once hired, or when the chapter ends, the pick leaves that tavern's menu.
