Bob chose "World truth vs your fate". The sanctuary's lore and prophecy are true facts about the world, its foes and the adversary, such as a weakness or where a quest lies. The fortune teller reads the party's own near future, such as what its next encounter or event holds. She is paid in money and can be cryptic or double-edged. Rejected: the same hints at a different price, an unreliable seer, and omens instead of hints.
