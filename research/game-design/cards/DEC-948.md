EE-01 brief (F1/02, audit finding EE-01). DEC-821 (Bob, provisional under DEC-914): when a quest pick resolves, won, retreated or fell, the group's turn ends. On main the iPad menu skips the Quest row, and a plain-scene quest has two ways out: it is done, or the group leaves it partway (DEC-151). The quest scene's own screens are C3-14's (#437) and are not built.

- **The row.** While the group's space holds an open quest marker (the current chapter's, not done), the menu's first row is "Quest" (the label the building menu already uses), with the cost line "1 move". With 0 moves it is greyed with "No moves left.". No other words, no badge.
- **The pick** takes 1 move (MEC-036 .8) and opens the quest at once (DEC-084). Until C3-14 draws its screens, the iPad shows the quest's options as the menu's buttons, with the pack's own labels; a hero pick uses C3-14's approved lines "Who takes the corruption?" and "Who takes the reward?"; and a "Leave" button leaves the quest. No new copy.
- **Resolves** means either way out: the quest is done (won), or the group leaves it partway (a retreat; its progress stays, DEC-151). A fall in a battle quest is C4's and calls the same turn end.
- **Then the turn ends at once** (DEC-821, DEC-149): moves left go to 0 and the turn-end losses run once (DEC-472, DEC-473). The iPad is back on the building menu, every pick greyed, Leave open (as C3-14's EV-09 expects), and a done quest's row is gone.
- **A third corruption** in the quest still loses the game at once (DEC-676); nothing after it runs.

Reason: DEC-821 says "won, retreated or fell"; leaving a plain scene partway is its retreat. Returning to the menu with the turn spent is how Uncertain's turn end already looks (DEC-468), so the table learns one picture.

Not picked: leaving partway does not end the turn (a group could open a quest, look, and keep walking; DEC-821's "retreated" says it ends); the encounter closes by itself after the quest (C3-14's EV-09 returns to the menu).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
