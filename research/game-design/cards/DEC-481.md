Bob chose "Five fixed moments".

The use button (UI-038) is offered only at these five moments. Its dialog lists what the group can use right then. Nothing waits for it (DEC-479).

1. **Turn start** (MEC-035 .11): day-long cards and virtues, and every potion except Siren's Song and Fortune's Favor (DEC-408, DEC-411, DEC-483).
2. **The move map, between moves** (MEC-035 .3): cards that move a group or a horde, such as One Thousand Strides, Kamaria's Carpet and Horn of the Elements. Also cards that act on a standing horde on the group's space: Lamp of Darkness, Golden Mace of Azkol and Amulet of Annihilation.
3. **Before each battle round** (MEC-038 .3, DEC-484): battle potions, scrolls, Inventive, Fulminating Silver and Tomas's Map. Spending one costs no round action.
4. **A battle's end** (MEC-038 .20, a won battle): Amulet of Hope's treasure instead of the money.
5. **The day's end** (MEC-034, after the event checks at .10 and before the skull drop at .35): Circlet of Conviction, offered on its holder's group's iPad.

**Armed cards** such as Blessed are not tied to a moment. They fire on their own trigger (DEC-480).

**Automatic effects** that need no choice, such as gear, passive treasures and corruptions, just apply. The board shows them where they happen.

Rejected: the button open for the whole turn and battle, which would undo DEC-411's "not between moves", and only three moments, with movers used at turn start.
