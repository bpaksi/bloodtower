Candidates: a straight cut (the POC's visual-to-visual rule), a short crossfade, or portraits that face and slide from left and right. Each speaker change counts toward the flash limit of 3 brightness jumps a second, so short lines need a minimum hold. Open too: does the engine fix one transition, or may a story pick one per line?
