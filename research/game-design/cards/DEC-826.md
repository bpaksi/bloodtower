Bob picked "Half the drops in Long". The supply stays 24 (DEC-776). In a Long game, count the nights on which the party's own schedule drops (DEC-564, DEC-822, DEC-824). Every second one of them has no drop. A night with a won quest has no drop and isn't counted (DEC-699).

Script, normal, with DEC-821 to DEC-825 in play:

| Heroes | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long, before | 57% | 58% | 63% | 85% |
| Long, half the drops | 74% | 87% | 96% | 96% |
| Days to win | 41 | 37 | 27 | 23 |

**Left under the floor:** a solo Long game, at 74%, is a little under DEC-823's 80%. It is also the longest game, at about 41 days.

Not picked: the same with a solo Long hero dropping on only every third of its nights; and skipping every third drop night (69, 78, 84 and 93% in the first sweep).
