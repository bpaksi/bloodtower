- **Deck:** the adversary has its own 8-card deck, built like a foe's (DEC-557) with two Rends and no Falter.
- **Phases:** at two thirds and one third of its wounds left it changes phase: a new painting, one more Rend in the deck, and its ability grows.
- **Tower:** arrival and each phase change play a full light and sound show in the tower slot (DEC-327).
- **Music:** its own track in 1.1 (DEC-328); 1.0 has no music.
- **False death:** optional per story (IDEA-063): at 0 wounds it rises once with a third of its wounds.
- **No shortcut:** Inventive cannot end it (DEC-549).
