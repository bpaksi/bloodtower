Bob chose "Yes, a Painting input", after asking whether the glow layer is the technique behind the landing painting's flickering windows. It is: UI-005's mask over the painting, glowing or flickering slowly (1–2 Hz with jitter, never a strobe), with a steady glow under reduced motion. A Painting takes one more optional input, a glow mask (windows, a fire, an adversary's eyes), next to its camera move (DEC-337). Base-game stills may use it too, since it adds no time (DEC-340). The motion still stops being a tool of its own. The tools are now Portrait, Painting, Scroll and Reveal. Whether the mask is an asset of its own stays with Q-103. Rejected: keeping the motion still as a separate tool, and keeping the flicker on the landing painting only.

Changed 2026-10-27 by DEC-347: the glow layer is for a story's Paintings only, not base-game stills.
