- **Gold (1.0):** 2 × the foe's level. A level-3 foe pays 6.
- **Bigger foes:** a disaster (DEC-028) or a lieutenant (DEC-074, 1.1) pays double gold. In 1.1 it also draws its card one pile up.
- **Piles (1.1, DEC-502):** levels 1 to 3 draw a potion, 4 to 6 gear, 7 and up a treasure (DEC-120).
- **No gold** for a retreat or a fall.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.
