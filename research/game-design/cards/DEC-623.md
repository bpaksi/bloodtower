The BT adapter writes `asset-job.json` from the pack (DEC-385). These are the top-level fields. The exact schema is fixed in asset-studio's contract doc when its code is planned.

- `schema` (a version number), `story` (the pack ID), `source` (a hash of the pack files it was made from, so a stale job is caught).
- `cast[]`: `id`, `voice` {provider, voice, style}, `portraits[]` {mood, prompt or source}, the 8 mouth frames and the anchor, and `refs[]` (reference sheets).
- `lines[]`: `id` (DEC-622), `speaker`, `text`, `mood`. Only lines that are voiced (DEC-349).
- `pictures[]`: `id`, `kind` (painting, portrait, foe, place, item, cover), `sizes` (`@dashboard`, `@ipad`), `prompt` or `source`, plus `camera` and `glowMask` for a Painting (DEC-343).
- `sounds[]`: `id`, `kind` (effect or ambience), `prompt` or `source`. There is no music in 1.0.
- Every entry has `status`, draft or final (R-AST-ORG-7). The adapter carries it over from the index.
