Playtest PT-18 (2026-11-12): the daily "Stay together, or split up?" prompt ran for every table, about 25 to 40 taps a game, against Bob's rule that the happy path has no exceptions.

- **Ruling.** Split party and Rejoin party are picked from the gear at any time in the day and take effect at the next day start, before the roll.
- **No prompt.** The groups card (no button) shows for 4 seconds only on a day when a queued change applied; nothing on other days, never a question. Bob, 2026-11-14, adopted (Q4.1); the playtest text said the card shows every day, Gamewright confirmed it on 2026-11-15 (DEC-932).
- **The queue stacks**, applied in the order picked. An opposite pick for the same hero cancels the queued one. The gear panel shows the queue. An action that cannot apply is dropped with a logged error (Q4.2).
- **Split** takes the hero the table picks from the gear panel, among heroes in groups of 2 or more (Q4.3). The 4 seconds is the existing auto-advance constant (Q4.4).
- **Host action.** The "host split" is built with this (Bob, 2026-11-14, Q7.2).

Supersedes the daily Regroup step of MEC-034 .38 to .42 and restores Bob's gear rule.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-12, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
