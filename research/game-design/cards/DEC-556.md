- **Size:** a seeded draw (DEC-011) between a quarter and a half of the normal hit, rounded up, at least 1. It varies, as Bob asked (DEC-319).
- **Falling:** a guard can drop to 0 and take a corruption. The guard still bleeds. MEC-013 still stops a game loss from one random effect.
- **Two guards:** the guard with more hit points left takes it; on a tie, the first in seat order. No split.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.
