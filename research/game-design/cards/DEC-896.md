Question from C3 feature 08: how do several holders, Tempted and the free cure combine?

**Price of one buy** (a rally, a virtue draw, a paid cure; Cleanse and rest have no gold price), in this order:
1. **Free cure.** The first cure a hero takes in a chapter costs 0 (DEC-700). A price of 0 stays 0: no later step touches it, and Tempted adds nothing.
2. **Base price.** From tuning. A cure costs the base price when the hero holds one corruption and the one risen price when they hold two (DEC-170, DEC-552). The risen amount is the held second-cure patch, not set here.
3. **Haggle die**, where there is a merchant (1.1).
4. **Silver Tongue**, a quarter off, rounded down, at least 1 off, on village and bazaar prices only (DEC-545). It never cuts the surcharge.
5. **Tempted +2**, added last and once per buy, however many heroes in the group hold Tempted. A buy has one price.
6. **Pay.** The whole pool must cover the total. The heroes present pay it in equal shares, and the heroes not there make up any shortfall (DEC-911, DEC-178). A buy the pool cannot cover is shown disabled.

**Penalties and triggers**
- **Per holder.** Cruel, Greedy, Feral and Lost each fire once per holder on their own trigger, and take gold from that holder's own part of the purse (DEC-176). Two Cruel holders each lose 2. A loss takes what the holder has and stops at 0; it never takes from another hero.
- **Order.** Within one pick: the buy is paid, then pick-triggered losses (Cruel, Greedy) fire in claim order, then Uncertain ends the turn, then turn-end losses (Feral, Lost) fire. They stack: an Uncertain holder who picks at a building also ends the turn on a building's space, so Feral fires too.
- **The move roll** takes the most corrupted hero's count, not the sum (DEC-684). Feeble drops that hero's helping virtue only.
- **Curing Tempted** is a buy, so a Tempted holder pays the +2 on it unless the cure is free. The corruption leaves after the payment.
