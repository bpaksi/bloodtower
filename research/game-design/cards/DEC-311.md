Bob chose "One foe ability each". Like an RtDT foe card, every foe type has one ability that always applies, and the foe panel (UI-026) shows it before the group picks Fight, Go around or Pay off. Fights then differ by foe, not only by number. Examples to test: Undead regrow 1 each lost round, Stealth strike before the first round, a Beast hits two heroes. Rejected: level and traits only, and abilities that arrive only on hard or in later chapters (a second difficulty knob, against DEC-185).
