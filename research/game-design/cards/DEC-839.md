Bob chose "Work folder owns it".

- The work folder's `index.json` holds `missing`, `draft`, `final` or `rejected` per asset ID. The studio's Approve and Reject, and nothing else, change it there. Only a person sets `final` (DEC-384).
- The job (`asset-job.json`) says only what to make. It has no `status` field.
- Export writes each shipped file's status into the export index, and Bloodtower's `assets:pack` copies it into `packs/<story>/assets/index.json` (DEC-386, R-AST-ORG-7).
- A lost work folder is restored from the OneDrive backup (DEC-386), not rebuilt from the pack.

Rejected: the job carrying status as a hint for a fresh work folder, and Bloodtower's pack index being the truth.
