Bob: "We need to story to define new ones. i.e. Dracula adds werewolf and vampire." Today a story only reskins an engine type and keeps its level and traits (MEC-058). A new type needs traits, an ability (DEC-311), a card deck (DEC-323) and a stand-in token.
