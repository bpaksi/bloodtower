**Recommendation.** The break picks any remaining seal, and the board says plainly that nothing new shows. The tower still gets harsher, as DEC-303 wants.

**Why:** 5 glyphs against one break per chapter means this is rare. It needs no extra drum move, and the skull pressure keeps its rhythm.

**Not picked:** turning a drum first so a glyph hides, which is a physical move for a rare case; skipping the break, which removes the tower's rising pressure.
