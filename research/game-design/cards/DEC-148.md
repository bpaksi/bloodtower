Bob: "Random to start, oldest to newest after. New parties are added to older so the older place is retained."

- The groups a story starts with get a seeded random order.
- After that, groups play oldest to newest every day.
- A group that splits off is the newest and plays last.
- When groups join, the joined group keeps the older group's place.
