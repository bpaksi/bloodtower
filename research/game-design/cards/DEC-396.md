Bob chose "Next to pack lint in bloodtower". The adapter is a new part of apps/bloodtower beside today's `pack:lint`, and shares its pack reader and lint. From the root it runs as `pnpm assets:needs <story>`, `pnpm assets:render <story>` and `pnpm assets:pack <story>`. It moves to packages/ only once a second app needs it. Rejected: a package in packages/ from the start, and an app of its own.
