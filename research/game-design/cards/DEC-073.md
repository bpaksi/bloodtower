Lieutenants are mini-bosses serving the adversary. The adversary's quests are deadline tasks with a penalty if failed. Traitors and spies hide among the friendly NPCs. Turned companions (DEC-071) join them. Rival bands were not picked; they wait for competitive play (IDEA-018).
