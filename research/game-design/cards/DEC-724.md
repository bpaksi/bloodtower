Bob picked "Tavern hire for gold" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session.

The app offers a seeded companion at the tavern as a pick with a price, through the buy pick (MEC-074). Players can plan for it, and it gives gold a good use. A story can still switch on the other ways (DEC-065): a chapter quest, an encounter offer or a quest reward.

Not picked: a wild encounter offer, both, and no default way.
