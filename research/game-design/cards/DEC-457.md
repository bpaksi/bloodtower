Bob asked Claude to rule the 17 quest items and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** Prevent 1 spirit loss per battle card.
- **Bloodtower:** While held, the holder's part of the purse (DEC-176) loses nothing to a corruption (Cruel, Greedy, Feral, Lost) or to an event. Item-fit.md proposed "a lost round costs no money", but a lost round costs hit points, not gold, in Bloodtower, so the Orb guards gold where gold is lost.
