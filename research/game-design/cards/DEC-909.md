Audit finding CB-05 (decision D3). DEC-567 adds one Rend per phase, DEC-649 keeps a revealed card out until all 8 are used, and DEC-658 fires every crossed phase in order. Where the Rend goes, and what happens to the card on the table, were not said. Only the deck's mutation is ruled here; a boss may still start with more than 8 cards.

- **Three piles:** the draw pile (cards not yet revealed), the discards (revealed this cycle), and the card face-up this round (DEC-649).
- **A phase fires:** one Rend is added to the draw pile, and the draw pile is shuffled with it, seeded, in the battle's own stream. Two phases in one round do this twice, in order (DEC-658).
- **Untouched:** the discards, and the card face-up. A card already revealed this round resolves as it would have (it lands only if the round is lost; DEC-323). The new Rend can be revealed from the next round on.
- **An empty draw pile** at a reveal: every card, the added Rends included, goes back and is shuffled (DEC-649's reshuffle, with more cards). If a phase fires with the pile empty, the new Rend is the pile's only card and the next reveal is that Rend.
- **The cycle** is the cards the deck holds, so after two phases it is 10, not 8. "No repeats until all are used" counts them all.
- **The foe panel** shows the cards left and the Rends left, so players can count (DEC-649).
- **Not a new wound or a heal.** The phases add no wounds (DEC-658).

Fixtures for R04: a seeded one-threshold and two-threshold trace with the pile at 5, 1 and 0 cards when the phase fires; assert the deck size, the Rend count and that the face-up card is the same.

Not picked: the Rend goes on top of the draw pile (the next card is a known Rend); the Rend goes to the discards (the phase does nothing until a reshuffle, up to 8 rounds); a full reshuffle of everything at the phase (the face-up card could come back).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-13, under Bob's delegation (his go via Warden for D1 to D6). Revise in playtest.
