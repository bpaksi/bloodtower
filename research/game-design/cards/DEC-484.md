Bob chose "Before, and each round". He then revised the cost the same day: "This would allow a group to see how the battle is going before spending an item. which is ok, but reading a scroll during battle should cost the reader the melee for that round." Asked which cards, he chose "Every spent card".

- **When:** the use button is live at the Fight or Run step (MEC-038 .3), which comes before round 1 and again before every round. That covers both "before the battle" (DEC-483) and each round.
- **Who spends:** the dialog asks which hero spends the card. The card takes effect at once.
- **The cost:** every held card spent in a battle (a potion, a scroll, Inventive, Fulminating Silver, Tomas's Map) costs the hero who spends it that round's fight number (DEC-129). That hero adds nothing to the party's strength that round, the same cost as softening (DEC-324). A hero who drinks Dragon Teeth heals but does not attack that round.
- **Siren's Song** ends the battle in a run, so its cost does not matter.
- **"This round" cards** such as the scrolls and Tomas's Map apply to the round about to be fought.
- **Kept:** spending a conditional Advantage to soften the foe's card is still a round action (DEC-324). It is not a held card.

Rejected: before the battle only; before the battle plus a card as a round action; a spent card that costs nothing (Bob's first answer, revised); and only scrolls, or scrolls and potions, costing the round.
