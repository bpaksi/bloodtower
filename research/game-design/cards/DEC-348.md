Bob chose "Yes, per story". A story's lint renders every shared face in every mood (DEC-334, DEC-346) under the story's filter preset and runs the avatar limits (DEC-207). A face that fails blocks the story until the story replaces that face with its own or changes its filter. This catches a dark filter swallowing a chin line or the edges before play. Rejected: checking the pool once unfiltered, and a warning that blocks only release builds.
