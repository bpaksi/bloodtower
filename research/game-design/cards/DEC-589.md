Bob wants the guardian room "provided special treatment. This could be used to build tension." He answered: "A threshold scene is optional for the story to provide, otherwise it is a surprise."

- **Threshold scene (a story slot, not required, AREA-093):** when the group picks the guardian's door, play stops at the threshold. The view half fills with the sealed door, the tower builds, and the group gets one last choice: go in, or Leave.
- **No threshold scene:** the guardian room is a surprise. The door opens like any other, and the guardian's own reveal cue (DEC-495) is the first sign.
- **Either way** the party gets no warning on the way there: no signs that grow closer, and its listen is a hint like any other (DEC-586).

Not picked: signs that grow as the party nears the guardian; a listen at the guardian's corridor that never lies.
