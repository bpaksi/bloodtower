Bob's list: "game stats, time start, end, length vs historical best and averages. Total win/loss and Story win/loss." He also accepted four refinements.

- **How it ended:** the win, or the reason for the loss.
- **Time:** the start and end clock times, and the active length, which leaves out pauses (Bloodtower spec D6).
- **Against history:** this game's active length beside the best and the average, for the same story at the same length and difficulty. The best is the fastest **win**.
- **Win/loss:** the total across all games, and for this story.

Every number is computed from the event log (DEC-107). Each hero's record (DEC-106) is still to be specified.
