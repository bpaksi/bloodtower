Bob picked "Grim and plain". The script is low fantasy: oaths, mud and cold iron, with few adjectives. A line is one or two sentences, short enough to read aloud at the table in one breath, as a Scroll line with no voice acting (DEC-636). The example he saw: "The herald's horse came back without him. The seal on his satchel is the Warden's."

This holds for 1.0 and 1.1 alike (DEC-832).

Not picked: heroic adventure with some humour; a folk-tale teller looking back; and dark, eerie horror close to Dracula's.
