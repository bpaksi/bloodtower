- **Core:** the same in every story (instant-loss guard, scene presence).
- **Configurable:** an engine mechanism each story fills with data (encounter tables, chapters).
- **Optional:** an engine module a story may switch on (dungeon crawl, hunter token).
- **Story:** lives in one story pack (Dracula's nights, the escort).
