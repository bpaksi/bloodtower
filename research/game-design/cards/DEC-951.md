F1/04 brief (wild quest Enter). Bob's DEC-641 makes a quest marker on a wild space a place to Enter, with only its quest and Leave. The engine today treats a place as a building, so a few things DEC-641 does not name need a reading.

- **When Enter shows.** While the space holds an open quest marker (the current chapter's, not done), exactly where a building's Enter shows: at the turn's start, after a walk, and on landing with no moves left (the Quest row is then greyed, as at a building). When the quest is done, or its marker is not live, the space is plain wild: no Enter.
- **The words.** The Enter button reads "Enter" (the label the move map already gives a place with no building type). The menu's title is the space's board name (for example "Muted Forest"). The menu is "Quest", then "Leave" (DEC-943's two rows). No Cleanse, no skulls line, no painting. No new copy.
- **Not a building.** Entering it does not add to the game's "Buildings entered" count.
- **Still the wild.** A group that ends its turn there draws a night event as in any wild space (DEC-017).

Reason: each is the reading that needs no new words and changes nothing else; a marker is a place, not a building.

Not picked: a new title or button line for wild markers (new copy for one case); counting the marker as a building entered (the stat's name says buildings); making the space safe from night events (no card says so).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
