Bob chose "Engine drift, story may track". With no camera track, the engine runs a slow default drift, a gentle push-in, so no still sits dead on the board. A story may supply a camera track (UI-003's contract) to aim the move. The track belongs to the moment, not to the picture, so one picture can be reused with different moves. This changes UI-003, where a still with no track does not move. Rejected: a story track or a plain still (the POC), and engine presets only.

Changed 2026-10-27 by DEC-347: the drift runs only in a story's Painting; base-game stills do not move.
