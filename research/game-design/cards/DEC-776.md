**Recommendation.** DEC-703 only covers Short and Standard, and DEC-502 moves Long to 1.1. A Long game has a 50% event chance in the wild and 8 to 10 Tomb rooms (DEC-603). Foes never get stronger with length (DEC-669).

**Skull supply:** still 24, and the simulator run must check it. P-003 found 24 is about a 24-day clock for 1 to 2 heroes, which a Long game may outlast. Raising the clock means fewer drops (for example no drop every third night), because the physical skulls are fixed.

**Note:** Dracula's Long is 14 quests over four chapters (DEC-753), so "5 or more chapters" is a default for new stories and a story may set its own.
