Bob: "Let's go with your recommendation, however, all/every Tower sounds, light is just skipped. Story boards doesn't really change. 1) one required difference would be Skull Drop will need to be a simulation."

Without the tower (chosen at the start, DEC-275, or mid-game, DEC-301), every tower node that plays a sound or lights is skipped; the Story board does not play them instead and does not change. A seeded draw behind a light show, such as the purse spin (DEC-114), still happens, with no show. The skull drop is simulated: the app draws, seeded, where each skull lands. What happens to seal breaks and glyphs without the tower is Q-193. Rejected: the app simulating seals and glyphs on a tower picture, and dropping every tower-only feature.
