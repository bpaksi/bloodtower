Bob picked Scroll and Reveal. A Scroll (UI-035) is words on parchment with an optional narrator voice, and it replaces the text card (UI-006). A Reveal (UI-036) shows an item, foe or reward large with its name for a few seconds, with no lines, so the base game can use it without slowing play (DEC-340). Both take the shared inputs (DEC-337). Bob did not pick the clip (UI-004): each story would need its own video, and it costs too much to make. Bob asked whether the glow layer is the landing painting's window flicker before choosing it (UI-005).
