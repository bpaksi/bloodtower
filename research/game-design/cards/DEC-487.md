Bob sent a screenshot of the RtDT app's dungeon screen as his answer to the picture question (Q-249): "I like this a lot and is fun to play. I want the design to start here." The screen is described in [app-dungeon-screen.md](../../return-to-dark-tower/app-dungeon-screen.md).

The Tomb's picture on the Story board is a top-down floor plan. Rooms appear as they are found, the current room glows, and open doorways mark where the party can go next. The Exit and the Advantage on the current room are always shown. The rest of the review (meter, choices, the round) starts from that screen and changes it only where Bloodtower needs to. Not picked: a side cross-section, or a full-screen room painting with a depth strip.
