Bob chose "Copied into the pack, in git". BT `pack` lints, then copies only shipped files into `packs/<story>/assets/`:

- `avatars/<id>/` in talking-avatar's contract layout (manifest, portrait, anchor, mouths, `lines/*.m4a` and timing).
- Other media as content-hashed WebP and m4a (R-AST-ORG-2).
- `index.json`: ID to file, media kind, size, captions, draft or final (DEC-202, R-AST-ORG-7).

Masters, WAVs and rejected drafts stay in asset-studio's work folder outside git, backed up to OneDrive. Rejected: committing only the index with media fetched at release, and committing everything.
