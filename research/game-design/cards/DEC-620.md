- **The story supplies:** a Painting of the sealed door for the view half and up to 3 narrator lines. Both are optional. A threshold with no Painting uses the engine's default sealed door.
- **No tower show in 1.0:** Tomb tower cues wait for 1.1 (DEC-609). The threshold's tower slot is empty until then.
- **The stance pick moves to the threshold.** At the sealed door the group picks bold, on guard or Leave. That's its last choice, now that it knows what waits. A door it listened down allows on guard or Leave only (DEC-587). A tomb with no threshold scene picks the stance at the doorway, as for any room.
