Bob chose "count becomes wounds". A foe that comes alone, such as a Dragon or a Titan, has a wound count where other foes have a head count. The engine cuts it the same way: a won round (MEC-038 .11) and a spell (.8) take wounds off, and it falls at 0. The foe panel (UI-026) says "wounds left" instead of a head count. Retreating, a standing foe and its status step work the same, with wounds kept. One damage rule covers every foe.

Rejected: hit points for lone foes, a second damage system, and a hidden count the players can't see.
