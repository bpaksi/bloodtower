Found by the first probe in the Barrow. Lightning and Fireball are "cast once per battle or dungeon" (DEC-122, DEC-379), so the Healer's Lightning in room 2 used it up for the guardian room too. A group may leave a tomb and come back (DEC-151, DEC-497), and the card doesn't say whether a new visit gives the spell back. The walk does not depend on it, so it did not stop.
