Bob chose "A rough read". The foe panel (UI-026) shows a word or a meter, such as Grim, Even or Good, never a percent. It changes as each hero picks an action on their iPad (UI-027), so the table can see that a spent Advantage or a spell tips the round. Rejected: the exact percent (too much like a spreadsheet) and hidden odds as in 1981.
