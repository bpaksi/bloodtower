Bob chose "Bard in place of Scholar".

The pool's six classes are OPT-009's roles with one swap: **Fighter, Healer, Magic user, Scout, Ranger, Bard.** The Bard replaces the Scholar and leans on talk and gold, to fit NPC talk (MEC-073) and the bazaar. Hero names stay OPT-009's placeholders; the Bard needs a new one. Each class's two intrinsic virtues are still Q-248.

Rejected: OPT-009's six unchanged, the D&D-style Fighter, Cleric, Wizard, Rogue, Ranger and Paladin, and a Paladin in place of the Scholar.
