CU/03's Q3.1 (Bob, 2026-11-14) left one thing open for Gamewright: whether a no-tower night also stops at the loss.

- **Ruling: yes.** The simulated drop is DEC-905's drop. After each skull lands, the loss check runs: the supply is 0, or 4 buildings anywhere are destroyed (DEC-915). If it is true, DEC-676's stop runs at once. The rest of the night's skulls are not drawn or placed, no further skull line shows, and the loss scene plays.
- **The line that did it still shows.** The skull that tipped the loss lands and its line ("A skull falls on <building>.") shows, then the loss scene.
- **The no-building error (Q3.1) is a guard, not a play path.** Two kingdoms with every building fallen is 8 destroyed, and the game is lost at the fourth (DEC-915). So that case cannot come up in a live game. It stays as built: no skull, one logged error, no fallback. A game that somehow starts a night with 4 or more fallen is lost by DEC-905, not by that error.
- **No change to CU/03's evals.** The stop is built in C3-10 (game end, #435), per DEC-905, with its R03 fixtures. Add fixture (e): a simulated night of 3 skulls where the second destroys the fourth building; the third is never drawn and has no line.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-15, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
