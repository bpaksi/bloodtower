Claude proposed reusing RtDT's 12 tomb rooms as traps, renamed and re-costed for Bloodtower. A trap is a flat loss of hit points, a potion, an item or a corruption, sometimes with a choice between two losses ("lose 4 HP or 1 potion"). The party makes no extra choice in a trap room, so going on or leaving stays the main tension. Bob: "I like your recommendation. I would like different types of traps (picked randomly) each with specific art work."

So the Tomb has a list of trap types. Entering a trap room draws one type at random, and the board shows that trap's own painting with its loss. The RtDT room texts are in [app-dungeon-screen.md](../../return-to-dark-tower/app-dungeon-screen.md#tomb-undead-advantage). Not picked: a trap the group answers with a response and a hidden roll; a trap that wounds one hero with no choice.
