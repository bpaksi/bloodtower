Loremaster (step 45) asked what Duel, Heal, Glyph, Disarm, Track and Lore do in 1.0, for CU/08's hero cards. No card, and no engine code, gives them a rule. They are the `ability` words of `packs/base/pack.yaml` (since C1, #206), written before DEC-125 (no attributes), DEC-373 and DEC-380 (two intrinsic virtues per class) and DEC-378 (Bard for Scholar). The engine only stores the field.

- **Ruling.** The words carry no rule and Loremaster writes no line for them. Each hero card's line (CU/08, `card.line`) shows the class's two intrinsic virtues from DEC-380, in that table's order. The base pack's `ability` value becomes those two names, for example "Shield Wall, Weapon Master". Bob's 2026-11-14 choice stands: the line is still the pack's `ability` field, and Loremaster's optional line per entry (row 7a) is now keyed on a virtue name.
- **What each is, in one line** (amounts DEC-545; all six classes are 1.0, DEC-502):
  - **Duel** (fighter) → Shield Wall takes the foe's hit for the party at reduced damage; Weapon Master is +1 Melee.
  - **Heal** (healer) → Lightning, a holy strike, 4, once a battle; Mend heals 3 to each hero in the group, once a day.
  - **Glyph** (magic user) → Magic Missile cuts the foe 2 every round; Fireball cuts it 4, once a battle. A glyph in Bloodtower is a tower piece (DEC-303), never a hero ability.
  - **Disarm** (scout) → Shadow is +1 Stealth; Slip Past skips one trap or one room's cost, once a quest.
  - **Track** (ranger) → Beast Hunter is +1 Beast; Pathfinder is +1 on the move roll in the wild.
  - **Lore** (scholar) → dropped (OPT-009, DEC-378, DEC-380). The Bard replaces the Scholar: Silver Tongue takes a quarter off prices; Rally is +1 Wild in battle.
- **Follow-up, not design:** a pack PR (Wright's gate) sets the six `ability` values and changes `role: scholar` to `bard`. The pack still carries the attribute fields DEC-125 removed. CU/08's evals use test-pack words ("Duel", "Heal") and are unaffected. Most of these virtues are not built yet; only Silver Tongue is in the engine.

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-15, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
