- **Grim:** under 35% to win the round.
- **Even:** 35% to 65%.
- **Good:** over 65%.

The word is what the table reads aloud; the meter (one lit part of three) is what they see across the room. Five bands would ask players to tell Grim from Bleak, which a rough read should not. For the UI-026 mockup.
