Bob chose "Built from engine parts". A story foe type names:

- its name and art (as a reskin does, MEC-058);
- a level range and its RtDT traits (DEC-068), so Advantages still match;
- one ability from the engine's list (DEC-311, Q-195);
- its card deck, picked from the engine's card pool (DEC-323, Q-213);
- the RtDT token that stands in for it on the board (DEC-321).

Example: Dracula's Werewolf, Beast and Stealth, ability "regrows on a lost round", token Shadow Wolves. New foes, no new rules, so the engine's balance and simulator still cover them. A reskin stays the light option. Rejected: reskins only, and abilities or cards a story writes itself.
