DEC-483 offers battle effects before every battle. DEC-324, DEC-411 and DEC-481 moment 3 also offer battle cards (potions, scrolls, Inventive) as a hero's round action, which costs that hero's fight number for the round. Some cards say "this round" (the scrolls, Tomas's Map) and some react to the battle going badly (Dragon Teeth's heal).
