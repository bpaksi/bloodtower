Bob picked "No repeats until all 8".

- **Start of a battle:** a seeded shuffle of the foe's 8 round cards (DEC-557, DEC-011).
- **Each round:** the next card is revealed (MEC-038 .28) and stays out of the deck.
- **After 8 rounds:** the 8 cards reshuffle.
- Rend comes at most once in 8 rounds, and players can count what is left.

Not picked: a free draw from all 8 every round; one deck per foe type kept across battles.
