DEC-489 sets the shape: trap types drawn at random, each a flat loss with its own painting. This asks for the list itself. The start is RtDT's 12 tomb rooms, each to be renamed and re-costed in hit points, potions, items and corruption. Each painting is an art piece in the game's oil-painting look (DEC-478), tracked as an `ART-` card once that card type lands.
