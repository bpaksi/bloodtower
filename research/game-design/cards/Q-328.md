On 2026-10-23 Bob ruled that Bloodtower uses the original games' names (DEC-509) and settled each rename (DEC-510 to DEC-529): set piece → quest, villain → adversary, money → gold, horde → foe, fight → battle, run → retreat, bounty → reward, story point → quest marker, hunter → hero. That session also swept about 210 cards, the glossary and the crosswalk to the new words, but its branch never merged. The sessions after it kept the old words: on 2026-10-28 the cards say "set piece" 211 times, "money" 167, "horde" 146 and "villain" 128.

Choices: sweep the cards to the new words now, keep the current words and park DEC-509 to DEC-529, or settle each rename again. The rulings were ported as they stood on 2026-10-23 and were not checked against the rulings made since.
