title: "May the chapter layer override settings too (a chapter's own clock length, encounter odds), or only hook slots?"
status: answered
layer: core
links:
  - { rel: part-of, to: AREA-093 }
---
DEC-538 lets the active chapter override hook slots. Settings such as clock length or encounter tables might also differ by chapter, which would make the chapter a full layer over every slot.
