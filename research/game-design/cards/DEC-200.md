Bob chose "Media kind, then use". The kinds: picture, clip, avatar (a portrait plus its 8 mouth frames and anchor), voice line, music, sound effect, tower cue and icon. Every slot (DEC-199) says which kind it takes. The moment kinds of AREA-046 (panning still, motion still, clip, talking avatar, text card) are ways of showing these files, not kinds of their own. Rejected: one asset kind per moment kind, and grouping by game role.
