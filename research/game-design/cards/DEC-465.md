Bob chose "The player picks".

- **Pick:** when a hero pays the sanctuary to remove a corruption (DEC-170), the hero's iPad lists that hero's corruptions, each with its drawback, and the player picks one.
- **Return:** the board names the card, and the player puts it back (DEC-370).

Rejected: the oldest corruption goes first, and a seeded random pick.
