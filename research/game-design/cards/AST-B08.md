**Proposal:** an optional module. A quest marker is a site a story places, moves and removes, and arriving there triggers what is on it, as at a building (MEC-004, IDEA-003). The objective and lure (DEC-001) are two uses a story can pick. The hunter token is a moving foe, not a building (MEC-001).

**2026-10-21:** DEC-001 (objective, lure and hunter) is parked. The quest tokens are only lead markers, placed when an NPC reveals a lead (DEC-087). Objective and lure are no longer live uses.
