Bob picked "Rest at the altar". The script found that the cards gave The Shrine only one option, *Pray at the altar*, which grants the oath-blade (DEC-673). A plain scene needs 2 or 3 options (MEC-077).

- **Pray at the altar:** the oath-blade, given to the hero the group picks (DEC-673).
- **Rest at the altar:** every present hero heals to full hit points. No blade.

It is the same trade as The Herald (DEC-672): help now, or one big edge in a later fight. Either one finishes the quest.

This is a 1.0 rule, so it has no release field. The pack (C3 feature 03) builds it.

Not picked: Take the old offerings (+10 gold, and one hero takes a corruption); and Leave an offering (pay 5 gold for the blade, as in P-001's Proving Grounds).
