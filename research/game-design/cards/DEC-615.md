Bob chose "2d3, floor 2". A group rolls two seeded three-sided dice, 2 to 6 with an average of 4, then DEC-102's modifiers apply, and the total never drops below 2. Doubles come up 1 time in 3 (Relentless, DEC-391). The values live in the tuning file as placeholders until the move-roll simulator and playtests settle them.

Which virtues count as helping movement is set on each virtue's own card (for example DEC-421, Trusted Maps).

Rejected: 2d4 with a floor of 2, and 2d6 with a floor of 3.
