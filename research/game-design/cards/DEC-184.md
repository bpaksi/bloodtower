Bob chose "Right: unmask and reward; wrong: lose the NPC". Accusing costs 1 move. If the party is right, the traitor is unmasked: a battle starts or their trick stops, and the story's reward is paid. If wrong, the innocent NPC leaves that place's menu for the rest of the chapter, and the real traitor stays hidden. Rejected: a corruption for a wrong accusation, and a wrong one that warns the traitor.
