Bob asked Claude to draft all 10 in one pass, for his review.

- **RtDT:** +2 Wild against the adversary. Gain at most 1 corruption when you battle a level 2, 3 or 4 foe.
- **Bloodtower:** The holder has an automatic +2 Wild Advantage in any battle against the adversary.
- **Why:** Keeps the printed Advantage. The corruption limit adds little, since a hero already gains at most one corruption a day. **Playtest:** she may only matter in the finale, so check she is worth hiring.
