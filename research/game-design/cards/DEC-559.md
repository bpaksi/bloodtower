| Trait | Effect on a landed trait card |
|---|---|
| Melee | the target loses a gear card (1.1); in 1.0, 1 more hit point |
| Magic | one extra skull drop at once (the tower's own drop, or simulated without it, DEC-305) |
| Stealth | the target loses a potion card (1.1); in 1.0, 1 more hit point |
| Humanoid | 1 skull on a building, the table picks (DEC-092) |
| Beast | the hit is half again as big, rounded up |
| Undead | the group loses gold equal to the foe's level |

- **Target:** the hero the card hits. A guard (DEC-319) takes the hit and the effect.
- **Held cards:** only an armed card that says so stops an effect (DEC-480).
- Once per landed trait card, never on its own.

Ruled by Claude at Bob's request (2026-10-29: "where possible make a good choice"). Numbers are placeholders for the tuning file (DEC-500, DEC-131) until playtest.

Accepted by Bob in the 2026-10-29 rulings review.
