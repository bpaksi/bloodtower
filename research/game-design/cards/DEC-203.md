The portrait is head and shoulders, facing the viewer, with the mouth clear. It comes with 8 mouth frames at the portrait's exact size and a mouth anchor box inside it (story-pack-format §2.1). The face sits in the top two thirds so the caption goes below the chin. Captions are always on, and a line never plays over a tower sound. Rejected: two speakers on screen, and dropping lip sync.
