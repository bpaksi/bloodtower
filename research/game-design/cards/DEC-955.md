Bob, 2026-11-27, via Warden. The key-moment list gains two kinds, each logged once for the thing it names.

- `km.battle_won`: a battle won.
- `km.tomb_cleared`: the Tomb's guardian beaten.

They sit with the kinds already named for the stats screen (quest done, item gained, building fallen, corruption taken, chapter won). A battle that is retreated or fallen is not `km.battle_won`. A Tomb left or failed is not `km.tomb_cleared`.
