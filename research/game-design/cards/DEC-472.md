Bob asked Claude to rule the 12 corruptions and said he would review the cards. Revise in playtest.

- **RtDT:** End your turn on a building's space: lose 2 spirit.
- **Bloodtower:** When the holder's group's turn ends on a building's space, the holder's part of the purse loses gold. The amount belongs to the numbers pass. This is on top of the -1 move every corruption gives (DEC-365).
