"Let's start with 6, but make it easy to add later."

Each hero is one entry in the engine's hero data: name, role, attribute values, innate abilities, portrait and voice. Adding a hero means adding an entry and its assets; no screen or rule changes. The claim screen lays out any number of heroes, not a fixed 6.
