Built in the bloodtower POC. The board shows a large join QR. Each iPad picks heroes from the engine pool until N are seated (DEC-098). A picked hero greys out on the other devices, and story-suggested heroes are marked. The button is "Pick Hero A", the count is "N of N picked", and the help is "Who this iPad speaks for at the table" (DEC-232). Do not say that only one hero hears the iPad. This is the party frame in the weekend mock stack. The R1 POC still takes the hero list from the story pack, so that changes when this is built.
