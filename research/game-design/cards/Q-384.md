Probe P-003, day 6, in the Barrow. The guardian revealed a Rend, and the Fighter used Shield Wall to guard the Bard. DEC-129 says a hero who doesn't make a normal attack "adds no fight number". DEC-130 says party strength is "the fight numbers of the heroes making a normal attack, plus matching Advantages". Neither says whose Advantages: the Fighter's Weapon Master (+1 Melee against Oreks) might still count while he guards. The oath-blade (+3 against The Warden) raises the same question in the finale.
