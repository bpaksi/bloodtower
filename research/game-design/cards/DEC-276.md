Proposed by Claude, first used in MEC-039. A fenced `railroad` block in the loop card:

- **Sections**, in template order: `enter`, `start`, `body`, `close`, `end`. A section with nothing in it is left out.
- **A node** is one line: its number (`.3`, short for MEC-039.3), its kind, a short label, then the IDs it uses. Kinds: `story`, `roll`, `check`, `change`, `board`, `prompt`, `show`, `tower`, `call` (DEC-268).
- **`repeat`** indents the nodes it repeats. Its last node is a `check` whose label says what sends it round again ("No: again"), so the loop-back is the exit test (DEC-137).
- **`track <name>`** lines under a `check` are its branches. They rejoin after the last track. An empty track is a bypass.
- Numbers run top to bottom and are never reused. A node added later takes the next free number, wherever it sits.
