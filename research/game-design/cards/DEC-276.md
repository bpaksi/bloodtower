Proposed by Claude, first used in MEC-039; Bob made it decided on 2026-10-27, as used in the eleven diagrams MEC-032 to MEC-039 and MEC-072 to MEC-075. A fenced `railroad` block in the loop card:

- **Sections**, in template order: `enter`, `start`, `body`, `close`, `end`. A section with nothing in it is left out.
- **A node** is one line: its number (`.3`, short for MEC-039.3), its kind, a short label, then its home card (DEC-277) and any other IDs it uses. Kinds: `story`, `roll`, `check`, `change`, `board`, `prompt`, `tower`, `call` (DEC-268, DEC-280).
- **`repeat`** indents the nodes it repeats. Its last node is a `check` whose label says what sends it round again ("No: again"), so the loop-back is the exit test (DEC-137).
- **`track <name>`** lines under a `check` are its branches. They rejoin after the last track. An empty track is a bypass.
- Numbers run top to bottom and are never reused. A node added later takes the next free number, wherever it sits.
- A body with no `repeat` plays once (the event and the picks, MEC-072 to MEC-075).
- A `check` on Continue may open both `enter` and `start`, each with its own tracks, since a track never crosses a section (MEC-032 to MEC-034).
- A `call` whose target depends on a type names the area that holds the types as its home (MEC-037 .5).
