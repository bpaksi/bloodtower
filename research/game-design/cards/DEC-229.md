Bob chose "Three groups". **Look up:** hero panel, roll, items. **Table:** pause and resume, reconnect tower, join another iPad, change who holds whom. **Leave:** Quit story. Version and the debug overlay sit small under the groups. Back and Replay are not in the Menu; they are on the bottom bar (DEC-227). The Menu itself is a centered dialog (UI-033). Rejected: one flat list, and keeping only game items on the iPad with tower and device items on the Story board's Menu.
