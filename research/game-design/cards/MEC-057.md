**What:** Tasks the villain sets the heroes, each with a deadline and a penalty. The engine shows it, counts down the days and applies the penalty if it isn't done.

**When:** When the story wants a race against the villain's plan, a choice between the plot and stopping a threat.

**Why:** A quest makes the villain act between set pieces and puts a clock the table can see on the board.

**Pack:** quest entries with task, deadline and penalty.
