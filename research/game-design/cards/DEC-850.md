D-02 §3. Replace a hero: 1 bite on the most-bitten hero, or 3 + the chapter number in hit points at 2 bites. Win Lenka: her loyalty drops a step, then she is taken at wavering. Destroy the blade: the blade is weakened a step, down to its floor. Darkness: 1 skull on the nearest standing building.

The Lantern stops the goal's strike from landing for its group, leaving only the hit-point loss less 2. Lenka takes 2 off her hero's loss. Strikes never take a quest object (G-1).
