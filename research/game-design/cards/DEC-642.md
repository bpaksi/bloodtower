Bob picked "One choice, engine effects". The first probe found the gap at *The Ambush* (DEC-636).

- **One step:** the story's narration, then 2 or 3 options the story words (MEC-006). The present heroes pick one together (DEC-019).
- **Effects:** each option takes its effects from a fixed engine list: a battle (foe type and level), a cost, a gold gain, a story or quest item (DEC-054, DEC-447), or a flag. The story writes no prompts or branches of its own (DEC-285).
- **Done:** the quest is done once the chosen option resolves. An option with a battle must win it. A retreat leaves the quest, and a fall fails it (MEC-037 .6).
- **Same shape as** the event loop's foes track (MEC-072 .14 to .19). The step diagram is MEC-077.

Not picked: a chain of 1 to 3 steps; narration, then a fixed Fight or Leave.
