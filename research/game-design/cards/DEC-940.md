D2 brief (bloodtower #500), open question OQ-D2-3. MEC-026 .18 marks the room cleared after each pass, and C4/06 EV-06 marks a paid trap room cleared. The F1 brief says "a room clears only on won", meaning a battle room whose foe still stands.

- **Ruling:** a trap is spent once its loss is paid. The room is marked cleared, whether no hero, some heroes or every hero fell. On any later entry it is an empty room: no trap is drawn and no loss is paid.
- **Battle rooms are unchanged.** F1's rule (and DEC-497, DEC-651) stands for them: a retreat or fall leaves the foe and the room uncleared. F1's "a room clears only on won" is read as about battle rooms; F1's evals test only battle rooms, so none changes.
- **Every hero fallen** still fails the Tomb and the quest (DEC-906); the cleared trap room is kept with the layout (DEC-491).

Reason: a trap has no foe left to meet. Keeping it live would charge a second loss for one room, with nothing on any card to say so.

Not picked: the trap room stays live after a total fall (a second loss for the same trap).

Decided by Gamewright, 2026-11-18, under Bob's delegation. Revise in playtest.
