Bob picked "Up to 3, one battle max" in the 2026-10-31 v1 readiness review.

- **Count:** an option lists 1 to 3 effects from DEC-642's engine list.
- **Battle:** at most one of them. It always resolves first (MEC-077 .4), whatever its place in the list.
- **The rest:** apply in the order the story lists them, and only when there was no battle or it was won (MEC-077 .6). A retreat or a fall applies none of them.
- **Lint:** the pack lint fails an option with more than 3 effects or two battles.

Not picked: one effect plus a battle (*pay 10 gold and take the Map* would need two options); any number of effects (hard to read on one picture with 2 or 3 choices).
