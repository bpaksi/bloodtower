Bob picked "1 space a day". In a Long game the adversary roams from The Barrow through three chapters (DEC-791), and at 2 spaces a day his arrival skulls cost 8 to 11 a game, as many as the night drops. At 1 a day he is as fast as a hunter. A group still outruns him easily, and the threat bar's days to arrival double.

Script, normal, with DEC-822 and DEC-824 in play (1, 2, 3 and 4 heroes):

| Pace | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 a day (before) | 81, 88, 89, 94 | 80, 80, 82, 92 | 46, 40, 46, 73 |
| 1 a day | 82, 88, 89, 93 | 82, 84, 84, 93 | 57, 58, 63, 85 |

Long stays below DEC-823's floor, which the Long clock has to fix. The rest of DEC-775 is unchanged: he starts in the Tower, drops 1 skull on arrival and then moves on (DEC-802).

Not picked: 2 a day with 2 days' rest at each building he skulls; 1 a day with 1 day's rest; and keeping 2 a day.
