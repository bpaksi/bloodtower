Bob picked "Engine three, plus story extras". The first probe found the clash at MEC-032 .14.

- **Engine settings, in every story:** Length offers only the lengths the story supports (1.0: Short and Standard, DEC-502). Difficulty is easy, normal or hard (DEC-059). Party size stays within the story's player range (DEC-098).
- **Story extras:** a story may add its own options below the three.
- **SCR-008:** stories no longer declare length, difficulty or party size. Proving Grounds' party-size setting becomes the engine's.

Not picked: each story declares every setting, with engine defaults for any it leaves out; the engine's three only, with no story options in 1.0.
