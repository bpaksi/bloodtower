**Recommendation.** "Merchant" survives in DEC-091, DEC-300 and MEC-074 from before the buildings were redefined (DEC-160). DEC-173 made the village word and rest only, and DEC-165 put trade at the bazaar. So a merchant is any seller the haggle die applies to: the bazaar's traders, a trade offer met in the wild (MEC-072 .23) and the Encampment (DEC-771).

The citadel's prices and the fence's prices don't haggle. Only the citadel sells potions (DEC-161).

**Why:** no card says this today, and a builder would have to guess.
