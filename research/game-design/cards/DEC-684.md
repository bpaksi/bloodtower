Claude's proposal, for Bob's review. Probe P-003 found the gap on day 22.
- **The rule:** the roll takes off the corruption count of the group's most corrupted hero. A pair with 1 and 1 takes 1 off. A pair with 2 and 0 takes 2 off. The floor of 2 still holds (DEC-615).
- **The panel:** the day roll panel (UI-021) names the hero whose corruptions it took off.
- **Why:** a group moves at its slowest hero's pace. A sum would grow with party size and punish a big party for keeping together. That is the opposite of what Regroup (DEC-640) is for.
- **Splitting** doesn't game it: the corrupted hero's own group still pays the full count.

Not picked: the sum; the average, rounded up.
