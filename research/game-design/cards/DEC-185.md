Bob chose "Foe levels rise; difficulty adds". The chapter's challenge step does one thing: the level of the foes drawn rises by one step each chapter. Setup difficulty adds a fixed offset on top, for example easy −1, normal 0 and hard +1. Players feel one knob. The steps and offsets are numbers for later. Rejected: several knobs climbing together, and a step set by each story.
