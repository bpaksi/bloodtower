Bob chose "Only a move". Talking to the sanctuary's NPCs costs 1 move and no gold, like the lord's audience (DEC-159). It gives one piece of lore per visit, so a party that wants more comes back. What a piece of lore can reveal is set by DEC-166 and by the story. Rejected: a donation, and lore earned by deeds.
