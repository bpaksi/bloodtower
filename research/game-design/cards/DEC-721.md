Bob picked "Story moments plus corruption" in the 2026-11-05 1.1 session.

- **Story:** a choice in a scene or talk can raise or lower loyalty a step.
- **Engine:** one fixed rule. A corruption on the companion's hero lowers it a step.

Retreating from a battle does not move it, since the game allows retreat as a tactic.

Not picked: story moments only (a thinly written story leaves loyalty stuck), and more engine rules for retreats, won quests and cures.
