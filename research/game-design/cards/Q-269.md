The item fit chart proposes a Bloodtower effect for all 83 kinds of card. Warriors become hit points, spirit becomes money and Reinforce becomes a building pick. These need Bob's pass, and three groups need a ruling of their own:

- **Advantage only:** Azkol's Banner, Azkol's Horn and Necklace of Haggling, whose abilities rely on systems Bloodtower doesn't have.
- **Story only:** Bezoar, Herbal Remedy and Tools of the Saboteur.
- **No use:** the Tempted corruption.

The numbers (how much money, how many hit points) belong to the numbers pass.
