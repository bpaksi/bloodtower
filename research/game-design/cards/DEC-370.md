Bob, earlier the same day: "I'd like to use the cards. Game will choose, player will select from deck and hold on to till lost. Advantages will be accumulated and tracked by game." After the item fit chart, Bob agreed: "Yes, please move forward with 'game uses the physical cards, skulls, companions and the trait effects'".

- **Kinds:** virtue tiles, potions, gear, treasures, quest items, corruptions and companions all behave the same way. The item fit chart shows every kind has a Bloodtower use.
- **Gain:** the app names the card from a seeded draw (DEC-067) and shows its art, name and effect on the Story board. The player takes that physical card or tile and keeps it by their hero board, as with corruptions (DEC-366).
- **Hold:** the card stays with the hero until it is spent or lost. The app always knows what every hero holds.
- **Effect:** the app applies every card. It counts each Advantage in battle (DEC-324) and applies each perk on the board, so no player does sums.
- **Return:** when a card is spent or lost, the app names it and the player puts it back.

Still open: how the 4 hero boards and 16 virtue tiles cover the classes (Q-268), and each card's Bloodtower wording (Q-269). Rejected: virtues and items only in the app, with the cards left in the box.
