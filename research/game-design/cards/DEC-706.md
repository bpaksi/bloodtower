Bob, G-C1 playtest: "iPad UI is better, but the corner art looks funny." The build shows four windows cut from the whole move-map mock, so each corner carries pieces of the mock's own words ("Mov…", "…ere"), its Back and Menu labels, and a cut edge of parchment.

- **The asset:** four engraved manuscript corners drawn as corners (ART-080). Outside the engraving they are transparent, so the screen's own background shows.
- **What a corner never holds:** words, buttons, labels, or parchment cut from a mock.
- **Placement:** a corner sits in its corner of the screen. No button, field or text sits under a corner, and the bottom bar (UI-032) clears the two lower corners.
