## Findings

1. **Spells make the finale safe.** Simulated, a party with casters beat The Warden 99 to 100% of the time in about 3 rounds, against 80% in about 8 with no casters. Ordinary fights with casters stayed within DEC-315's 2 to 4 rounds, and no-caster parties ran long at about 5. Bob ruled DEC-801: Lightning and Fireball each cut 4. The long no-caster fights remain open.
2. **The clock barely bites here.** Winning a quest skips that night's drop (DEC-699), so 14 quest wins mean 18 days and 14 skulls left. A party with no casters will take much longer, so this doesn't settle DEC-776's worry.
3. **A traitor quest played last leaves no time to accuse.** Chapters end at that night's close. It's fair, but a table may not see it coming, so the scene's line should warn them.
4. **Gold outruns prices:** about 20 gold a quest for four heroes, against gear at 12. Numbers pass.
5. **Haggle hard works with no gold,** so any party can force the camp's battle for free. That may be fine, but it's a check for the playtest.
6. **A lead isn't a required quest** (DEC-087), so Long is 14 required plus one optional. The map is corrected.
