# Probe P-003: The Ruined Keep, Standard, normal, Fighter and Bard, tower in play

A scripted 1.0 game played from the cards alone (DEC-634). Each step cites the card behind it. A rules gap stops the walk and becomes a `Q-`. Bob asked Claude to rule every one, so each `DEC-` here is **proposed**, for his review. A missing number gets a placeholder, logged below (DEC-638). P-001 and P-002 never reached chapter 3's quests, the Tower with a party that has no spells, the oath-blade, Tomas's Map, the last choice, a loss on skulls, or a tower lost for good. This walk aims at those (DEC-677). The cards are in AREA-098.

- **Story:** The Ruined Keep (Proving Grounds v1.0, AREA-097)
- **Settings:** Standard, normal, party of 2, on two iPads (DEC-677)
- **Heroes:** the Fighter: Shield Wall (guard) and Weapon Master (+1 Melee), fight number 3, 12 hit points. The Bard: Silver Tongue and Rally (+1 Wild), fight number 1, 8 hit points (P15). Neither casts a spell (DEC-380, DEC-545).
- **Tower:** in play. The link drops on day 9 and comes back on Reconnect. In the finale desk walk it drops for good (DEC-301).
- **Dice:** mulberry32 streams: 3001 the move roll (2d3), 3002 the purse wheel, 3003 battles, 3004 events, 3005 the tower's drop, 3006 the tomb deal, 3007 the random foe, 3008 the Tower's rooms.
- **Table policy:** split for chapter 1, and stay together from the Tomb on. The Fighter guards when the revealed card would fell the Bard, but never when one won round would finish the foe. Retreat when a hero is at or below the foe's level (2 higher for a hero with 2 corruptions), but never at a foe count of 2 or less. From day 10, keep a move for a retreat before a quest battle. Heal by rally when the gold allows, else rest at the nearest village. Once the supply is 12 or less, Cleanse a skulled building when a move is spare. Put each skull on the fullest building in its kingdom that has fewer than 3.

## Findings (no ruling needed)

- **The skull supply decided the game, not the battles.** With two heroes it is a 24-day clock, plus one day per Cleanse (DEC-686). Chapters 1 and 2 took 8 days. Chapter 3's two quests took 17 more, and the Tower opened on the last night. The numbers pass should fit each length to the clock, for example a bigger supply at Standard or no drop on the day a chapter is won.
- **Neuri's Hex breaks a pair.** Hex zeroes one of only two fight numbers every round, so the Fighter and Bard fought the Neuri at 25% to 40% where Oreks gave 50%. *The Last Stand* took five visits over 15 days. The numbers pass might make Hex zero the lowest fight number, or skip it for parties of two or fewer.
- **A pair without spells can't climb the Tower.** The basement guard, at level 3 and count 13, beat a full Fighter and Bard. The level 4 and 5 stair guards would be at 43% and 37%, with counts of 17 and 21. With the oath-blade, The Warden (47%, cut 5) is easier than his own stair guards. P-001 found the same inversion with four heroes. The numbers pass should lower the guard levels or counts, or let a shrine heal more.
- **Guarding nearly stops progress.** When the Fighter guards, a pair's strength falls from 6 to 2, or 1 under Hex (DEC-679). He guarded 8 rounds and won 2 of them. Each one kept the Bard standing, but pushed the foe's fall back a round.
- **The guard on a retreat worked.** With DEC-680 the Fighter took four retreats' last attacks for 1 to 2 each. No retreat felled anyone, unlike P-002.
- **Keeping a move for a retreat costs time.** From Irontops, *The Last Stand* needs 3 moves: 1 to walk, 1 to pick, 1 kept for a retreat. A roll of 2 means another day on the staging space. Irontops is wild, and its nights brought two disasters and a hazard.
- **Gold ran out by day 15.** Five rallies at 4 gold used up the purse and every win. After day 15 every heal was a 2- to 3-day rest at a village. The 8-gold cure was never affordable. P-002 found the same with one hero.
- **Skulls piled into Zenon while it was closed.** By day 19, Plains of Plovo and Anza each had 3, and Akartus 1: 7 of 16 on the board, in a kingdom the heroes couldn't reach until chapter 3. No building fell, because the tower held 8.
- **Splitting for chapter 1 was quick.** Two solo groups finished both quests in 2 days, and Regroup on day 4 put the party back together at the sanctuary (DEC-640, DEC-678).
- **The tower drop and Reconnect were smooth.** Nothing the table needed was a tower node, so a skipped sound lost nothing (DEC-683).
- **The Barrow's guardian needed three visits.** Each heal trip to the citadel, 2 spaces away, cost about 2 days.
- **Tomas's Map and the oath-blade were saved for a boss never reached.** P-002 saw the same with the Map. A story item meant for the finale may need an earlier use, or the Map may need to be spendable in the Tower's guard rooms. It already is (DEC-461), but the table saved it.
