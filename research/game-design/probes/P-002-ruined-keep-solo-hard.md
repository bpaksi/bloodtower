## Findings (no ruling needed)

- **A lone hero on hard can't clear chapter 2.** Oreks at level 3 give a strength-3 Ranger 33% a round, and Shield wall cuts only 1 a win. Count 12 means about 11 wins, or about 33 rounds, against a hit of 3 or more on each lost one. The guardian, level 4 with count 16, is out of reach. DEC-564's +1 fight number and +2 hit points don't cover hard's +1 level. The numbers pass should rerun the solo rows of DEC-564 at each difficulty (Q-302).
- **Shield wall is crushing for a weak party.** The minimum cut of 1 halves a small party's progress. It may need a floor of 2 when only one hero is present, or a lower count.
- **A solo hero runs out of gold.** After day 7 the Ranger never had gold again: falls cost gold, and level 3 wins were out of reach. The 10-gold cure was never affordable, so corruption only climbed, and each one cut the move roll. The numbers pass should check a no-income floor, such as a small daily wage or a free sanctuary cure once a chapter.
- **Corruption snowballs through the move roll.** At 2 corruptions the Ranger rolled 2 most days. That's barely enough to walk between the village and the Barrow, so each try cost about 3 days.
- **Retreat isn't safe at low hit points.** Twice the last attack did what the retreat was meant to avoid. The table policy "retreat at twice the level" was too late against a Rend. The retreat screen might show the worst card left in the shuffle.
- **Howl undoes a weak party's work.** Room 1 went from 10 to 7, then back to 9. Against a slow party, Howl's +2 outweighs a won round's cut of 1.
- **The night is the real killer.** Of the 4 falls, 2 came from night events (DEC-565 kept them from being a third). One came at a village, where the full bazaars had pushed a skull.
- **The skull dump fills, then spills onto the rest stops.** With 1.0's bazaars at 3 skulls each, the table had to put skulls on the villages it slept in. That brings night events to the one safe place (DEC-093).
- **The no-tower path held up.** The simulated drop, the skipped cues and the chapter close's skipped seal step needed no ruling. The supply fell by exactly 1 a day.
- **Saved items never found a use.** The table kept Tomas's Map for the guardian and never reached it. The oath-blade waits for a Tower the walk never reached.
- **Proving Grounds' player range isn't written down.** DEC-098 gives it a default of 4. The walk assumed 1 to 4, as in DEC-564's rows. The story card should say so.
