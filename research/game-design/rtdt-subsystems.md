# RtDT app-driven subsystems: survey and reuse

**History (DEC-610):** written before the design cards. Where it disagrees with a card, the card wins; known stale lines say "Superseded by". Gamewright, 2026-10-14. Sources: official rulebook [v2](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/RtDT_Rulebook-v2-web.pdf) and [two-page](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Return_to_Dark_Tower-rules-two-page.pdf), [Alliances rulebook](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/RTDT_Alliances-Rulebook.pdf), [Covenant rulebook](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/RtDT_Covenant_Rulebook-web.pdf), plus local notes (research/return-to-dark-tower/). Anything marked **unverified** has no source we checked. BGG was not consulted in depth.

Ratings: **strong** = reuse as a generic set piece; **maybe** = reuse only in part or later; **skip**.

| # | Subsystem | How it works (source) | What's fun | Reuse as a set piece | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dungeons | Quest action on a dungeon token. The app reveals rooms one by one, with one Advantage-improve per room. Cleared rooms persist, you choose to go deeper or leave, and the target room completes the quest (rulebook v2) | Push-your-luck, hidden map, persistence | Already E20 | strong |
| 2 | Foe battle | Draw face-down app cards equal to the foe's level, apply Advantage arrows, each foe has a critical-hit card (v2; local game-overview) | Tense reveals, spending Advantages | Already the Dashboard battle screen + E18 | strong (exists) |
| 3 | Adversary battle | After the main goal. 5 cards; you may retreat after at least one card; improvements persist between attempts (v2) | Multi-attempt boss, siege feel | Generic "boss over several visits" finale with persistent progress (e.g. the Dracula night-5 assault) | strong |
| 4 | Quests (companion / adversary / main goal) | App offers quests; you complete one with the Quest action at a marked space with the required items/resources; failing gives an outcome; quest markers on board (two-page rulebook; E19) | Clear goals, races against the month | Already E19 objectives; add a generic "fetch/requirement quest" template | strong |
| 5 | Monthly quest chain / event timing | App fires events per skull drop: foes spawn, foes strike, etc. (two-page rulebook) | Unpredictable pressure | E16/E19 already cover pressure; a per-night event chain template | maybe |
| 6 | Foe status escalation | Foes left on the board escalate panicked → lethal and have strike events (local game-overview) | Ticking clock that punishes ignoring threats | Generic "festering threat" token whose cost grows each round | strong |
| 7 | Reinforce + haggle die | At a building, optionally roll the haggle die first: results range from bonuses (e.g. +3 warriors, +1 potion) to cancelling the reinforce (v2) | Small gamble | Generic "bargain" beat: bet a story item for a better or worse result. Low-cost add-on | maybe |
| 8 | Cleanse | Heroic action to remove the skulls from a building (v2) | Tower-board interaction | Base game already covers it; no story layer needed | skip |
| 9 | Heroic tests + Tower dungeon (competitive) | Complete 3 tests at locations by spending/holding resources; each lets you move a seal; then the Tower dungeon from a space next to the Tower; first to find the relic wins (v2) | Race, personal goals | Private per-hunter goal cards that unlock a final crawl. Fits private iPads, but it's competitive while we're co-op | maybe |
| 10 | Alliances: guilds | Influence heroic action at a guild hall (+2 influence); spend 5 influence to raise rank; ranks 2–4 recruit guild companions; monthly guild quest with reward or penalty (Alliances rulebook) | Faction track, investing for companions | Generic "faction favor" track (e.g. Szgany, villagers) with E18 companions at thresholds | strong |
| 11 | Alliances: apocalyptic threats | Rulebook intro: "battle apocalyptic forces of blight, omen, fire, and frost" (Alliances rulebook). Mechanics **unverified** | Escalating doom | Not studied enough to rate | maybe (research) |
| 12 | Covenant: monuments + offerings | App assigns monuments to kingdoms, replacing buildings. Make offerings to build them; a completed monument changes the reinforce effect and fires beneficial events (Covenant rulebook) | Building toward permanent boons | Generic "restore a site" project: e.g. reconsecrate a church step by step to unlock a cure/rest effect | strong |
| 13 | Covenant: wastelands | App places wasteland tokens. The space loses its terrain, and a hero entering it can't leave that turn (Covenant rulebook) | Board gets worse, routing puzzle | Generic "blighted space" token from story events. Cheap | maybe |
| 14 | Covenant: doom skulls | Separate skull type (Covenant rulebook); effect details **unverified** | More tower pressure | Needs the tower (Phase 2) | skip (POC) |
| 15 | Glyphs / seals | Seals come off the tower; while a glyph faces your kingdom, that action costs 1 spirit (local game-overview) | Tower state shapes choices | Tower control is Phase 2 | skip (POC) |
| 16 | Companions | Gained from quests; their effects and events are tracked in the app (rulebook; Alliances) | Allies with personality | Already E18 | strong (exists) |

## Shortlist (new generic set pieces)
1. **E20 Dungeon crawl** (drafted; Dracula crypt D-DRA-14).
2. **Multi-visit boss** (from adversary battle): retreat allowed after one round, progress persists.
3. **Festering threat** (from foe status): a threat whose cost grows each round it's ignored.
4. **Faction favor track** (from Alliances guilds): spend story items to raise favor, with companions unlocking at thresholds.
5. **Restore a site** (from Covenant monuments): a multi-step offering project that unlocks a boon.
Maybe later: bargain/haggle beat, blighted spaces, private goal cards → final crawl.
