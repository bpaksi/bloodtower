Move roll simulator

A group rolls its moves (seeded), adds 1 for each virtue that helps movement, takes 1 away for each corruption, and never goes below a floor (DEC-102). Moves pay for walking and for each building action alike, 1 each (DEC-078), so a low total can strand a group in a village. The dice, the floor and which virtues help are the numbers still open.
