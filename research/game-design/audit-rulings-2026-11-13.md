Gamewright, 2026-11-13. Design only. These answer Wright's request, with Bob's go via Warden, for rulings on D1 to D6 of the Dark Tower consolidated design audit of 2026-11-12, and for a ruling or a park on Hammer's two design items. Design rulings are Gamewright's under Bob's standing rule (relayed by Warden). Each ruling is a card (DEC-905 to DEC-914, Q-454, Q-455, under AREA-123) or a card edit; this note is the index.

Scope: every ruling is for 1.0. Bob's lock of 2026-11-12 (finish 1.0 before any 1.1 design) holds: nothing here designs 1.1. Where a ruling touches a 1.1 card, it says "parked".

Authority used: DEC-610 (a controlling card beats an old document), DEC-352 (a partly replaced card is trimmed, not parked). A higher DEC number is not an override on its own, so each conflict below is settled by the later and narrower Bob card, or by the card that is actually in the release.

## D1: LC-01, terminal-loss timing

- **Ruling:** an empty supply or a fallen kingdom is checked right after each skull drops or lands and ends the game at once, by DEC-676's stop (open loops end, no other group plays, MEC-032 .43 to .49). If several are true, the reason line names a third corruption, then an empty supply, then a fallen kingdom.
- **Reason:** DEC-676's claim that these two losses can only happen at the close was false from DEC-559 on (a Magic card drops a skull in a battle), and the chapter close places freed skulls after .11 has run. DEC-686's own title says the loss "is checked when a drop empties it". This is the same stop Bob chose for the third corruption.
- **A won chapter can still be lost:** a seal break's freed skull that destroys a kingdom's last building loses the game at .27; the chapter's won cue, hook and stamp do not play.
- **Cited:** DEC-044, DEC-559, DEC-676, DEC-686, DEC-093, DEC-092, MEC-033, MEC-032. Dracula's DEC-885 stands as its own order and is not generalized (1.2).
- **Card edits:** new DEC-905. Trimmed: DEC-676 (the other-losses bullet), DEC-686 (the ".11 after the drop" line), DEC-559 and DEC-044 (one line each). MEC-033 (Exit text; .11 and .27 refs and text; a `uses` link) and MEC-034 (.19 text and refs; a body line).
- **Lock check:** no Bob choice is reversed. DEC-676's pick ("At once") is extended to the other two losses, and the claim it rested on is trimmed.
- **Tests:** R03 fixtures are in the card.

## D2: QD-05 and CB-06

**QD-05, trap casualty (DEC-906).**
- **Ruling:** the loss is the trap's number after on guard halves it and Advantages soften it. A hero whose hit points are at or below it falls: one corruption and 1 hit point at once. Exact zero and a shortfall are the same case, never two corruptions, never a hero left at 0. No sit-out in the Tomb (sitting out is a battle rule). If every present hero falls, the Tomb result is failed, the quest fails, no gold is lost (a trap has no foe level, DEC-177), and the corruptions stay. A third corruption loses the game at once.
- **Reason:** it is the smallest rule that joins DEC-606 (pay what you can, plus a corruption) and DEC-119 (0 hit points is a corruption and a return at 1) without a benched player. DEC-714's floor of 1 stays a plain-scene rule.
- **Cited:** DEC-606, DEC-496, DEC-119, DEC-607, DEC-676, DEC-642, DEC-714, MEC-026 .15 and .18.
- **Companions (1.1, parked):** DEC-800's swap applies to a trap fall as to a battle fall. Nothing to design.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-906. DEC-606 and DEC-119 get one line each. MEC-026 (.15 text and refs, one body sentence, a `uses` link).

**CB-06, final cut and Spines (DEC-907).**
- **Ruling:** a foe that is gone does nothing more. A won round cuts first; if the cut leaves the foe gone the battle is won at once and Spines does not fire. Shield wall changes the cut itself and still applies. If the foe still stands, Spines fires, and if it fells the last standing hero the battle is fallen. The final cut and the group's fall can therefore never meet. If a later ability ever lets them meet, the foe's falling wins (won, reward once, fallen heroes keep the corruption and return at 1, no gold lost).
- **The order at every point in a battle:** game loss, then foe gone, then every hero fallen. It runs after the Tomb stance effect, after a before-round-1 ability, after spells (DEC-643), and after a round.
- **F1 confirmations, recorded:** (1) the entry terminal gate routes as the package says; MEC-038 alone applies the stance once, on a genuine entry; Continue replays nothing. (2) TB-03: a foe killed by Bold never uses its later pre-round ability. Both are Gamewright rulings of 2026-11-13, confirmed to Wright; this card is where to cite them.
- **Reason:** one principle ("a dead foe does nothing") answers TB-03 and CB-06 together and matches DEC-643. It costs the party nothing and removes the mutual case.
- **Cited:** DEC-119, DEC-553, DEC-607, DEC-643, DEC-671, DEC-676, MEC-038.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-907. DEC-553 (one added line). MEC-038 (.41 and .11 text and refs; a body line; `uses` links to DEC-907 to DEC-909).
- **Tests:** F1's TB-01 to TB-14 stand as written. Add one: Spines on the final cut does not fire; Spines on a non-final cut felling the last hero closes fallen.

## D3: CB-04 and CB-05

**CB-04, Ward (DEC-908).**
- **Ruling:** at MEC-038 .8 the order is: (1) the Ward cancels one spell, the weakest cast in the round it first meets, if it has not yet; (2) the surviving flat cuts (DEC-655); (3) halving (the Titan's Colossus), to each surviving spell. The cancelled spell is cast and used up (a once-a-battle spell is spent). The Ward is used once per battle. Equal cuts give the same result.
- **Example:** Lightning and Magic Missile in one round: the Missile is cancelled, the foe takes 4. Cast alone in round 1, a Missile draws the Ward and Lightning lands in full in round 2.
- **Reason:** it is seat-independent (DEC-655 forbids tap or seat order), needs no prompt, and leaves a table skill (lead with the small spell).
- **Cited:** DEC-553, DEC-655, DEC-801, DEC-123, MEC-038.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-908; DEC-553 one line; MEC-038 .8 text and refs.

**CB-05, boss deck (DEC-909).**
- **Ruling:** three piles: the draw pile, the discards, the card face-up. A phase shuffles one new Rend into the draw pile (seeded). The discards and the face-up card stay, and the face-up card resolves as it would have. An empty draw pile at a reveal returns every card, added Rends included, and shuffles. A phase with an empty pile makes the new Rend the pile's only card. Two phases in one round do this twice, in order. The cycle is the cards the deck holds (9 or 10 after a phase). The foe panel shows the cards left and the Rends left.
- **Reason:** it uses DEC-649's piles unchanged, makes the phase bite on the next round, and never replays a card already shown.
- **Cited:** DEC-567, DEC-649, DEC-658, DEC-557, DEC-011, DEC-323.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-909; DEC-567, DEC-649 and DEC-658 get one line each; MEC-038 .28 text and refs.
- **Tests:** R04 seeded traces in the card (pile at 5, 1 and 0 when the phase fires).

## D4: QD-06, emergency relocation

- **Ruling:** the marker stays. When the 4th skull destroys the building holding an unfinished quest marker, its space stays a place to Enter (DEC-641) with only the quest, then Leave, on its menu. The quest keeps its identity and progress (DEC-151); credit is paid once; the destroyed building still counts toward a fallen kingdom. No relocation exists in 1.0.
- **Reason:** DEC-093 (Bob, 2026-10-21) says the marker moves; DEC-613 (Bob, 2026-10-29) says a 1.0 quest stays on its one site; DEC-777 parks moving quests. A move would need a destination, bounds and a confirmation that nothing defines. Staying needs none, keeps DEC-088 ("never lost") and keeps the quest reachable, since a space is never removed.
- **Cited:** DEC-093, DEC-088, DEC-613, DEC-641, DEC-151, DEC-777, DEC-377, MEC-035, MEC-036.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-910; DEC-093 trimmed (the "moves" line replaced); DEC-613 one line; MEC-036 (Enter text and a `uses` link); MEC-035 (one clause).
- **Parked (1.1):** a destination rule, with MEC-025 and DEC-020.
- **Lock check:** this settles a conflict between two Bob cards in favor of the later and narrower one (DEC-613). It reverses neither. Listed for Warden below.

## D5: BE-05, purse against present group

- **Ruling:** affordability is the whole pool, as the board shows it (DEC-007, DEC-176). The heroes present pay in equal shares, odd coins by DEC-646; a short present hero is covered by the other present heroes; only if the present heroes together are short does the rest come from the heroes not there. The pool falls by exactly the price; no hero goes below 0. Costs that name a hero's own gold (Cruel, Greedy, Feral, Lost, DEC-896; a companion's price, DEC-726) take only from that hero's part and are never covered. A gain is split across the heroes present (DEC-178).
- **Reason:** "freely shared" and a pool on the board mean the acting group is never shown a total it cannot spend. MEC-003 says who takes part and who is hit, and gold is not a hero's body.
- **Cited:** DEC-176, DEC-178, DEC-646, DEC-177, DEC-896, DEC-726, MEC-003, MEC-074.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-911; DEC-176 and DEC-178 one line each; DEC-896 step 6 reworded; MEC-074 (.9 text and a `uses` link).
- **Lock check:** both readings are lawful under Bob's cards. I read DEC-176's "freely shared" as the stronger. If Bob wants only the present group's gold to pay, DEC-911 is the one card to flip. Listed for Warden below.

## D6: LC-03 and BE-02

**LC-03, due drops (DEC-912).**
- **Ruling:** one saved counter N of counted nights. A night with a won quest drops nothing, does not move N, and shows "+1 day". Otherwise N goes up by 1. Solo: 1 skull on odd N. Pair: 1 skull unless N is a multiple of 4. Three or four heroes: 1 skull on odd N, 2 on even N. The same due count drops into the tower, drives the simulated drop (each skull its own seeded kingdom), and counts the days-left band forward (assuming no quest wins), plus 1 for a Cleanse.
- **Example, a pair:** night 2 is a won-quest night, so night 3 is N=2 and night 5 is N=4. A calendar count would have skipped night 4.
- **Reason:** DEC-824 and DEC-826 already say a won-quest night is outside the count; this makes solo and 3 or 4 heroes the same. One counter is the simplest thing to save and to test.
- **Cited:** DEC-699, DEC-822, DEC-824, DEC-564, DEC-703, DEC-686, DEC-305, MEC-034.
- **Parked (1.1):** the Long overlay (DEC-826) sits on top, using the same N.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-912; one line on DEC-699, 822, 824, 564 and 826; MEC-034 (.11, .36 text and refs; a body line; `uses` links).
- **For the next probe:** check the solo and 3 or 4 hero counts against P-006's script. If a cell moves more than about 3 points, Bob picks (DEC-817). Observed, not verified: `dropsThisNight` in `engine/loops/day/rules.ts` takes a `night` number and still gives 1 skull a night to 1 or 2 heroes. DEC-822 and DEC-824 wait for their own code session (AREA-120), and `night` must then be the counted night.

**BE-02, Fortunate against the move gate (DEC-913).**
- **Ruling:** once per day, a group holding Fortunate that takes a pick at a building may take a second pick at the same building for no move. While that is pending and the menu still has a pick, the visit does not end with 0 moves left (.15), and .8 takes no move for it. A quest pick (DEC-821) or Uncertain (DEC-468) ends the turn first and the free pick is lost, so a group that wants both takes the quest second. Any pick is legal second, but Cleanse stays once per building per visit (DEC-572). Inspiring heals once per visit (DEC-548); the free pick does not heal again.
- **Reason:** the free pick is meaningless if the last move can kill it, and one principle for turn-ends (they happen right after the pick that causes them) keeps EE-01's result contract simple.
- **Cited:** DEC-399, DEC-548, DEC-572, DEC-821, DEC-468, DEC-389, MEC-036.
- **Card edits:** new DEC-913; DEC-399 (the Inspiring line trimmed, one line added); DEC-821 and DEC-468 one line each; MEC-036 (Exit text, .8 and .15 text and refs, `uses` links).
- **Tests:** R02 fixtures are in the card.

## Hammer's items

**H1, choices may not matter (Q-454, parked).** In P-006 a plain script wins 83 to 97% at normal. DEC-823 sets about 80% as a floor for a plain party on purpose, so a first table wins most games; choices are meant to matter at hard (DEC-830) and in how a table plays the clock. No rule change. The first human playtest (P01, and Bob's Nov 16 tower run) asks at the end of each game, per decision type, whether it changed the result. The experiment (a random, a plain and a careful policy; a splitting policy at hard) is P-008, bloodtower #475, parked with 1.1.

**H2, rules tuned to win rates (DEC-914).** DEC-821, 822, 824 and 826 each get a one-line "Provisional" note. They stay in force and are built as written. They change only with Bob, by a new card that refines the old one. DEC-817 already keeps 1.0 numbers with Bob; this does not move a number. DEC-825 and DEC-827 are the same class and are not edited here.

**H3, tower never load-bearing (Q-455, parked).** Listed in Wright's mapping, not in the request, so it is logged and parked rather than ruled. DEC-305 and DEC-306 stand: a game must be winnable without the tower. The 1.0 tower already carries the skull clock and the held-skull seal breaks. A tower-decided battle draw would be a new mechanic, parked with P-008's tower cell (PT-05).

## Locks touched, for Warden

No ruling reverses a Bob lock. Three touch one, each a conflict between two Bob cards that one reading settles:
- **D1:** DEC-676's bullet that "the other losses" are checked at .11 is trimmed. Bob's "At once" is extended, not reversed.
- **D4:** DEC-093's "a quest marker there moves" is trimmed in favor of DEC-613 (stays put). If Bob wants a relocation in 1.0 anyway, it needs a destination rule he picks, and DEC-613 changes.
- **D5:** DEC-176 ("freely shared") and DEC-178 (the group that spends pays) can be read two ways. DEC-911 takes the first. If Bob prefers the other, DEC-911 is the one card to flip.
Three more are number-sensitive and carry a "check" line, not a Bob call: D6a's counted nights (check against P-006's script), D2b (Spines never fires on the final cut, a small buff), and D3a (the Ward cancels the weakest spell, which makes the Striga easier than the strongest-first reading).
