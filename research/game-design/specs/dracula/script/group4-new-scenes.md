## Calls made (story taste, mine)

1. **Long Night: the hero at the door is "a hunter"**, never named or addressed, so the copy works whether the group or the engine picks the bitten hero. Lenka's one line comes before the choice.
2. **Long Night: Lenka is kept on every route.** The latch costs a bite, the talk costs a skull on the Village, and her coldness on the latch route ("does not look at any of you") carries the loyalty drop. The end hook is the same for every route.
3. **Banquet: the host is a tall shape seen from behind**, which keeps the Count unseen until his lair. The illusion is the empty bench with the keys still moving.
4. **Swap: the wight's tell is "does not blink" and "too wide" a smile.** "Someone is knocking" behind the stone stands for the missing hero, with no one hearing anything.
5. **Heart of Sorrow: a heart of glass on chains** (from the i6 Guardian of Sorrow). The halberds have "no hands to hold them".
6. **Castle moments: the Count is a hand (win-lenka) or a voice through the wight (replace-a-hero).** Lenka refuses in five words.
7. **"Night" is avoided** in all VO across groups 1 to 4, with "dark" and "till dawn" used instead. `opening.03` now says "It is All Hallows' Eve" (was "Tonight"). The screen title "The Long Night" is D-01's quest name.
