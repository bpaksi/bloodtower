# Lenka's look (Illusionist brief)

**Status:** DRAFT. Loremaster, 2026-11-10, 4:20 PM ET. Gamewright ruled (same day) that Lenka's face shows from chapter 1. Her everyday look is a travelling hood with her face clear. At the reveal her face changes; it is not uncovered. This replaces Bob's Oct 16 lock (veiled or hidden until the reveal) and the D-02 / D-06 "veiled until the Tome" art line.

The Count's look is unchanged: silhouette, hand, mist, cloak, until his lair. Lenka never becomes a foe.

## Age

- About 19, as the script already has her (the burgomaster's foundling daughter).
- Keep her this age in every shot, including after the reveal. The change is a mark and a stillness, not years added.

## Face (from chapter 1)

- Fair, cool colouring, a little colourless from mourning and a Carpathian winter.
- Dark brown, almost-black hair in one long braid, worn forward over a shoulder or down the back, with a few loose strands at the temples.
- A clear oval face: a straight serious brow, deep-set dark brown eyes, a straight nose, a wide stubborn mouth that does not smile easily.
- Expression: earnest, tired, watchful. Warmth is in the voice (Leda), not in a soft face.
- The face is fully readable in every shot she is in, from the Burgomaster's House onward, including her talking-avatar portrait.

## Everyday outfit

- A dark travelling hood — the hood of a plain dark cloak — over a simple black mourning dress.
- Anton's heavy seal ring on a cord at her throat.
- Plain boots for the road. No jewellery but the ring.
- The hood may sit back or be drawn up against weather. It is ordinary travel wear. It never hides her face, and it is never a veil.

## The reveal

The script's reveal is `n4-finding-the-tome.05~vo-01`: "The last page is fresh ink. She has come again, in the burgomaster's house. Ludmila's soul. Lenka's face. She says no." The Count's book names her as Ludmila returned, still refusing him. Ludmila's gallery portrait stays covered (locked). The reveal is the Tome, not a cloth coming off a face or a canvas.

What changes, from that scene on:

- It is the same face: same bones, same braid, same stubborn mouth. She is still Lenka.
- A cold violet flush rises under the skin at her mouth and under her eyes, the colour of the mountain violets that mark Ludmila throughout (pressed in the Tome, under Lenka's pillow, in Ludmila's hand).
- For a beat her expression goes still and older, like painted flesh. The mark stays from the Tome onward.
- That is the visible mark of "Ludmila's soul, Lenka's face." It is not a veil coming off, not fangs, not a bite, and not the pallor of the turned. She never becomes a foe.

Use `portrait.lenka` (everyday, no mark) until the Tome, then `portrait.lenka~revealed` (the marked face) in every later Lenka shot, including the rescued ending. The Tome painting itself has no figures; the change is on her portrait and on any later scene that shows her.

## Ludmila

- White dress, mountain violets in her hand. A family likeness to post-reveal Lenka (the same bones), in the older fashion, not a second Lenka in mourning.
- `portrait.ludmila` is for her one Tome line only. Do not show her face before that line.
- The canvas in the Portrait Gallery stays under its cloth.

## Do

- Show Lenka's face from chapter 1, in portraits and in any story painting she appears in.
- Keep age, braid, hood, mourning dress and the ring consistent.
- Switch to the marked face from `n4-finding-the-tome` on.
- Keep hunters as silhouettes, hands or backs. Keep the Count unseen until the lair.

## Don't

- No veil, no lace, no cloth over her face, in any shot.
- No covered, turned-away, or shadowed-until-reveal Lenka used to hide who she is.
- No spoiler of the violet mark, or of the older painted stillness, before `n4-finding-the-tome`.
- No "unveiling" gesture at the reveal.
- No making her a foe, a bride of the Count, or Ludmila in a white dress while she is still Lenka.
- Do not lift the cloth from Ludmila's gallery portrait.
- Do not change the Count's look rules.
