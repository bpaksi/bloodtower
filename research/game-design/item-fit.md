# Item fit: RtDT cards and tiles in Bloodtower

Can Bloodtower use RtDT's virtues, potions, gear, corruptions, treasures (magic items) and quest items as physical pieces, all behaving the same way? This chart takes each known piece, its RtDT effect, and the Bloodtower effect it would become. Bob asked for it on 2026-10-27, after ruling the direction: **the game chooses, the player takes the physical piece and holds it until it is lost or spent, and the game tracks the Advantages.**

Status: Bob adopted the physical cards (DEC-370), skull removal (DEC-371) and the foe trait effects (DEC-372) on 2026-10-27. The Bloodtower effects below are still Claude's proposals, to be settled card by card in Q-269. RtDT texts come from [items-and-virtues.md](../return-to-dark-tower/items-and-virtues.md) and the Rulebook v2.0; they are paraphrased.

## One behaviour for every kind

| Step | How it works |
|---|---|
| Gain | The game names the piece from a seeded draw (DEC-067, DEC-366) and shows it on the Story board |
| Take | The player takes that physical card or tile and keeps it by their hero |
| Hold | Kept until spent or lost; the game knows what every hero holds |
| Effect | The game applies it: Advantages counted for battle, perks applied on the board |
| Return | When spent or lost the game names it, and the player puts it back (deck bottom, stack or box) |

Hold limits in RtDT: potions any number, gear one of each type, treasures 4, quest items any number, corruptions 2 (a 3rd loses), virtues 3 tiles plus 2 printed on the hero board plus 1 kingdom tile.

## Translating RtDT terms

Most RtDT texts name resources Bloodtower dropped. Each gets one stand-in:

| RtDT | Bloodtower stand-in |
|---|---|
| Warriors (lose N) | Hit points (DEC-039) |
| Gain warriors | Heal hit points |
| Spirit | Money (it buys virtues and removes corruption, DEC-127, DEC-170) |
| Double your move | +moves on the move roll (DEC-102) |
| Reinforce | A pick at a building (DEC-157: no generic Reinforce) |
| Cleanse | **None yet.** Bloodtower has no way to remove a skull (see Gaps) |
| Home kingdom | The group's current kingdom (heroes have no home kingdom) |
| Month | Chapter (DEC-133) |
| Battle card losses | A lost round's hit (DEC-015, DEC-323) |
| Remove a foe | Remove a standing horde (DEC-320) or cut a horde |
| Monthly quest | A set piece or lead |

Fit: **Direct** works as written once terms are swapped; **None** has no Bloodtower home; **Reword** keeps the idea with a new trigger or cost; **Advantage only** keeps the card's Advantage but drops an ability with no Bloodtower home; **Story only** needs a story to give it meaning; **?** means the card text is still missing.

## What the chart shows

- **It can work.** Of the 87 kinds, 36 fit directly and 44 with a reworded trigger or cost. 3 treasures keep only their Advantage, 3 quest items need a story to mean anything, and only 1 corruption (Tempted) has no home.
- **Skull removal touches nine pieces.** Bloodtower has no Cleanse and no other way to take a skull off a building. Five pieces remove skulls themselves (Infused, Purifying Breath, Blessed Sceptres, Hallowed Reliquary, Acorns of the White Oak), Generous and Lamp of Hope widen a Cleanse, Relic of Light rewards one and the Feverish corruption forbids one. Bloodtower needs a skull-removal rule, or these become something else.
- **Guards are common.** Azkol's Idol, Golden Wolf Pelt, Wraps of Invisibility, Leather Armor and Blessed all shrink a hit, which backs the guard kind (DEC-367).
- **Corruptions mostly cost money.** Seven of the twelve take spirit, which becomes money. With DEC-365's -1 move on top, Bloodtower's corruption is a mix of a slower group and a leaking purse.
- **Six heroes, four boards.** RtDT's starting virtues are printed on 4 hero boards, but Bloodtower has 6 heroes (DEC-099). Two classes would have no board, or starting virtues become app-only.
- **Six per class does not fit the tiles.** 16 tiles cannot give 6 classes 6 virtues each (DEC-156). Tiles shared between classes, or fewer virtues per class, would fix it.
- **Spells have no piece.** RtDT has no spell card or tile, which supports keeping spells apart from virtues (Bob, 2026-10-27, still open). The three Scroll treasures (spend for +4 of one trait) are the nearest thing.
- **Rulings this changes:** DEC-370 partly replaces DEC-368 (tiles stay in the box). DEC-121 (spells are virtues) and DEC-156 (six per class) wait on Q-268.
- **Still missing:** the Spymaster's Stealth virtue name. The 4 kingdom virtues came from Bob's tile images on 2026-10-27.
