# Return to Dark Tower — Items and virtues

Potions, gear, treasures and hero virtues in the RtDT base game, from a Perplexity report Bob ran on 2026-10-27 ([the PDF](assets/items-and-virtues-perplexity-2026-10-27.pdf)), plus a Claude web check the same day. Effects are paraphrased from community sources unless marked; check the physical cards before relying on exact wording. No code here.

RtDT's item kinds are potions, gear, treasures and quest items. Virtues are hero abilities, not items. Items can be used only on your own turn.

## Potions

18 cards in the base box. One use each: spend the card for its effect. Carry any number. Gained by drawing the top card of the potion deck, for example from a citadel's free Reinforce. The Relic Hunter's *Crafty* virtue doubles the number on a spent potion.

| Potion | Effect | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Potion of Dragon's Teeth | Gain 6 warriors | Read in unboxing and playthrough |
| Potion of Purifying Breath | Remove up to 2 skulls from any building | Read in unboxing |
| Potion of the Siren Song | Move any foe up to 2 spaces | Read in unboxing |
| Potion of the Golden Sun | Gain spirit (amount not confirmed) | Seen in a playthrough |
| Potion of Fortune's Favor | Not found | Name only |
| Potion of the Thousand Strides | Not found | Name only |

## Gear

18 cards in six types. Gear lasts until an effect makes you lose or spend it. Carry at most one of each type. Chosen, not drawn, from the gear stacks, for example at a bazaar's free Reinforce.

| Gear | Effect | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Longswords | 1 Melee Advantage | Video |
| Blessed Sceptres | After you Reinforce, remove a skull from a building of your choice | Video |
| Dusky Cloaks | Not found | Name from photo |
| Trusted Maps | Not found | Name from photo |
| Brass Talismans | Not found | Name from photo |
| Leather Armor | Not found | Name from photo |

## Treasures ("magic items")

22 cards in the base box. Stronger than gear, often an Advantage plus an ability; some last, some are spent. Carry up to 4; gaining a 5th means losing one.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Market | 3 treasures face up; a taken one is replaced from the deck |
| Gaining | Choose a face-up treasure when an effect grants one |
| Bazaar | Spend 2 spirit at a Reinforce for a treasure instead of the free gear |

**No list found.** Names in videos and captions disagree. Lead: BoardGameGeek thread 2838905 ("Return to Dark Tower – Expansion Info") mentions a full contents spreadsheet.

## Virtues

Each base hero has 2 starting virtues printed on the hero board, 3 virtue tiles that start inactive, and a kingdom virtue from where they sit. A citadel Reinforce plus 5 spirit unlocks one tile. The box holds 16 tiles: these 12 hero tiles plus 4 kingdom virtues.

### The 12 unlockable hero virtues (base game)

| Hero | Virtue | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Brutal Warlord | Callous | After a battle in which you lost at least 10 warriors, gain a treasure from the market |
| Brutal Warlord | Inspiring | After you Reinforce, gain 6 warriors |
| Brutal Warlord | Relentless | If you double your move, gain +1 Wild Advantage |
| Relic Hunter | Lucky | When you spend (not lose) a treasure, gain the top card of the treasure deck |
| Relic Hunter | Crafty | When you spend a potion, double the number on it |
| Relic Hunter | Inventive | Spend 4 potions to remove a foe from your space |
| Spymaster | Fortunate | You may Reinforce twice per turn at the same building (FAQ: two separate Reinforces) |
| Spymaster | Unseen | When you finish a monthly quest, you may remove a foe instead of gaining spirit |
| Spymaster | Resourceful | At the end of each month, gain 15 warriors |
| Orphaned Scion | Blessed | Spend 1 spirit to prevent up to 6 warrior losses from a battle card or dungeon room |
| Orphaned Scion | Infused | At the start of your turn, remove 1 skull from a building in your home kingdom (confirmed on Bob's photo IMG_5687) |
| Orphaned Scion | Anointed | +1 Wild Advantage for each building with no skulls on or next to your space |

## Notes for Bloodtower

- **Fit with the virtue kinds (DEC-367).** Of the 12 base tiles, 2 are Advantages as they stand (Relentless, Anointed), and Blessed is close to a guard. Most of the rest are perks that run on systems Bloodtower dropped: warriors and spirit (DEC-039, DEC-124), generic Reinforce (DEC-157), home kingdoms and monthly quests. They would need rewording. None is a spell.
- **Still missing:** the base heroes' other starting virtues, the 4 kingdom virtues, 2 potion effects, 4 gear effects and all 22 treasures. Photos of the cards would close these.
