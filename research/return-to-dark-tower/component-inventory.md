# Return to Dark Tower — Component Inventory

Inventory of every physical piece across the official Return to Dark Tower (RtDT) product line, compiled October 2026.
Counts come from the published rulebook, BoardGameGeek, the 2022 BackerKit campaign and retailer listings (see `sources.md`).
Where sources disagree or a detail could not be confirmed, it is flagged **[unverified]**. Check against the physical box when it matters.

### Base game roster (names)

**Heroes (4):** Brutal Warlord, Orphaned Scion, Relic Hunter, Spymaster.

**Adversaries (level 5, 8 total incl. Kickstarter stretch goals):** Ashstrider, Utuk-Ku the Ice Herald, Empress of Shades, Gravemaw, Bane of Omens, Lingering Rot, Isa the Exile, The Gaze Eternal. (Taken from the Dark Horde miniature list, which mirrors the base-game adversaries.)

**Foes (12 types, 4 at each of levels 2, 3 and 4):** Brigands, Oreks, Shadow Wolves, Spine Fiends, Frost Trolls, Lemures, Clan of Neuri, Widowmade Spiders, Dragons, Mormos, Striga, Titan. **[which foe is which level is unverified — check the foe cards]**

**Main goals:** 9 in the base game. The recommended first game is *Recover Azkol's Treasures* vs. *Ashstrider*. Full list **[unverified]**.

**Companions named in sources:** Miras the Horselord (uses caravan tokens), Letha the Dryad (uses siege tree tokens), Hakan the Artificer, Yana, Tomas, Xyr, Grigor, Haraswa, Kamaria (Kamaria's Carpet treasure). Which are main vs. recruitable, and base vs. expansion, **[unverified]**.

**Kingdoms:** North, South, East, West. Terrain types: Desert, Hills, Mountains, Lake, Forest, Grasslands.

**Traits / Advantage types (6 + wild):** Beast, Magic, Humanoid, Melee, Undead, Stealth; Wild counts as any.

**Glyphs (on the Tower, revealed as seals come off):** Banner, Cleanse, Battle, Quest, Reinforce.

## 6. The Tower (hardware summary)

Details for the controller work belong in `research/tower/`; this is just the component-level view.

- Roughly 300 mm tall, three AAA batteries (per a retailer article; reviewers report it drains batteries fast).
- Bluetooth link to the app; the Tower itself has no game logic exposed to players.
- Skulls dropped in the top trigger a drop sensor, then may be held inside or tumble out of an opening into one of the four kingdoms.
- Internal layered drums rotate; this re-routes where skulls fall and which glyphs face which kingdom.
- 12 seals cover openings. When the app calls for a seal removal, LEDs at the base indicate the side and a light behind the seal indicates which one.
- Lights and sound play on events; the app shows a low-battery warning (the % readout was disabled in v1.2.2 because it fluctuated).
- Led by Tim Burrell-Saward (Beasts of Balance), electrical engineering by Charlie Bruce.
