# Return to Dark Tower: Rulebook Analysis

A summary of the official [Rulebook v2.0](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/RtDT_Rulebook-v2-web.pdf) in its own words, not a transcription; Bob supplied it on 2026-10-27. Check the rulebook for exact wording. One known gap: hero setup below says three active virtues are printed on the hero board; the rulebook (p6, p9) and [items-and-virtues.md](items-and-virtues.md) have two hero virtues plus the kingdom virtue tile.

## Overview
- **Type:** Cooperative fantasy board game (Restoration Games, 2023, rulebook v2.0) with a companion app and the Tower, a powered device with 12 seals.
- **Goal:** Complete the main goal, then defeat the adversary before the sixth month ends.
- **Players:** Heroes from the Four Kingdoms win or lose together. A competitive mode is covered on page 24.
- **Hard requirement:** The official app on a Bluetooth device is required.

## Setup Flow
1. **World setup:** Place the board so each kingdom faces a different player. Install Tower batteries, confirm all 12 seals, and place the Tower in the center.
2. **Supply and market:** Set aside warriors, spirit, and 24 skulls. Create 6 gear stacks of 3 identical cards. Draw 3 treasures face up as the market.
3. **Hero setup:** Each player starts with three active virtues printed on the hero board, places three inactive hero virtue tiles, takes a home kingdom virtue, and starts at the home citadel with 7 warriors and 1 spirit.
4. **App setup:** Sync the app to the Tower, pick mode, difficulty (Heroic default, Gritty harder), players, main goal, adversary, and foes (one each of level 2, 3, and 4). Entering a game seed recreates a setup.
5. **Recommended first game:** Recover Azkol's Treasures with Ashstrider.

## Key Concepts
### Resources and Items
- **Warriors:** Unlimited (use a proxy if they run out). Used for battles, dungeons, and quests.
- **Spirit:** Unlimited. Moves farther, gains virtues, removes corruptions, and boosts reinforcing.
- **Items:** Potions (unlimited), gear (one of each type), treasures (up to four), quest items (unlimited). Duplicate gear or a fifth treasure must be lost.

### Corruptions (the main penalty)
- Gained when a hero must lose resources they lack, must place skulls on destroyed buildings, has a home-kingdom building destroyed, or is told to gain one.
- Maximum two. If a hero would gain a third corruption, the cooperative game ends in defeat; in competitive mode, that hero is eliminated. Normally an unresolved loss effect causes one corruption, even if several resources are short. Separate battle cards are separate effects. Building destruction and explicit corruption effects are exceptions to the one-per-effect limit.

### Skulls and Buildings
- Each building holds up to three skulls. A fourth destroys the building and removes its skulls. The home hero gains a corruption.
- The heroes lose when required to take a skull from the supply and none remain—not simply when the supply first becomes empty.

### Glyphs
- As seals are removed, glyphs appear. A glyph facing your home kingdom costs 1 spirit to use the matching action (Banner, Quest, Battle, Reinforce, or Cleanse). A seal is a Tower component, not an action.

### Foes
- Each foe has a level (number of battle cards drawn), traits (Advantage types), a strike event, and a status (from least to most threatening: panicked, unsteady, ready, savage, lethal). Status worsens if foes linger.

## Turn Structure
1. **Start of turn:** Optional Banner action (cannot be saved).
2. **Middle of turn:** Any order of Move, one Heroic action (Cleanse, Battle, or Quest), and one Reinforce.
3. **End of turn:** Drop a skull into the Tower. Events may follow, such as foes striking or spawning, Tower rotation, seal removal, companion events, or new market wares.

Once you drop a skull, you cannot use items that turn.

## Actions
- **Move:** Up to your move value. Spend 1 spirit before moving to double it. Nothing blocks movement.
- **Heroic action (one per turn):** Gain 2 spirit after any heroic action.
  - **Cleanse:** Remove all skulls from a building on your space.
  - **Battle:** Defeat a foe on your space. Draw cards equal to its level and use Advantages to improve them.
  - **Quest:** Complete a quest or explore a dungeon.
- **Reinforce (once per turn, at a building):**
  - Citadel: free potion, or spend spirit for a virtue.
  - Sanctuary: free spirit, or spend spirit to remove all corruptions.
  - Village: free warriors, or spend for more warriors.
  - Bazaar: free gear, or spend for a treasure.
  - Haggle die (optional before reinforcing): may add 3 warriors, a potion, or a gear, or cancel the reinforce.

## Advantages
- Six types matching foe and dungeon traits: Beast, Magic, Humanoid, Melee, Undead, Stealth. Wild counts as any type.
- Automatic (black) advantages are free. Conditional (gray) ones cost resources.
- Max 10 advantages per heroic action. Each room in a dungeon can be improved only once.

## Monthly Quests and Duration
- Each month (after the first) the app offers a **companion quest** (new companion or quest item) and an **adversary quest** (failure advances the adversary).
- Average turns per month depend on player count (6 to 9 turns for 1 to 4 players in the table; Gritty has fewer).
- The game lasts six months.

## Winning and Losing
- **Win:** Complete the main goal, then defeat the adversary (five battle cards in one battle, with optional retreat after at least one card).
- **Lose immediately:** A hero gains a third corruption, a required skull cannot be taken from the supply, or the sixth month ends.

## Competitive Mode (page 24)
- Each hero must complete three heroic tests, enter the Tower dungeon, and find the relic.
- Heroes can trade if all agree. Eliminated heroes make their home kingdom dormant.
- If the sixth month ends, Azkol wins and no one does.

## Analysis
- **Pressure mechanics:** Skulls are both the timer and the penalty. Each turn requires dropping one skull into the Tower; it does not necessarily emerge immediately. Cleansing returns skulls to the supply, while destroyed buildings remove their skulls from the game.
- **Corruption budget:** Two corruptions is a tight limit. Gaining a corruption on a building loss or a failed resource loss can cascade quickly.
- **Resource tension:** Spirit is needed for movement, virtues, and glyphs. Taking the Heroic action also refills spirit, so it is a steady resource.
- **Cooperative balance:** Heroes share the win condition, so planning and trading matter. Notably, the rulebook encourages discussing plans, but each hero makes final decisions.
- **Difficulty levers:** Gritty means fewer turns per month. Every standard setup includes a level 4 foe; foe and adversary selections change the challenges rather than making that level optional.
- **Replay value:** Game seeds let you reproduce a setup, which suits a player who tests assumptions and compares outcomes.

## Rulebook Notes and Limitations
- The rulebook defers many specifics to the app, including events, adversary behavior, and foe status changes. The printed rules alone cannot predict a full game.
- Some terms (for example, the exact effect of certain virtues or companions) depend on card text not included in this document.
