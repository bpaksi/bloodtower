# Characters: the four hunters

> **1.0 cut (2026-10-28):** this describes the full Dracula. For 1.0, [DEC-508](../../game-design/cards/DEC-508.md) overrides it where they differ. The full version is kept in [dracula-v2](../dracula-v2/characters.md).

Status: draft for reaction. Nothing here is decided.

Four heroes, one per player. Each has a public reason for being in Vellmoor and a private tie to the Count. The private tie is something the app can reveal later as a story beat, a personal quest, or a weakness the Count exploits.

Setting shorthand: **Vellmoor** is a valley village under the Count's castle (the tower). The four kingdoms of the board are the valley's four parishes.

---

## Sister Agnes Hallow (the cleric)

**Role:** healer and warder. Blesses gear, holds back thralls, cleanses fallen buildings.

**Appearance:** Tall and gaunt, mid-forties, in a grey habit gone brown at the hem from travel. Close-cropped iron-grey hair under a plain coif. Pale grey eyes that rarely blink. A shiny burn scar covers her right palm in the shape of a cross. She carries a battered tin reliquary on a leather strap and a walking staff shod with silver.

**Mannerisms:**
- Hums plainchant under her breath when she's afraid, slightly off key.
- Answers questions with questions.
- Touches the scar with her thumb before she lies.
- Eats very little and gives most of her bread away.

**Backstory:** Agnes kept the infirmary at the Convent of Saint Ysolde in the high pass. Ten winters ago, a fevered traveler was carried in during a snowstorm. She nursed him for three nights. On the fourth, half the sisters were dead and the rest were gone into the dark. Agnes survived because she had fallen asleep in the chapel holding the reliquary; the burn on her palm is where she gripped it when something tried to drag her out. The Church called it plague and closed the convent. She has walked the roads ever since, looking for the traveler's face.

**Private tie:** The traveler was the Count. She knows what he looks like, but he spared her deliberately, and she doesn't know why.

**Voice line:** "You keep a fire burning all night, innkeeper. What are you afraid of?"

## Tomas Varga (the warrior)

**Role:** front-line fighter. Absorbs damage, breaks doors, leads assaults.

**Appearance:** Broad and heavy in the shoulders, fifties, with a grey-shot black beard and a face like a split log. Missing the last two fingers of his left hand. Wears a wolf-pelt cloak over old castle livery he has dyed black to hide the crest. Carries a long-hafted axe and a soldier's short sword.

**Mannerisms:**
- Always sits with his back to a wall and his eyes on the door.
- Taps the axe haft three times before he speaks to a stranger.
- Calls everyone younger than him "lad" or "lass," including the cleric.
- Drinks heavily but never seems drunk.

**Backstory:** Tomas was born in Vellmoor and served twenty years in the castle guard. The old lords were hard men, but men. Then the Count came, and the guard was told to stop asking where the servant girls went. Tomas stopped asking for three years. When his own niece was summoned to the castle, he finally walked out, taking her with him over the wall. They lost two fingers and a horse in the escape. He came home because the village is dying and he believes he owes it.

**Private tie:** His niece never fully recovered. She sleeps in the day and won't eat. He has told no one.

**Voice line:** "I stood his gate for three years. I know which door squeaks."

---

## Wren Kessel (the hunter)

**Role:** scout and ranged fighter. Tracks, finds hidden crypts, kills from a distance.

**Appearance:** Wiry and weathered, late twenties, short and quick. Ragged chestnut hair tied back with a strip of black funeral ribbon. Dirt worked permanently into her knuckles. A heavy crossbow on her back and a bandolier of silver-tipped bolts, each one scratched with a name. Dark green oilskin coat, gravedigger's boots.

**Mannerisms:**
- Says very little. When she does talk, it's blunt.
- Smells the air when she walks into a room, and isn't subtle about it.
- Chews cloves constantly. They help with the smell.
- Keeps count on her fingers. Nobody knows of what.

**Backstory:** Wren is the daughter of Vellmoor's gravedigger. For years she helped her father bury the village dead, and she was the first to notice that some of the graves were empty by spring. Her father went to the castle to complain to the steward. He came back after a week, smiling and pale, and she buried him herself, properly this time, with a stake. She has been hunting ever since. Each bolt carries the name of someone she put back in the ground.

**Private tie:** One bolt is blank. It's for her mother, who disappeared the same week. Wren believes she's still somewhere in the castle.

**Voice line:** "Grave in the churchyard's been dug from the inside. Thought you'd want to know."

## Emeric Lutz (the unlikely hero)

**Role:** scholar and alchemist. Brews remedies, reads clues, solves the riddle of the true coffin.

**Appearance:** Small and slight, barely twenty, with round wire spectacles that are always slipping down his nose. Ink-stained fingers, a too-big tweed coat with patched elbows, and a satchel that clinks. Wears a string of garlic bulbs around his neck that he insists is "purely precautionary."

**Mannerisms:**
- Talks fast when nervous, which is always.
- Quotes almanacs, herbals, and folklore with page numbers.
- Polishes his spectacles whenever someone raises their voice.
- Faints at the sight of blood, then wakes up and takes notes.

**Backstory:** Emeric was apprenticed to Master Gerhardt, an apothecary in the city, who spent his last years writing a treatise on "the Vellmoor sanguine affliction." Gerhardt died suddenly in a locked study, his throat untouched and his blood gone. Emeric inherited the half-finished treatise, a crate of reagents, and debts. He came to Vellmoor to finish the book and prove his master wasn't mad. He is terrified of everything and has come anyway.

**Private tie:** Gerhardt's notes include a letter from the Count, politely asking him to stop his research. Emeric hasn't read the last page yet; it's in a cipher.

**Voice line:** "According to Halbrook's Herbal, page two hundred and twelve, garlic is only effective when fresh. These are — these are fresh, aren't they?"
