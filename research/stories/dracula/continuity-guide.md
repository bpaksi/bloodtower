## 0. Global rules (settled)

- **Every game has all four hunters** (Agnes, Tomas, Wren, Emeric). A hunter can be **away** from a scene or a stretch of moments (`~no-<hunter>` variants, or "miss the fight" after following the mourner). Away means not in frame and not speaking.
- **A hunter never appears in their own absent variant.** `~no-wren` art has no Wren in it at all: not in the background, not as a silhouette. The speaker swap must be visible (e.g. `lid-choice~nailed~no-wren`: Tomas holds the hammer).
- **Stills are full-bleed.** No border, no frame, no vignette card edge.
- **Faces match the portraits.** A character's face, hair and signature kit in a still is the face on their portrait/ref sheet. One face per character (corruption and arc-portrait questions are pending with Gamewright, see §4).
- Style lock: pen-and-ink, dense crosshatch, muted colour, no text in image (bible §0). One palette per time of day: night interior candle-yellow on blue-black; dawn grey with soft pink; mist white. One palette across a scene (audit: `.02` sepia vs `.lid-choice` blue-night is wrong).
- Dashboard art never shows `.private.*` content. Spoiler table: §1.11.

## 1. Characters: fixed look, arc changes, spoiler rules

### 1.1 Sister Agnes Hallow
- **Fixed:** tall, gaunt, mid-40s; grey habit browned at the hem; **plain coif** (not a veil) over cropped iron-grey hair; pale grey eyes; battered tin reliquary on a leather strap (empty); silver-shod walking staff; **cross-shaped burn scar on the RIGHT palm** whenever that hand shows (chars:15). No crucifix (no source names one).
- **Gesture:** thumb on the scar before she lies / at tense moments (chars:20; n1-burgomasters-house.md:42; n23-sergius-blade.md:38).
- **Arc:** night 2–3 Holy Symbol site sets reliquary in the niche (n23-catacombs-of-saint-ysolde.md:87). Rescued: scar stays, pain goes (n5-ending-rescued.md:34). Turned: reliquary drops, **burn gone** (n5-ending-turned.md:19; intended, C-13).
- **Corruption:** pending (Gamewright/Bob). Until ruled, none drawn.
- **Spoilers:** never show the traveler's face in her memories before `n23-sergius-blade.05`; never show "The one who prayed" painting before the gallery (n5-portrait-gallery.md:35).

### 1.2 Tomas Varga
- **Fixed:** broad, heavy-shouldered, 50s; grey-shot **black** beard; face like a split log; **wolf-pelt cloak** over castle livery **dyed black, no visible crest**; long axe + short sword (chars:35). **LEFT hand missing the last two fingers, always** (chars:35; tok:154,208).
- **Gesture:** taps the axe haft three times before speaking to a stranger (chars; n1-lantern-and-bell.md:20).
- **Arc:** may carry Sergiu's sword (n23-sergius-blade.md:43). Turned: lowers the axe, faces the hunters like the gate (n5-ending-turned.md:23).
- **Spoilers:** ten fingers = lookalike only (n23-catacombs-of-saint-ysolde.md:43,93). Dashboard never shows a ten-fingered Tomas.

### 1.3 Wren Kessel
- **Fixed:** wiry, short, late 20s; ragged **chestnut** hair **tied back with black funeral ribbon**; dirt in the knuckles; **dark green oilskin coat**, gravedigger's boots; **crossbow on her back**; bandolier of silver-tipped bolts each scratched with a name; chews cloves (chars:55,60).
- **Hair (C-10, settled):** cut short-ish years ago but long enough to tie; same length all game. Ilka's "You've cut your hair" means since Wren was a child.
- **Arc:** after the funeral a new bolt reads D-O-R-K-A (n2 `.09.private.wren`, private only). Rescued: "all your bolts have names now". Endures: mother bolt still unfired. Turned: crossbow drops, she smells the air and smiles.
- **Spoilers:** the mother bolt reads **blank/illegible** in all art until n5-final-confrontation.md:24 (C-2).

### 1.4 Emeric Lutz
- **Fixed:** slight, ~20; **round wire spectacles with real lenses** (draw refraction when large in frame), slipping down his nose; ink-stained fingers; **too-big tweed coat with patched elbows**; **garlic string at the neck**; clinking satchel (chars:75). No resemblance to a famous boy wizard (audit).
- **Arc:** n1 arrives soaked (open:43). Turned: spectacles fall, not picked up (n5-ending-turned.md:31).
- **Spoilers:** plain-glass spectacles = lookalike only (n23-catacombs-of-saint-ysolde.md:55).

### 1.5 Dorka Halasz (settled)
- **Only ever shown risen or at rest.** Never as the living girl.
- **Look:** 19; **pale**, **no colour in the cheeks**; **her wedding dress** (white) (n2-burgomasters-funeral.md:15); two bites at the neck (one old, one after death, no bruising) — the collar must not hide them, but they are only drawn large in the `examine-body` private art.
- **At rest, before rising** (`.02`, `.03`, investigate, `.lid-choice*`): face **still, eyes closed**, peaceful and too pale. No fangs, no smile, no open eyes (spoiler guard for `.04`).
- **Risen** (`.04`–`.06`): sitting up / standing in the wedding dress, **a too-sweet, unsettling smile** ("the sweetest smile", `.04`). Holy-symbol variant: shields her eyes and still smiles (`.04~holy-symbol`). Her speaking portrait is the risen look.
- **After `.07`:** at peace, **garlic in her mouth**, "only a girl in a wedding dress again".
- Flower crown on the delivered portrait: not in any source; drop it (if kept, it must persist into every still).

### 1.6 Anton Morosz
- **Fixed:** ledger-keeper burgomaster (30 years), chain of office; round, soft face, not a fighter. **Not mistakable for the Count** (no tall gaunt figure, avoid the white-moustache old-man read; n23-sergius-blade.md:30).
- **Death (C-5, settled):** fell on the stair at Lenka's door; carried down to the parlour; poker still in his hand at the house.
- **Funeral:** in a shut coffin on trestles; his coat (inside the coffin with him, searched at `anton-coat`) holds a sealed holy-water flask and the "lady's rooms" scrap. Heard only as remembered voice.

### 1.7 Lenka Morosz
- **Fixed:** 19, foundling, presses mountain violets (cast:37). **Ludmila's face** (n5-portrait-gallery.md:26–27). Hair/eyes/build unspecified → **BOB Q5**.
- **Before taken:** never on screen. **Captive:** white dress, high room; never bitten, no marks (rulings.md:33). Final: holds the last violets, opens the window.
- **Where held (C-8, settled):** "the lady's rooms" = the high room at the top of the stair behind the gallery, in the east wing.
- **Spoilers:** no Lenka face on the dashboard before captivity art, and any earlier face is the Ludmila spoiler → **BOB Q2**. No open window with Lenka in pre-ending art.

### 1.8 The Count (Vlad)
- **Fixed:** tall, all in black, pale long fingers (tok:141); one ageless face across 400 years of portraits (n5-portrait-gallery.md:6); the dark-haired brother (tok:110,117). Beautiful very old handwriting (cream letter, Tome).
- **Age by night:** **BOB Q1** (default until ruled: Stoker, one face, old/white-moustached when hungry nights 1–3, younger and darker after feeding).
- **History (C-7, settled):** he is the 400-year lord who **came back** in Tomas's guard years.
- **Spoilers:** face hidden before `n23-sergius-blade.03` (hand, mist, silhouette, castle only). At the funeral he is **not shown at all**; only his servant.

### 1.9 Funeral NPCs
- **Dorka's mother:** grieving older woman, black shawl; kneels at the foot of Dorka's coffin; bars the window if warned (n2-burgomasters-funeral.md:121; question-mother privates). Non-speaking NPC (C-12).
- **Castle servant:** a man **in castle grey, cloaked**; carries a lantern; bows like the traveler's servant at Saint Ysolde; lays a pressed violet (n2-burgomasters-funeral.md:100). Non-speaking on screen; his words are only quoted in hunters' thoughts (C-12).
- **Hanne:** stout innkeeper, cleaver in apron, garlic (open:25; cast:34). Reads the words badly from Oskar's book at `.01`.
- **Mourners:** the village on benches; anonymous.
