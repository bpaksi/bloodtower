## Shared look

- **Palette:** cold teal and moonlit grey-blue, near-black stone, with red only for wine, blood and his cloak's lining.
- **Line and texture:** ink hatching and aged parchment for maps and documents; paintings for scenes (DEC-478).
- **Shapes:** pointed arches and arcades, tall thin towers, heavy stone against empty sky.

The maps show adventure spoilers, so they stay reference for designers and don't guide what players see early.
