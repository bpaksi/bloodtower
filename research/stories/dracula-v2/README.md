## Seed ideas (Claude, for reaction)

- **Tone:** gothic horror, a seasonal one-shot for Halloween rather than a multi-session saga.
- **The tower as the castle:** the Count's keep. Night falls when the tower wakes; red/purple LED washes, bells and wolves for sounds.
- **Day/night clock:** daytime turns are safe for travel and preparation; at night the vampire hunts. Sunrise is the win timer, or the deadline to reach the crypt.
- **Skulls as the thrall:** each skull drop is a villager turned. Skulls on a building mean that town has fallen and must be cleansed.
- **Hunter's kit:** garlic, holy water, silver, stakes, a crucifix as gear; sanctuaries supply them.
- **Find the coffin:** like the 1981 keys, the party gathers clues to which crypt holds the true coffin (one per kingdom, three are decoys). Drum glyphs mark the crypts.
- **Bitten heroes:** a hero who loses a fight is bitten instead of dying, gains power but slowly turns (a corruption-style track). Cure before the final night.
- **Final confrontation:** storm the castle drum by drum, then the duel at dawn.
