Ravenloft is a Gothic-horror adventure about heroes trapped in a mist-shrouded valley ruled by a vampire: Count Strahd von Zarovich. To escape, they must unravel his history, find tools that can help destroy him, and survive his haunted castle—while Strahd actively hunts and manipulates them. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

I’m covering the original _I6: Ravenloft_ module by Tracy and Laura Hickman, published in 1983, rather than the much larger fifth-edition _Curse of Strahd_ adaptation. **Spoilers** throughout. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

## Story overview

### 1. The heroes enter a trap

A mysterious letter draws the adventurers toward Barovia, ostensibly to help its inhabitants. They travel through ominous woods into a valley enclosed by deadly supernatural fog. Above the frightened village stands Castle Ravenloft, the home of its tyrannical ruler, Strahd. The setup quickly turns from “answer a call for help” into “you are trapped in the vampire’s territory.” [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

### 2. Ireena is the immediate human stake

Strahd has been attacking the burgomaster’s household and pursuing his adopted daughter, Ireena Kolyana. Her father is dead, and she has already suffered Strahd’s bites. She and her brother, Ismark, give the party a personal reason to oppose him beyond simply escaping Barovia. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

Ireena resembles Tatyana, the woman Strahd loved centuries ago. That resemblance links the present danger to the tragedy underlying the adventure. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

### 3. A fortune reading provides direction

The fortune-teller Madam Eva can offer cryptic clues about objects that will help the heroes defeat Strahd and where they will find him. Rather than exploring the castle solely for treasure, the party has specific mysteries to solve and destinations to interpret. [dungeonofsigns.blogspot](http://dungeonofsigns.blogspot.com/2016/02/i6-ravenloft-review.html)

The important objects are:

- The Sunsword: a magical weapon useful against Strahd.
- The Holy Symbol: another powerful aid against the vampire.
- The _Tome of Strahd_: his written history, revealing the origins of his undeath and obsession. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

### 4. The castle reveals the original tragedy

Strahd fell in love with Tatyana, who loved his younger brother, Sergei. Interpreting her rejection as a consequence of his age, Strahd made a pact with evil powers and murdered his brother. Tatyana threw herself from the castle rather than accept him, and Strahd became a vampire. His present pursuit of Ireena repeats that history of jealousy, possession, and violence. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

Castle Ravenloft becomes both the adventure’s main dungeon and the physical record of that tragedy: towers, old chambers, servants, crypts, traps, and supernatural manifestations. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

### 5. Strahd fights a campaign, not just a final battle

Strahd does not simply wait in the last room. He stalks the party, strikes at inconvenient moments, uses deception and magical influence, and withdraws when threatened. The heroes search for him while he wears them down. Ultimately, their objective is to destroy him; an optional epilogue reunites Ireena with her lost lover and releases them from their recurring tragedy. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

## Set pieces versus random encounters

The useful distinction is between encounters attached to particular locations, encounters generated during movement, and story elements randomized before or during play. Ravenloft uses all three; “randomized” does not always mean “random wandering monster.” [dungeonofsigns.blogspot](http://dungeonofsigns.blogspot.com/2016/02/i6-ravenloft-review.html)

These are prepared scenes, hazards, or discoveries that the party encounters by reaching the relevant place—not necessarily in a fixed sequence.

### Random encounters

The original module has separate encounter tables for daytime, nighttime, and the castle interior. These generate danger between the keyed locations and prevent the castle from feeling like a collection of static rooms. [tasker](https://tasker.land/2025/02/13/review-i6-ravenloft-for-advanced-dungeons-dragons/)

Creatures and threats highlighted in accounts of the original adventure include:

- Wolves and other undead-horror staples such as zombies, ghouls, and ghosts.
- Vampires and witches among the castle’s inhabitants.
- Rust monsters on the castle’s random encounter table—a distinctly traditional D&D element amid the Gothic atmosphere. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

These encounters supply uncertainty and ongoing pressure while the set pieces supply distinctive scenes, clues, and hazards. Strahd’s own interventions add a third source of danger: purposeful attacks by the antagonist, rather than simply whatever a wandering-monster roll produces. [dungeonofsigns.blogspot](http://dungeonofsigns.blogspot.com/2016/02/i6-ravenloft-review.html)

### Randomized story structure

The card system changes major features of the adventure:

- Where the Sunsword, Holy Symbol, and _Tome of Strahd_ are located.
- Strahd’s designated location.
- His particular objective for this version of the adventure. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

An important original-module detail: pursuing Ireena is not the only possible immediate plan. Strahd’s randomized objective can instead involve replacing a player character, creating an evil magic item, or destroying the Sunsword. His tragic history remains the same, but his current scheme changes how he acts toward the party. [en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenloft_(module)>)

## What the adventure feels like

Think of it as a haunted-castle hunt with an intelligent predator moving through the same space as the heroes. The village supplies the stakes, the fortune reading supplies direction, the castle supplies exploration and memorable horror scenes, and the random encounters and Strahd’s attacks keep the expedition unstable. Its story emerges from that combination rather than from a rigid sequence of scripted battles. [dungeonofsigns.blogspot](http://dungeonofsigns.blogspot.com/2016/02/i6-ravenloft-review.html)
