**Status:** proposed, for Bob at G-plan with [C2-session-shell.md](C2-session-shell.md).
**Source:** the 2026-10-31 planning answer to Bob's "Things to consider before planning v1.0" (question 3: "Is the scope of v1.0 too big. Should be break it into milestones?"), the 1.0 cut (DEC-502, DEC-508, DEC-691), the [release punch list](../../../research/game-design/release-1.0-punch-list.md), and the evening walkthrough (AREA-100).

1.0 still has the full battle loop, a Tower finale with no content yet, the Tomb, corruption, the heroes with their virtues, a new story and its art. It is split into four milestones. Each ends in a game the table can play, and each has its own plan and its own G-signoff (`docs/sdlc.md`: one plan per milestone).

## Rules across the four

- **The order is fixed; the step lists are not.** Only C2 is planned in full now. C3's plan is written when C2's audit is accepted (G-A in C2), because the audit decides what C3 keeps, reworks or replaces. C4 and C5 are planned when their open questions are answered, so no step is planned against a question that may still turn.
- **Each step builds 1.0 cards only.** A step lists the deliverables it builds as `Builds:` (DEC-355, DEC-505), and only 1.0 deliverables (DEC-696). A step that writes a pack lists its story's area, AREA-097 for The Ruined Keep, instead of each story card (DEC-697).
- **The 1.0 gap list is shared out.** DEC-696 makes the 1.0 list of `pnpm graph gaps` the to-do list. Each milestone plan names the gaps it closes. G-1.0 needs the list empty.
- **Dracula is not in 1.0.** It ships in a drop after 1.0 (DEC-691). The only Dracula change in these plans is C2 step 2, which takes Long out of its settings (DEC-502, DEC-508).
- **Tower safety and the error rule hold in every step**, as in the root `CLAUDE.md`.
