# CREDITS — shared/dt1981

All assets in this directory are **original code-generated recreations** made for Bob's game.

- Audio: synthesized from measured pitch/duration tables as 1-bit-style square waves. No samples from copyrighted recordings were copied into the output.
- Slides: procedural SVG pen-and-ink **homages** (not traces or copies of Milton Bradley film cels or other copyrighted art), rasterized to PNG.
- Story-neutral bookend assets only.

Reference material used for measurement and visual style study remains under `/workspace/research/dark-tower-1981/` and must not be shipped.
