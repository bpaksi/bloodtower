# POC v2 flow rules (Gamewright, Oct 15 2026, draft)
Target: apps/burgomaster-funeral-poc/docs/poc-v2-flow.md. Source: Bob's POC feedback, Oct 15 2026.

V2-1 Screen roles. Shared cut scenes, narration and shared audio play on the Dashboard. iPads and phones handle each hunter's choices and private scenes.
V2-2 Host controls on both screens. Begin, Continue, Won/Lost and the pause menu work on the Dashboard and on any hunter device. One shared lock: the first press counts, the other screens update right away, and the press is logged with which device made it.
V2-3 Next step. The Dashboard always shows one large next-step prompt, e.g. "Waiting on Mara, Tomas". Each hunter shows a status (choosing, locked, sitting out). Devices show the same prompt and highlight it when the hunter it belongs to is the one who has to act.
V2-4 Queue. When several hunters choose in the same step, nothing plays until every present hunter has locked in. Results then play on the Dashboard one at a time, in seat order. Private results play on the owning device at the same moment their slot comes up. Each queued result is logged.
V2-5 Replay. Every screen has a "Replay last" button. Replaying is local: it doesn't move the game forward or change other screens.
V2-6 Story log. A recap of every moment played so far, in order. Any shared moment in it can be replayed. Private moments are listed and playable only on the owning hunter's device.
V2-7 Hunter tabs. A device with more than one hunter shows one tab per hunter, with a badge when that hunter has a choice waiting.
V2-8 Dashboard sidebar. Hunter list on the left; selecting a hunter shows that hunter's public view on the right. The main area is the story view. Private content never appears on the Dashboard.
V2-9 After the POC (already designed, R-WIZ): splash screen, then the setup wizard.
