# POC v2 flow rules (Gamewright, Oct 15 2026, draft)
Target: apps/burgomaster-funeral-poc/docs/poc-v2-flow.md. Source: Bob's POC feedback and rulings, Oct 15 2026.

V2-1 Screen roles. The Story board is display only, with no interaction at all: the Mac opens it full screen in a browser and shows it on a TV. Shared cut scenes, narration and shared audio play there. iPads and phones handle each hunter's choices, private scenes and all controls.
V2-2 Host controls on iPads only. Begin, Continue, Won/Lost and the pause menu are on the iPads only; any joined iPad can press them. One shared lock: the first press counts, every screen updates right away, and the press is logged with which device made it. Joining is by a QR code shown on the Story board.
V2-3 Next step. The Story board always shows one large next-step prompt, e.g. "Waiting on Mara, Tomas". Each hunter shows a status (choosing, locked, sitting out). Devices show the same prompt and highlight it when the hunter it belongs to is the one who has to act.
V2-4 Queue. When several hunters choose in the same step, nothing plays until every present hunter has locked in. Results then play on the Story board one at a time, in seat order. Private results play on the owning device at the same moment their slot comes up. Each queued result is logged.
V2-5 Replay. Every screen has a "Replay last" button. Replaying is local: it doesn't move the game forward or change other screens.
V2-6 Story log. A recap of every moment played so far, in order. Any shared moment in it can be replayed. Private moments are listed and playable only on the owning hunter's device.
V2-7 Hunter tabs. A device with more than one hunter shows one tab per hunter, with a badge when that hunter has a choice waiting.
V2-8 Story board sidebar. Hunter list on the left, display only: it follows the hunter currently acting, or cycles through hunters when no one is acting, and shows that hunter's public view on the right. The main area is the story view. Private content never appears on the Story board.
